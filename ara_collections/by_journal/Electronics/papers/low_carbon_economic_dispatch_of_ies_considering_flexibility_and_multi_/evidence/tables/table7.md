# Table 7: Results of multiple independent runs of various algorithms

- **Source**: Table 7, Section 4.3 (page 21). Located in the upper-middle of the page.
- **Caption**: "Results of multiple independent runs of various algorithms."
- **Screenshot**: table7.png

Statistics over 30 independent runs per algorithm (population size 50, 200 iterations). Metric is TOPSIS relative closeness of the Pareto-optimal solution to the ideal solution; runtime in seconds. IPSO = improved PSO (this paper's method).

| Algorithm | Maximum | Mean | Variance | Runtime/(s) |
|-----------|---------|------|----------|-------------|
| PSO | 0.8 | 0.786 | 7.892 × 10⁻⁵ | 134 |
| DBO | 0.82 | 0.808 | 6.485 × 10⁻⁵ | 117 |
| IPSO | 0.86 | 0.849 | 5.733 × 10⁻⁵ | 105 |
