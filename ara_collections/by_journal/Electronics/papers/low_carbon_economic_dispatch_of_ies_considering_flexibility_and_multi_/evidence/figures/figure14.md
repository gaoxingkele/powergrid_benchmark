## Trend summary
Per §4.2.3 the EV charge/discharge price fluctuates between 0.48 and 1 yuan/kWh, giving EVs a flexible window to time charging/discharging. Price is low (≈0.48) overnight, steps up to ≈0.9 during the day with a ≈1.0 peak near hour 14, then declines in the evening. Values approximate; stated bounds 0.48–1 yuan/kWh are verbatim.
