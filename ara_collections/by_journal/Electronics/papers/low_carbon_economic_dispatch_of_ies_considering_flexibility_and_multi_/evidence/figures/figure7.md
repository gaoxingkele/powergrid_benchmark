## Trend summary
Cooling supply tracks the cooling-load line, peaking midday (≈1000+ kW around hours 12–16). Electric Chiller supplies the majority of cooling (larger stacked segment), with the Absorption Chiller supplying the remainder, consistent with the §4.2.1 statement that cooling is "mainly provided by electric refrigerators, and the remaining cooling power is supplemented by absorption refrigerators."
