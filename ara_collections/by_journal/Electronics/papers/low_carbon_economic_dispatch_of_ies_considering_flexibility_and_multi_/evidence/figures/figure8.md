## Trend summary
Scenario 5 (with EV participation) lies at or above Scenario 2 at almost every hour; the gap is largest during the EV grid-connection window (≈18:00–08:00), where system flexibility is "significantly improved" (§4.2.1). Both dip to a minimum near 12:00 (Scenario 5 ≈0.20), the low point the sensitivity study (§4.2.4, Figure 15) targets. Values approximate; the 12:00 Scenario-5 value 0.2 is stated verbatim in text.
