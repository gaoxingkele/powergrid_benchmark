| 1–7 | ≈0.2 | ≈0.5 | ≈0.4 |

## Trend summary
Per §4.2.2 the stated maxima are electricity 1.2, cooling 0.6, heat 0.6 yuan and minima electricity 0.2, cooling 0.15, heat 0.15 yuan. Electricity price rises sharply to its 1.2 plateau in the evening peak (≈19:00–22:00); when the electricity–thermal/cooling price gap is large (e.g. 19:00–22:00) the aggregator performs energy substitution to cut cost. Values approximate except the stated maxima/minima.
