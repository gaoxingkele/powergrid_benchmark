## Trend summary
Demand response is largest (most negative, curtailment/shift-out) in the high-price evening window; the deepest negative bars (approaching −700 to −800 kW) occur around hours 19–22, consistent with §4.2.2 noting energy substitution from 19:00–22:00 when electricity price is high. Positive (shift-in) bars appear in low-price hours and near hour 24. Magnitudes approximate.
