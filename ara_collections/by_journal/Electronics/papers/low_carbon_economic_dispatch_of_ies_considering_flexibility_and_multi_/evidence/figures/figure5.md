## Trend summary
The stacked supply bars track the electrical-load overlay across 24 h. Positive contributions come chiefly from WT, PV, Electricity Purchase, Gas Turbine, and battery/EV discharge; negative bars (below zero, to ≈ −2000 kW around hours 10–16) represent Electric Boiler / Electric Chiller consumption and battery/EV charging plus electricity selling. Per the §4.2.1 text: the system tends to purchase electricity from the grid to reduce carbon emissions, and EVs replace batteries for charge/discharge to balance electrical power. Segment magnitudes approximate — see Table 5 for scenario aggregates.
