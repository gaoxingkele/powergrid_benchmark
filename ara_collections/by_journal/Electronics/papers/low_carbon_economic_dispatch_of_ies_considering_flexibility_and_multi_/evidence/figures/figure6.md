## Trend summary
Positive heating supply is dominated by Gas Turbine and Electric Boiler, tracking the heating-load line across the day; negative bars are heat-storage charging (HST.ch) and Absorption Chiller heat consumption. Per §4.2.1: under the carbon-trading mechanism the system reduces gas-turbine output and uses more electric boiler; the heat storage tank releases heat at 12:00 when supply is insufficient to meet the balance. Magnitudes approximate.
