## Trend summary
Each EV category charges/discharges in its own grid-connection window (Table 4). Positive charging bars cluster in early-morning and low-price hours; negative discharging bars appear in high-price periods. Per §4.2.3, Category I EVs discharge during high-price periods after connecting, initiate charging at 21:00, discharge again at 22:00, then recharge before disconnection to leave fully charged — illustrating price-responsive arbitrage. Magnitudes approximate.
