## Trend summary
Thermal demand response shows mostly negative (curtailed/shifted-out) bars through the midday hours (≈ −100 to −200 kW around hours 10–18) and positive shift-in bars in the evening (around hours 20–22, up to ≈ +100 to +150 kW). Pattern is complementary to the electricity DR — energy is substituted across carriers to reduce cost. Magnitudes approximate.
