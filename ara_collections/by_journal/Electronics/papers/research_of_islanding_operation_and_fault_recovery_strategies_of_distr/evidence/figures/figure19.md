After-reconstruction voltage (orange) at/above before (yellow) for essentially all nodes; the profile
shows a deep dip near node ~11-12 (fault split at S9) and a jump up around node ~13 where a DG island
forms. Green difference bars positive across most nodes. Reconstruction into two DG islands + main
reconnection raises the minimum voltage 0.0046 pu and cuts losses 5.64 kW (Table 5). Per-node values
≈ approximate; endpoints exact from Table 5.
