## Trend summary
The 500 generated wind curves (Figure 2) are clustered (K-means, Section 4.2) into 5 representative
curves with distinct shapes and time-shifted peaks. Each is time-varying with 1-2 humps over the day;
peaks roughly 450-650 kW. The reduction preserves diversity (curves clearly separated) while making
the scenario-weighted optimization tractable. Exact values unreadable; only shape/ordering reliable.
