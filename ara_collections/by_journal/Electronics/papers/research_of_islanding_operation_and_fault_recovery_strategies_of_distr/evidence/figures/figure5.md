## Trend summary
The 500 PV scenarios (Figure 4) reduced by K-means to 5 representatives. Each retains the single
daytime bell (zero at night, single mid-day peak) but peaks are shifted in time/height (≈450-550 kW),
capturing prediction-error diversity. Sharper single peaks than the wind representatives (Figure 3),
reflecting PV's deterministic diurnal envelope plus stochastic error. Exact values unreadable.
