## Trend summary
3D waterfall of 500 PV output scenarios over one typical day, generated with a normal-distribution
prediction-error model (Eq. 42). Output is zero at night (curve edges) and rises to a broad mid-day
plateau ≈600 kW peak, forming a characteristic single daytime bell. Scenario spread (uncertainty
band) is largest during the daytime ramp. Individual curves unreadable; ensemble envelope is the
usable content. Reduced to 5 representatives in Figure 5.
