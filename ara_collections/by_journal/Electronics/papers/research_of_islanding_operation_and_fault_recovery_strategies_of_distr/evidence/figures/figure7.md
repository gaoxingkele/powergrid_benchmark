## Trend summary
Island 2 with node 6 = wind power. All node voltages across all 5 periods stay within
[1.0884, 1.1] pu — inside the [1.08, 1.1] safe band, no limit violation. Node voltages vary
modestly by period as wind output and load change (wind proportion 31.65%/34.09%/28.85%/26.04%/33.21%
of supply). The horizontal "Average value" plane in panel (a) marks the mean. Confirms voltage safety
holds even at the peak new-energy penetration of 34.09%.
