## Trend summary
For each node the after-reconstruction voltage (orange) lies at or above the before-reconstruction
voltage (yellow); the green difference bars are all ≥0 and largest at mid-feeder nodes. Voltage
profile dips through nodes ~5-17 then jumps up at node ~18 (island/DG boundary). Reconstruction raises
the whole profile, lifting the minimum voltage and cutting losses. Exact per-node values ≈ approximate;
minimum-voltage endpoints are exact from Table 3.
