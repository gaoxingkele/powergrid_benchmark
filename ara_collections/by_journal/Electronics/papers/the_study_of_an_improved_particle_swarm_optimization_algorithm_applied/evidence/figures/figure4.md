## Trend summary
Amplitude is strictly non-negative and decays monotonically (no oscillation across zero) from ≈10 toward ≈0, approaching zero by iteration ≈70-80. Illustrates the progressive/asymptotic-convergence regime that (per Eq. 34) applies when t > Tmax/2: one-sided damped approach to the optimum for stable late-stage refinement. Contrast with Figure 3, whose early amplitude swings to both signs.
