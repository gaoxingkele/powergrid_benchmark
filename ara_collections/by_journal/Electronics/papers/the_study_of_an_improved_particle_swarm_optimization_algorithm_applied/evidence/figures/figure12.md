## Trend summary
Irradiance is zero overnight, rises after sunrise (~05:00-06:00) to a midday peak of ≈2.2 kW/m^2 around 12:00-13:00, then falls back to zero by ≈19:00-20:00 — a single bell-shaped daytime profile. This drives the PV output (Eq. 1) and is consistent with the PV column of Table 5 (PV output non-zero only from 05:00 to 20:00, peaking at 93.28 kW at 14:00-15:00).
