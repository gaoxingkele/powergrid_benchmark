## Trend summary
Temperature holds at the ≈26 deg C floor overnight/early morning, climbs to a mid-afternoon peak (≈29.7-29.8 deg C around 13:00-14:00), then falls back to ≈26 deg C by night. Because PV conversion efficiency falls as cell temperature rises (Eq. 1), the afternoon temperature peak coincides with the period when the PV output-vs-irradiance relationship is most temperature-derated.
