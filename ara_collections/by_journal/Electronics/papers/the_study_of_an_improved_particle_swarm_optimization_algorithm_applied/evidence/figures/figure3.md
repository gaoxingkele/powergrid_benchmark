## Trend summary
Amplitude oscillates with both positive and negative excursions early (widest at the start, |amplitude| up to ≈10), then the envelope contracts and settles to a near-zero flat band by iteration ≈50. Illustrates the oscillatory-convergence regime that (per Eq. 33) applies when t <= Tmax/2: perturbations near the optimum explore both sides before damping. Exact per-point values are unreadable; only the contracting-envelope trend is reliable.
