## Trend summary
Wind speed stays in a narrow ≈2.6-4.2 m/s band all day, peaking mid-morning (≈4.2 m/s near 10:00-11:00) with a secondary rise in late afternoon. The low absolute wind speed is consistent with the small WT output (max ≈4.27 kW at 09:00-10:00 in Table 5) relative to the 10 kW WT rating.
