## Trend summary
Three regimes: (1) purchase from grid during 00:00-04:00 and 22:00-24:00 (renewables idle, thermal + storage insufficient); (2) self-sufficient, no interaction, 05:00-09:00 and 16:00-21:00; (3) sale to grid 10:00-15:00 when PV+WT exceed load. The largest sale (≈ -268 to -269 in Table 5 Interactive Power, i.e. exported) occurs at 11:00-13:00. Signs in this figure (sale positive) are opposite to Table 5's Interactive Power sign convention (export negative); magnitudes agree. Exact values are in Table 5.
