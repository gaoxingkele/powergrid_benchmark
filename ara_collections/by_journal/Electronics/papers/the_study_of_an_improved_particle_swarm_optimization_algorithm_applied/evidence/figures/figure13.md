## Trend summary
The four panels jointly show the merit-order dispatch over 24 h: DG (thermal) carries the bulk baseload (200-550 kW range); PV follows the daytime irradiance bell (0-93 kW); WT is a small contributor (<5 kW); ESS charges (negative) during the high-PV midday window and discharges (positive) overnight/evening for peak-shaving and valley-filling.
