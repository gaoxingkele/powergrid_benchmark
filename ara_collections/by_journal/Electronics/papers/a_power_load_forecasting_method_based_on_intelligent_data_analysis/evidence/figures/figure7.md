## Trend summary
The raw hourly load decomposes into 8 IMFs plus a residual (RES). Frequency decreases
monotonically from IMF1 (highest) to RES (lowest). Per the text: IMF1–IMF3 are high-frequency with
no regular pattern (stochastic components); IMF4–IMF7 show clear periodicity (periodic components);
IMF8 and RES show prominent trend. This frequency-to-role separation is the qualitative basis for
per-component modeling. Panel amplitude values are estimated (≈); the point being made is
structural (role separation), not exact amplitudes. Supports C04 and C05.
