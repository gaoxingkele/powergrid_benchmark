## Trend summary
A highly non-stationary, spiky hourly consumption series: a persistent ≈3–4 kW·h baseline with
frequent short peaks to ≈5–7 kW·h and a handful of large isolated spikes up to ≈11 kW·h. There is
visible quasi-daily repetition (regular clusters of peaks) superimposed on random bursts — the
motivation for decomposing into periodic + trend + random components. No printed data labels, so
values are estimated (≈). Supports C05 (raw signal to be decomposed in Figure 7).
