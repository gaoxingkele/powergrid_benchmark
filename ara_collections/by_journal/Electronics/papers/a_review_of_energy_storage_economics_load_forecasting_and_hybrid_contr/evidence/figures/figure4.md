## Visual description
- **Components**: **Renewable Energy Sources** (PV + wind icon) → **Renewable Energy Inverter** (DC→AC); **Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS)** → **BESS Inverter** (DC→AC); a vertical **Microgrid AC Bus**; **Grid** (tower icon) and **AC Loads** on the right.
- **Connections**: RES and BESS each reach the shared AC bus **through their own inverter** (labelled DC on the source side, AC on the bus side); dashed AC lines from the bus to Grid and AC Loads (bidirectional exchange).
- **Annotations**: explicit DC/AC labels on every link, emphasizing the number of conversion stages.
- **What it conveys**: the modular, retrofit-friendly AC-coupled topology — generation and storage are functionally separated, but energy passes through multiple DC→AC (and back) conversion stages, which lowers overall efficiency compared to DC-coupled architectures (§2.2.1).
