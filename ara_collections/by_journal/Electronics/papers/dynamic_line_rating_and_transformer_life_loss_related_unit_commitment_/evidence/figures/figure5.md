## Trend summary
The interface power flow oscillates between roughly −320 and +130 MW over the day, with the most
negative excursion near hour 15 (≈ −320 MW). Throughout the day the flow stays inside the ±≈350 MW
section-capacity band, i.e., the interface never exceeds its limit — the paper concludes "the
transmission capacity of the interfaces did not exceed their limits during the operation day,
ensuring the security of critical regional transmission lines." Values approximate; the security
conclusion (no over-limit) is the robust reading. Supports C01.
