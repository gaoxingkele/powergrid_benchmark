## Trend summary
Load demand (left axis) rises to a mid-day peak (≈7500–7700 MW near hour 12), dips in the afternoon,
shows a secondary evening bump (≈5600 MW near hour 20), then falls overnight. Wind power forecast
(right axis, much smaller scale, 200–450 MW band) is anti-correlated in places with load and is
comparatively flat/noisy. Note the dual-axis scaling: wind is on the 0–1000 MW right axis, so it must
not be read against the load (left) axis. Readings are approximate against gridlines.
