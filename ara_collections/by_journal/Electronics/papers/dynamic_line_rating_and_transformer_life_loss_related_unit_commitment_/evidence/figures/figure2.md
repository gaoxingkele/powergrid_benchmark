## Trend summary
All five regional curves are single-peaked, rising from ≈17–25 °C in the early hours to a mid-
afternoon peak around hours 15–17 (≈32–36 °C) then declining. Area 3 runs hottest for most of the
day and Area 2 coolest; the spread between hottest and coolest region is ≈6–8 °C at the peak. This
regional heterogeneity (Area 3 hot, Area 2 cool) is exactly the driver of the dispatch reallocation
discussed for Figure 4. Values are read against gridlines and are approximate; the ordering
(Area 3 ≳ Area 1 ≳ Area 5 ≳ Area 4 ≳ Area 2 near the peak) is the robust reading.
