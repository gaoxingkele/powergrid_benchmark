## Trend summary
The Conventional Model hot-spot temperature rises steeply with ambient temperature, peaking around
hours 15–17 at ≈110–111 °C — well above the IEC maximum allowable 98 °C — driving accelerated aging.
The TL-TF Model stays essentially flat near ≈98–99 °C across the whole day, i.e., held near the rated
bound. The Y-axis is truncated (starts at 96 °C), which visually exaggerates the gap, but the
qualitative fact — conventional exceeds 98 °C substantially while TL-TF hugs 98 °C — is robust and
matches the text. This is the mechanism evidence for C02 (pricing aging caps hot-spot temperature).
Readings are approximate against gridlines.
