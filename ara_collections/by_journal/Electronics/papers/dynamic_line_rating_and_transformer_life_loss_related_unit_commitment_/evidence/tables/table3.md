# Table 3: Impact of wind curtailment penalty and transformer investment cost on system operation

Costs in 10^4 CNY; penalty coefficient in CNY/(MW·h); transformer investment cost in 10^4 CNY.

| Wind Curtailment Penalty Coeff. | Transformer Investment Cost | Generation Cost | Start-Up/Shutdown Cost | Wind Curtailment Cost | Transformer Loss Cost | Total Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100  | 230 | 7.15 | 0.30 | 0.45 | 0.03 | 7.93 |
| 300  | 230 | 6.99 | 0.43 | 0.32 | 0.04 | 7.78 |
| 500  | 230 | 7.01 | 0.50 | 0.21 | 0.04 | 7.76 |
| 1000 | 230 | 6.97 | 0.72 | 0.13 | 0.05 | 7.87 |
| 500  | 190 | 7.07 | 0.53 | 0.21 | 0.07 | 7.88 |
| 500  | 260 | 7.05 | 0.52 | 0.20 | 0.08 | 7.85 |

Trend (§5 text): as the penalty rises, total cost is U-shaped — 7.93 → 7.78 → 7.76 (minimum near
500) → 7.87. Both raising and lowering transformer investment cost (vs 230) increase the transformer
loss cost (0.04 → 0.07 at 190; 0.04 → 0.08 at 260).
