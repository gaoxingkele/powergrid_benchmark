## Trend summary
The original load decomposes into exactly 4 single-frequency IMFs ordered from low to high frequency: IMF1 = trend (large, positive, non-zero-crossing), IMF2 = periodic regularity, IMF3–IMF4 = high-frequency fluctuation/anomaly content with decreasing amplitude. Data span = first 7 days of selected months across all four seasons (6720 points at 15-min intervals). Exact amplitudes not printed as labels — readings are visual estimates.
