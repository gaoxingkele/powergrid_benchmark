## Trend summary
PV output follows a clear single-hump diurnal shape (zero at night, peak near midday), in contrast to wind's ragged profile — the two DG types are complementary. The five reduced scenarios differ mainly in daytime amplitude (envelope from Scenario 2 low to Scenario 4 high), preserving PV randomness while sharing the deterministic day/night structure.
