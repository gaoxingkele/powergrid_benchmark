## Visual description
- **Components**: a schematic layout of the IEEE 33-node distribution network drawn as a planar graph. Nodes 1–33 are plotted as black dots along horizontal branches at different vertical levels (main feeder at y≈3 carrying nodes 1–18; an upper lateral y≈5 carrying nodes 23–33; a lower lateral y≈1 carrying nodes 19–22).
- **Annotations**: two nodes are marked with a red star (★) indicating the EVS/energy-storage sites selected by the optimization — node 13 (on the main feeder, y≈3) and node 33 (at the end of the upper lateral, y≈5).
- **Axes**: X = node horizontal index (0–20), Y = branch/level (0–7); axes are layout coordinates, not measurements.
- **What it conveys**: the MOPSO-selected siting solution places the two allowed EVS energy-storage units at nodes 13 and 33 (consistent with "maximum number of nodes that can be connected to EVS is 2"). Text (§5, p.11, verbatim): "the average voltage of node 13 is reduced by 29.5% compared to the level before the consideration of EVS, and the average voltage of node 33 is reduced by 21.5% compared to the level before the consideration of EVS". Supports C01 and C05.
