## Trend summary
For every hour the stacked positive contributions plus imports balance the green Load line, and negative bars (ESS charging, power sold to grid, net export exchanges) absorb surplus — i.e. supply exactly meets demand each hour in both panels. WT is the dominant renewable contributor for MG1; grid purchases (blue) fill peak-load hours; ESS bars alternate sign across the day (charge in low-price/low-load hours, discharge at peaks) realizing peak-shaving/valley-filling. Panel (b) under ±10% disturbance keeps the same balanced structure with modestly larger grid-purchase and sale bars. Per-hour magnitudes are not reliably readable; aggregate purchase/sale totals are in Tables A2 (normal) and A3 (disturbed). Note MG1's non-renewable unit is MT in this legend (MG1 is stated to use a DG in §2.1; the figure legend labels it MT).
