## Trend summary
Same balanced structure: hourly supply (stacked positives + grid purchase) tracks the Load line while negative bars represent charging/sales/exports, in both panels. MG3 carries the highest load levels of the three microgrids (Y-axis to ~200 kW) and is a large net purchaser from the main grid (Table A2: 396.2862 kW purchased). Under disturbance (b) the schedule shifts modestly but power balance is maintained at every hour. Per-hour values are not reliably readable at this resolution; aggregate purchase/sale figures are in Tables A2 (normal) and A3 (disturbance), and per-MG costs in Tables A4/A5.
