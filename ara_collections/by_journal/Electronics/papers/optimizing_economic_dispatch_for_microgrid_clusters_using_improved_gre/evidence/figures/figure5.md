## Trend summary
Three-tier TOU structure. The grid purchase price (highest series) and the sale-back price (lowest series) both step up from an off-peak valley through a shoulder level to two daily peaks (around midday and evening), then drop back at the end of the day. The inter-MG trading price is essentially flat (~0.45) across all 24 hours, sitting between the off-peak and peak grid prices — making intra-cluster exchange attractive when grid prices are high and grid purchase attractive when grid prices are low. Exact values not printed; readings are estimated against gridlines.
