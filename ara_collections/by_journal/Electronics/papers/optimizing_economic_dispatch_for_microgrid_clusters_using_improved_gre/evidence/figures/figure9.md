## Trend summary
As in Figure 8, positive stacks + imports meet the Load line each hour while negative bars (charging, sales, exports) absorb surplus, in both panels. MG2's legend shows a DG as its dispatchable unit (note: §2.1 describes MG2 as the microturbine microgrid; the figure legend labels its non-renewable unit DG). MG2 sells a large amount to the main grid (largest sale total among the three MGs per Table A2, 297.6848 kW), consistent with prominent negative "Sold to the Main Grid" bars. Disturbance panel (b) preserves the balanced structure with slightly altered magnitudes. Per-hour magnitudes unreadable; totals in Tables A2/A3.
