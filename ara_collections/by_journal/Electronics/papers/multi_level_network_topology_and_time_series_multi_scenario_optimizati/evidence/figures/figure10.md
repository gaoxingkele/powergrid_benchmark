## Visual description
- **Components**: The modified IEEE33 feeder after the "consider DC" planning (Table 5). Distributed generation blocks are annotated: PV(120), PV(60), WT(180), WT(180), WT(240), WT(240), PV(240), PV(120), PV(120). Added load points 33–37 attach at their planned buses.
- **Connections**: Red segments mark DC-modified/new DC lines (e.g. around nodes 12–13, 16–17, 19–20, 22–23, 23–24, 29–30, 30–31 and new DC lines 35–17, 36–31, 37–24); black segments remain AC (including new AC lines 33–1, 34–10). DG units connect at nodes 7, 13, 17, 20, 23, 24, 27, 29, 31.
- **What it conveys**: The realized hybrid AC/DC topology where DC sub-systems concentrate at feeder ends and high-DC-density nodes, so most DG connects within DC sub-systems, cutting commutation links and converter cost.
