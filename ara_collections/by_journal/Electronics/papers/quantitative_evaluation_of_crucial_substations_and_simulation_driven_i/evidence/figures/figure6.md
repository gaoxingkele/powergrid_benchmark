## Visual description
Six panels (same layout/axes as Figures 4–5) showing the simulated evolution when low-criticality substation No. 6's commissioning is postponed from 2020 to 2025:
- **(a)/(b) Status in 2020**: substation No. 6 (baseline position ≈(11.5,17), northwest-central) is absent from the 2020 commissioned set — only labels 1–5 appear; its relatively few loads are absorbed by neighboring substations with modest extra 10 kV feeders (panel b's feeder pattern is close to the baseline Figure 4b).
- **(c)/(d) 2025 program**: No. 6 (red) is commissioned; a red 110 kV tie and a comparatively small number of new red 10 kV feeders appear near it; 2025 also shows more new 110 kV substation construction than the No. 1-delay scenario (consistent with Table 6: 110 kV substation cost 33.8742 vs baseline 22.5828 million CNY in 2025).
- **(e)/(f) 2035 program**: new 220 kV substation (purple, ≈(14.5,10)) and further additions; overall topology closely resembles the baseline Figure 4e,f.
- **What it conveys**: The low-density contrast case for C05 — because No. 6 has low load density and limited supply coverage, its delay displaces less load over shorter distances, producing a visibly milder 10 kV response and a total incremental cost of only 1.63% (Table 6) vs 3.95% for No. 1. Paired with Figure 5 it demonstrates the cost-criticality gradient behind the sequencing rule (C03). Supports C03, C04, C05 (E04).
