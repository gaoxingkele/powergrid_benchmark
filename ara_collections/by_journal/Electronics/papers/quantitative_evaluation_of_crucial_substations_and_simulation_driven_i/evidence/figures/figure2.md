## Visual description
- **Components**: A two-terminal 110 kV supply chain between terminal nodes "1" and "2" (orange circles) with intermediate substations carrying loads P_a, P_j, P_k (downward arrows); distance annotations l_Σ (full span between the 220 kV terminals), l_j' (from node 1 side) and l_j (toward node 2) marked with double-headed arrows above the chain.
- **Connections**: power flows P_1a (arrow from node 1 into the first section "1a") and P_2k (arrow from node 2 into the last section "2k") feed the chain from both ends; middle spans drawn dashed (normally-open/lightly loaded interior), solid multi-conductor segments at the two ends.
- **Annotations**: section labels P_1a and P_2k under the end segments; load arrows P_a, P_j, P_k dropping from intermediate substations.
- **What it conveys**: In each two-terminal (dual-supply) 110 kV network the maximum line loads concentrate in the two end sections 1a and 2k, so only those sections need loading constraints — the structural justification for Eqs. 22–23 (P_1a and P_2k limited by √3·I_max·U·cosφ, computed from the load-weighted distance shares l_j/l_Σ, neglecting reactive loads).
