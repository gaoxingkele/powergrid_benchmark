## Visual description
Six panels, all on x/y coordinate axes in km (0–30 × 0–25), showing the on-schedule (baseline) evolution:
- **(a) Status of 110 kV grid in 2020**: 220 kV substations (dark navy), original 110 kV substations (green), the six new 110 kV substations (red, labeled 1–6), connected by the brown 110 kV loop backbone. New substations 1–6 sit at approx. (17,7.5)=1, (20.5,18.5)=2, (13.5,17)=3, (14,12)=4, (22.5,10.5)=5, (11.5,17)=6.
- **(b) Status of 10 kV grid in 2020**: same substations with the 516 10 kV distribution feeders (thin grey) radiating from each substation to load nodes.
- **(c)/(d) 110 kV and 10 kV planning program for 2025**: evolved topology; new 110 kV substations (red) and new 110/10 kV lines (red) added by the GA; legend adds "new 220kV substation" (purple).
- **(e)/(f) 110 kV and 10 kV planning program for 2035**: further expansion; one new 220 kV substation (purple, ≈(16.5,11)) appears by 2035; additional red 110 kV spurs and 10 kV feeders.
- **Annotations**: legends per panel distinguish original substation / new 220 kV substation / new 110 kV substation / original line / new line.
- **What it conveys**: The baseline evolutionary trajectory (E03) with all six substations commissioned on schedule before 2020 — the benchmark topology and investment reference against which the delay scenarios (Figures 5–6) are differenced. Its costs are Table 6's "Original planning program" rows. Supports E03/E04 baseline; context for C04.
