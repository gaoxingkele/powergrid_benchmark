## Visual description
- **Components**: Two terminal nodes labeled "1" and "2" (orange circles) at the ends of a horizontal chain of intermediate nodes (blue circles) connected by multi-conductor line segments; one node hangs below the chain, attached by a short vertical double line and labeled "breakout connection"; the upper chain is labeled "loop network".
- **Connections**: node 1 — intermediate nodes — node 2 form the loop-network backbone (some inner spans drawn as dashed segments); the breakout ('3T'/tee) substation taps the backbone at a mid-span point rather than being inserted in series.
- **Annotations**: text labels "loop network" (pointing at the backbone) and "breakout connection" (pointing at the tee-connected node); nodes 1 and 2 explicitly identified in the text as node 1 and node 2.
- **What it conveys**: The two interconnection architectures the 110 kV routing model admits — a loop-network configuration between two terminal supply nodes, plus '3T' breakout connections that let a new substation tap existing 110 kV infrastructure. This is the topology the routing constraints (Eqs. 17–21) and the automatic reconfiguration mechanism operate on; structure mirrored in logic/solution/method.md (Stage B2).
