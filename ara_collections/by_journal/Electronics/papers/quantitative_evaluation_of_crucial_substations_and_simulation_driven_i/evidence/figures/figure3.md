## Visual description
- **Axes**: X = "x coordinate/(km)", linear, 0–~27 (ticks to 25); Y = "y coordinate/(km)", linear, 0–25. Colorbar at right: load density scale 0–50 (ticks every 5), white → dark navy with increasing intensity.
- **Shows**: The case region partitioned into 220 load zones (polygons). Each zone contains a pink/magenta circle marking its equivalent load node centroid; polygon fill intensity encodes local load density per the 0–50 colorbar.
- **Spatial pattern**: darkest (highest-density) zones cluster in the center and center-south of the region (around x≈12–20, y≈8–17), with several dark pockets on the east edge (x≈25–27, y≈8–9); the periphery (far south, far west, northeast fringe) is mostly near-white (low density).
- **Demonstrates**: The heterogeneous spatial load distribution (aggregate 2013.74 MW over 220 equivalent nodes in base year 2020) that makes delay impact substation-specific — dense central areas are where a delayed substation forces long 10 kV transfers onto already-loaded feeders. Context for C05 (load density as the operative cost driver) and input data for E03–E05.
