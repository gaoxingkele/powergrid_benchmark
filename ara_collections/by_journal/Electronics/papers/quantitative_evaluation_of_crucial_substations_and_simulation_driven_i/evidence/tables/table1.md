# Table 1 - Santy scale and its corresponding meanings

**Source**: Table 1, §2.2 (p. 6)
**Caption**: "Santy scale and its corresponding meanings."
**Screenshot**: table1.png (rendered from PDF page 6)
**Extraction type**: raw_table

| Importance Scale | Meaning (Substation Carrying Capacity) |
|---|---|
| 1 | The two elements before and after are equally important. |
| 3 | The former element is slightly more important than the latter one. |
| 5 | The former element is significantly more important than the latter one. |
| 7 | The former element is far more important than the latter one. |
| 9 | The former element is extremely more important than the latter one. |
| 2, 4, 6, 8 | Intermediate value on a scale of 1 to 9. |
| 1/k, k = 1,2,3,…,9 | If the ratio of relative importance of factor i to factor j is c_ij, then the ratio of factor j to factor i is c_ji = 1/c_ij. |

**Notes**: The 1–9 pairwise comparison scale used in AHP (the paper writes "Santy"; standard AHP uses the Saaty scale).
