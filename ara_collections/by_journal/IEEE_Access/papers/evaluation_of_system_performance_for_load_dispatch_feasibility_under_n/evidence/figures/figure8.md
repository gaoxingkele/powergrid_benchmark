## Trend summary
The tallest ranking bar (rank 4) is Cy3 (bus 7); rank 3 is Cy2 (bus 2); the two 400 MW outages
(Cy6, Cy7) and the bus-23 outages (Cy8, Cy9) occupy the lower rank numbers (1 and 2), which the paper
treats as the most-critical top positions. The ranking is by percentage cost rise and is identical
across Case 1, 2(a), 2(b) (supports C02, C07).
