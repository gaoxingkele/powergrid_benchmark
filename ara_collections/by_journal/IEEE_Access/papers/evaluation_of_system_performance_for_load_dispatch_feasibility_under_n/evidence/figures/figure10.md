## Trend summary
For Case 2(c) a rank of 1 is the most expensive/uneconomic contingency and a rank of 5 the least-cost
option. The 400 MW outages (Cy6, Cy7) take rank 1; the 350 MW bus-23 outage (Cy9) rank 2; the 155 MW
bus-23 outage (Cy8) rank 3; bus 2 rank 4; bus 7 rank 5. This confirms that the outage of the largest
generating unit (400 MW at buses 18 and 21) most increases system risk (supports C02, C07).
