**Trend**: On F1 the base COA has the highest Mean (2.1763×10^10 at D=20; 1.0096×10^11 at D=50) — highest error — while FDBCOA1 gives the lowest (6.49×10^9; 6.41×10^10). The paper reads this as COA being accurate-in-low-dim but high-variance/unstable, and FDBCOA1 having the most controlled std growth (best scalability); FDBCOA3 scales worst among the FDB variants.
