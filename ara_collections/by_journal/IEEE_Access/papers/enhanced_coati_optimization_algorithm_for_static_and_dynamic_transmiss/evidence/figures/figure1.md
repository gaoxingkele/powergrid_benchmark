## Panels
Six panels for (P, D): (30,5), (30,30), (30,100), (100,10), (100,30), (100,100). Series: COA (red squares), FDBCOA1 (blue), FDBCOA2 (green), FDBCOA3 (purple).

## Trend summary
COA (red) encloses the largest area (worst Friedman scores) in nearly every panel, sitting on the outer ring across most F1–F10 axes. FDBCOA1 traces the smallest / most homogeneous area (best average, most stable), with FDBCOA2 and FDBCOA3 intermediate. The visual ordering (FDBCOA1 tightest, COA widest) matches the numeric Friedman ranks in Tables 2–4 (FDBCOA1 rank 1, COA rank 4). Supports C01.
