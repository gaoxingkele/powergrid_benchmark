## Panels
Six panels for (P, D): (30,15), (30,20), (30,100), (50,20), (50,30), (100,20). Series shown: COA (red squares), FDBCOA1 (blue), FDBCOA1-OBL1 (green), FDBCOA1-OBL5 (purple), FDBCOA1-OBL7 (orange).

## Trend summary
COA (red) again encloses the largest area (worst). The FDBCOA1/OBL1/OBL5/OBL7 traces are closely grouped and much tighter; the paper reports FDBCOA1-OBL5 is "consistently minimum across many test functions." This matches the numeric ranking in Table 10 (OBL5 rank 1, COA rank 10). Supports C03.
