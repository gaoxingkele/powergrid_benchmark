- **Connections**: solid black lines = existing lines; red dashed = to-be-added lines without generation resizing; blue dashed = to-be-added lines with generation resizing. Multiple parallel dashed lines are drawn between buses 2, 6, 4 etc. (the added n-lines).
- **Annotations**: legend distinguishes existing / added-without-resizing / added-with-resizing.
- **What it conveys**: the network topology and the two distinct optimal expansion plans (with vs without generation resizing), visually corresponding to the line additions in Tables 14 (n2-6:4, n3-5:1, n4-6:2) and 15 (n3-5:1, n4-6:3). Supports C04, C08.
