# Figure 4: Clustering Result of PV Power

**Source**: Page 17 of the original paper

**Description**: Clustering results for historical annual photovoltaic (PV) power output data. The figure shows representative daily PV generation profiles with characteristic bell-shaped curves (peak at midday, zero at night). The 95% confidence intervals highlight the variability around each cluster center. Different clusters may capture seasonal variations in solar irradiance.
