# Figure 5: Clustering Result of Load Power

**Source**: Page 17 of the original paper

**Description**: Clustering results for historical annual load demand data from the Southwest China regional grid. The figure shows representative daily load patterns with typical morning ramp-up, midday peak, and evening peak characteristics. The 95% confidence intervals reflect the uncertainty around typical load behavior. These clustered load profiles, combined with wind and PV clustering results, form the scenario inputs for the bi-level planning model.
