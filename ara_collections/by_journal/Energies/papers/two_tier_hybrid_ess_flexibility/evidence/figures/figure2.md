# Figure 2: HESS Planning Process

**Source**: Page 11 of the original paper

**Description**: Flowchart of the bi-level optimization solution process for Hybrid Energy Storage System planning. The diagram shows:
- **Upper level**: IWAA-driven planning determining storage location, capacity, and power
- **Lower level**: VMD-PSO operational optimization computing flexibility, voltage, and loss metrics
- **Feedback loop**: Operational results inform iterative refinement of planning decisions
- The process creates a closed loop between long-term planning and short-term operation
