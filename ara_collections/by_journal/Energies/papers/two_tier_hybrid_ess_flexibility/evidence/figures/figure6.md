# Figure 6: Results for Each IMF

**Source**: Page 19 of the original paper

**Description**: VMD decomposition results showing the four Intrinsic Mode Functions (IMF1-IMF4) obtained from decomposing the total energy storage system target power. The IMFs exhibit progressively decreasing fluctuation amplitudes with increasing modal order:
- **IMF1**: Highest frequency, most rapid fluctuations
- **IMF2**: Medium frequency
- **IMF3**: Lower frequency  
- **IMF4**: Lowest frequency, smoothest component

After decomposition, IMFs with center frequency above the median threshold are assigned to Li-ion batteries (high-frequency components), and those below the threshold are assigned to flow batteries (low-frequency components).
