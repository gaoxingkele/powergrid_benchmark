# Figure 7: Energy Storage System Output Power

**Source**: Page 20 of the original paper

**Description**: Power curves of the two battery types after VMD-PSO frequency division:
- **Lithium-ion battery power (high-frequency)**: Rapid charge/discharge cycles tracking fast fluctuations
- **Flow battery power (low-frequency)**: Smoother power profile handling bulk energy shifting

The figure demonstrates the complementary operation: Li-ion handles high-frequency components while the flow battery handles low-frequency components. The reconstruction consistency between the decomposed components and the original target output is verified with maximum error of 1.14e-13 and RMSE of 4.74e-15.
