# Table 6: Comparison of results of different methods

Uncertainty-handling method comparison at the same planning scenario. "Deterministic Optimization here refers to stochastic optimization based on multiple scenarios." Values in CNY 10^4/year.

| Annual Cost (CNY 10^4) | Deterministic Optimization | Robust Optimization | Distributionally Robust Optimization |
|------------------------|--------|--------|--------|
| Lines Equivalent Annual Investment Cost | 146.14 | 146.14 | 146.14 |
| SOP Equivalent Annual Investment Cost | 16.11 | 30.48 | 22.11 |
| Interconnection Switches Equivalent Annual Investment Cost | 1.34 | 1.34 | 1.34 |
| SOP Operation and Maintenance Costs | 0.16 | 0.30 | 0.22 |
| Interconnection Switches Action Costs | 0.07 | 0.07 | 0.07 |
| ESS Operation and Maintenance Costs | 123.83 | 138.49 | 129.38 |
| DR Costs | 291.35 | 342.75 | 324.47 |
| Wind and Solar Power Penalty Costs | 0 | 5.28 | 2.51 |
| Network and SOP Loss Costs | 391.37 | 449.43 | 418.43 |
| Revenue From The Purchase and Sale of Electricity | 6059.86 | 5884.29 | 5972.86 |
| Net Profit of One Year | 5089.49 | 4770.01 | 4928.18 |

Reported (Section 5.5): deterministic has highest net profit but is "too ideal"; robust is most conservative with the least profit; DRO net profit is between the two and is improved by "more than 3%" vs the traditional robust method.
