## Visual description
The figure shows voltage profiles across all 69 buses for combined DG, CB, and EVCS integration at three hosting factors (30%, 40%, 50%), compared with the base case. The CGO-optimized profiles show near-flat voltage profiles at approximately 0.99-1.0 p.u. across all 69 buses, even at 50% EV penetration. The base case shows the characteristic voltage drop below 0.92 p.u. for buses 46-69.

## Trend summary
The system maintains nearly flat voltage profiles across all buses, with voltages between approximately 0.99 and 1.0 p.u. at all three hosting factors. The curves for 30%, 40%, and 50% HF are nearly overlapping, showing that coordinated CB placement effectively neutralizes the voltage impact of increasing EV penetration. This represents the best voltage performance among all tested configurations.
