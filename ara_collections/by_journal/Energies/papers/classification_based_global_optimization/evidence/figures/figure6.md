## Visual description
- **Components**: Single-line diagram of IEEE 69-bus radial distribution network showing all 69 buses with 68 distribution branches in a radial tree configuration.
- **Connections**: Substation at bus 1. The main feeder runs from bus 1 through multiple buses, with lateral branches at various points. The longest feeder section spans buses 1 through 27 and also the segment from bus 46 through bus 69. All bus numbers are labeled.
- **Annotations**: The larger 69-bus network has significantly more branches and longer feeders than the 33-bus system, creating more challenging voltage regulation and loss conditions.
- **What it conveys**: The increased complexity and feeder length of the 69-bus test network, which serves as a scalability validation for the CGO method. The extended feeders (buses 46-69) are where voltage drops below 0.92 p.u. in the base case.
