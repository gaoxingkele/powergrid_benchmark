## Visual description
The figure shows voltage profiles across all 33 buses for combined DG, CB, and EVCS integration at three hosting factors (30%, 40%, 50%), compared with the base case. The profiles show voltages very close to 1.0 p.u. across almost all buses, with negligible variation between the three hosting factor cases. The base case curve shows severe voltage drop while the CGO-optimized cases are nearly flat.

## Trend summary
Voltages are maintained close to 1.0 p.u. across all 33 buses, with minimal variation as EV penetration increases. Compared to Figure 7 (without CBs), the voltage profiles are markedly flatter and closer to nominal, demonstrating the stabilizing effect of coordinated reactive power compensation. The curves for 30%, 40%, and 50% hosting factors are nearly indistinguishable, indicating robust voltage regulation even at high EV penetration.
