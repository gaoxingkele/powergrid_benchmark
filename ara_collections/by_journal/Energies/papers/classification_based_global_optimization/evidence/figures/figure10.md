## Visual description
The figure shows voltage profiles across all 69 buses for DG and EVCS integration at three hosting factors (30%, 40%, 50%) compared with the base case. The base case shows severe voltage drop in the latter half of the feeder (buses 46-69), dropping below 0.92 p.u. The CGO-optimized cases show significantly improved voltage profiles, maintaining voltages above approximately 0.976 p.u. across all buses.

## Trend summary
DGs and EVCS integration effectively mitigates voltage sag, particularly in the latter half of the feeder (buses 50-69) where base-case voltages drop below 0.92 p.u. The improvement is most dramatic for buses 50-69. Higher EV penetration (50% HF) shows slightly lower voltages than 30% HF. The voltage at bus 61 (one of the DG placement locations) shows a local improvement. Voltage profiles remain above 0.976 p.u. for all optimized cases.
