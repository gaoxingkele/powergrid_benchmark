# Figure 15: TPC 83-Bus System

**Page**: 21

**Description**: Single-line diagram of the Taiwan Power Company (TPC) 83-bus real distribution system. The system has 83 buses and 96 branches (83 sectional switches + 13 tie-line switches). Initial open switches are S84-S96. The system operates at 11.4 kV with a load of 28.35 MW and 20.7 MVar.
