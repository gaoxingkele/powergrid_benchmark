# Table 2: Wind Farm Power Change Constraints [24]

**Source**: Page 8 of the PDF. Cited from reference [24] (MeShengwei, 2022).

**Description**: Cost parameters used in the energy storage investment cost calculation.

| Parameter | Value | Unit |
|-----------|-------|------|
| Cost factor related to energy storage power (k_p) | 5,000,000 | CNY/MW |
| Cost factor related to storage capacity (k_q) | 6,000,000 | CNY/MWh |
| Unit price of energy storage system operation and maintenance (k_F) | 500 | CNY/MW |
| The discharge price of energy storage system (k_V) | 300 | CNY/MWh |
| Annual decline rate of energy storage installation costs (alpha) | 5 | % |
| Total number of energy storage replacements (tau) | 2 | times |
| Energy storage life (a) | 10 | years |
| Battery cycle life (N) | 8000 | cycles |

**Note**: The heading in the original paper reads "Wind farm power change constraints" but the content describes cost parameters. The paper likely had a labeling inconsistency.
