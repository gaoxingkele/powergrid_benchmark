# Table 1: Wind Farm Power Change Constraints

**Source**: Page 5 of the PDF. Extracted from the paper text.

**Description**: Maximum allowable active power change limits for wind farms connected to the grid, based on installed capacity.

| Installed Capacity of Wind Farms P_N (MW) | Maximum Change Limit of Active Power in 10 min (MW) | Maximum Change Limit of Active Power in 1 min (MW) |
|--------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| P_N < 30                                   | 10                                                   | 3                                                   |
| 30 <= P_N <= 150                           | P_N/3                                                | P_N/10                                              |
| P_N > 150                                  | 50                                                   | 15                                                  |

**Note**: For the 40 MW wind farm in this study, the limits are 13.33 MW (10-min) and 4 MW (1-min).
