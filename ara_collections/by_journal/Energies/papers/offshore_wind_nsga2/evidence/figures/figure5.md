# Figure 5: Influence of Rated Power and Rated Capacity on Output Power Fluctuation of Offshore Wind Power

**Source**: Page 10 of the PDF.

**Description**: Surface/contour plot showing how varying rated power and rated capacity affects the output power fluctuation rate (sigma) of the offshore wind farm after storage configuration. The fluctuation rate decreases from 22.48% to 18.63% with increasing storage size, and the volatility reduction rate increases from 5.29% to 21.18%.
