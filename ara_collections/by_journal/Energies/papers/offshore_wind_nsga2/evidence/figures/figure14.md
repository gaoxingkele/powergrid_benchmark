# Figure 14: The Pareto Frontiers of Different Schemes

**Source**: Page 18 of the PDF.

**Description**: Two Pareto frontiers comparing Scheme 2 (spot market participation) and Scheme 3 (peak-valley arbitrage strategy). (a) shows the Pareto frontier of Scheme 2 where net cost = investment cost minus spot electricity sales revenue. (b) shows the Pareto frontier of Scheme 3 where peak-valley arbitrage provides additional economic benefit. The optimal configuration for Scheme 2 (by ideal point method) is 8 MW / 37 MWh.
