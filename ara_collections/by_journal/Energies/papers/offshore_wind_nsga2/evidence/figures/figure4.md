# Figure 4: Influence of Rated Power and Rated Capacity on Energy Storage Investment Cost

**Source**: Page 10 of the PDF.

**Description**: Surface/contour plot showing how varying the rated power (10-30% of wind farm rated power, i.e., 4-12 MW) and rated capacity (1-4 hour continuous working time, i.e., 4-48 MWh) affects the total investment cost of the energy storage system. Investment cost increases from approximately CNY 67.04 million to CNY 520.45 million.
