# Figure 3: Pareto Frontier of NSGA-II Multi-Objective Optimization

**Source**: Page 9 of the PDF.

**Description**: The Pareto frontier obtained by NSGA-II for the weekly (Dec 9-15) optimization under Scheme 1. The x-axis represents energy storage investment cost (million CNY) and the y-axis represents output power volatility (%). The curve shows the optimal trade-off: as investment cost increases, volatility decreases.
