# Table A1: Relative Degradation of Surrogate Indices Under Severe Perturbation

## Source
Appendix A.1, Pages 22-23

## Content
Comparison of relative degradation of multiplicative form (proposed) vs additive form under severe perturbation (critical-line-drop condition) for three representative backbone-grid schemes.

## Key Values
| Backbone Scheme | Multiplicative Form | Additive Form |
|----------------|-------------------|--------------|
| Economic | 10.64% | 2.82% |
| Transitional | 14.21% | 4.83% |
| Resilience | 14.20% | 4.82% |

The multiplicative form exhibits significantly larger degradation, indicating stronger discriminative capability for resilience differences.
