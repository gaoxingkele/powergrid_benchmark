# Table 4: Performance Comparison of Aggregation Forms

## Source
Section 6.4, Page 17

## Content
Comparison of arithmetic mean (proposed), geometric mean, and unweighted average weighting schemes for the pumped-storage hub effect across five independent Pareto solution sets. Evaluated using IGD+, HV, Spread, and F2 mean.

## Key Values
| Weighting Scheme | IGD+ | HV | Spread | F2 Mean |
|-----------------|------|----|--------|---------|
| Arithmetic Mean (Proposed) | 0.007524 | 0.108089 | 0.849734 | 0.474463 |
| Geometric Mean | 0.007632 | 0.107918 | 0.849441 | 0.474818 |
| Unweighted Average | 0.008262 | 0.107140 | 0.858419 | 0.476291 |

Arithmetic mean achieves best or highly competitive performance in most metrics.
