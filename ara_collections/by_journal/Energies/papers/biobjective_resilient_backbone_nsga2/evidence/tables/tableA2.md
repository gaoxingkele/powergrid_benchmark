# Table A2: Statistical Results of F2 Under Different Alpha Values

## Source
Appendix A.3, Page 23

## Content
Sensitivity analysis of the hub effect influence coefficient alpha (0.1, 0.3, 0.5) on the resilience mismatch index F2 under baseline and severe perturbation scenarios.

## Key Values
| Alpha | Baseline | Severe Perturbation |
|-------|----------|-------------------|
| 0.1 | 0.5321 +/- 0.0307 | 0.6327 +/- 0.0591 |
| 0.3 | 0.4982 +/- 0.0266 | 0.5994 +/- 0.0660 |
| 0.5 | 0.4644 +/- 0.0250 | 0.5660 +/- 0.0735 |

F2 decreases continuously with increasing alpha without abnormal jumps. Baseline consistently lower than severe perturbation across all alpha values.
