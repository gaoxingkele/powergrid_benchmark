## Trend summary
CC NG (light blue) sits high across most hours — near maximum utilization — reflecting its low production cost. DH and CT NG follow. Some technologies (constant low-price escalators) show quasi-constant, near-rectangular curves. ST NG and ICE NG appear only at the far left (few hours, high output) covering demand peaks due to higher bid prices. Exact per-hour reads unreliable; energy totals live in text/`evidence`.
