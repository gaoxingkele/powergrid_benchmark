## Visual description
- **Panel (a)**: Hourly NFE (bar) over 168 hours with total cleared amounts of UR, DR, SU, SD overlaid (line/stacked area). Shows periods where total redispatch exceeds NFE due to network/UC constraint mitigation.
- **Panel (b)**: UR and DR cleared amounts by technology (CC NG, DH, CT NG, ST NG) — stacked area/bar.
- **Panel (c)**: SU and SD cleared amounts by technology — stacked area/bar.
- **Panel (d)**: Activations of constraint (31) — indicates hours where units below P^min force mandatory SU or SD.
- **Panel (e)**: SRR provision by technology — shows how each technology contributes to SR.
- **Panel (f)**: DAM overloaded line numbers — identifies which branches are overloaded.
- **Panel (g)**: DAM overload power (MW) — magnitude of overloads.

## Trend summary
Week 9 has the highest total redispatched energy (210.2 GWh). Key observations: CC NG is shut down only with negative NFE lasting longer than MDT; CT NG SU bids cleared only with positive NFE; hours with greatest redispatch require overload mitigation.
