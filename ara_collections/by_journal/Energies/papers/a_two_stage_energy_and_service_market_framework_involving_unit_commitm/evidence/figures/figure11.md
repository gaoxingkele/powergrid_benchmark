## Trend summary
After ASM the overloads on all 11 branches are solved within the maximum loading (bars capped near 100%), and max loading occurs for fewer hours than the DAM case (Figure 7), for all branches EXCEPT F31 and F129 — where clearing the cheapest units (whose PTDF is concordant with the branch flow) keeps some loading. Confirms the ASM redispatch relieves DAM congestions. Directional reading.
