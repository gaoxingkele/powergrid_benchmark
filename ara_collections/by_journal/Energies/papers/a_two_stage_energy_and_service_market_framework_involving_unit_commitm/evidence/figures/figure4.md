## Trend summary
Five distinct diurnal shapes. The {H1,H5,H13} group (green) has the most pronounced evening peak (≈0.10–0.12 p.u. around hours 18–21), while {H6,H10,H15} (light blue) is nearly flat around ≈0.04 p.u. all day. The other groups have moderate mid-day plateaus around 0.045–0.055 p.u. The profiles express the DAM bidding strategy of DH units (90% of daily inlet energy bid to DAM in base case).
