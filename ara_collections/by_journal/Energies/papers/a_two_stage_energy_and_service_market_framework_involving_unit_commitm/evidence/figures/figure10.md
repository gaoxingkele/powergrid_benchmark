## Trend summary
Panel (b): DSM is always sufficient to cover SRR (DSM−SRR ≥ 0 for both DAM and ASM, curves nearly identical — very slight DAM→ASM variation). Panel (a): after DAM the USM−SRR curve dips negative for 741 time steps (USM < SRR); after ASM the curve is lifted so it is always non-negative — SU and DR clearances create the needed upward margin to satisfy constraint (44).
