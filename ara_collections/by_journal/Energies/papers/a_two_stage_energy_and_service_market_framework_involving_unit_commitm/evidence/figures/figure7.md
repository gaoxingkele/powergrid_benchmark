## Trend summary
Overloads are most frequent on F31 and F32 (highest overloading hours, ≈1100–1500 h), since they are close to the most convenient (cheapest) units. Contiguous lines F96, F97, F104 also overload. Max loading exceeds 100% on all 11 branches (up to ≈160% on F31). This DCLF-after-DAM result is the network-feasibility problem the ASM redispatch must resolve.
