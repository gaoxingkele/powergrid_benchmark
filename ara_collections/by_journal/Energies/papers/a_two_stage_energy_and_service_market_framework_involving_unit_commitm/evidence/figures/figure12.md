## Trend summary
The heatmap shows weekly and seasonal patterns of upward redispatch (UR + SU). Highest upward cleared amounts occur in weeks 9 (days 57–63, 98.5 GWh) and lowest in week 2 (days 8–14, 55.1 GWh). The pattern reflects periods with high NFE or significant overload mitigation requirements.
