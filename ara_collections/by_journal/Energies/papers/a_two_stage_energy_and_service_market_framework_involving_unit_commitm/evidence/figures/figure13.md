## Trend summary
The heatmap shows weekly and seasonal patterns of downward redispatch (DR + SD). Highest downward cleared amounts occur in week 43 (days 295–301, 98.5 GWh) and lowest in week 15 (days 99–105, 55.1 GWh). The pattern is complementary to the upward redispatch heatmap.
