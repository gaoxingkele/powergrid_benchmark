## Visual description
- **Components**: A horizontal time axis with four market sessions in sequence: DAM, IM (both under "Market Operator"), then ASM, then BM (both under "Transmission System Operator"). Time markers: D−1, D, Real-time.
- **Connections**: Left-to-right temporal flow DAM → IM → ASM → BM. A green arrow links Market Operator block to Transmission System Operator block.
- **Annotations** (feature callouts above each session):
  - DAM: "Merit-order zonal market clearing price"; "Preliminary generation and load schedules based on cleared bids"; "Interzonal power flow boundaries."
  - ASM: "Pay-as-bid auction market"; "Reserve procurement by power margins"; "Congestion management"; "UC constraints"; "RES and load forecast update."
  - BM: "Activation of reserved energy according to occurred contingency"; "Remuneration of activated energy."
- **What it conveys**: The sequential Italian/European market structure — energy markets (DAM/IM) managed by the Market Operator upstream, ancillary service (ASM) and balancing (BM) markets managed by the TSO downstream. Motivates the paper's DAM→ASM two-stage modeling scope.
