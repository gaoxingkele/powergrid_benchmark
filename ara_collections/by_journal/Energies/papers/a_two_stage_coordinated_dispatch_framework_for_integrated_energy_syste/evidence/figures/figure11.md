## Visual description
- **Plot kind**: Line chart (three overlaid curves)
- **Axes**: X = hour (0–24), Y = load (MW)
- **Series**: Case 1 (Baseline), Case 2 (PDR), Case 3 (PDR+V2G)

## Trend summary
Case 3 sits at the frontier with lowest load peaks and highest valley fill. Case 2 is intermediate. Case 1 has the highest peaks and deepest valleys, with no flexibility at all. PDR alone reduces the peak-to-valley gap by 15.0%; adding V2G deepens the reduction to 31.9%.
