## Visual description
- **Plot kind**: Line chart (three overlaid curves)
- **Axes**: X = hour (0–24), Y = electricity price ($/MWh)
- **Series**: Case 1 (Baseline), Case 2 (PDR), Case 3 (PDR+V2G)

## Trend summary
Case 1 sits on a flat baseline with no scarcity signal. Case 2 (PDR) narrows the peak-to-valley gap but peak price still spikes. Case 3 (PDR+V2G) smoothes anomalies: V2G discharge adds capacity through peaks to hold spikes down; overnight charging lifts the valley price. The higher off-peak price in Case 3 is useful — it flattens the curve and spares ramping wear.
