## Visual description
- **Components**: Energy input side (wind, solar, gas, thermal, geothermal), internal conversion stage (CHP, P2G, heat pumps, absorption chillers), energy storage (batteries, hot water tanks, ice storage, gas storage), terminal demand (industrial, commercial, residential, V2G EV charging)
- **Connections**: Multi-energy flows from input through conversion devices to storage and demand, with cyber-physical system (CPS) management center providing unified control
- **Annotations**: The IESO manages the co-dispatch across all carriers
- **What it conveys**: The integrated architecture coupling electricity, heat, gas, and cooling with centralized operator control
