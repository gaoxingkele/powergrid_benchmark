# Related Work

## PDR and TOU Pricing

### RW01: Tan et al., 2022 (Ref. [8])
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.energy.2021.121875
- **Type**: baseline
- **Delta**: Established TOU pricing model based on cross-price elasticity matrix for integrated electricity and heat networks.
- **Claims affected**: C01
- **Adopted elements**: Cross-price elasticity formulation; TOU pricing as DR foundation.

### RW02: Huang et al., 2022 (Ref. [11])
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.energy.2021.122576
- **Type**: bounds
- **Delta**: Stackelberg game between IES operator and multi-energy consumers for optimal retail price recovery.
- **Claims affected**: C01
- **Adopted elements**: Two-level pricing framework concept.

### RW03: Gao et al., 2024 (Ref. [9])
- **DOI**: 10.3390/en17194705
- **Type**: bounds
- **Delta**: Multi-energy PDR with shiftable and interruptible loads, carbon trading.
- **Claims affected**: C01
- **Adopted elements**: Multi-energy PDR framework.

## V2G and EV Integration

### RW04: Egbue et al., 2022 (Ref. [15])
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.ijepes.2022.108094
- **Type**: baseline
- **Delta**: UC model with optimal V2G operation using aggregated EV dispatch with normal-distribution arrival/departure.
- **Claims affected**: C05
- **Adopted elements**: Statistical EV mobility modeling (Eqs. 48–49).

### RW05: Gan et al., 2024 (Ref. [16])
- **DOI**: 10.1109/TIA.2024.3449440
- **Type**: bounds
- **Delta**: V2G coordination with wind fluctuation damping and curtailment reduction.
- **Claims affected**: C02, C05
- **Adopted elements**: V2G-wind coordination concept.

### RW06: Yang et al., 2021 (Ref. [19])
- **DOI**: 10.1109/TSG.2020.3033616
- **Type**: bounds
- **Delta**: Linear branch flow model for radial distribution networks with V2G voltage impact.
- **Claims affected**: C01
- **Adopted elements**: Distributive network modeling approach.

## P2G and Electricity–Gas Coupling

### RW07: He et al., 2021 (Ref. [22])
- **DOI**: 10.1109/TPWRS.2021.3058103
- **Type**: extends
- **Delta**: Distributionally robust scheduling of integrated gas-electricity systems with DR; P2G-based surplus wind conversion.
- **Claims affected**: C02
- **Adopted elements**: P2G as wind curtailment mitigation mechanism.

### RW08: Zhang et al., 2021 (Ref. [20])
- **DOI**: 10.1109/TIA.2021.3071017
- **Type**: baseline
- **Delta**: Day-ahead IES dispatch with P2G and dynamic pipeline networks.
- **Claims affected**: C02
- **Adopted elements**: P2G modeling; gas network constraints.

### RW09: Yang et al., 2022 (Ref. [23])
- **DOI**: 10.1109/TSG.2022.3161689
- **Type**: baseline
- **Delta**: Security-constrained co-dispatch with Weymouth pipeline-flow equation directly in the problem.
- **Claims affected**: C05
- **Adopted elements**: Security-constrained power–gas framework.

### RW11: Yan et al., 2023 (Ref. [28])
