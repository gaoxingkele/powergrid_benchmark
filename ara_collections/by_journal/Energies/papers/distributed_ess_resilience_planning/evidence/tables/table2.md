# Table 2: System of Quality Demand Indicators

**Source:** Section 3.1.2, p.9 of the paper.
**Screenshot:** ![Table 2](tables/table2.png)

**Description:** Defines 7 quality demand indicators (I1–I7) used in the priority index model, categorized into three groups: power supply reliability needs, electricity quality needs, and quality service needs. Each indicator has a name and explanation of its meaning.

**Claims supported:** C01

**Indicator Definitions:**

| Indicator | Category | Name | Meaning |
|-----------|----------|------|---------|
| I1 | Reliability of power supply needs | Expectation of power supply reliability | The difference between the actual power supply reliability and the expected reliability within the statistical time |
| I2 | Reliability of power supply needs | Primary load share | Proportion of primary load in the supply area to the total load during the statistical period |
| I3 | Reliability of power supply needs | Value of loss per unit of electricity shortage | Ratio of GDP to total electricity consumption in the supply area |
| I4 | Electricity quality needs | Frequency nonconformance rate | Ratio of time when power supply frequency exceeds allowable range to total statistical time |
| I5 | Electricity quality needs | Voltage deviation | Ratio of actual voltage to rated voltage within the specified time period |
| I6 | Electricity quality needs | Peak-to-valley difference | Ratio of peak-to-valley difference to maximum load over the statistical time period |
| I7 | Quality service needs | Number of complaints | Total number of reliability-related customer complaints including outage complaints and power quality complaints |

**Notes:** Indicators are calculated at the planning stage using standard distribution network data. The typical daily load profile contains 96 sampling points (15-minute resolution). Annual statistical indicators cover long-term operating conditions. When node-level statistics are unavailable, feeder- or area-level indicators are allocated proportionally.
