# Table 4: Equipment Parameters of Each DESS

**Source:** Section 4.3, p.21 of the paper.
**Screenshot:** ![Table 4](tables/table4.png)

**Description:** Cost and performance parameters used for DESS equipment in the case study.

**Claims supported:** C02, C03

**Parameters:**

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Cost per unit capacity | 2000 CNY/kW |
| Unit power cost | 600 CNY/kW |
| Annual O&M cost per unit of capacity | 10 CNY/kW |
| Annual O&M cost per unit of power | 90 CNY/kW |
| Charge and discharge efficiency | 0.95 |
| Energy storage upper SOC limit factor | 0.9 |
| Energy storage lower SOC limit factor | 0.2 |
| Service life | 12 years |

**Note:** These parameters are used in the multi-objective optimization model for lifecycle cost (F1) and economic benefit (F2) calculations. The discount rate is 0.05.
