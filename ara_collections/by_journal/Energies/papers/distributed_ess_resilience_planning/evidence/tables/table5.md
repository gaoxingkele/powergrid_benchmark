# Table 5: Corresponding Storage Planning Results for Each Case

**Source:** Section 4.3, p.21–22 of the paper.
**Screenshot:** ![Table 5](tables/table5.png)

**Description:** Comparison of DESS planning outcomes across three cases: global traversal (Case 1), one-shot priority ranking (Case 2), and sequential priority-based planning (Case 3). Reports average primary load share, maintenance cost, economic benefit, configured storage nodes and capacities, and belonging block.

**Claims supported:** C02, C03

**Results:**

| Metric | Case 1 | Case 2 | Case 3 |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| Average primary load share | 0.33 | 0.60 | 0.62 |
| Maintenance cost (CNY/year) | 443,400 | 732,600 | 762,600 |
| Economic benefits (CNY/year) | 136,300 | 455,900 | 865,200 |
| Configured energy storage nodes | 20, 41, 70 | 49, 121, 157 | 49, 121, 147 |
| Installation capacity (kWh) | 632, 605, 498 | 733, 1166, 1076 | 733, 1166, 1176 |
| Belonging Blocks | 7, 20, 32 | 26, 21, 21 | 26, 21, 15 |

**Key observations:**
- Case 1 yields scattered nodes with lowest economic benefit (136,300 CNY/year)
- Case 2 has two DESS units in the same block (Block 21), showing spatial concentration
- Case 3 distributes across three different blocks (26, 21, 15) with highest economic benefit (865,200 CNY/year)
- Case 3 achieves the highest average primary load share (0.62)
