# Figure 7: Spain Data — Cumulative Net Load; Typical Day

- **Page**: 19 of 33
- **Caption**: Spain data. Cumulative net load; typical day, discretized into four average net demand levels (S1, S2, S3, and S4).
- **Description**: Shows actual Spain net demand data processed into a typical day profile. The cumulative net load is discretized into four stages: S1 (peak, duration 0.05), S2 and S3 (intermediate, each duration 0.2), and S4 (low demand, duration 0.55). Each stage has a duration and a proportion (p.u.) relative to the average net load.
