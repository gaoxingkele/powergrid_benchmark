# Figure 1: Load Duration Curve of Net Demand with 4 Stages

- **Page**: 7 of 33
- **Caption**: Load duration curve of net demand with 4 stages, S1, S2, S3 and S4.
- **Description**: Illustrates the discretization of the net demand load duration curve into four average demand levels. Stage S1 represents peak demand (shortest duration, highest depth). Stages S2 and S3 represent intermediate demands. Stage S4 represents low demand (longest duration, lowest depth). The sum of products between durations and depths equals 1, maintaining average net demand.
