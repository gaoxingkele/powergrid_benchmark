# Table 2: Virtual Power Line — Battery Status

- **Page**: 8 of 33
- **Caption**: Virtual power line - battery status.
- **Description**: 2x2 matrix describing the four possible ESS charge/discharge statuses based on net demand level and power flow direction (Fij). When demand side Fij is positive (high grid usage): Discharge. When supply side Fij is negative (high grid usage): Charge. When demand side Fij is positive (low grid usage): Charge. When supply side Fij is negative (low grid usage): Discharge.
