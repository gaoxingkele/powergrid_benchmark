## Description
Table 10. Economic indicators and costs under different scenarios for the standalone [Gen/PV/B] system.

## Source
Page 24 of the original PDF.

## Extracted Data
See PDF page 24 for the full table. Key findings: Shortest payback period = 1.7-2.0 years at highest solar irradiance (7.0 kWh/m2/d) and lowest inflation (5-10%), achieving IRR = 49-60%. At 30% inflation, payback extends to 4-8 years with IRR dropping to 12-23%.

## Notes
- Data values are as reported in the original paper.
- NPC = Net Present Cost; LCOE = Levelized Cost of Energy; IRR = Internal Rate of Return.
- G = Grid; PV = Photovoltaic; WT = Wind Turbine; Gen = Diesel Generator; B = Battery.
