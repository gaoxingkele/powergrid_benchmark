## Visual Description
The figure presents a flowchart of the hybrid ACO-FHO-DE algorithm's iterative solution process. It starts with "Grid Initialization" and proceeds to "Upper-layer Topology Optimization" targeting minimization of power loss and maximization of flexible branch factor. The resulting topology is then passed to the "Lower Layer," where "Multi-Scenario Robust Dispatch" minimizes operational cost, renewable curtailment, and flexibility degradation. A "Convergence Check" decision node determines whether the solution feeds back to the upper layer for re-optimization or outputs the final co-optimized topology and dispatch scheme.
