## Visual Description
The figure illustrates the two-layer architecture of the proposed optimization framework. The upper layer performs "Network Structure Optimization" using "FBF (Branch Flexibility Indicator)" and "Network Loss" as objectives. The optimized topology is passed to the lower layer, which performs "Grid Distributed Robust Optimization (DRO)" and "Source-Load Regulation." The feedback loop arrows indicate that dispatch results from the lower layer feed back to the upper layer for iterative refinement.
