## Subfigures

### (a) Output Curves of Wind and PV
Two line curves showing the predicted power output (MW) over 24 hours (time axis t=1 to t=24). The wind power curve shows a smoothed representative profile with higher output during early morning and late evening hours. The PV curve follows the typical solar diurnal pattern with peak output around noon.

### (b) Load Curves of Grid 1, Grid 2, and Grid 3
Three load curves showing the electricity demand (MW) over 24 hours for each of the three interconnected grids. Each grid shows a typical daily load pattern with morning ramp-up, midday peak, and evening peak. Grid 1 and Grid 2 show higher peak loads than Grid 3.

## Axes
- X-axis (both subfigures): Time (hours 1-24), linear scale
- Y-axis (a): Power output (MW), linear scale
- Y-axis (b): Load (MW), linear scale

**Note:** The wind power profile shown is a smoothed representative curve used for visualization. Actual wind power variability is captured through 500 uncertainty scenarios using Latin Hypercube Sampling [Source: Page 11].
