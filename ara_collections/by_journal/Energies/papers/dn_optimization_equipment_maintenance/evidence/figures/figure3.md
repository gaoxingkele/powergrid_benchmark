## Visual Description
The figure shows the topology of the modified CPS62-node active distribution system with three interconnected grids (Grid 1, Grid 2, and Grid 3). Black solid lines represent feeder sections connecting numbered nodes. Red dotted lines represent normally open tie-lines that can be closed for grid interconnection. PV generation units (blue diamonds) are installed at nodes 14, 35, and 49. Wind turbines (red triangles) are located at nodes 6, 26, and 55. Energy storage systems (green squares) are at nodes 8, 31, and 50. The layout shows the radial structure typical of distribution networks.
