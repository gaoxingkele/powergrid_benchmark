**Notes:**
- All values in CNY 10,000
- Scheme 1 has the lowest total operation cost (3.6346) but the highest comprehensive cost (3.7743) due to large flexibility deficit cost (0.1685)
- Scheme 3 achieves the lowest comprehensive cost (3.6686) despite slightly higher operation cost than Scheme 1
- Flexibility deficit cost reduces by 89% from Scheme 1 to Scheme 3 (0.1685 -> 0.0184)
- Wind-solar curtailment cost is lowest in Scheme 3 (0.0147), indicating better renewable utilization
