| Variable | Value | Units |

Notes:
- Ḡ / G_ = per-unit steam turbine maximum / minimum output.
- GSTH = hot steam-turbine startup power output (equals the steam minimum, 80 MW).
- GSTC = cold steam-turbine startup power output (low load, 30 MW, to prevent thermo-mechanical damage).
