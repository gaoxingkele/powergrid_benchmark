# Table 2: Startup and shutdown ramps

- **Source**: Table 2, Section 3 (Results and Discussion), page 11
- **Caption**: "Startup and shutdown ramps."
- **Screenshot**: table2.png
- **Extraction type**: raw_table
- **Object location**: Top-middle of page 11, below Table 1 continuation.

Fixed hourly energy blocks (MW) applied during hot (H), warm (W), and cold (C) startup ramps and during shutdown. Rows are successive hours (H1…H6) of the ramp sequence.

| Hour | H-Startup | W-Startup | C-Startup | Shutdown |
|------|-----------|-----------|-----------|----------|
| H1 | 50 | 50 | 50 | 210 |
| H2 | 100 | 100 | 100 | 100 |
| H3 | 150 | 100 | 100 | 50 |
| H4 | 210 | 150 | 100 | 0 |
| H5 | 0 | 210 | 150 | 0 |
| H6 | 0 | 0 | 210 | 0 |

Notes:
- Hot startup reaches the minimum output (210 MW) in 4 hourly blocks; warm in 5; cold in 6 — colder states need more blocks to reach minimum output.
- Shutdown descends from the minimum generation output (210 MW) to 0 over 3 blocks.
