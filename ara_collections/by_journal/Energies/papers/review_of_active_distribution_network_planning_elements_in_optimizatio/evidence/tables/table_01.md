# Table 1

**Caption:** Key characteristics of passive and active distribution networks.

**Page:** 2

**Description:** Comparison table showing differences between passive and active distribution networks across six characteristics: power flow direction (unidirectional vs. bidirectional), control and monitoring (limited/manual vs. near real-time), flexibility sources (almost none vs. demand response, EVs, storage, DERs), voltage and congestion control (network reinforcement vs. DERs, storage, reactive power control), investment driver (infrastructure upgrades vs. flexibility + selective reinforcement), and system role (passive vs. active system participant).
