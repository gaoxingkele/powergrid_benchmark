# Table 3

**Caption:** Planning objectives of distribution network models.

**Page:** 5

**Description:** Table classifying planning objectives into three categories. Economic objectives: minimization of CAPEX and OPEX, maximization of assets capacity with fixed TOTEX, maximization of net profit value. Technical objectives: maximization of system reliability, improvement of voltage deviation, minimization of network losses. Environmental objectives: minimization of carbon emissions, maximization of renewable DG penetration, maximization of EV charging stations. Each objective includes supporting references.
