# Table 9

**Caption:** Comparison of mathematical formulations in OPF.

**Page:** 14

**Description:** Table comparing five mathematical formulations. Linear Programming: advantages (computationally efficient, scalable, guarantees global optimality); disadvantages (low accuracy, oversimplifies nonlinear power flows, solutions may be physically infeasible). Mixed-Integer Linear Programming: advantages (handles discrete decisions, global optimality via branch-and-bound); disadvantages (high computational demand NP-hard, relies on linearization). Mixed-Integer Non-Linear Programming: advantages (high-fidelity AC power flow and discrete variables, most realistic); disadvantages (computationally intensive, non-convex risk of local optima). Convex Relaxations: advantages (global optimality if exact, tractable and reliable); disadvantages (relaxation gap, handling discrete variables requires MILP). Metaheuristic Methods: advantages (handles nonlinear and non-convex models, model-agnostic, good for large-scale); disadvantages (no guarantee of global optimality, solution quality difficult to benchmark).
