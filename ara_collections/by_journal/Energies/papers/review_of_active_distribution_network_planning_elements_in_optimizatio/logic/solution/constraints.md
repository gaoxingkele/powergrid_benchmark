# Constraints

## Limitations of the Review Methodology

1. **Temporal scope:** The systematic search primarily covered 2020-2025 literature. Earlier foundational work (pre-2020) is included only where it provides essential background; some recent developments from late 2025 may be omitted due to publication lag.

2. **Database coverage:** Searches were limited to IEEE Xplore, Scopus, Web of Science, ScienceDirect, and Google Scholar. Relevant work published in other venues (e.g., conference proceedings not indexed in these databases, technical reports from utilities, industry white papers) may not be captured.

3. **Literature volume:** From approximately 500 initial records identified following PRISMA guidelines, the review was refined to 104 relevant references. Some potentially relevant studies may have been excluded during the screening process.

4. **Simulation-based study focus:** The review prioritizes simulation-based studies while excluding non-peer-reviewed sources. This may introduce bias toward academic research over industry practice, limiting the practical applicability perspective.

5. **GenAI field velocity:** Generative AI is a rapidly evolving field. New model architectures and applications emerge continuously; the taxonomy presented represents the state as of the review's publication date (December 2025).

6. **Geographic bias:** The reviewed literature predominantly represents research from specific regions (Europe, China, North America), potentially underrepresenting ADN planning challenges and solutions in developing countries or regions with different grid characteristics.

7. **Quantitative synthesis limitations:** As a narrative review (not a meta-analysis), the review does not provide quantitative effect sizes or statistical comparisons between different planning approaches, limiting the ability to rank methods by performance.

8. **Overlap between GenAI categories:** Some reviewed GenAI applications employ hybrid models (e.g., VAE-GAN) that span multiple categories; the categorical assignment in the taxonomy represents the predominant model type, which may not capture the full methodological nuance.

9. **OPF formulation comparison scope:** The comparison of OPF formulations (Table 9) is qualitative based on literature characterization rather than empirical benchmarking on standardized test cases, limiting the ability to make quantitative performance claims.
