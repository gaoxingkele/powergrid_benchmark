# Constraints (Scope Limitations of the Survey)

## Scope Boundaries

1. **Geographic scope**: The review focuses primarily on research published in international English-language journals and conferences, without explicit geographic restriction.

2. **Temporal scope**: The survey covers optimization techniques from approximately 45 years ago (from 2025) through the present, organized into three historical periods, but does not systematically cover every year within this range.

3. **Technical focus**: The review concentrates on MOOPF in the context of electrical power systems. Related domains (e.g., other energy systems, non-power-system MOO applications) are outside scope.

4. **Technique coverage**: While the review covers classical and intelligent optimization techniques comprehensively, the depth of treatment varies. Classical methods receive a more condensed treatment, while metaheuristic and hybrid approaches receive more detailed attention, reflecting their prominence in contemporary MOOPF research.

5. **Application coverage**: 41 papers are under detailed assessment. While this provides broad coverage, it does not represent a fully exhaustive census of all MOOPF publications.

6. **Exclusion of proprietary/commercial systems**: The review focuses on academic and open literature; proprietary optimization systems used by utilities or commercial software vendors are not explicitly covered.

7. **Single versus multi-objective boundary**: The review explicitly focuses on multi-objective formulations. Single-objective OPF is discussed only where contextually relevant to understanding the MOOPF landscape.

8. **Hardware/implementation constraints**: Practical implementation challenges related to hardware limitations, communication infrastructure, and real-time deployment constraints are acknowledged but not deeply explored.

9. **No empirical benchmarking**: As a review paper, no new computational experiments are conducted. Comparisons between methods are qualitative and based on reported results in the surveyed literature.
