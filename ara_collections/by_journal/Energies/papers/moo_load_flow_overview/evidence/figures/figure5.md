# Figure 5: Power System Topologies

**Caption:** (a) Radial architecture, (b) Loop architecture, (c) Mesh architecture.

**Description:** Three diagrams illustrating different power system topological configurations:
- (a) Radial/Star: Simplest configuration with feeders branching from a single source
- (b) Loop: Closed-path configuration offering alternative supply routes
- (c) Mesh: Highly interconnected network with multiple parallel power flow paths

**Source:** Page 5 of the original PDF.

**Key Insights:**
- The topology of a power system determines its cost, adaptability, and dependability
- Radial: Low cost, simple, but vulnerable to single-point failures (common in distribution)
- Loop: Improved reliability at the cost of more complex protection
- Mesh: Maximum reliability, highest cost, typical of high-voltage transmission grids
- Network topology directly impacts the feasible solution space and optimization constraints in MOOPF studies
