# Figure 6: Methods to Address Optimization Problems

**Caption:** Methods to address optimization problems [72-77].

**Description:** A classification diagram showing the hierarchy of optimization methods:
- Exact (Deterministic) methods: LP, NLP, MIP, etc.
- Not-exact (Stochastic) methods:
  - Heuristic: Simple heuristics, Metaheuristics
  - Artificial Intelligence: Machine learning, Deep learning, Reinforcement learning

**Source:** Page 7 of the original PDF.

**Key Insights:**
- Provides a comprehensive taxonomy of optimization methods applicable to power systems
- Distinguishes between exact (deterministic) methods that produce guaranteed optimal solutions and not-exact (stochastic) methods that search for near-optimal solutions
- Heuristics are further divided into simple heuristics (constructive/local search) and metaheuristics
- AI-based methods represent the newest category, growing in importance for large-scale data-driven operations
- Forms the basis for the detailed comparison in Table 1
