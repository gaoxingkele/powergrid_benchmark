# Figure 7: Illustration of the Concept Pareto Optimality

**Caption:** Illustration of the concept pareto optimality [78].

**Description:** A diagram showing the concept of Pareto optimality with a set of solutions plotted in objective space. Non-dominated solutions form the Pareto front, while dominated solutions lie inside the front.

**Source:** Page 10 of the original PDF.

**Key Insights:**
- Solutions 3 and 4 "dominate" solutions 7, 8, and 9 (improvement in all objectives)
- Solution 7 dominates solutions 8 and 9
- The set of non-dominated solutions comprises the Pareto front
- Without additional preference information, all Pareto optimal solutions are considered equally good
- Multi-objective optimization has two goals: find solutions close to the Pareto front AND find a diverse set of solutions
