# Figure 8: Categories of Multi-Objective Optimization Algorithm

**Caption:** Categories of Multi-Objective Optimization Algorithm.

**Description:** A diagram categorizing MOO algorithms into five groups:
1. A priori methods (preferences expressed before optimization)
2. A posteriori methods (Pareto front generated first)
3. No preference methods
4. Multi-objective evolutionary algorithms (MOEAs)
5. Data-driven methods (neural networks, reinforcement learning)

**Source:** Page 12 of the original PDF.

**Key Insights:**
- The three traditional categories are based on when preferences are articulated: prior, posterior, or not at all
- MOEAs are commonly used because they can be applied to many types of challenges and search domains
- Data-driven methods represent the newest category, leveraging advances in ML to reveal complex relationships between objectives
- MOEAs and AI-based algorithms are identified as excellent choices for solving MOO issues
