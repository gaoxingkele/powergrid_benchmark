# Figure 1: Evolutionary Trajectory of Optimization Techniques

**Caption:** Evolutionary trajectory of optimization techniques in power systems.

**Description:** A chronological diagram illustrating three historical periods of optimization technique development in power systems:
1. Traditional period (pre-1980s to early 1990s) -- LP, NLP, MIP
2. Metaheuristics and AI period (1990s to late 2000s) -- GA, PSO
3. Modern Hybrid and Advanced period (2010s to present) -- Hybrid approaches, robust optimization, stochastic programming

**Source:** Page 2 of the original PDF.

**Key Insights:**
- Shows the progression from mathematically rigorous deterministic methods to nature-inspired stochastic methods to modern hybrid approaches
- Each period represents a response to increasing grid complexity
- The modern period focuses on managing uncertainty from RES integration and smart grid technologies
