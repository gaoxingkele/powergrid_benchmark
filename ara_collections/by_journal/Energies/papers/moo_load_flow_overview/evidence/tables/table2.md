# Table 2: An Example of MOOPF Solution Based Meta-Heuristic Algorithms (Pareto Front)

**Caption:** An example of MOOPF solution based meta-heuristic algorithms (Pareto front).

**Content:** A small table with three example Pareto-optimal solutions for a power system operator minimizing three objective functions: CO2 emissions (kg/h), generation cost ($/h), and voltage deviation (VD).

| Solution | CO2 (kg/h) | Cost ($/h) | VD |
|----------|------------|-----------|-----|
| 1 | 400 | 900 | 0.016 |
| 2 | 300 | 950 | 0.014 |
| 3 | 200 | 1000 | 0.011 |

**Source:** Page 10 of the original PDF.

**Key Insights:**
- Illustrates the fundamental MOOPF trade-off: minimizing CO2 emissions increases generation cost
- The Pareto front enables decision-makers to choose their preferred operating point based on policy considerations
- Demonstrates that no single solution simultaneously optimizes all three objectives
