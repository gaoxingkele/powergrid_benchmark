# Table 3: Applications of Multi-Objective Optimization in Power Systems

**Caption:** Applications of multi-objective optimization in power systems.

**Content:** A year-by-year mapping of publications (2010-2025) across five application categories:
1. Electrical Network Reconfiguration
2. Economic Dispatch/Optimal Power Flow
3. Power Distribution Planning
4. Operational Planning with RESs Integration

**Source:** Pages 19-20 of the original PDF.

**Key Insights:**
- Applications were restricted to basic operations like reactive and economic power dispatch between 2011 and 2015
- Interest rapidly increased between 2016 and 2020, extending to RES integration and emissions reduction
- Since 2021, MOO has been applied to broader issues: demand response, distributed generation, and system planning
- Research gaps include lack of attention to reliability, sustainability, and social/environmental impacts
- The majority of research remains limited to benchmark test systems, lacking real-world validation
