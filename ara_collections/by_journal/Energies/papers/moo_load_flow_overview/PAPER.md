Hence, the concept of multi-objective optimal power flow (MOOPF) in power systems has become a crucial tool for power system management and planning. # Multi-Objective Optimization of Load Flow in Power Systems: An Overview

**Authors:** Bansendeka Theo Nyingu, Lebogang Masike, Mwana Wa Kalaga Mbukani  
**Venue:** Energies (2025)  
**DOI:** 10.3390/en18226056  
**Type:** Review Paper

## Domain and Scope

This review paper surveys the field of multi-objective optimal power flow (MOOPF) in power systems. It covers the evolution of optimization techniques from classical deterministic methods through metaheuristics to modern hybrid and AI-based approaches. The paper categorizes optimization techniques, reviews their applications in power systems, and identifies research gaps and future directions.
