- **Visual description**: IEEE 33-bus system topology showing the optimal reinforcement measures and line failure scenarios at the 17th hour of the second state under the second wind-speed scenario, with reinforcement cost CNY 24 x 10^4. Shows four post-fault island regions: left part supplied by upper-level grid (connected to main grid), blue region supplied by DG1, purple region supplied by EV2 and DG3, red region supplied by MEG2 and EV1. Tie lines (8-21, 9-15, 12-22, 18-33, 25-29) close to form islands.
- **Key insight**: After fault occurrence, the distribution network is divided into islands maintaining radial structure. Resources (MEGs, EVs, DGs) are allocated to prioritize critical load supply in each island, demonstrating the coordinated optimization of flexible resources with line hardening.
