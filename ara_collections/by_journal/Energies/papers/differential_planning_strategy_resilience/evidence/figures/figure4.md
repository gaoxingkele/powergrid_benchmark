# Figure 4: Comparison of Case 1 and Case 2

- **Source**: Page 15, Section 5.2
- **Screenshot**: `figure4.png`
- **Figure type**: quantitative_plot
- **Extraction method**: visual_description
- **Reading confidence**: low
- **Claims supported**: C01
- **Axes**: Scenario condition (categorical: no damage / considering failure probability) on horizontal; Total cost (CNY 10^4, linear scale) on vertical
- **Trend summary**: Bar chart comparing Case 1 (traditional single-level hardening, no DDU) and Case 2 (proposed DDU multi-level hardening) under two conditions: (a) when hardened lines do not fail ("no damage"), and (b) when the probability of line failure is considered. When no damage occurs, DDU model cost is higher (CNY 6.71 million vs lower for Case 1). When failure probability is considered, DDU model cost is lower than Case 1.
- **Key insight**: When no damage occurs after hardening, DDU model cost is higher because more sophisticated modeling requires investment. However, when failure probability is considered, DDU model cost is reduced by CNY 2.37 million compared to single-level, because the multi-level framework provides more flexible and cost-effective reinforcement combinations.
