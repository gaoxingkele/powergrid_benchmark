- **Axes**: Candidate lines (categorical: 1-2, 2-3, 3-23, 23-24, 31-32, 32-33, 3-4, 5-6) on horizontal; First-order Sobol' index Si (continuous, linear scale 0-1) on vertical. Multiple budget levels shown as grouped bars.
- **Trend summary**: At low budgets (12-15), main feeder lines 1-2 and 2-3 and key tie line 31-32 have highest Si values. As budget increases to 18-21, contributions expand to secondary lines (3-23, 23-24, 3-4, 5-6). At higher budget (24), lines 31-32 and 32-33 maintain dominant contributions while secondary lines become comparable. This reveals a "core first, then broader coverage" investment trajectory.
- **Key insight**: The Sobol' indices reveal the relative importance of each line reinforcement for system resilience. Lines with consistently low Si values across all budgets have limited impact and can be deprioritized. The ranking is budget-dependent, changing from main-feeder-dominated at low budgets to multi-point collaboration at high budgets.
