- **Key insight**: Total-effect indices (STi) for lines 31-32 (0.35) and 32-33 (0.29) are significantly higher than their first-order indices (shown in Figure 6), implying strong interaction effects with other reinforcements and operational decisions (tie-line switching, DG/MEG supply coordination). Lines 1-2 (0.18) and 2-3 (0.19) have lower STi values because their contribution is mostly direct (first-order), which is already captured in the main effect.
