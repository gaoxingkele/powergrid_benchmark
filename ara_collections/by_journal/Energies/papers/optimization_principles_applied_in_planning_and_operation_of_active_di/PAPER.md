## Scope

This ARA covers a 4-page Special Issue editorial published in *Energies* (2024) by Rene Prenc. The editorial surveys optimization principles for active distribution network (ADN) planning and operation. It introduces twelve optimization objectives, several methodological approaches, and highlights five selected papers from the Special Issue. No experimental results, tables, or figures are present.
