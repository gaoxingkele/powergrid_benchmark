## Trend summary
In Scenario 2 (PV inoperable due to rain, higher electricity prices), the IGA-optimized schedule shows that the system operator avoids purchasing electricity during peak price hours. The natural gas distribution between the CHP unit and gas boiler is reasonably allocated to compensate for the absent PV generation while ensuring stable electricity and heat supply. The algorithm efficiently utilizes wind energy to avoid curtailment. Exact values not reported in text.
