## Trend summary
The top subplot shows the charging (positive) and discharging (negative) power of the battery ESS over 24 hours in Scenario 1. The ESS charges during periods of low electricity prices and discharges during periods of high electricity prices, providing economic benefits and alleviating operational pressure. The bottom subplot shows the ESS state of charge (SOC) varying within the permitted range (0-100%), starting and ending at 50% as required. Exact power and SOC values at each hour are not reported in the source text.
