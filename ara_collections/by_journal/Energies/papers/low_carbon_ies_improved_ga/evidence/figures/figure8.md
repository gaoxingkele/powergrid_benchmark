## Trend summary
The figure shows the violation magnitude for each of the 49 equality constraints (24h electric load balance + 24h heat load balance + initial ESS SOC constraint) after IGA optimization in Scenario 1. All violations are within 0.2 kW, with the maximum violation at 0.20 kW as reported in Table 1. This demonstrates that IGA achieves high precision in handling equality constraints despite load magnitudes on the order of hundreds of kW. Most constraints show violations well below 0.2 kW.
