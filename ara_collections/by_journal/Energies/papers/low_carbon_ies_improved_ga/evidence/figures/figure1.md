## Visual description
- **Components**: The diagram shows an IES with the following subsystems:
  - Renewable energy sources: Wind power and Solar power generation feeding into the electrical system
  - CHP unit (Combined Heat and Power): consumes natural gas to produce both electricity and heat
  - Gas boiler (GB): consumes natural gas to produce supplementary heat
  - Battery Energy Storage System (ESS): stores excess electricity
  - Waste Heat Recovery Unit (WHU): recovers waste heat from CHP exhaust
  - Power grid: external electricity connection for purchasing/selling electricity
  - Natural gas pipeline: external gas supply
  - Electrical load and Thermal load: consumer demand endpoints
  - Chimney: exhaust gas emission
- **Connections**:
  - Natural gas pipeline feeds both the CHP unit and GB
  - CHP produces electricity (to electrical bus) and heat (to thermal bus + WHU)
  - WHU recovers CHP waste heat and routes to thermal load
  - GB produces additional heat routed to thermal load
  - Solar PV and Wind turbines feed electricity to the electrical bus
  - ESS can charge from or discharge to the electrical bus
  - Power grid connects to the electrical bus for import/export
  - Electrical load draws from the electrical bus; Thermal load draws from the thermal bus
- **What it conveys**: The IES integrates electricity, natural gas, and heat networks with multiple generation, conversion, and storage components to serve electrical and thermal loads.
