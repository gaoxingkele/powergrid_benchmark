## Trend summary
In Scenario 3 (same load and prices as Scenario 1, but PV inoperable due to rain), the IES reduces electricity purchases during peak price periods (15:00-17:00) while increasing CHP unit output to meet electrical load demands. The gas purchase is allocated between CHP and GB to maintain stable supply of both electrical and thermal loads throughout all time periods. Exact values not reported in text.
