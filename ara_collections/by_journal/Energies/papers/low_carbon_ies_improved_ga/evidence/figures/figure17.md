## Trend summary
The figure shows the Pareto front (set of non-dominated solutions) obtained by IGA and MGA for Scenario 1. IGA's Pareto front lies to the lower-left (better in both objectives) relative to MGA's Pareto front, indicating that IGA finds solutions with both lower operating costs and lower carbon emissions. This superiority is attributed to IGA's cyclic crossover operation (preserving parental genetic structure) and polynomial mutation (maintaining population diversity), which together avoid premature convergence and improve global search ability. The best IGA solution on the Pareto front (selected via equal weighting w1=w2=1) achieves o1=3202.13 CNY and o2=4216.66 m3, while the best MGA solution achieves o1=3290.70 CNY and o2=4330.03 m3 (Table 1).
