# Table 3: Annualization-Factor Sensitivity for Different Economic Lifetimes

| Parameter / Lifetime | 10 Years | 20 Years | 30 Years |
|---------------------|----------|----------|----------|
| Straight-line factor (1/L) | 0.100 | 0.050 | 0.033 |
| CRF (r = 8%) | 0.149 | 0.102 | 0.089 |
| Interpretation | Conservative recovery | Typical asset | Wind-life extension |

**Key findings**:
- Longer project horizons reduce annualized capital burden
- CRF (Capital Recovery Factor) is higher than straight-line at all horizons due to discounting
- Extended lifetime (30yr) would make capital-intensive assets (wind turbines, storage) more economically attractive
- Interpretation is provided as sensitivity analysis since full re-optimization would require rerunning the planning algorithm
