# Figure 4: Scenario Distribution and Reduction

**Source**: Page 12 of the PDF.

**Visual Description**:
A scatter plot showing the scenario space with:
- **X-axis**: Average Wind Power (p.u.), ranging from approximately 0.36 to 0.50
- **Y-axis**: Average Load (MW), ranging from approximately 8.5 to 12.5 MW

**Plot elements**:
- **Gray scatter points**: The initial N=1000 Monte Carlo generated scenarios, distributed broadly over the wind-load space
- **Red scatter points**: The selected N=500 representative scenarios after the scenario reduction procedure

**Distribution characteristics**:
- The original scenarios (gray) cover a roughly elliptical region centered around average wind power ≈0.43 p.u. and average load ≈10.5 MW
- The representative scenarios (red) maintain the same overall distribution shape and density, confirming representativeness
- There is a negative correlation between average wind power and average load (higher wind tends to coincide with lower load, which is meteorologically plausible — windy periods often coincide with lower demand)

**Data extraction**: Not available — the figure demonstrates the qualitative representativeness of the scenario reduction, not specific values for individual scenarios.
