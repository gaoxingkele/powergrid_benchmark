# Figure 5: Input Data for the Case Study

**Source**: Page 13 of the PDF.

**Visual Description**:
Three-panel figure (a, b, c) showing the input data for the case study.

## Panel (a): Uncertainty Scenarios
- **X-axis**: Time (h), 0-24 hours
- **Y-axis**: Power (MW), ranging from ≈0-500 MW
- **Red envelope**: Range of electrical load scenarios (500 scenarios shown as band)
- **Blue envelope**: Range of wind power generation scenarios (500 scenarios shown as band)
- The envelopes show the variability across scenarios throughout the day
- Load has mid-day peaks; wind has higher nighttime variability

## Panel (b): Grid Electricity Prices
- **X-axis**: Time (h), 0-24 hours
- **Y-axis**: Price ($/MWh), ranging from 0-250 $/MWh
- **Blue line with markers**: Buy Price (TOU - Time of Use tariff)
  - Off-peak (night/early morning): ≈40-50 $/MWh
  - Mid-peak: ≈100-120 $/MWh
  - Peak (mid-day): ≈200-250 $/MWh
- **Red line with markers**: Sell Price (FiT - Feed-in Tariff)
  - Constant at ≈40-50 $/MWh throughout the day
- The spread between buy and sell prices creates arbitrage opportunities

## Panel (c): Hydrogen Demand Profile
- **X-axis**: Time (h), 0-24 hours
- **Y-axis**: Hydrogen Demand (Nm³/h), ranging from 0-500 Nm³/h
- **Black bars**: Show hourly hydrogen demand
- Demand is zero from 00:00-08:00
- Demand is high from 09:00-17:00 (rigid working hours window)
- Peak demand ≈400-500 Nm³/h during mid-day hours

## Data extraction
Values shown are approximate — exact scenario data is available from the authors upon reasonable request.
