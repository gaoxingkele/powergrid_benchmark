# Figure 1: Workflow of the Proposed DSS Architecture

- **Source**: Energies 2026, 19, 3243, page 10
- **Caption**: Workflow of the proposed decision support system (DSS) architecture.
- **Screenshot**: `figure1.png`
- **Figure type**: diagram
- **Extraction method**: Rendered page 10 at 2x scale
- **Reading confidence**: High
- **Visual description**: A flowchart diagram showing the eight-stage DSS architecture. The workflow progresses from (1) LLM-assisted criterion extraction from policy/strategy documents, through (2) secondary-data decision matrix construction, (3) normalization (benefit/cost classification and min-max normalization), (4) fuzzy-interval extension, (5) weighting and scoring (equal-weight baseline with entropy, CRITIC, hybrid benchmarks), (6) persona-based personalized scoring, (7) fairness and explainability layer, to (8) simulated-agent validation laboratory. Outputs include investment readiness scores, persona-specific rankings, and validation results.
