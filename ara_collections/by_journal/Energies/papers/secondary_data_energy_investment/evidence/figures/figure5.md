# Figure 5: Relationship Between Baseline Investment Readiness Score and GDP per Capita

- **Source**: Energies 2026, 19, 3243, page 22
- **Caption**: Relationship between baseline investment readiness score and gross domestic product (GDP) per capita. The dashed line represents the fitted linear trend line, showing the overall association between GDP per capita and the baseline readiness score across the country sample.
- **Screenshot**: `figure5.png`
- **Figure type**: quantitative_plot (scatter plot)
- **Extraction method**: Rendered page 22 at 2x scale
- **Reading confidence**: High
- **Axes**: X-axis: GDP per capita (Constant 2015 US$); Y-axis: Baseline investment readiness score (0-1 scale)
- **Data / trend summary**: Scatter plot of 36 countries showing a generally positive but non-linear relationship between GDP per capita and readiness score. Higher-income countries generally achieve stronger readiness scores, but the distribution is not fully linear. Countries with very high GDP per capita do not automatically occupy the top positions (showing scatter above and below the regression line), while some countries with lower income levels show stronger readiness than their income position alone would suggest. The dashed linear trend line indicates Pearson r = 0.727. Notable outliers include resource-constrained but institutionally strong countries above the line and some higher-GDP countries with weaker readiness below the line.
