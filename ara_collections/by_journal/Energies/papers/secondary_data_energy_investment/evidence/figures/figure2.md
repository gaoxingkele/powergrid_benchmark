- **Visual description**: A data-flow diagram complementing Figure 1. It traces the data processing route from raw document and indicator collection (policy documents, public data sources: World Bank WDI/WGI, OWID/Ember, IRENA, NASA POWER, Eurostat) through normalization, fuzzy representation, weighting, ranking, validation, and investment-readiness interpretation. The diagram clarifies which inputs are transformed at each stage and how empirical outputs are produced.
