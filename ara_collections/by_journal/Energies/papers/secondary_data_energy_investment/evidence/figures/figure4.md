# Figure 4: Contribution-Based Readiness Profiles of Selected Countries

- **Source**: Energies 2026, 19, 3243, page 20
- **Caption**: Contribution-based readiness profiles of selected countries. Green upward arrows indicate criteria that strengthen the country's strategic energy investment readiness score, while red downward arrows indicate criteria that constrain or reduce the country's relative readiness position.
- **Screenshot**: `figure4.png`
- **Figure type**: mixed (quantitative_plot with annotated arrows)
- **Extraction method**: Rendered page 20 at 2x scale
- **Reading confidence**: High
- **Visual description**: A criterion-level contribution visualization showing Norway, Portugal, and Turkiye as selected cases. For each country, the 18 criteria (C01-C18) are listed with green upward arrows indicating criteria where the country's normalized score exceeds the mean (positive contribution to relative readiness) and red downward arrows indicating criteria where the country's normalized score is below the mean (constraining contribution). Norway's strongest contributors are energy security indicators and institutional quality; its constraints include solar irradiance, electricity demand, and renewable installed capacity. Portugal's strengths are in sustainability and solar resource; constraints are in market size and GDP per capita. Turkiye's strengths are demand growth and solar resource; constraints are inflation, regulatory quality, government effectiveness, and GDP per capita.
- **Trend summary**: Norway's profile is dominated by energy security and institutional quality. Portugal has a transition-oriented profile with sustainability strengths. Turkiye has an opportunity-constrained profile with demand/resource potential offset by institutional and macroeconomic weaknesses.
