# Figure 3: Dimension-Level Readiness Profiles of Selected Countries

- **Source**: Energies 2026, 19, 3243, page 17
- **Caption**: Dimension-level readiness profiles of selected countries.
- **Screenshot**: `figure3.png`
- **Figure type**: quantitative_plot
- **Extraction method**: Rendered page 17 at 2x scale
- **Reading confidence**: High
- **Axes**: Radar/spider chart with six axes representing the six readiness dimensions: Macroeconomic Feasibility, Institutional Capacity, Energy Security, Sustainability and Decarbonization, Market and Demand Conditions, Technical Resource Potential. Each axis represents the mean normalized score for criteria within that dimension.
- **Data / trend summary**: The chart shows profiles for selected countries including Norway, Denmark, United States, Sweden, Switzerland, Portugal, Turkiye, and Poland. Norway shows strong Energy Security, Institutional Capacity, and Sustainability performance. Denmark and Switzerland display balanced profiles particularly in Institutional Capacity and Macroeconomic Feasibility. The United States has the strongest Market and Demand profile. Sweden performs strongly in Sustainability and Energy Security. Turkiye shows stronger Technical Resource Potential and Market/Demand but weaker Institutional and Macroeconomic readiness. Poland records lower scores in Sustainability, Market/Demand, and Technical Resource Potential.
