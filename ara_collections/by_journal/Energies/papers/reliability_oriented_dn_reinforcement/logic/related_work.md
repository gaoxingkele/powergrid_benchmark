# Related Work

## RW01: Distribution System Planning Fundamentals
- **Reference**: [1] Abid, M.S.; Apon, H.J.; Morshed, K.A.; Ahmed, A. Optimal planning of multiple renewable energy-integrated distribution system with uncertainties using artificial hummingbird algorithms. IEEE Access 2022, 10, 40716-40730.
- **Key Contribution**: Optimal planning of multiple renewable energy-integrated distribution systems considering uncertainties.
- **Relation to Paper**: Establishes the foundation for distribution system planning with renewable integration.

## RW02: Multi-Microgrid Operation and Reconfiguration
- **Reference**: [2] Kalantari, A.; Lesani, H. Operation Scheduling of Distribution Network with Photovoltaic/Wind/Battery Multi-Microgrids and Reconfiguration considering Reliability and Self-Healing. Int. J. Energy Res. 2024, 2024, 5724653.
- **Key Contribution**: Coordinated scheduling of PV, wind, and battery microgrids with reconfiguration reduces interruption frequency and duration while enhancing self-healing.
- **Relation to Paper**: Supports the use of coordinated scheduling for reliability improvement through reconfiguration. The proposed framework extends this to long-term reinforcement planning.

## RW03: Distribution System Reliability with DERs and EV Charging
- **Reference**: [3] Sangeetha, P.; Suresh Babu, G.; Vidyasagar, E. Reliability Assessment of Distribution System with the Integration of Distributed Energy Resources and Electric Vehicle Charging Stations. WSEAS Trans. Power Syst. 2025, 20, 14-28.
- **Key Contribution**: Demonstrates that DERs and EV charging stations can improve overall system reliability when supported by energy storage.
- **Relation to Paper**: Provides evidence that DER integration benefits reliability, justifying the paper's focus on DG-supported restoration and islanding.

## RW04: Distribution Automation and Reliability
- **Reference**: [4] Luo, F.; Ge, N.; Xu, J. Power Supply Reliability Analysis of Distribution Systems Considering Data Transmission Quality of Distribution Automation Terminals. Energies 2023, 16, 7826.
- **Key Contribution**: Analyzes how distribution automation and communication infrastructure enhance reliability benefits of renewable integration.
- **Relation to Paper**: Supports the framework's reliance on switching operations and automated restoration.

## RW05: Reliability Evaluation of Power Systems
- **Reference**: [5] Allan, R.N.; Billinton, R. Reliability Evaluation of Power Systems; Springer: New York, NY, USA, 2013.
- **Key Contribution**: Foundational text on analytical reliability assessment techniques including FMEA under N-1 contingency conditions.
- **Relation to Paper**: The paper builds on this analytical approach for its reliability assessment methodology.

## RW06: Monte Carlo Simulation for DG Reliability
- **Reference**: [6] Wang, P.; Billinton, R. Reliability benefit analysis of adding WTG to a distribution system. IEEE Trans. Energy Convers. 2001, 16, 134-139.
- **Key Contribution**: Used time-sequential MCS with ARMA models to assess reliability benefits of wind turbine generators.
- **Relation to Paper**: Identified as computationally intensive approach that the proposed analytical method aims to improve upon.

## RW07: Feeder Planning Model
- **Reference**: [7] Wang, M.; Yang, M.; Fang, Z.; Wang, M.; Wu, Q. A practical feeder planning model for urban distribution system. IEEE Trans. Power Syst. 2022, 38, 1297-1308.
- **Key Contribution**: Urban distribution system feeder planning model.
- **Relation to Paper**: Provides reliability parameter data (failure rates, repair times) used in the proposed model.

## RW08: MCS for Islanding Assessment
- **Reference**: [8] Conti, S.; Rizzo, S.A. Monte Carlo simulation by using a systematic approach to assess distribution system reliability considering intentional islanding. IEEE Trans. Power Deliv. 2015, 30, 64-73.
- **Key Contribution**: MCS-based assessment of distribution reliability under intentional islanding.
- **Relation to Paper**: The proposed analytical method aims to achieve similar accuracy with lower computational burden.

## RW09: Analytical POT-Based Assessment
- **Reference**: [9] Conti, S.; Nicolosi, R.; Rizzo, S. Generalized systematic approach to assess distribution system reliability with renewable distributed generators and microgrids. IEEE Trans. Power Deliv. 2012, 27, 261-270.
- **Key Contribution**: Probability Outage Table (POT) approach for load shedding and islanding safety policy assessment.
- **Relation to Paper**: Analytical approach that the paper extends by incorporating hierarchical restoration modes and multi-source uncertainty.

## RW10: Islanded Mode Reliability
- **Reference**: [10] Atwa, Y.M.; El-Saadany, E.F. Reliability evaluation for distribution system with renewable distributed generation during islanded mode of operation. IEEE Trans. Power Syst. 2009, 24, 572-581.
- **Key Contribution**: Reliability evaluation for distribution systems with renewable DG during islanded operation.
- **Relation to Paper**: Similar focus on islanded operation reliability; the proposed framework adds infrastructure investment optimization.

## RW11: Hybrid DG and Automation Planning
- **Reference**: [11] Pirouzi, S.; Zaghian, M.; Aghaei, J.; Chabok, H.; Abbasi, M.; Norouzi, M.; Shafie-khah, M.; Catalao, J.P. Hybrid planning of distributed generation and distribution automation to improve reliability and operation indices. Int. J. Electr. Power Energy Syst. 2022, 135, 107540.
- **Key Contribution**: Combined DG and automation planning to minimize operational costs while improving reliability.
- **Relation to Paper**: Similar optimization objective; the proposed framework adds hierarchical contingency management.

## RW12: Time-Sequential Simulation
- **Reference**: [12] Wang, P.; Billinton, R. Time-sequential simulation technique for rural distribution system reliability cost/worth evaluation including wind generation as alternative supply. IEE Proc.-Gener. Transm. Distrib. 2001, 148, 355-360.
- **Key Contribution**: Time-sequential simulation for reliability cost/worth evaluation with wind generation.
- **Relation to Paper**: Identified as computationally intensive; the paper's analytical approach offers efficiency gains.

## RW13: Analytical DG Reliability Method
- **Reference**: [13] Zou, K.; Agalgaonkar, A.P.; Muttaqi, K.M.; Perera, S. An analytical approach for reliability evaluation of distribution systems containing dispatchable and nondispatchable renewable DG units. IEEE Trans. Smart Grid 2014, 5, 2657-2665.
- **Key Contribution**: Analytical reliability evaluation for systems with both dispatchable and nondispatchable renewable DG.
- **Relation to Paper**: Analytical approach similar in spirit to the paper's probabilistic model but without the hierarchical restoration framework.

## RW14: Smart Distribution Reliability
- **Reference**: [14] Celli, G.; Ghiani, E.; Pilo, F.; Soma, G.G. Reliability assessment in smart distribution networks. Electr. Power Syst. Res. 2013, 104, 164-175.
- **Key Contribution**: Sequential MCS and Markov chain modeling for smart distribution network reliability.
- **Relation to Paper**: Identified as computationally heavy; scales poorly with system state growth.

## RW15: Islanding with Telecontrolled Switches
- **Reference**: [15] Conti, S.; Faraci, G.; La Corte, A.; Nicolosi, R.; Rizzo, S.A.; Schembra, G. Effect of islanding and telecontrolled switches on distribution system reliability considering load and green-energy fluctuations. Appl. Sci. 2016, 6, 138.
- **Key Contribution**: Effect of islanding and telecontrolled switches on reliability under renewable fluctuations.
- **Relation to Paper**: Examines similar islanding concepts; the proposed framework integrates these within long-term planning optimization.

## RW16: Analytical Adequacy with Protection
- **Reference**: [16] Chen, C.; Wu, W.; Zhang, B.; Singh, C. An analytical adequacy evaluation method for distribution networks considering protection strategies and distributed generators. IEEE Trans. Power Deliv. 2014, 30, 1392-1400.
- **Key Contribution**: Analytical adequacy evaluation considering protection strategies.
- **Relation to Paper**: Method to simplify generation and load levels; the paper uses a probabilistic scenario approach instead.

## RW17: Probabilistic-Chronological Modeling
- **Reference**: [17] Alotaibi, M.A.; Salama, M. An efficient probabilistic-chronological matching modeling for DG planning and reliability assessment in power distribution systems. Renew. Energy 2016, 99, 158-169.
- **Key Contribution**: Probabilistic-chronological matching model for DG planning and reliability assessment.
- **Relation to Paper**: Prior work by the same author; the current paper extends this with hierarchical restoration and reinforcement planning.

## RW18: Active Network Operational Limits
- **Reference**: [18] Escalera, A.; Hayes, B.; Prodanovic, M. Reliability assessment of active distribution networks considering distributed energy resources and operational limits. In CIRED Workshop; IET, 2016; pp. 1-4.
- **Key Contribution**: Reliability assessment considering operational limits in active distribution networks.
- **Relation to Paper**: Considers operational limits but overlooks combined DG intermittency and synchronized grid connectivity.

## RW19: Restoration Sequence and Network Constraints
- **Reference**: [19] Li, W.; Wang, P.; Li, Z.; Liu, Y. Reliability evaluation of complex radial distribution systems considering restoration sequence and network constraints. IEEE Trans. Power Deliv. 2004, 19, 753-758.
- **Key Contribution**: Restoration sequence modeling with network constraints for radial distribution systems.
- **Relation to Paper**: Focuses on restoration but does not include DG intermittency or long-term reinforcement.

## RW20: Smart Distribution Reliability Assessment
- **Reference**: [20] Guo, H.; Levi, V.; Buhari, M. Reliability assessment of smart distribution networks. In Proc. ISGT Asia, 2015; pp. 1-6.
- **Key Contribution**: Reliability assessment methods for smart distribution networks.
- **Relation to Paper**: MCS-based approach with high computational burden.

## RW21: DG Impact on Radial Systems
- **Reference**: [21] Neto, C.; da Silva, M.G.; Rodrigues, A.B. Impact of distributed generation on reliability evaluation of radial distribution systems under network constraints. In Proc. PMAPS, 2006; pp. 1-6.
- **Key Contribution**: DG impact on radial system reliability under network constraints.
- **Relation to Paper**: Examines dispatchable DGs but overlooks intermittency impact.

## RW22: DER Impact Under Load Transfer Restrictions
- **Reference**: [22] da Silva, A.M.L.; Nascimento, L.C.; da Rosa, M.A.; Issicaba, D.; Lopes, J.A.P. Distributed energy resources impact on distribution system reliability under load transfer restrictions. IEEE Trans. Smart Grid 2012, 3, 2048-2055.
- **Key Contribution**: Analytical cut-set techniques with chronological MCS for power transfer capacity evaluation.
- **Relation to Paper**: Bridges analytical and simulation approaches; the paper extends this to reinforcement planning.

## RW23: Reliability-Oriented Reconfiguration
- **Reference**: [23] Karafotis, P.A.; Evangelopoulos, V.A.; Georgilakis, P.S. Reliability-oriented reconfiguration of power distribution systems considering load and RES production scenarios. IEEE Trans. Power Deliv. 2022, 37, 4668-4678.
- **Key Contribution**: Integration of SAIDI and ENS into expansion models for efficient switch placement.
- **Relation to Paper**: Similar theme of reliability-oriented planning; the paper adds coordinated contingency hierarchy.

## RW24: Expansion Planning with Voltage Quality
- **Reference**: [24] Villarreal, M.I.Z.; Tellez, A.A.; Krishnan, N.; Garcia, M. Expansion Planning of Electrical Distribution Systems Considering Voltage Quality and Reliability Criteria. Energies 2025, 18, 2822.
- **Key Contribution**: Distribution expansion planning considering voltage quality and reliability criteria.
- **Relation to Paper**: Provides reliability parameter values (failure rates, repair times) used in the paper.

## RW25: Weather-Aware Probabilistic Framework
- **Reference**: [25] Aguila Tellez, A.; Krishnan, N.; Garcia, E.; Carrion, D.; Ruiz, M. A Probabilistic Framework for Reliability Assessment of Active Distribution Networks with High Renewable Penetration Under Extreme Weather Conditions. Energies 2025, 18, 6525.
- **Key Contribution**: Weather-aware probabilistic framework for capturing non-monotonic PV impact on reliability.
- **Relation to Paper**: Similar probabilistic approach but focused on weather extremes; the paper addresses average conditions.

## RW26: Distributionally Robust Expansion
- **Reference**: [26] He, C.; Zhou, Y.; Liu, X.; Nan, L.; Liu, T.; Wu, L. Reliability-constrained distributionally robust expansion planning of integrated electricity-gas distribution system with demand response. Energy 2025, 321, 135409.
- **Key Contribution**: Distributionally robust expansion planning for worst-case renewable output scenarios.
- **Relation to Paper**: Robust optimization approach complements the paper's probabilistic approach.

## RW27: DER-Based Black Start and Restoration
- **Reference**: [27] Konar, S.; Srivastava, A.K. MPC-based black start and restoration for resilient DER-rich electric distribution system. IEEE Access 2023, 11, 69177-69189.
- **Key Contribution**: DERs as black-start units with valid switching sequence during sequential restoration.
- **Relation to Paper**: Related to self-healing concept; the paper integrates restoration within a planning framework.

## RW28: IPSO for Switch and DG Placement
- **Reference**: [28] Alanazi, A.; Alanazi, T.I. Multi-Objective Framework for Optimal Placement of Distributed Generations and Switches in Reconfigurable Distribution Networks: An Improved Particle Swarm Optimization Approach. Sustainability 2023, 15, 9034.
- **Key Contribution**: Improved PSO with chaotic inertia weights for switch and DG placement optimization.
- **Relation to Paper**: Metaheuristic approach for similar problem; the paper uses GA instead of PSO.
