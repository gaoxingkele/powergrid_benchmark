## Description
A comparative table listing the four optimization algorithms (DE, PSO, CA, GWO) with their performance characteristics, pros, and cons. DE offers high exploration and moderate speed with strong global search capability but is sensitive to F and CR settings. PSO provides fast convergence but may get stuck in local minima. CA uses cultural learning for adaptive search but is more complex and slower. GWO offers balanced exploration and exploitation.
