# Figure 1

## Caption
Probability distribution functions fitted for stochastic variables: (a) Temperature histogram. (b) Solar irradiance histogram. (c) Wind speed histogram.

## Page
6

## Description
Three histograms showing fitted PDFs: Green line on each histogram represents the PDF fitted to the behavior of the stochastic variable. Weibull distribution used for wind speed and solar radiation; Gaussian Mixture Model (GMM) used for temperature due to its bimodal behavior.
