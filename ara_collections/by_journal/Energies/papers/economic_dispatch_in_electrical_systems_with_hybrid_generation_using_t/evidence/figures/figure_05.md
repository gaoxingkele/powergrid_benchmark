# Figure 5

## Caption
Figure 5. Prediction of renewable resources. (a) Temperature prediction. (b) Solar irradiance prediction. (c) Wind speed prediction.

## Page
14

## Description
Three prediction graphs generated using Monte Carlo simulation with 1000 iterations per variable. Each curve shows the average prediction (central blue line) with shaded area representing the 10th and 90th percentiles of simulated values: (a) temperature over 24 hours, (b) solar irradiance over daylight hours, (c) wind speed over 24 hours. The upper bound (maximum limit) is used as input to the dispatch model.

Section 5.1, "Renewable Energy for Both Scenarios"
