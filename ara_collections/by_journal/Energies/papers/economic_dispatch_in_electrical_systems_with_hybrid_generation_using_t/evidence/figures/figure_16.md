## Description
Two pie charts (or bar charts) showing the percentage contribution of each generation type (hydroelectric, thermoelectric, photovoltaic, and wind) in both scenarios: (a) Scenario 1 (low water availability) where thermal generation compensates for reduced hydro output, and (b) Scenario 2 (high water availability) where hydroelectric contribution increases. Renewable sources (wind and solar) remain constant across both scenarios.
