## Description
Four stacked bar charts showing the energy contribution of each generation unit (thermoelectric, hydroelectric, photovoltaic, and wind) over 24 hours as proposed by each optimization technique in Scenario 1 (low water availability). Each subplot compares how the four algorithms dispatch generation from different sources to meet demand.
