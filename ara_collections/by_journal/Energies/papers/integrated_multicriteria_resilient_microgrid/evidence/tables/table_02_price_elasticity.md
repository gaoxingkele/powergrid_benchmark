Self-elasticities (diagonal) are negative for peak (-0.1), mid-peak (+0.01, unusual), and valley (-0.1). Cross-elasticities represent the shift of demand between periods in response to price changes. These values are adopted from Nayak et al. (2021) [Ref 53].
