# Figure 7: Wind Speed MCS Scenarios

**Source**: Page 17 of the published PDF.

**Caption**: Wind speed uncertainty scenarios based on Monte Carlo simulations.

**Content**: Visualization of 50 Monte Carlo-generated scenarios for wind speed (m/s) derived from LSTM forecast MAE (0.14 m/s) with a 25% uncertainty range. The scenario ensemble represents the stochastic nature of wind resource availability, informing the MOPSO optimization of WT capacity under uncertainty.
