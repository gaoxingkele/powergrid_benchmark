# Figure 6: Solar Irradiance MCS Scenarios

**Source**: Page 17 of the published PDF.

**Caption**: Solar irradiance uncertainty scenarios based on Monte Carlo simulations.

**Content**: Visualization of 50 Monte Carlo-generated scenarios for solar irradiance (W/m^2) derived from LSTM forecast MAE (19.97 W/m^2) with a 25% uncertainty range. The ensemble of scenarios captures the stochastic variability in solar resource availability, enabling robust capacity sizing and operational planning under uncertainty.
