# Figure 8: Load Demand MCS Scenarios

**Source**: Page 17 of the published PDF.

**Caption**: Load demand uncertainty scenarios based on Monte Carlo simulations.

**Content**: Visualization of 50 Monte Carlo-generated scenarios for load demand (kW) derived from LSTM forecast MAE (24.68 kW) with a 25% uncertainty range. The scenarios capture both natural load variation and potential uncertainty from customer response to DRP pricing volatility, enabling robust load-serving capability design.
