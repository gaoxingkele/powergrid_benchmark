# Figure 2: Evaluation Index System of Generalized Adequacy

**Source:** Page 13, Section 3.3.1, Yin et al. 2025.
**Caption:** "The evaluation index system of generalized adequacy."

## Type: Diagram

## Visual Description

A hierarchical tree diagram showing the 10-indicator evaluation system organized into three primary dimensions:

### Economic Indicators
- Levelized Cost of Energy (LCOE) -- denoted C_ave

### Technical Indicators (Generalized Adequacy -- 7 indicators)
- **Power and Energy Adequacy:**
  - Loss of Load Hours (LOLH or H_LOL)
  - Expected Energy Not Served (EENS)
  - Conditional Value at Risk of EENS (EENS_CVaR)
- **Flexibility Adequacy:**
  - Flexible Ramp Capacity Margin (I^{mile}_{F,T})
  - Flexible Ramp Rate Margin (I^{rate}_{F,T})
- **Inertia Adequacy:**
  - Minimum Inertia Requirement (H_min)
  - System Inertia Margin (A_H)

### External Indicators
- Renewable Energy Utilization Rate (eta)
- Carbon Emission Intensity (S_carb)

Total: 10 evaluation indicators across 3 dimensions.
