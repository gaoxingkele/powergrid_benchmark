# Figure 7: Long-Term Energy Storage Monthly Charge/Discharge Profile and Load Shedding Ratio

**Source:** Page 20, Section 4.2, Yin et al. 2025.
**Caption:** "Long-term energy storage monthly charge/discharge profile and load shedding ratio."

## Type: Quantitative Plot

## Visual Description

The figure shows long-term energy storage operation across a full year (monthly timescale):

**Upper panel(s): Long-term storage monthly charging/discharging behavior**
- X-axis: Month (January through December).
- Y-axis: Storage energy (MWh) or power (MW).
- Shows the cumulative charge and discharge of long-duration storage across seasons.
- Demonstrates energy shifting from high-renewable months to low-renewable months (e.g., storing excess summer PV for winter use).

**Lower panel(s): Load shedding ratio**
- X-axis: Month.
- Y-axis: Load shedding percentage.
- M1 shows load shedding concentrated in summer months (July--August) when PV declines in evening and winter months.
- M2 shows that long-term storage reduces load shedding across all months by transferring energy across daily and seasonal boundaries.

The figure provides evidence that long-term storage significantly enhances the system's power and energy adequacy at seasonal timescales.
