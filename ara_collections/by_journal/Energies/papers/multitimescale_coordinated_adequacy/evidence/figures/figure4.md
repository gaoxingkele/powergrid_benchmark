# Figure 4: Improved 24-Bus Topology

**Source:** Page 18, Section 4.1, Yin et al. 2025.
**Caption:** "Improved 24-bus topology."

## Type: Diagram

## Visual Description

A single-line diagram of the modified IEEE 24-bus reliability test system showing:

- **24 bus nodes** arranged in a meshed configuration, numbered 1 through 24.
- **Transmission lines** connecting the nodes, with transmission capacity ratings of 500 MW and 800 MW.
- **Thermal power generators** at existing bus locations (unchanged from the original IEEE 24-bus system).
- **Wind farm locations:** Nodes 3 and 5 (250 MW each, expandable to 750 MW max).
- **PV station locations:** Nodes 7 and 13 (250 MW each, expandable to 750 MW max).
- **Short-term energy storage candidate nodes:** 1, 7, 23 (duration <= 4 h).
- **Long-term energy storage candidate nodes:** 13, 18, 20 (duration > 4 h).

The topology follows the standard IEEE 24-bus RTS with generation, transformation, and load buses connected by a 230 kV and 138 kV transmission network.
