## Type: Quantitative Plot

## Visual Description

A dual-panel or overlaid time-series plot showing:

**Scenario M2 (no frequency security constraints):**
- Operational system inertia H_sys(t) plotted over time (hours of the year).
- Minimum inertia requirement H_min shown as a threshold line.
- H_sys frequently falls below H_min, indicating inertia inadequacy.
- This occurs because thermal units are backed down when renewables serve most load.

**Scenario M3 (with dynamic frequency security constraints):**
- H_sys(t) remains consistently above H_min throughout the simulation.
- The pre-constraint approach accounts for coordinated response of energy storage and thermal power during frequency events.
- Results in a positive inertia margin (A_H = +2.90% per Table 5).

The figure demonstrates that without frequency constraints (M2), the system inertia margin is negative (-17.31%), and with them (M3), it becomes positive (+2.90%).
