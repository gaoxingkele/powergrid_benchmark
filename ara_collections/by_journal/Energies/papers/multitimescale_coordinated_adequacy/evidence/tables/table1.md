# Table 1: Extreme Scenario Types and Configuration Methods

**Source:** Page 6, Section 2.2.1, Yin et al. 2025.
**Caption:** "Extreme scenario types evaluated and their configuration methods."

## Data

| Extreme Scenario | Scenario Configuration Method | Proportion of Total Scenarios |
|---|---|---|
| Extreme heat with no wind | Randomly select a period during summer in the scenario year, where wind power output significantly decreases to nearly zero, and load demand rapidly increases by approximately 10%. | 0.01 |
| Extreme cold with no sun | Randomly select a period during winter in the scenario year, where photovoltaic output significantly decreases to nearly zero, and load demand rapidly increases by approximately 10%. | (same as above) |
| Severe drought | Randomly select a period during summer in the scenario year, where hydropower output significantly decreases to nearly zero, and load demand rapidly increases by approximately 10%. | (same as above) |

## Notes

- Each extreme scenario has a probability of approximately 1--2% based on historical statistics.
- All scenarios are constructed based on recent three-year historical meteorological and power system data.
- Parameter settings and durations reviewed by experts for physical and engineering validity.
- Scenarios are randomly embedded in sequential Monte Carlo sampling.
- The proportion of total scenarios column shows a single value (0.01) spanning all three rows in the original table, indicating the probability per scenario.
