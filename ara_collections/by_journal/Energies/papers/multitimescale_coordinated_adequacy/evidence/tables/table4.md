## Notes

- Long-term energy storage values show two numbers (e.g., 150/150) indicating planned capacity at different nodes (nodes 13, 18, 20).
- M1 has the largest wind deployment (926.99 MW) but almost no storage, resulting in high load shedding.
- M4 has the highest PV (585.76 MW) and short-term storage (750 MW), supporting both energy shifting and frequency regulation.
- Transmission line construction decreases from 5 (M1) to 2 (M4) due to coordinated distributed resource placement reducing congestion.
