# Table 2: Indicator Symbols and Definitions

**Source:** Page 14, Section 3.3.1, Yin et al. 2025.
**Caption:** "Indicator symbols and definitions."

## Data

| Indicator Symbol | Indicator Definitions | Indicator Symbol | Indicator Definitions |
|---|---|---|---|
| E_LOLE | Loss of load expectation (LOLE) | H_LOL | Loss of load hours (LOLH) |
| EENS | Expected energy not served (EENS) | / | Conditional value at risk (CVaR) |
| E_CVaR_ENS | The CVaR value of EENS | RoCoF | Rate of change of frequency |
| I^{mile}_{F,T} | The flexibility ramping capacity margin | I^{rate}_{F,T} | The flexibility ramping rate margin |
| H_min | The minimum system inertia | A_H | The system inertia margin |
| H_sys | The system equivalent inertia | C_ave | Levelized cost of energy (LCOE) |
| eta | The renewable energy utilization rate | S_carb | The carbon emission intensity |

## Notes

- This table maps indicator symbols to their definitions for the 10-indicator evaluation system.
- The table also includes E_LOLE and RoCoF as supplementary indicators (though E_LOLE is not one of the seven generalized adequacy indicators, it is related).
- The "/" in the right column references the CVaR concept, which is formally denoted as EENS_CVaR.
