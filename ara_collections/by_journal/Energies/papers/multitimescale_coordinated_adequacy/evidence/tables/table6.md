## Notes

- Using the 95% demand percentile (excluding top 5% of extreme hourly peaks) reduces shortage-related indicator values compared to Table 5 (max-based).
- The relative ranking of schemes remains unchanged: M4 > M3 > M2 > M1.
- This confirms that the superiority of M4 is a structural property, not an artifact of extreme-peak weighting.
- Inertia margins are generally more negative for M1/M2 and more positive for M3/M4 compared to Table 5, showing sensitivity to demand quantification. However, ranking consistency validates robustness.
