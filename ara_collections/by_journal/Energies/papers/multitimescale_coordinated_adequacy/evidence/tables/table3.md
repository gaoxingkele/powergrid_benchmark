## Notes

- The symbol "√" indicates the resource type or constraint is included in the scenario.
- M1: Traditional power grid coordinated planning method (renewables + short-term storage + transmission).
- M2: Adds long-term energy storage for cross-day timescale energy shifting.
- M3: Adds dynamic frequency security constraints (RoCoF and frequency nadir).
- M4: Full generalized adequacy planning with iterative extreme-scenario embedding.
