# Table 8: PROMETHEE-II Ranking Results

**Source:** Page 24, Section 4.4, Yin et al. 2025.
**Caption:** "PROMETHEE-II Ranking Results."

## Data

| Scenario | Net Flow | Rank |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | -2.26 | 4 |
| M2 | -0.68 | 3 |
| M3 | 0.94 | 2 |
| M4 | 2.00 | 1 |

## Notes

- Net flow phi(a) = phi^+(a) - phi^-(a), where phi^+(a) is outflow and phi^-(a) is inflow.
- Positive net flow indicates the scheme is generally preferred over others.
- M4 achieves the highest net flow (+2.00), confirming it as the optimal scheme under the combined AHP-entropy weighting and PROMETHEE-II framework.
- The ranking is monotonic with scenario comprehensiveness: M4 (full generalized adequacy) > M3 (frequency constraints) > M2 (long-term storage) > M1 (traditional baseline).
- This ranking is consistent regardless of whether adequacy indicators are computed at maximum demand or 95th percentile demand.
