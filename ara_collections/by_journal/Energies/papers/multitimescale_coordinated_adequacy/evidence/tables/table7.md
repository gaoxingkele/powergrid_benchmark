# Table 7: Evaluation Results and Indicator Weights of Planning Schemes Under Four Scenarios

**Source:** Page 24, Section 4.4, Yin et al. 2025.
**Caption:** "Evaluation results and indicator weights of planning schemes under four scenarios."

## Data

| Indicator | M1 | M2 | M3 | M4 | Combination Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Levelized Cost of Energy ($/kWh) | 1.92 | 1.69 | 1.61 | 1.30 | 0.22 |
| Expected Loss of Load Hours (h) | 6.15 | 4.68 | 3.54 | 2.91 | 0.16 |
| Expected Energy Not Supplied (MWh) | 1175.31 | 940.78 | 602.28 | 291.48 | 0.13 |
| Conditional Value at Risk of Expected Energy Not Supplied (MWh) | 7987.50 | 6783.61 | 1512.18 | 1316.64 | 0.09 |
| Flexible Ramp Capacity Margin (MW) | -356.34 | -432.78 | -600.62 | -441.81 | 0.04 |
| Flexible Ramp Rate Margin (MW/h) | 9.21 | -50.23 | -285.17 | -126.36 | 0.04 |
| System Inertia Margin (%) | -19.25 | -17.31 | 2.90 | 10.26 | 0.08 |
| Minimum Inertia Requirement (MW-s) | 1.46 x 10^4 | 1.45 x 10^4 | 1.52 x 10^4 | 1.54 x 10^4 | 0.06 |
| Renewable Energy Utilization Rate (%) | 95.10 | 94.81 | 96.13 | 96.00 | 0.03 |
| Carbon Emission Intensity (t/MWh) | 1.48 x 10^-2 | 1.46 x 10^-2 | 1.21 x 10^-2 | 1.30 x 10^-2 | 0.15 |

## Notes

- LCOE has the highest combination weight (0.22), followed by carbon emission intensity (0.15), LOLH (0.16), and EENS (0.13).
- The combination weight is derived from game-theoretic integration of AHP (subjective) and entropy weight (objective) methods.
- Note that M3's LOLH value here is 3.54 h, while in Table 5 it is 3.14 h -- this discrepancy suggests the paper uses different calculation versions for the comparison section.
- M4 achieves the lowest LCOE ($1.30/kWh), lowest LOLH (2.91 h), and lowest EENS (291.48 MWh), while attaining the highest inertia margin (10.26%).
- Carbon emission intensity slightly increases from M3 to M4 (1.21 x 10^-2 vs. 1.30 x 10^-2), possibly due to larger renewable capacity requiring more thermal backup.
