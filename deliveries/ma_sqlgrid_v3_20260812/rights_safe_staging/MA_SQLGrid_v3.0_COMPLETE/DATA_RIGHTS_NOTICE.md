# Data and Rights Notice

This email package contains author-controlled code, the manuscript, figures,
derived numerical evidence, and redistribution-safe metadata. It does not
include third-party BIRD databases or raw source records. It also excludes the
raw GridDB SQLite file and question corpus because the existing rights inventory
requires explicit author or licence-owner clearance before redistribution.

The package includes the GridDB data card, canonical derived rows, aggregated
results, constructed-state scores, and historical-pool ledgers needed to inspect
the reported analyses. When licence conditions permit, restricted source data
may be made available to editors and reviewers through the corresponding author.
Reviewer access does not itself authorize public redistribution.

No credentials, `.env` file, model weights, accident-run directories, obsolete
manuscripts, or third-party article PDFs are included.
