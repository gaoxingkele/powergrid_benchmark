## Draft letter body

Dear Editors of *Information*,

Please consider our manuscript, “MA-SQLGrid: An Auditable Coordination and
Evaluation Framework for Power-Grid Text-to-SQL,” by Bijing Liu, Chenglong Sun,
and Yong Yang.

The study examines an information-processing problem: why reference-free
validation can leave a correct database query available but unselected.
It specifies a traceable candidate-selection interface and separates failure
at candidate production, eligibility, scoring, and tie resolution. The
contribution is a bounded diagnostic framework rather than a claim that adding
agent roles improves accuracy.

In the retained 180-question synthetic GridDB case study, the post-result
partition identifies 42 questions without a correct candidate, no complete
losses of correct candidates at eligibility, six scoring failures, and 33 or
32 tie-order losses. The two selectors match 99 and 100 references, below
the strongest fixed source at 129. These unfavorable comparisons are retained
because they distinguish insufficient candidate production from insufficient
decision information. Separate BIRD generation experiments provide non-grid
context but are not presented as replication of the core selection diagnosis.

The manuscript addresses *Information* readers interested in auditable
information systems, natural-language database access, and evaluation of
AI-assisted decisions. Its practical lesson is to locate the decision stage
that loses correct alternatives before adding further validation complexity.
The manuscript states the limitations of the development-visible synthetic
case study and does not claim independently validated power-grid semantics.

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,

Yong Yang, corresponding author
