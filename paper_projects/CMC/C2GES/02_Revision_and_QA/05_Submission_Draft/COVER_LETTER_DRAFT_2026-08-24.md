# Cover Letter — C2GES

Status: **submission-ready draft**. The corresponding author must read the final PDF and confirm the declarations in the journal portal before upload.

Dear Editors of *Applied Sciences*,

Please consider our manuscript, “C²GES: Structure-Aware Extractive Summarization of Long Power-System Technical Reports with Typed-Path Graphs,” by Bijing Liu and Yong Yang.

The manuscript presents a deterministic, source-linked framework for navigating long power-system technical reports. It combines lexical relevance, text-role signals, a typed sentence graph, a path-deletion diagnostic, and page-level traceability. Seven conditions are evaluated at two extraction budgets on 12 development and 15 test reports selected from the North American Electric Reliability Corporation corpus. The results are reported conservatively: the path-deletion term changes scores and selections but does not improve the predefined ROUGE-L endpoint, and the matched-word post-run analysis does not establish a length-controlled advantage. The contribution is therefore an auditable implementation, a portable rights-safe verification subset, and a transparent negative component diagnosis rather than an unsupported claim of operational benefit.

The work fits *Applied Sciences* as an applied artificial-intelligence and power-systems study focused on traceability, reproducibility, and the limits of current evidence. The manuscript distinguishes automated textual-proxy diagnostics from physical causality and from qualified power-grid expert validation.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and has been approved by all authors. The authors declare no conflicts of interest. Funding, author contributions, data availability, and generative-AI assistance are disclosed in the manuscript. Source PDFs and verbatim derived text subject to third-party restrictions are excluded from the public package; the rights-safe manuscript-bound package is identified by the frozen repository tag stated in the Data Availability Statement.
