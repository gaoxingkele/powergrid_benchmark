## Why the route changed

The former gate required three facts that local automation cannot create:

1. a genuinely unseen, rights-cleared external-series corpus;
2. two independent human annotators, including a qualified power-system or
   reliability-event expert;
3. an institutional approval or exemption decision before human recruitment.

The available external series were accessed before freezing, and the available
labels were generated by DeepSeek and Codex. They are scientifically useful as
post-access exploratory evidence and automated error discovery, respectively,
but cannot satisfy the confirmatory E1/E2 conditions.

The manuscript was therefore converted to the diagnostic route already allowed
by the revision plan. Its title no longer says `Structure-Aware` or
`Typed-Path Graphs`; its abstract, research questions, contribution statement,
discussion, conclusion, ethics statement, and data-availability statement now
use the bounded claim set.
