# E2 Annotator Information and Consent Record

Status: `DRAFT / INSTITUTION MUST APPROVE OR WAIVE`

## What participation involves

Each annotator receives a blinded packet containing source-context windows and
candidate units identified only by study codes. Tasks cover unit validity,
discourse role, typed-edge support and direction, typed-path coherence, source
faithfulness, and critical omission. Annotators do not see system names, scores,
hypotheses, or the other annotator's labels during independent annotation.

## Foreseeable risks and safeguards

The work is non-clinical but may involve fatigue, professional-identification risk,
and restricted third-party report text. Records use coded identifiers; the public
release excludes names, contact details, timestamps that identify individuals,
restricted passages, and raw private context packets. Annotators may pause and may
withdraw under the institution-approved procedure without penalty.

## Data recorded

- coded annotator identifier and qualification basis;
- training completion and calibration performance;
- task labels, confidence or cannot-judge choices, and permitted comments;
- independent completion timestamps and file hashes;
- adjudication changes recorded separately after agreement is frozen.
