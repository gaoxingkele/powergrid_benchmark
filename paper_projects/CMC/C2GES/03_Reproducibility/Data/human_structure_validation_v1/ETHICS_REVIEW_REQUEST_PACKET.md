# Institutional ethics review / exemption request packet

Status: draft for submission to the responsible institution; not an approval

## Project summary

The study asks two adult technical annotators to evaluate extraction-unit validity, discourse-role labels, typed-edge support and direction, typed-path coherence, and source faithfulness in power-system incident reports. No clinical intervention is involved. The research records professional judgments, agreement, and error categories; therefore the responsible institution must decide whether this constitutes human-subject research and whether approval, exemption, or another determination applies.

## Materials to submit

1. Current protocol title and version.
2. Principal investigator and responsible institution.
3. Intended annotator population and inclusion/exclusion criteria.
4. Recruitment text and compensation, if any.
5. Annotation guide and blank form.
6. Exact report excerpts or context shown to annotators and their access restrictions.
7. Data collected about annotators, including qualifications and timestamps.
8. Privacy, pseudonymization, retention, access-control, and deletion plan.
9. Risks: professional-identification risk, confidentiality, workload/fatigue, and exposure to restricted third-party text.
10. Consent or information sheet and withdrawal process.
11. Publication and data-sharing plan, including the commitment not to publish identifiable annotator information or restricted report text.
12. Statement that LLM outputs are training or stress fixtures only and do not replace human labels.

## Prepared repository attachments

- `ANNOTATION_PROTOCOL.md` and `annotation_schema.json`: task definitions and machine-readable labels;
- `annotation_form_blank.csv`: blank scientific response form;
- `E2_RECRUITMENT_TEXT.md`: recruitment language with investigator fields still to complete;
- `E2_ANNOTATOR_INFORMATION_AND_CONSENT.md`: information/consent draft requiring institutional approval or waiver;
- `E2_DATA_MANAGEMENT_PLAN.md`: data classes, access separation, integrity, retention, and deletion controls;
- `ANNOTATOR_RECRUITMENT_AND_INDEPENDENCE_SPEC.md` in the prospective external directory: staffing and separation-of-duty requirements;
- `ETHICS_OR_EXEMPTION_RECORD.md`: the final decision record to be completed only from the institution's signed determination.

The remaining blanks require the responsible investigator's identity, institution,
workload/compensation decision, approved storage location, retention policy, and
the institution's formal review outcome. They must not be inferred by software.
