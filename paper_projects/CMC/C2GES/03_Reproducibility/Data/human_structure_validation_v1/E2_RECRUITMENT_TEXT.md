# E2 Annotator Recruitment Text

Status: `DRAFT / USE ONLY AFTER INSTITUTIONAL AUTHORIZATION`

## Invitation

You are invited to assist with a research study evaluating an extractive
summarization method for power-system technical incident reports. The task asks
you to judge whether extracted units are well formed, whether proposed discourse
roles and relations are supported by the supplied source context, whether typed
paths are coherent, and whether selected passages remain faithful to the source
or omit critical information.

Participation is voluntary. The expected workload, compensation (if any), data
access restrictions, retention period, withdrawal procedure, and contact details
must be inserted below before this invitation is issued. Do not participate if
you helped generate the system predictions or would otherwise be unable to make
an independent blinded judgment.

## Eligibility

- Annotator A must have documented experience in power-system operation,
  protection, reliability-event analysis, or technical incident investigation.
- Annotator B must be an independent technical-document researcher or a second
  domain expert who completes the same training.
- Both annotators must be adults and able to comply with the report-access and
  confidentiality conditions specified by the institution.
