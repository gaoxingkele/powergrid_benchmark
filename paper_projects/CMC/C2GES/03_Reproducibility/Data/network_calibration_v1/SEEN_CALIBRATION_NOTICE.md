# Network calibration evidence boundary

Access date: 2026-09-10

The reports in `CALIBRATION_SOURCE_MANIFEST.csv` were located on official AEMO and ENTSO-E websites and were not found in the pre-existing C2GES report registry before access. They become **seen calibration material** as soon as they are downloaded or inspected.

Rules:

- Every listed series is permanently ineligible for the prospective E1 confirmatory external test.
- Source PDFs remain in a private local cache and are not included in a public release until the corresponding redistribution terms have been reviewed by a human rights holder/author representative.
- Only non-verbatim structural and distribution statistics may enter the public calibration profile.
- No report text, title, organization name, manuscript text, or author information may be sent to an LLM API.
- LLM-generated records are fictional stress-test fixtures. They cannot support claims about real-world system performance, human construct validity, or operational benefit.
- LLM output cannot replace either of the two independent human annotators.
- Publicly accessible reports and synthetic data do not determine whether the planned human annotation requires institutional ethics approval or exemption. That decision must be documented by the responsible institution.

This directory records research preparation, not completion of E1, E2, or an ethics gate.
