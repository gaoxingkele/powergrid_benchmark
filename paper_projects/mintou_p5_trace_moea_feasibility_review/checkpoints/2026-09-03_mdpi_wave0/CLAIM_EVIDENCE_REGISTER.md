**Investment Effectiveness Optimization Strategy based on Hybrid Multi-objective Evolution**.

Legacy/read-only results retain their original scope; the canonical `p5_s4`
namespace contains `NO_RESULTS` at this stage.

## Writing gate

Title, abstract, contribution list, method, experimental setup, results,
discussion, limitations, and conclusion must use the same status. A claim can
be upgraded only after its configuration, raw outputs, aggregation code,
statistical result, and manuscript location are registered. A combined ablation
supports only a joint conclusion; proxy, sensitivity, historical, and
event-summary studies keep the qualifiers stated above.
