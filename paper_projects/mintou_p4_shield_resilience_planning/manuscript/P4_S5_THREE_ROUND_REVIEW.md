# P4 Stage-5 Three-Round Scientific Closure

Scope: logic, methodology--statistics, and theory--innovation review of the current p4 manuscript, equations, executable code paths, preserved run-level evidence, sensitivity/boundary archives, companion disclosure, and cited figures. No optimizer, sensitivity, AC, or human-utility experiment was run in this stage.

## Round 1: Logic and Claim Alignment

Verdict: closed after revision.

- The title, abstract, contribution list, discussion, and conclusion now identify the study as proxy-based resilience planning. They do not convert reliability/survivability indices or fixed-mapping AC cases into physical resilience, recovery, deployment, or human-utility evidence.
- The eight archive labels remain five active proxy configurations; four reference-configuration blocks are independent optimizer samples, not four uncertainty regimes. The inactive DER multiplier remains disclosed in all held-out and sensitivity interpretations.
- The framework result remains separated from component attribution. Repair is resolved within SHIELD; scenario screening, periodic re-screening, hybrid GA/DE, and survivability in environmental selection are not promoted beyond their null or joint-ablation evidence.
- Search/evaluation separation is described as a protocol property that prevents direct scenario reuse. No causal quality benefit is assigned to disjoint evaluation because no same-draw control exists.

## Round 2: Methodology and Statistics

Verdict: closed with the declared exploratory and descriptive boundaries retained.

- The primary stochastic analysis unit is one independently seeded optimizer invocation within method and archive label. Method-hashed seeds make the groups unpaired. Pooled archive means remain descriptive; inference remains within label with Holm correction.
- Deterministic-rule duplicate rows retain effective sample size one. The one-at-a-time sensitivity sweep remains exploratory at ten seeds per point with nominal, multiplicity-unadjusted p-values; the unresolved population-60 result is retained.
- Boundary intervals remain pointwise and multiplicity-unadjusted within seven predeclared settings. Hypervolume is compared only within setting because changed formulations receive setting-specific bounds. Clipping frequency, infeasible-singleton cost-scale distortion, and the adverse tight-budget GA-only contrast remain visible.
- The 17,920-versus-51,200 workload contrast is now defined explicitly as optimization-loop plan--scenario row arithmetic. Reference-bound construction and held-out final-front evaluation are excluded, so the 65% value is not a full-pipeline or instrumented call reduction. The null quality contrast and higher archived runtime remain coupled to this result.
- The 1296 AC rows remain fixed mapped cases based on seed-0 action-kind counts. They carry no optimizer-level p-values, nodal-plan identities, independent optimizer replications, or causal interpretation.

## Round 3: Theory and Innovation

Verdict: closed with novelty narrowed to the evidenced question.

- The independent contribution is the optimizer--uncertainty interface: population-dependent worst-$K$ exposure, search/scoring draw separation, and direct no-screen/fixed-worst-$K$ controls. Standard NSGA-II selection, hybrid variation, and repair are not claimed as individually novel.
- Dynamic re-screening is unresolved against a generation-1 fixed subset, and hybrid variation is unresolved against DE-only. The paper therefore presents them as implemented defaults rather than necessary innovations.
- Companion overlap is explicit. P4 and `mintou_p3_samode_distribution_planning` share the planning runner, source extraction, 72-action builder, binary/budget structures, evaluation/statistics utilities, seeded archive machinery, and AC mapping code. The companion's archived AC panel covers four SimBench networks; p4's separate evidence extends the mapping to CIGRE MV and IEEE 33-bus. Neither paper's rows support the other's algorithmic conclusions.
- Operational resilience, post-event recovery, utility preferences, and human decision utility remain outside scope because they were not directly tested.
