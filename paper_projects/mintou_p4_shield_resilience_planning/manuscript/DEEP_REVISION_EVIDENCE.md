## Primary Estimand and Analysis Unit

The primary performance outcome is standard five-dimensional hypervolume of the budget-feasible, raw-objective non-dominated final front under fixed, method-independent evaluation bounds and a reference point of 1.1 in each clipped normalized dimension. For stochastic comparisons, the statistical analysis unit is one independently seeded optimizer invocation within an archive label and method. Each stochastic method has 30 invocations in each of eight labels. The scored object for run $(m,e,r)$ is its final population re-evaluated on the common 16-scenario evaluation draw, reduced to a raw-objective front, and then normalized/clipped for hypervolume. The sampled worst-envelope score reuses the mean-front plans rather than building a separate worst-objective front.

The primary inferential estimand is the within-label difference in the distribution of held-out hypervolume between SHIELD-MOEA and a stochastic opponent. Two-sided Mann--Whitney U tests are Holm-corrected within each label. The actual optimizer seed hashes include the method name, so these are unpaired samples; the archive's `seed` field stores only indices 0--29. The 5.09% pooled mean margin over NSGA-II with post-hoc final-population repair and 5.56% pooled margin over plain NSGA-II aggregate $8\times30$ records per stochastic method and are descriptive summaries over archived blocks, not a pooled hypothesis test. Four labels share the reference p4 objective configuration, so the pooled result is not an equal-weight summary of eight distinct regimes. The corrected inference table records 32/32 Holm-significant wins against four stochastic baselines. Weighted Sum and Deterministic Planning each have one effective result per label; their 16 gaps are descriptive.

The stage-3 supplementary estimand is the within-setting difference in mean held-out HV between SHIELD-MOEA and NSGA-II+Repair, with separate gaps to GA-only, DE-only, and fixed-worst-$K$. Its analysis unit is one new independently seeded invocation within a setting and method ($n=30$). Seven one-at-a-time settings vary budget factor, scenario count with $K/|\mathcal S|=1/4$, or the action-gain scale in $S$. Pointwise 5000-resample percentile intervals are multiplicity-unadjusted. Because the bounds are recomputed once for each changed formulation, absolute HV levels are not compared across settings.

The screening-specific estimands are narrower:

1. the within-label held-out-HV contrast between SHIELD-MOEA and `Ablation-NoScenarioScreen`;
2. the deterministic difference in static optimization-loop plan--scenario objective-row arithmetic under the declared implementation schedule, excluding reference-bound construction and held-out evaluation; and
3. the within-label held-out-HV contrast between periodic re-screening and `Control-FixedWorstK`.

These estimands do not establish a screening quality gain: the first and third contrasts are unresolved in all eight archive labels. They isolate SHIELD-MOEA's independent scientific question as whether selective scenario exposure, separated from final scoring, changes held-out front quality and static evaluation-row workload, and whether repeated population-dependent updates add anything beyond a one-time worst-$K$ subset.

The AC analysis unit is different. It is one method/reference composition from one selected planning experiment, mapped to one network and one operating scenario. Twelve planning/reference configurations, three experiments, six networks, and six scenarios yield 1296 cases. The exported composition is the seed-0 compromise solution, and the mapping retains only counts of reinforcement, storage, DER, and automation actions. AC feasibility requires convergence, all bus voltages in $[0.95,1.05]$ pu, and all line loadings at or below 100%. These cases are descriptive repeated checks under common mappings and scenarios; they are not independent optimizer seeds and carry no p-values.

## Comparison Budget and Data Visibility

The main archive contains 2640 records: 11 methods $\times$ 8 labels $\times$ 30 stored invocations. Inference treats the stochastic methods as $n=30$ per label and the two deterministic rules as $n=1$ per label despite their repeated provenance rows. The targeted mechanism archive adds 720 runs for GA-only, DE-only, and fixed-worst-$K$ controls over the same eight labels and 30 seed indices. The one-at-a-time sensitivity sweep uses 10 seeds per point and nominal, multiplicity-unadjusted p-values, so it is exploratory. The new boundary archive adds 1050 complete rows: 7 settings $\times$ 5 methods $\times$ 30 independent method-specific optimizer seeds. It uses new search/evaluation scenario seeds 2026081301 and 2026081302 and is not pooled with the main archive.

All methods receive the same fixed search scenario draw (NumPy seed 20260713) within a label. Full-scenario baselines use all 16 search scenarios; SHIELD-MOEA selects an active worst-$K$ subset. All methods are scored on the same 16-scenario evaluation draw generated with seed 20260714. The unseen-stress label changes held-out $\lambda$, $\delta$, and $\sigma$ ranges, but only $\lambda$ and $\sigma$ enter p4 scores. Evaluation scenarios are never passed into search. Normalization bounds come from the empty plan, every single-action plan, and 2048 seeded budget-feasible random plans; method outputs do not set the scale. Those reference-plan evaluations and the variable-size held-out front evaluations are outside the 17,920-versus-51,200 optimization-loop workload denominator. Raw objectives determine non-dominance, hypervolume alone clips normalized values to $[0,1]$, and the compromise equal-sum rule uses unclipped affine values.

The boundary runner freezes the 18 source subnets sufficient to reconstruct all 72 candidates and verifies the three raw SimBench input hashes before snapshotting. Within each setting it computes one reference-set bounds vector before running any method and records the same bounds digest on every run. It scores the same raw front with primary clipped HV at $1.1^5$, an unclipped audit at $1.1^5$, and clipped HV at $1.2^5$. The local NSGA-II+Repair runner preserves the declared sparse sampling, two-point crossover, bit-flip mutation, constraint handling, and post-hoc repair contract under installed pymoo 0.4.1; this environment difference is why these rows supplement rather than replace the main archive.

The AC layer sees only exported seed-index-0 compromise action-kind counts for `deterministic_vs_scenario`, `der_uncertainty`, and `outage_contingency`; it does not retain the selected candidate identities. The middle label is not a DER-aware planning contrast. Fixed method-independent rules reconstruct placements on four SimBench MV networks, CIGRE MV, and IEEE 33-bus. Reinforcement parallels the most-loaded lines, storage is assigned to weak-voltage load buses at 3% net load, DER is assigned to high-load buses at 4%, and automation has no steady-state effect. No optimizer receives AC results, and no per-network tuning is performed.

## Negative and Null Results

- Removing scenario screening is nominally 0.48% lower in pooled mean HV than SHIELD-MOEA, but no significant difference is detected in any of eight labels. This is a null difference test, not evidence of equivalence.
- Periodic re-screening is statistically inseparable from a worst-$K$ subset fixed after generation 1 in all eight labels. Dynamic adaptation is not established as necessary.
- Hybrid GA/DE variation is statistically inseparable from DE-only in all eight labels. The hybrid is a motivated default, not a resolved source of the headline gain.
- Removing survivability from environmental selection produces pooled mean HV 0.27467 versus 0.27396 for the full method, a nominal 0.26% advantage, with no significant difference in any label. The ablation retains survivability in worst-$K$ screening, so it does not support a claim about fully hiding resilience information during search. The coordinate is retained for decision support, not as a detected performance mechanism.
- The 65% screening quantity is static optimization-loop code-path arithmetic, not an instrumented counter or a full-pipeline budget. Reference-bound construction and held-out evaluation are excluded. It is paired with no detected held-out-HV difference and does not produce a wall-clock advantage on the current closed-form proxy: mean runtime is 0.0889 s for SHIELD-MOEA and 0.0792 s without screening. This quality--workload asymmetry is a result, not a limitation to hide.
- At population 60, the exploratory sensitivity comparison with NSGA-II is unresolved ($p=0.104$). Sensitivity p-values are nominal and uncorrected.
- SHIELD-MOEA does not lead the AC composition check: aggregate feasibility is 0.685 versus 0.694 for NSGA-II+Repair. On IEEE 33-bus both reach 0.333, showing limited transfer of the fixed composition mapping.
- Removing outage exposure during search yields 0.574 mapped AC feasibility versus 0.685 for SHIELD-MOEA and higher mean maximum loading (67.0% versus 52.7%). This is an illustrative association under one fixed export/mapping protocol, not a seeded causal estimate or validation of the original nodal plans.
- Sampled worst-envelope HV uses the worst values among 16 evaluation scenarios. It is a finite-sample diagnostic, not a bound on unobserved tail behavior.
- The configured DER multiplier $\delta$ is absent from every p4 optimization/scoring objective. `der_uncertainty` is therefore an inactive-factor replicate, the DER component of the unseen-range label is inactive, and no planning-stage DER-output robustness conclusion is supported.
- The eight labels comprise five active objective configurations, with the reference configuration repeated four times under independent optimizer seed streams. Pooled margins and win counts describe that archive rather than eight distinct uncertainty regimes.
- NSGA-II+Repair applies the same candidate-drop rule only after constrained NSGA-II returns its population; SHIELD repairs initialization and every offspring batch. The 5.09% comparison is not timing-matched.
- The configured MOEA/D uses 35 Das--Dennis reference directions, not the nominal population size 40 in the top-level JSON. Its poor result applies to that penalty-based 35-member configuration.
- In the boundary archive, 441/1050 fronts require low-side clipping (960 objective components). Clipping preserves all 28 mean-gap directions across opponents and settings but materially changes gap magnitudes, especially for the tight budget and the 0.75 survivability-action scale.
- The boundary reference set includes every singleton without feasibility filtering. Its cost bounds are approximately -32,195 to 676,102 while tested budgets are 754.4--1104, compressing feasible cost differences; method-independent scaling is not engineering-calibrated scaling.
- DE-only has a higher primary mean than the full hybrid in all seven boundary settings, but every pointwise interval spans zero. Fixed worst-$K$ is unresolved in all seven. Under the tight budget, full-minus-GA-only is -0.00326 with a pointwise 95% interval [-0.00582, -0.00061]. The 21 mechanism intervals are multiplicity-unadjusted, so this isolated adverse result is not a confirmatory GA-only superiority claim.

## Shared Assets and Independent Contribution

The named companion is `mintou_p3_samode_distribution_planning`, identified in the current p4 manuscript by its method/manuscript name CARS-MODE. The shared-infrastructure disclosure must be symmetric in both projects and must not be reduced to "candidate generation only." Source inspection shows that p3 and p4 use:

- the same `mintou_real_planning.py` runner, SimBench subnet extraction, and 72-action candidate builder;
- the same binary-plan data structures, budget-feasibility representation, fixed-bound hypervolume helpers, seeded run/archive machinery, and statistical table generation, with paper-specific branches;
- four common proxy coordinates (cost, loss, voltage risk, and reliability), while p3 uses hosting capacity as its fifth objective and p4 uses survivability; and
- the same `mintou_pandapower_validation.py` infrastructure and fixed composition-to-network mapping, applied to separately exported paper-specific compositions and experiment selections. The companion's archived evidence covers four SimBench networks and six operating cases, whereas p4's separate extension covers those four networks plus CIGRE MV and IEEE 33-bus, again with six cases.

This is shared research and evaluation infrastructure, not shared evidence for the two algorithms. The reciprocal disclosure should state the same boundary in `mintou_p3_samode_distribution_planning` and this project: common code, public source data, benchmark construction, evaluation utilities, and AC mapping are shared; the research questions, method-specific search mechanisms, configurations, run archives, comparisons, and conclusions are paper-specific.

No shared p3/p4 planning source was changed for stage 3. The local p4-only boundary runner and its frozen profile live inside this isolated worktree, so p3 claims and p3 archives were not modified.

SHIELD-MOEA's independent contribution is the scenario-exposure question: worst-$K$ search-phase screening, separation of search and scoring draws, and the direct test of periodic re-screening against no-screening and fixed-worst-$K$ controls in a load/DER/outage proxy setting. The companion's operator/strategy self-adaptation question cannot be used as evidence for screening, and SHIELD-MOEA's results cannot be used as evidence for the companion. The shared AC validator likewise supplies separate composition checks, not a joint algorithm comparison.

Stage `p4_s3_boundary_experiments` created run `p4_s3_boundary_20260813`. All 1050 invocations completed. The runner refuses to overwrite its outputs, and `evidence/manifests/p4_s3_boundary_manifest_20260813.json` verifies eight hashed controlling/generated assets and one bounds digest per setting.

The primary SHIELD-minus-NSGA-II+Repair gap is positive in all seven settings: 0.01013--0.01885 under clipped $1.1^5$, or 3.46--7.20% relative to the comparator; every pointwise 95% interval lies above zero. The sign remains positive without clipping and at clipped $1.2^5$ (2.09--5.06% relative range). These are within-setting exploratory results. They do not remove the comparator's repair-timing difference, do not calibrate costs, and do not turn the seven settings into a pooled estimand.

Broader claims would require new evidence rather than prose changes: independently optimized candidate pools on another network family, nodal siting/sizing with AC power flow in the optimization loop, element-level outage and post-event recovery modeling, and monetary calibration. Until such work exists, the proxy and composition-level qualifiers remain mandatory.
