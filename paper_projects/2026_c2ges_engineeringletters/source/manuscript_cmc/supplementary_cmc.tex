\noindent This supplement documents FEVER conversion details, training hyperparameters, and additional split statistics for the main paper. All quantitative tables use human-gold FEVER evidence labels.

\section{FEVER Conversion}
\label{sec:s1}

We convert the filtered FEVER evidence-selection release into the C\textsuperscript{2}GES sentence-ID schema. For each claim--document instance:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Wikipedia lines become sentences with IDs $s000,s001,\ldots$;
  \item human evidence line IDs become gold evidence sentence IDs;
  \item the claim becomes the query;
  \item SUPPORTS/REFUTES becomes the role label.
\end{itemize}
Instances without recoverable gold evidence lines are discarded. Conversion scripts are in \texttt{source/code/prepare\_fever\_benchmark.py}.
