Support labels are deliberately source-specific:

- `PRIMARY-FULL`: claim-bearing full text inspected at the primary publisher/venue.
- `PRIMARY-ABSTRACT`: only the primary identity/abstract record supports the stated facts.
- `AUTHOR-FULL`: an author-supplied full manuscript supports details, while the primary record supports identity.
- `LOCAL-FULL`: the complete approved local PDF was inspected and its hash recorded.
- `OFFICIAL-REPO`: only release/provenance facts were taken from the work's official repository.
- `UNVERIFIED`: inaccessible or absent claim-bearing content; excluded from conclusions.

For DOI records, `RW0/C0` means no exact DOI match in the 2026-08-26 Crossref/Retraction Watch snapshot and no correction/update relation in the Crossref record inspected 2026-08-28. The snapshot provider, URL, commit, and checksum are recorded in `P1_CITATION_VERIFICATION_V2.md`. This bounded registry result is not a universal guarantee.

## 2023/2026 lineage boundary

The 2023 EHST paper and 2026 Sustainable Energy, Grids and Networks paper form a same-author, same-domain **conference-to-journal lineage**, but they are not treated as interchangeable versions. The conference paper verifies a one-hour-ahead CAISO comparison on 2014–2022 data. The journal work uses 2019–2024 CAISO data and a multi-scale wavelet/Transformer method. The inspected journal content did not expose an exact lead horizon, so that field remains UNVERIFIED. Claims supported by one lineage member are not silently transferred to the other.

## Bounded advance

Within this explicitly verified corpus, direct curtailment papers cover CAISO system-level point prediction, a later multi-scale model, and single-site conformal forecasting. Adjacent work covers learned retrieval, retrieval-augmented prompting, probabilistic analog retrieval, similar-day taxonomies, synthetic benchmarks, and load-forecasting architectures. None of the inspected records was verified to combine all of the present benchmark's specific protocol elements: a public RTS-GMLC-derived retrospective curtailment-risk proxy, explicit 1 h and 24 h lag tasks, a transition/onset slice with pre-test support accounting, checkpoint-matched learned-space retrieval controls, common-seed paired comparisons, multiplicity adjustment, and preserved negative/null findings.

That supports only a **bounded protocol/evidence advance over the explicitly verified corpus**. It does not establish a globally new method, operational deployment, observed dispatch curtailment, exhaustive coverage, state-of-the-art exclusion, or superiority over values reported in other papers. The present study's proxy target and retrospective data visibility remain materially different from CAISO and wind-farm curtailment targets, so no cross-paper numerical ranking is admissible.
