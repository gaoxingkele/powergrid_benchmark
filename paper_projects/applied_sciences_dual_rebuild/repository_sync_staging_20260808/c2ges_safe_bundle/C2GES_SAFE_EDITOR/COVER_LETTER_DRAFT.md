# Cover Letter Draft — Applied Sciences

Dear Editors,

Please consider the manuscript “Causal and Counterfactual Graph-Enhanced
Extractive Summarization (C²GES) for Power Grid Maintenance Reports” for
publication in *Applied Sciences*.

We disclose an important title-to-evidence boundary at submission. “For Power
Grid Maintenance Reports” states the intended downstream application, whereas
the evaluated population is a selected public NERC reliability, disturbance,
event-analysis, recommendation, and assessment technical-report proxy corpus.
The manuscript does not claim validated effectiveness, safety, or operator
usefulness on utility maintenance work orders or inspection records. Because
we have retained the original title for continuity, we ask the handling editor
to decide explicitly whether this intended-use title is acceptable with the
conspicuous population disclaimer in the first sentence of the Abstract.

The contribution is a deterministic, auditable extractive workflow with a
typed textual graph and node-deletion path diagnostic that is mathematically
distinct from weighted degree. The formal ablation is negative: Full C²GES is
below the strict no-counterfactual graph condition by about 0.0033 ROUGE-L at
both sentence budgets, with intervals crossing zero. A completed
post-unblinding development-only search selected zero counterfactual weight in
12/12 leave-one-report-out folds. We report both findings prominently and do
not claim that the counterfactual channel improves lexical overlap.

Full C²GES has higher ROUGE-L than Semantic-MMR and TextRank on the retained
split, but the comparisons use equal sentence counts rather than equal word
budgets. Full averages 103.0/214.5 more words than Semantic-MMR and
110.7/199.9 more words than TextRank at K=5/K=10. We therefore make no claim of
length-controlled superiority. This diagnostic was derived from the immutable
ledger without rerunning selection or changing a prediction.

The safe editor package includes the manuscript, PDF, complete non-verbatim
calibration evidence, tests, code snapshots, rights-safe 40-report inventory,
post-run output-length audit, a 1,575-row non-verbatim selected-ID-to-page
locator, exact sign-flip records, independent audits, incident history, and
clean-unpack figure/build receipts. Source PDFs, full extracted datasets, and
the verbatim 210-row prediction ledger are excluded. The restricted ledger may
be provided only after the authors confirm the applicable third-party
permissions.

The corresponding-author email, exact funder wording and role, final AI-use
provenance, author declarations, repository synchronization/release receipt,
