                                       MA-SQLGrid is an experimental read-only decision-support framework for auditable                                2

                                       Abstract                                                                                                        6


                                       Natural-language access to power-grid databases requires traceable schema grounding,                            7

                                       database-enforced read-only execution, and explicit treatment of missing evidence. This                         8

                                       paper presents MA-SQLGrid, a five-role software architecture coordinated through an                             9

                                       append-only blackboard and deterministic adjudicator. The implemented boundary uses                            10

                                       SQLite read-only mode, query_only, an authorizer, disabled extensions, resource limits,                        11

                                       and fail-closed named-state coverage. Earlier GridDB and BIRD experiments evaluate                             12

                                       frozen prompting workflows, not autonomous multi-agent generation. The only audited                            13

                                       prospective experiment is a separate 700-call component study; it tests presented-value and                    14

                                       reference-free selection components rather than the five-role system. A deterministic no-                      15

                                       generation release-v3 re-execution applies three selectors to a shared 5760-attempt evidence                   16

                                       collection for eight historical candidates on 180 GridDB questions. A supersession record                      17

                                       classifies this release as descriptive because its frozen pre-run tests accessed same-item                     18

                                       gold-derived v2 outcomes. Fixed order, validation-only ranking, and complete three-                            19

                                       witness selection obtained 80/180, 100/180, and 101/180, respectively. The latter two                          20

                                       selectors had top-score ties on 130/180 questions, with mean tie multiplicities 5.4000 and                     21

                                       5.3889; reversing candidate order yielded 117–118/180. Their sole difference was Q039,                         22

                                       where a constructed nullable-column witness rejected SELECT * and retained an explicit                         23

                                       projection. This 1/180 trace is semantically ambiguous and is not evidence of a general                        24

                                       counterfactual, robustness, coordination, or multi-agent gain. The evidence supports a                         25

                                       reproducible architecture with tested safety and bounded-evidence mechanisms, but not                          26

                                       end-to-end agent superiority, qualified power-grid semantic validity, deployment safety, or                    27

                                       universal robustness.                                                                                          28

                                     1. Introduction                                                                                    31


                                           Power-grid organizations maintain relational data about assets, locations, topology,         32

                                     sensor readings, work orders, technicians, and maintenance activity. SQL provides precise          33

                                     access to these records, but it requires knowledge of table names, join keys, identifiers,         34

                                     units, aggregation conventions, and local coding rules. Text-to-SQL systems aim to reduce          35

                                     this access barrier by translating a natural-language request into an executable query. In an      36

                                     engineering workflow, however, a fluent query is not sufficient evidence of correctness. A         37

                                     query can execute while selecting the wrong status field, omitting a required filter, using        38

                                     an unintended join path, or returning the expected number of columns with the wrong                39

                                     content.                                                                                           40

                                           Cross-database benchmarks such as Spider and BIRD have made schema generaliza-               41

                                     tion and realistic database content central evaluation concerns [1,2]. Schema-aware models,        42

                                     constrained decoding, context selection, decomposition, and execution guidance address             43

                                     different failure surfaces [3–7]. These methods also expose a design problem: generation           44

                                     steps are frequently bundled together. A system may change schema serialization, value             45

                                     presentation, domain normalization, structural hints, candidate count, and repair policy           46

                                     simultaneously. An observed score difference then cannot be assigned cleanly to schema             47

                                     linking, reasoning, or validation.                                                                 48

                                           The original MA-SQLGrid concept addresses this problem through five specialized              49

                                     roles. A Query Analyst interprets the request, a Schema Cartographer maps it to the                50

                                     database, an SQL Synthesizer packages candidate queries, a Validation Engine checks safety         51

                                     and execution, and a Counterfactual Critic records evidence from named database states. A          52

                                     separate deterministic adjudicator resolves the resulting evidence. This separation makes          53

                                     the trace inspectable: a reviewer can distinguish what the analyst inferred, which schema          54

                                     elements were retained, which candidates were proposed, which candidates executed, and             55

                                     why a final candidate was selected or rejected.                                                    56

                                           The architecture must nevertheless be separated from the evidence used to evaluate           57

                                     it. The inherited GridDB factorial study was not a multi-agent execution. It used one              58

                                     model generation per question and prompt cell, followed by deterministic parsing, read-            59

                                     only validation, execution, and offline scoring. The BIRD study likewise compared four             60

                                     frozen prompting procedures rather than five interacting agents. These assets can motivate         61

                                     modules, supply baselines, and test replay interfaces, but they cannot be renamed as results       62

                                     of the new coordination core.                                                                      63

                                           This distinction leads to three questions. First, can role-specific handoffs be imple-       64

                                     mented with a database-enforced, gold-isolated, fail-closed boundary? Second, what do the          65

                                     completed experiments establish about context packages, presented values, public-database          66

                                     transfer, and constructed-state agreement? Third, when generation is held fixed at eight           67

                                     already frozen candidates per question, how do fixed-order, validation-only, and complete          68

                                     metamorphic selectors behave under the same evidence-collection operations? The third              69

                                     question estimates historical-pool offline selection only; schema grounding is retained as         70

                                     trace evidence and no five-role end-to-end, new-generation, dialogue, or deployment effect         71

                                     is estimated.                                                                                      72

                                           The paper makes four contributions. First, it defines a testable five-role coordination      73

                                     core, a database-enforced SQLite executor, and a deterministic adjudicator whose required          74

                                     counterfactual coverage fails closed. Second, it conservatively integrates a 1440-prediction       75

                                     GridDB factorial experiment, an audited prospective 700-call component study, a 25,920-            76

                                     row multi-state ledger, and a 5000-call BIRD comparison while retaining their original             77

                                     identities and evidence classes. Third, it reports a deterministic descriptive re-execution        78

                                     over the existing eight-slot pool using three query-blind metamorphic witnesses with dis-          79

                                     tinct database hashes; all methods choose only after the same shared 32-attempt evidence           80

                                     2. Related Work                                                                                     93


                                     2.1. Text-to-SQL Evaluation and Schema Generalization                                               94


                                          Text-to-SQL systems map a natural-language question and a database schema to                   95

                                     a query. Spider established a cross-domain setting with previously unseen databases,                96

                                     drawing attention to table selection, joins, grouping, nesting, and ordering [1]. BIRD              97

                                     extends public evaluation toward larger and more content-rich databases [2]. A benchmark            98

                                     result depends on the exact split, evaluator, database engine, execution boundary, and              99

                                     model configuration. We therefore report only the completed BIRD Mini-Dev protocol and              100

                                     make no claim about public datasets that were discussed but not run.                                101

                                          Exact SQL match and execution evaluation answer different questions. String match              102

                                     can penalize equivalent SQL, whereas result equality can reward nonequivalent queries               103

                                     on an insufficiently discriminating database state. A robustness evaluation should retain           104

                                     predicted SQL, record every failure, and specify the state on which equality was observed.          105

                                     MA-SQLGrid uses strict result equality for inherited snapshot analyses and separately re-           106

                                     ports constructed-state agreement and bounded metamorphic invariance. Neither auxiliary             107

                                     endpoint is semantic certification.                                                                 108



                                     2.2. Schema Linking, Context Packages, and Output Contracts                                         109


                                          Schema linking connects question spans to tables, columns, values, and foreign-key             110

                                     relations [3,8,9]. Full-schema context preserves recall but can increase token cost and             111

                                     distraction. Compact context reduces the search space but risks dropping a required join            112

                                     key or predicate. Value examples can disambiguate coded attributes, while question-                 113

                                     derived instructions can identify aggregation, result shape, ordering, or a limit.                  114

                                          The inherited GridDB factorial study isolates two implemented prompt factors: a full-          115

                                     DDL/global-values package versus a compact/domain-grounded package, and absence                     116

                                     versus presence of a composite structural/SQL-operation hint. The compact package is not            117

                                     a pure schema-length manipulation because it also changes value presentation and adds               118

                                     corpus-tailored normalization. The hint is not merely formatting because it can include             119

                                     aggregation, grouping, projection, ordering, and limit guidance. We retain these precise            120

                                     labels instead of assigning broader causal interpretations.                                         121

                                          Constrained generation and execution guidance reduce invalid candidates. PICARD                122

                                     constrains decoding so inadmissible SQL continuations can be rejected during generation             123

                                     [4]. CHESS combines contextual grounding and SQL synthesis components [7]; other                    124

                                     prompt-based methods investigate decomposition and representation choices [5,6,10]. MA-             125

                                     SQLGrid differs at the coordination boundary: its new core accepts externally produced              126

                                     candidates, checks them with deterministic contracts, and records evidence before selection.        127

                                     It does not claim that the current lexical cartographer supersedes learned schema-linking                 128

                                     systems.                                                                                                  129



                                     2.3. Agentic Decomposition and Auditable Control                                                          130


                                          Agentic decomposition can make intermediate decisions explicit, but naming pipeline                  131

                                     stages as agents does not establish a multi-agent benefit. Such evidence requires a com-                  132

                                     parison that holds the model, data, decoding configuration, candidate count, and physical                 133

                                     generation budget constant. Otherwise, a multi-candidate system can improve simply                        134

                                     because it receives more samples.                                                                         135

                                          MA-SQLGrid treats an agent as a role with a typed contract rather than assuming                      136

                                     that every role must be a separately hosted model. The Analyst and Cartographer can                       137

                                     be deterministic or model-assisted; the Synthesizer can receive candidates from a frozen                  138

                                     generator; the Validator uses a fixed read-only policy; the Critic consumes named-state                   139

                                     evidence; and the Adjudicator is deterministic. This design allows components to be                       140

                                     replaced without changing the blackboard schema and prevents gold-guided selection                        141

                                     from being hidden behind an opaque debate label.                                                          142



                                     2.4. Power-Grid Data and External Validity                                                                143


                                           Power-system datasets combine topology, equipment attributes, measurements, time                    144

                                     series, costs, and constraints. RTS-GMLC and SimBench provide reproducible engineering                    145

                                     resources that can be transformed into relational structures [11,12]. Their availability does             146

                                     not automatically create a text-to-SQL benchmark: intended projections, units, ordering,                  147

                                     ties, and reference SQL require semantic review. The current RTS-GMLC, SimBench, and                      148

                                     NERC-derived candidates are consequently machine-adjudicated silver resources, not                        149

                                     expert gold.                                                                                              150

                                           DKA-SQL is a direct power-grid text-to-SQL precedent [13]. Its published corpus,                    151

                                     method, models, and evaluation boundary differ from ours. Because no official imple-                      152

                                     mentation was used, the current BIRD protocol is neither a DKA-SQL reproduction nor a                     153

                                     head-to-head comparison.                                                                                  154

                                     3.1. Task and Evidence Boundary                                                                           156


                                         For question xi , read-only database Di , and introspected schema Si , a generator                    157

                                     produces one or more candidates                                                                           158

                                     Each candidate must satisfy a single-statement read-only contract. The accepted leading                   160

                                     form is SELECT or WITH; comments, multiple statements, write operations, schema changes,                  161

                                     attachments, and pragmas are rejected. A candidate is eligible for adjudication only when                 162

                                     it is safe and executable.                                                                                163

                                            The offline evaluator may later compare the selected query with a reference result.                164

                                     Let Ei = 1 when the selected SQL executes and its result equals the frozen reference result               165

                                          MA-SQLGrid comprises five specialist roles coordinated by a deterministic controller.                                 202

                                     Figure 1 is derived from the implemented source rather than an aspirational free-form                                      203

                                     agent diagram. The controller is not a sixth reasoning model: it orders calls, applies the                                 204

                                     registered rule, and seals the trace. Messages receive consecutive sequence numbers, and                                   205

                                     the complete trace is serialized canonically to a SHA-256 digest.                                                          206

                                          The Query Analyst produces a structured question-only intent record containing                                        207

                                     lexical tokens, detected aggregations, ordering requirements, and an explicit limit when                                   208

                                     present. It does not select SQL or see correctness. The Schema Cartographer maps lexical                                   209

                                     intent to tables, columns, and foreign-key edges. The current skeleton uses deterministic                                  210

                                     overlap and records unmatched tokens; a richer GridDB adapter adds disclosed domain                                        211

                                     rules and graph expansion.                                                                                                 212

                                          The SQL Synthesizer packages externally produced SQL strings. It contains no model                                    213

                                     client, performs no hidden API call, canonicalizes whitespace and semicolons, removes ex-                                  214

                                     act duplicates without changing first occurrence, and assigns stable identifiers. The Valida-                              215

                                     tion Engine separates lexical admissibility from the executor trust boundary. The executor                                 216

                                     bound into release v3 opens a frozen database through a URI with mode=ro&immutable=1,                                      217

                                     enables SQLite query_only, disables loadable extensions, and installs an authorizer that                                   218

                                     admits selection, reads, selected functions, and recursive-query opcodes. Writes, DDL,                                     219

                                     transactions, attachments, pragmas, metadata enumeration, dangerous file/extension func-                                   220

                                     tions, and reads outside configured table/column allowlists are denied. A progress handler                                 221

                                     enforces registered time and opcode limits, and result fetching enforces a row cap. Every                                  222

                                     attempt records database and SQL hashes, authorization or resource failure, elapsed time,                                  223

                                     row count, and result hash; no automatic retry removes a failure.                                                          224

                                          After R3 review, a separate FINAL executor variant adds explicit function allowlisting,                               225

                                     maximum projected width, a raw scalar-cell byte boundary, and a budget for the total                                       226

                                     returned result. BLOBs and memory views are charged at their original payload length                                       227

                                     before their public representation is replaced by a digest; the total budget charges the                                   228

                                     original payload plus deterministic row, column, type-envelope, delimiter, and container                                   229

                                     3.4. Deterministic Adjudication and Abstention                                                        247


                                          Eligibility always requires safety and successful execution. The default rule assigns            248

                                     40 validation points for safety, 40 for execution, 10 for question-derived aggregate-shape            249

                                     conformity, 5 for question-derived ordering conformity, and up to 5 for lexical value hits. In        250

                                     no-counterfactual mode, ties preserve original candidate order. In required-counterfactual            251

                                     mode, incomplete coverage fails closed before ranking and the registered pass threshold is            252

                                     enforced. If no candidate remains eligible, the controller abstains. These are deterministic          253

                                     recorded engineering rules, but v3 does not establish that their design preceded access to            254

                                     derived outcomes for the 180 items. Weight, pass-threshold, and reversed-order results are            255

                                     therefore descriptive sensitivity analyses.                                                           256

                                     3.5. Inherited GridDB Factorial Design                                                                            259


                                          The paired design crosses context package c ∈ {0, 1} and composite hint h ∈ {0, 1}.                          260

                                     5. Discussion                                                                                           474


                                     5.1. What the Evidence Supports                                                                         475


                                           The combined evidence supports four bounded findings. First, explicit structural                  476

                                     and SQL-operation instructions strongly alter projected-column adherence, but surface                   477

                                     conformity does not establish correct execution. Second, the audited prospective 700-                   478

                                     call component experiment finds that presented value evidence matters for one frozen                    479

                                     Qwen snapshot while showing no effect for Granite; this is component evidence, not a                    480

                                     five-role outcome. Third, candidate selection and prompting are backbone-dependent: the                 481

                                     component selector did not meet its registered efficacy rule, and the descriptively best                482

                                     BIRD method differed between Qwen and Granite. Fourth, on the historical eight-slot                     483

                                     pool, validation ranking changed the observed finite-set score relative to fixed order, but             484

                                     complete witness evidence changed only Q039, and top-score ties on 130/180 items made                   485

                                     results strongly dependent on arbitrary source order.                                                   486

                                           These findings justify separating intent, schema grounding, synthesis, validation, and            487

                                     state evidence. They do not establish that five roles are superior to one call or that newly            488

                                     generated multi-agent candidates would reproduce the offline gain. The architecture’s                   489

                                     demonstrated value is methodological and safety-bounded: typed contracts, database-                     490

                                     enforced eligibility, complete-evidence gates, deterministic selection, abstention, incident            491

                                     retention, and a sealed gold boundary make later comparisons reproducible and falsifiable.              492



                                     5.2. Interpreting the Retrospective Replay                                                              493


                                          The replay asks whether inherited ledgers contain enough distinct executable candi-                494

                                     dates to exercise the coordination interfaces. For 172 of 180 questions, they do. This reduces          495

                                     implementation risk and identifies eight fail-closed cases that require a registered fallback           496

                                     or a new matched generation policy.                                                                     497

                                          The replay cannot estimate a coordination effect. Its pool mixes two backbones                     498

                                     and four prompt packages, so generation condition and diversity are confounded. It                      499

                                     also lacks reference-free counterfactual equivalence evidence. Post hoc gold scoring of                 500

                                     selected outputs would remain exploratory and cannot substitute for a prospective matched               501

                                     comparison.                                                                                             502



                                     5.3. Interpreting the Descriptive Re-Execution                                                          503


                                           The release-v3 study hash-binds the selection view, rules, code, tests, and state inputs          504

                                     and verifies the software fact that each execution path directly loaded raw gold only after             505

                                     all blackboards were sealed. This does not establish outcome blindness: its frozen test suite           506

                                     had accessed gold-derived v2 evaluation rows for the same items before v3. The additive                 507

                                     supersession notice is the first authority for interpreting the immutable release package               508

                                     and invalidates its historical prospective, prespecified, and outcome-unseen labels. The                509




                                                                                                      https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 8, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                               17 of 20




                                     study is therefore a deterministic descriptive re-execution over a historical candidate pool.      510

                                     Equal candidate budget means that each selector received the same eight pre-existing slots;        511

                                     it does not mean equal historical model calls between source conditions, and it does not           512

                                     turn those candidates into contemporaneous agent outputs.                                          513

                                          The small contrast between validation-only and complete-witness selection is a mech-          514

                                     anism trace, not a gain estimate. V3 uses three different query-blind database hashes              515

                                     and exercises an irrelevant-relation witness, an index/storage witness, and a nullable             516

                                     schema-extension witness. These are constructed operator families rather than indepen-             517

                                     dent samples from a deployment population. The full gate changes only Q039 because                 518

                                     M3 exposes wildcard-projection arity while preserving explicit-column projections. The             519

                                     natural-language request itself admits unresolved status and date-boundary interpretations,        520

                                     so the frozen-reference match cannot be promoted to power-grid semantic correctness. An            521

                                     earlier v1 diagnostic used three same-hash insertion files and is retained as an excluded          522

                                     incident; v2 is retained as a provenance-incomplete predecessor. The supersession notice           523

                                     excludes their prospective interpretations and none of these predecessors supports the             524

                                     current scientific claim.                                                                          525

                                          The tie audit quantifies a second limitation: 130/180 questions have multiple highest-        526

                                     scoring candidates for each adjudicating selector, with approximately 5.4 candidates in the        527

                                     average top-tie set. The original-order rule consequently chooses source slots without se-         528

                                     mantic evidence and selects Qwen-origin candidates on 178/180 items. Reporting only the            529

                                     0.5556 default or choosing the 0.6556 reverse-order sensitivity after outcome access would         530

                                     be misleading. A later rule-development study requires development-only calibration,               531

                                     richer reference-free evidence, and a genuinely untouched evaluation resource.                     532



                                     5.4. Power-Grid Use and Reproducibility                                                            533


                                           Database-enforced read-only execution, resource limits, syntactic validity, and result-      534

                                     shape conformity are necessary safeguards, not operational certification. A safely executed        535

                                     query can still return the wrong assets, omit time bounds, misinterpret codes, or mishandle        536

                                     ties. Table/column/function allowlists constrain SQL operations inside this local evaluator;       537

                                     user authorization requires a separately authenticated identity, institutional access policy,      538

                                     and data-owner decision. The additive FINAL raw-cell-byte, budgeted-result-byte, and               539

                                     output-width controls were not used in release v3 and cannot be invoked to reinterpret its         540

                                     results. They bound returned values within one Python/SQLite process, not SQLite’s entire          541

                                     temporary-memory or process-memory footprint. Multi-state witnesses reduce accidental              542

                                     snapshot equality but cannot cover all distributions or schema changes. Generated SQL              543

                                     and returned source rows require authorized human inspection before any maintenance,               544

                                     protection, dispatch, or asset-management decision.                                                545

                                           Negative results are part of the contribution. Zero of nine primary factorial execution      546

                                     tests survives correction. The deterministic component selector meets its efficacy rule for        547

                                     neither snapshot. No registered multi-state effect survives correction. Granite and Qwen do        548

                                     not share the same best BIRD method. Immutable ledgers, direct re-execution, registered            549

                                     test families, portable manifests, and explicit incidents preserve these outcomes rather than      550

                                     collapsing them into a success narrative.                                                          551



                                     6. Limitations and Future Work                                                                     552


                                          The primary domain corpus is one synthetic database with eight tables, 98 rows,               553

                                     and 180 evaluation questions. Its evaluation partition was visible during development.             554

                                     The 70 structural groups are dependence proxies, and 58 are singletons. Results cannot             555

                                     be generalized to production schemas, permissions, missing-data patterns, units, access-           556

                                     control policies, or workloads.                                                                    557




                                                                                                  https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 8, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                                18 of 20




                                           Only two quantized local snapshots were evaluated. Model family, size, quantization,          558

                                     serving software, and serialization may interact. The compact package bundles schema                559

                                     reduction, values, normalization, and serialization; the hint bundles projection, aggregation,      560

                                     grouping, ordering, and limit rules. The inherited factorial experiment identifies package          561

                                     effects rather than contributions of individual internal rules.                                     562

                                           The five-role core and frozen release-v3 executor pass 30 unit/adversarial/regression         563

                                     tests; the separate FINAL executor passes 14 additive tests, including four new BLOB-               564

                                     accounting and trace/boundary methods. The 21-entry freeze manifest is not a test count,            565

                                     and the invalid transient 33/33 run is excluded. The system has not completed a new-                566

                                     generation experiment on an evaluation resource whose outcomes were previously inac-                567

                                     cessible to the study process. Its Analyst and Cartographer are deterministic skeletons,            568

                                     its Synthesizer packages external candidates, and its adjudication weights have not been            569

                                     independently optimized. Release v3 descriptively re-executes selection over historical             570

                                     candidates and previously evaluated items; it does not estimate generation, communica-              571

                                     tion, latency, token, or autonomous-agent effects. Existing formal-v5 agreement labels are          572

                                     gold-relative and intentionally excluded from selection.                                            573

                                           RTS-GMLC, SimBench, and NERC question–SQL assets remain machine-adjudicated                   574

                                     silver data. Qualified review is required for intended projections, units, ordering, tie            575

                                     handling, and result granularity before external accuracy is reported.                              576

                                           Future work should execute a new hash-locked, call-matched comparison of four con-            577

                                     temporaneous generation conditions: a single direct candidate; staged question/schema               578

                                     handoffs with one candidate; a fixed multi-candidate budget with validation and adjudi-             579

                                     cation but no counterfactual evidence; and the same candidate pool with a pre-registered            580

                                     multi-family reference-free state suite. Model snapshot, question order, database state,            581

                                     decoding, candidate count, physical calls, and evaluator must be held constant. The pro-            582

                                     tocol must pre-register execution accuracy, valid SQL, abstention, tokens, latency, failure         583

                                     taxonomy, clustered inference, multiplicity, and incident retention. The candidate-order            584

                                     rule must be chosen on development-only evidence before the untouched evaluation set is             585

                                     opened.                                                                                             586

                                           Further work can replace lexical grounding with a trained schema linker, add a                587

                                     low-confidence full-schema fallback, compare deterministic adjudication with budget-                588

                                     matched voting, and evaluate risk–coverage calibration. Qualified power-system/database             589

                                     reviewers must independently assess domain semantics; LLM or author-assisted triage                 590

                                     cannot be relabeled as independent expert gold. Every change should receive a new                   591

                                     protocol identity rather than modifying a completed run.                                            592



                                     7. Conclusions                                                                                      593


                                           MA-SQLGrid reframes power-grid text-to-SQL as an auditable coordination problem.              594

                                     Five typed software roles separate question analysis, schema mapping, candidate packag-             595

                                     ing, safety and execution validation, and named-state criticism. A deterministic controller         596

                                     records their handoffs, selects only eligible candidates, and abstains when required evi-           597

                                     dence is incomplete. Here, multi-agent denotes this inspectable role decomposition, not             598

                                     demonstrated benefit from five autonomous language-model agents. The frozen executor                599

                                     enforces read-only and resource limits at the SQLite boundary rather than relying on lexical        600

                                     filtering alone; the separate FINAL variant adds tested raw-cell-byte, budgeted-result-             601

                                     byte, width, and function controls that are not retroactive to v3 and are not whole-process         602

                                     memory isolation.                                                                                   603

                                           The inherited experiments provide a substantial but bounded evidence base. The                604

                                     1440-prediction GridDB study shows strong projected-column instruction uptake but                   605

                                     no primary execution effect surviving Holm correction. The 700-call component study                 606




                                                                                                   https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 8, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                                           19 of 20




                                     supports presented values for one Qwen snapshot, not Granite, and does not validate its                        607

                                     selector under the registered rule. The 25,920-row state study finds no corrected factorial                    608

                                     agreement effect. The 5000-call BIRD protocol supplies a non-grid public comparison                            609

                                     whose best method depends on the backbone and whose methods use unequal call counts.                           610

                                     The retrospective replay confirms candidate coverage for 172 of 180 questions without                          611

                                     accuracy claims.                                                                                               612

                                          The descriptive offline re-execution records 80/180 for fixed order, 100/180 for                          613

                                     validation-only, and 101/180 for complete witnesses. Both adjudicating selectors have                          614

                                     highest-score ties on 130/180 questions, average tie multiplicity is approximately 5.4, and                    615

                                     reverse order changes the complete-witness sensitivity result to 117–118/180. The sole                         616

                                     Q039 difference is a constructed, outcome-exposed, semantically ambiguous projection                           617

                                     trace. These facts, the historical mixed-source pool, development-visible GridDB, and                          618

                                     absent qualified semantic review prevent a general multi-agent superiority or power-grid                       619

                                     deployment claim. The original title is retained as the framework identity; robust refers                      620

                                     only to explicitly tested mutation-denial, bounded-execution, complete-evidence, and                           621

                                     three-witness mechanisms, not universal or operational robustness.                                             622
