# Cover Letter Draft

Dear Editors of *Applied Sciences*,

Please consider the manuscript entitled “MA-SQLGrid: A Robust Multi-Agent
Framework for Text-to-SQL in Power Grid Databases” for publication as an
Article in *Applied Sciences*.

The manuscript presents an auditable five-role software architecture for
power-grid text-to-SQL, a database-enforced read-only execution boundary, and
deterministic evidence-based adjudication. It reports positive, null, and
adverse findings from the retained experimental records without claiming an
end-to-end five-role accuracy benefit, qualified power-grid semantic validity,
deployment safety, or universal robustness. The original title identifies the
framework; the manuscript explicitly bounds the empirical meaning of “robust”
to the implemented and tested safeguards.
