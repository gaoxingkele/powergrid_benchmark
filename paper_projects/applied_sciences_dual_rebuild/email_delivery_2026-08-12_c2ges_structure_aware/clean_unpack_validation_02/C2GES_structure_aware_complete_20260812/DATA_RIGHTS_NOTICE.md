# Data Rights and Reproduction Notice

The study uses public NERC technical reports as a maintenance-oriented proxy corpus. Public accessibility does not by itself grant redistribution rights.

Accordingly, this email package excludes:

- third-party source PDFs;
- extracted sentence-level or paragraph-level verbatim text;
- any artifact that would reconstruct substantial portions of a source report.

The package includes:

- rights-safe report metadata and source URLs;
- inclusion and split information;
- non-verbatim aggregate and paired metric data;
- output-length and selection-page diagnostics;
- the exact formal configuration, analysis code, and regression tests.

Editors and reviewers may request restricted research artifacts from the corresponding author. Access remains subject to the rights holder's terms and cannot be guaranteed merely by request.
