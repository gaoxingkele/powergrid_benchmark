                                       Featured Application                                                                                            1


                                       MA-SQLGrid is a research framework for traceable natural-language access to structured                          2

                                       power-grid records. It exposes intent, schema, candidate, execution, and selection evidence                     3

                                       so that generated SQL and returned rows can be inspected before consequential use.                             4



                                       Abstract                                                                                                        5


                                       Power-grid databases encode asset, maintenance, status, temporal, and topological seman-                        6

                                       tics that can make executable SQL answer the wrong question. We present MA-SQLGrid, a                           7

                                       five-role architecture that organizes intent analysis, schema mapping, candidate normal-                        8

                                       ization, read-only execution validation, and constructed-state evidence through a shared                        9

                                       blackboard and deterministic controller. Robustness under tested conditions denotes mu-                        10

                                       tation denial, bounded execution, and fail-closed handling of incomplete evidence. The                         11

                                       evaluation combines GridDB generation, component, and constructed-state studies; BIRD                          12

                                       Mini-Dev experiments across 11 public databases; and a fixed historical pool containing                        13

                                       eight candidates for each of 180 GridDB questions. Context and presented-value effects                         14

                                       depended on the frozen model snapshot. Within the historical pool, validation-aware                            15

                                       ranking increased reference matches from 80/180 to 100/180, while complete three-witness                       16

                                       evidence yielded 101/180. Both adjudicating selectors produced frequent top-score ties                         17

                                       and remained sensitive to candidate order, indicating that the available evidence could                        18

                                       not consistently distinguish semantically competing candidates. MA-SQLGrid provides a                          19

                                       practical and reproducible architecture for traceable power-grid Text-to-SQL research. The                     20

                                       historical-pool comparison remains descriptive; unseen, expert-reviewed grid queries and                       21

                                       budget-matched generation are required to establish broader efficacy.                                          22



                                       Keywords: text-to-SQL; multi-agent systems; power-grid databases; schema grounding;                            23

                                       execution validation; constructed-state testing; reproducibility                                               24

                                           The risk is amplified by the semantics of grid records. An identifier that looks numeric      35

                                     may be a categorical code; a timestamp can refer to sensing, issuance, acknowledgement,             36

                                     or closure; and an asset status may describe the asset, an event, or a work order. The              37

                                     same request may therefore admit several executable queries with different engineering              38

                                     meanings. A useful workflow should expose how the request was interpreted, how schema               39

                                     and values were grounded, what SQL candidates were considered, which candidates                     40

                                     executed, and why one candidate was selected. When the available evidence is incomplete,            41

                                     the workflow should be able to abstain rather than conceal the ambiguity.                           42

                                           Cross-database benchmarks such as Spider and BIRD have made schema generaliza-                43

                                     tion and realistic database content central evaluation concerns [1,2]. Spider 2.0 extends this      44

                                     setting to enterprise workflows with large schemas and multiple SQL dialects, while Multi-          45

                                     SQL broadens context-dependent evaluation across diverse operations [3,4]. Schema-aware             46

                                     models, constrained decoding, context selection, decomposition, and execution guidance              47

                                     address complementary failure modes [5–9]. Recent systems also use multiple roles for               48

                                     generation, review, or repair [10–13]. Most studies optimize one or more of these stages,           49

                                     but fewer examine generation, database-enforced validation, state evidence, candidate               50

                                     selection, and trace retention as one reproducible decision process. This gap is important for      51

                                     grid data because an execution success alone provides weak evidence about field meaning,            52

                                     time scope, status coding, or join intent.                                                          53

                                           MA-SQLGrid addresses this systems problem through a five-role multi-agent archi-              54

                                     tecture. The Query Analyst represents question intent; the Schema Cartographer maps                 55

                                     that intent to tables, columns, values, and relationships; the SQL Synthesizer normalizes           56

                                     and identifies candidate statements; the Validation Engine records read-only execution              57

                                     evidence; and the Metamorphic-State Critic records outcomes on named constructed states.            58

                                     A deterministic controller coordinates these roles through a shared blackboard trace and            59

                                     either selects an eligible candidate or abstains. The controller is not a sixth reasoning           60

                                     agent. It enforces message order, evidence requirements, and the registered selection rule.         61

                                     This division makes errors localizable: a reviewer can determine whether a failure arose            62

                                     during intent analysis, schema mapping, candidate production, execution, state testing, or          63

                                     adjudication.                                                                                       64

                                           The empirical design examines this workflow from complementary angles. GridDB                 65

                                     experiments vary schema context, structural hints, and presented values on a controlled             66

                                     power-grid schema. A constructed-state study examines whether observed results persist              67

                                     across registered database transformations. BIRD Mini-Dev tests the portability of frozen           68

                                     prompting procedures across 11 public, non-grid databases. Finally, a no-generation                 69

                                     study applies fixed-order and evidence-aware selectors to the same historical pool of eight         70

                                     candidates per GridDB question. These studies do not form a single accuracy benchmark;              71

                                     together, they show where context, validation evidence, and deterministic selection affect          72

                                     observable behavior.                                                                                73

                                           The study addresses three research questions:                                                 74


                                     1.    How do schema context, value evidence, and prompting organization affect executable           75

                                           results for frozen models on GridDB and public databases?                                     76

                                     2.    When candidate generation is held fixed in a historical pool, how does evidence-aware         77

                                           adjudication change the selected query and its reference match relative to fixed order?       78

                                     3.    How stable are these observations across model snapshots, constructed database                79

                                           states, score ties, and candidate order?                                                      80

                                     2. Related Work                                                                                    97


                                     2.1. Schema Linking, Context Construction, and Evaluation                                          98


                                           Text-to-SQL systems map a natural-language request and database schema to an                 99

                                     executable query. Spider established cross-domain evaluation on unseen databases, and              100

                                     BIRD expanded the setting toward larger databases with richer content [1,2]. Spider 2.0 and        101

                                     MultiSQL further emphasize enterprise-scale schemas, varied SQL operations, and context-           102

                                     dependent workflows [3,4]. These benchmarks make schema generalization visible, but                103

                                     their scores still depend on the database engine, evaluator, split, and model configuration.       104

                                           Schema linking connects question spans to tables, columns, values, and foreign-              105

                                     key relations [5,14,15]. Full-schema context protects recall but increases token cost and          106

                                     distraction. Compact context reduces the search space but can omit a bridge table or coded         107

                                     field. Retrieval, graph pathfinding, and bidirectional table–column selection seek a better        108

                                     balance [16–21]. Value examples add another signal because the intended field may be               109

                                     clearer from stored codes than from its name. The GridDB studies in this paper examine             110

                                     these context and value choices as observable components of a larger workflow.                     111

                                           Evaluation also needs more than one notion of match. String comparison can penalize          112

                                     equivalent SQL, while result equality can reward different queries that happen to coincide         113

                                     on one database snapshot. Test-suite accuracy and multiple database states reduce some ac-         114

                                     cidental equivalence, provided that the state transformations preserve the intended relation.      115

                                     MA-SQLGrid therefore reports strict execution equality, constructed-state agreement, and           116

                                     bounded metamorphic invariance as distinct endpoints. Together they describe execution             117

                                     behavior; expert-reviewed business semantics remain a separate validation layer.                   118



                                     2.2. Multi-Role Generation, Validation, and Selection                                              119


                                          Constrained decoding, decomposition, contextual grounding, and execution-guided               120

                                     repair address different stages of SQL production [6–9,22]. Recent workflows add question          121

                                     rewriting, attention-oriented decomposition, partition-and-drill reasoning, preference opti-       122

                                     mization, active database probing, or dialect-aware bootstrapping [23–28]. These methods           123

                                     mainly improve generation or repair. MA-SQLGrid focuses on the interfaces that connect             124

                                     candidate production to database-enforced validation and final selection.                          125

                                          SQL-oriented language-model teams distinguish candidate diversity from explicit               126

                                     role coordination [10]. Multi-agent Text-to-SQL systems have used decomposition and                127

                                     tools, question-dependent routing, and review–rebuttal–revision consensus [11–13]. MA-             128

                                     SQLGrid assigns stable responsibilities to five roles and stores every handoff in a shared         129

                                     blackboard trace. The SQL Synthesizer may package candidates produced elsewhere, while             130




                                                                                                  https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 12, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                                          4 of 25




                                     the Validation Engine, Metamorphic-State Critic, and deterministic controller operate on                      131

                                     explicit evidence. This design makes the trace, rather than only the final SQL, the unit of                   132

                                     reproducibility.                                                                                              133

                                          Read-only execution and evidence-aware adjudication are complementary. Database                          134

                                     controls prevent an admissible evaluation from mutating the source, whereas adjudication                      135

                                     compares only candidates that satisfy the required safety, execution, and state-evidence                      136

                                     gates. A stored trace cannot guarantee sound reasoning, but it can reveal whether an error                    137

                                     entered through intent analysis, schema mapping, generation, execution, or selection. This                    138

                                     diagnostic role motivates the framework even when candidate generation is held fixed.                         139

                                     3. Materials and Methods                                                                                  155


                                     3.1. Problem Formulation and System Overview                                                              156


                                         For question xi , read-only database Di , and introspected schema Si , a generator                    157

                                     produces one or more candidates                                                                           158



                                                                          Yi = {yei1 , . . . , yeik }.                                  (1)    159



                                     Each candidate must satisfy a single-statement read-only contract. The accepted leading                   160

                                     form is SELECT or WITH; comments, multiple statements, write operations, schema changes,                  161

                                     attachments, and pragmas are rejected. A candidate is eligible for adjudication only when                 162

                                     it is safe and executable.                                                                                163

                                            The offline evaluator may later compare the selected query with a reference result.                164

                                     Let Ei = 1 when the selected SQL executes and its result equals the frozen reference result               165

                                     under the registered evaluator, and Ei = 0 otherwise. Reference SQL, reference results, Ei ,              166

                                     and any derivative correctness field are forbidden from all five agents and the Adjudicator.              167

                                     Evaluation occurs only after the blackboard is sealed.                                                    168

                                            This boundary yields three distinct objects. A candidate is admissible when its syntax             169

                                     and requested operations satisfy policy, executable when it completes within the registered               170

                                     database limits, and correct under the frozen evaluator when its result matches the reference             171

                                     after sealing. Admissibility is neither executability nor correctness. The manuscript pre-                172

                                     serves all three labels because collapsing them would let an executable but semantically                  173

                                     wrong query appear validated.                                                                             174

                                            The framework’s response contract contains the selected SQL, stable candidate and                  175

                                     source identifiers, referenced tables and columns, execution-state and result hashes, state-              176

                                     coverage summary, abstention or selection reason, and the sealed trace digest. Figure 1                   177

                                     shows how these records move through the five-role multi-agent architecture. The controller               178

                                     is not a sixth reasoning model: it schedules the roles, checks their typed messages, applies              179

                                     the deterministic rule, and seals the shared blackboard trace before offline evaluation.                  180

                                            The response contract supports traceability rather than user authorization. A calling              181

                                     application must authenticate the user, restrict the accessible database view, and decide                 182

                                     whether returned rows may be displayed. Generated SQL and execution success do not                        183

                                     replace those institutional controls.                                                                     184

                                     3.4. Five-Role Multi-Agent Architecture                                                                                       221


                                          MA-SQLGrid comprises five specialist roles coordinated by the controller shown                                           222

                                     in Figure 1. Messages receive consecutive sequence numbers, and the complete trace is                                         223

                                     serialized canonically to a SHA-256 digest. Each role has a restricted input and produces                                     224

                                     one typed output for the next stage.                                                                                          225

                                          The Query Analyst produces a structured question-only intent record containing                                           226

                                     lexical tokens, detected aggregations, ordering requirements, and an explicit limit when                                      227

                                     present. It does not select SQL or see correctness. The Schema Cartographer maps lexical                                      228

                                     intent to tables, columns, and foreign-key edges. The current skeleton uses deterministic                                     229

                                     overlap and records unmatched tokens; a richer GridDB adapter adds disclosed domain                                           230

                                     rules and graph expansion.                                                                                                    231

                                          The SQL Synthesizer packages external SQL without model calls, canonicalizes for-                                        232

                                     matting, removes exact duplicates while preserving first occurrence, and assigns stable                                       233

                                     identifiers. The Validation Engine used by the historical-pool study opens the database                                       234

                                     with mode=ro&immutable=1, enables query_only, disables extensions, and installs an au-                                        235

                                     thorizer. It denies writes, DDL, transactions, attachments, pragmas, metadata enumeration,                                    236

                                     dangerous functions, and reads outside table/column allowlists; progress and fetch han-                                       237

                                     dlers enforce time, opcode, and row limits. Every attempt retains database/SQL hashes,                                        238

                                     failures, timing, row count, and result hash without hidden retry.                                                            239

                                          A later executor revision adds function allowlisting, projected-width, raw-cell-byte,                                    240

                                     and total-result-byte limits. Fourteen tests cover BLOB denial, total-budget and boundary                                     241

                                     behavior, trace retention, oversized text or results, and wide projections. These controls                                    242

                                     were not used to produce the historical 80/100/101 counts. Table and column allowlists                                        243

                                     constrain query scope but do not authenticate users or provide row-level authorization;                                       244

                                     both executor variants establish tested SQLite mutation denial and scoped execution                                           245

                                     bounds.                                                                                                                       246




                                                                                                                      https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 12, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                                  8 of 25




                                          The threat model assumes untrusted SQL but a trusted host, SQLite library, configured            247

                                     allowlist, and immutable database. It does not address a compromised process, OS escape,              248

                                     side channels, built-in malicious extensions, or upstream authorization errors. Production            249

                                     use requires process isolation, least-privilege views, authenticated context, logging, and            250

                                     independent security review.                                                                          251

                                          Failure handling is intentionally fail-closed and non-retrying. A parse denial, au-              252

                                     thorizer denial, timeout, opcode limit, row limit, width limit, cell limit, or total-result           253

                                     limit creates a retained attempt record and makes that candidate ineligible for the current           254

                                     decision. The controller cannot silently weaken a limit or substitute a repaired query. A             255

                                     repair procedure may be studied as a separate registered generation step, as in BIRD B3,              256

                                     but its extra physical call and visibility must remain explicit.                                      257

                                          The Metamorphic-State Critic (historically named the Counterfactual Critic) con-                 258

                                     sumes named state results, rejects duplicate state identifiers, and records evaluated, passed,        259

                                     failed, and missing states. When state evidence is optional, missing evidence remains                 260

                                     diagnostic and does not break prior no-state behavior. When the caller requires state                 261

                                     evidence, coverage must equal the complete registered state set and the pass threshold                262

                                     must be met; otherwise the candidate is ineligible. Thus an incomplete 1/1 record cannot              263

                                     outrank a complete 10/11 record. Gold-relative labels from the constructed-state study                264

                                     remain forbidden during selection.                                                                    265

                                          The blackboard is append-only at the application layer: a role posts a typed message             266

                                     and cannot rewrite an earlier message. The controller checks role, message type, sequence             267

                                     number, and required fields before acceptance. Canonical serialization fixes key order                268

                                     and whitespace before hashing. This makes two runs comparable at the trace level and                  269

                                     exposes an unexpected hidden field or changed handoff. It does not prevent a malicious                270

                                     host process from altering memory or files; process isolation and remote attestation are              271

                                     outside scope.                                                                                        272

                                          Information flow is intentionally asymmetric. The Analyst receives the question but              273

                                     no candidate SQL. The Cartographer receives the question, intent record, and introspected             274

                                     schema. The Synthesizer receives external candidate strings and assigns identities but                275

                                     cannot execute them. The Validator receives one candidate plus a scoped executor. The                 276

                                     Critic receives named-state outcomes but no reference correctness. Only the deterministic             277

                                     Adjudicator receives the eligible evidence summaries. Gold enters a separate offline                  278

                                     evaluator after the decision message and board digest exist.                                          279



                                     3.5. Read-Only Validation and Evidence-Aware Adjudication                                             280


                                          Eligibility always requires safety and successful execution. The archived 100-point              281

                                     representation assigns 40 points for each of those two properties, followed by 10 for                 282

                                     question-derived aggregate-shape conformity, 5 for question-derived ordering conformity,              283

                                     and up to 5 for lexical value hits. Because every ranked candidate has already passed                 284

                                     both hard gates, the first 80 points are constant and contain no ranking information. The             285

                                     effective evidence score is therefore e(y) = 10Ishape (y) + 5Iorder (y) + 5V (y) for an eligible      286

                                     candidate y, with V (y) ∈ [0, 1]. When named-state evidence is optional, the controller               287

                                     selects an arg max e(y) and ties preserve the predefined candidate order. When named-state            288

                                     evidence is required, incomplete coverage or failure of the specified pass threshold makes            289

                                     y ineligible before the same ranking. If the eligible set is empty, the controller abstains. This     290

                                     gate–score–tie function is mathematically equivalent to the archived implementation and               291

                                     shows that safety and execution are eligibility controls rather than discriminative evidence.         292

                                     The retained weight, pass-threshold, and order analyses are descriptive because the project           293

                                     history does not establish that every design choice preceded access to derived outcomes for           294

                                     the 180 items.                                                                                        295




                                                                                                     https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 12, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                              9 of 25




                                           The 40/40/10/5/5 allocation, equivalently hard safety and execution gates followed          296

                                     by a 10/5/5 evidence score, is an illustrative deterministic policy rather than a learned or      297

                                     calibrated weighting. Its formal invariants are that unsafe or non-executable candidates          298

                                     are never selected; required named-state coverage is complete before threshold evaluation;        299

                                     gold-derived correctness is absent from the decision record; equal scores use the predefined      300

                                     order; and an empty eligible set yields abstention. These invariants support conformance          301

                                     testing and replay determinism, while semantic correctness and calibrated confidence              302

                                     require additional evidence.                                                                      303

                                           Question-derived shape checks are deliberately weak. They detect cues such as “how          304

                                     many”, “highest”, “first”, and explicit limits and compare them with the projected aggregate      305

                                     or ordering structure. They do not infer business semantics such as whether “active” refers       306

                                     to an asset, alarm, or work-order state. Lexical value hits likewise reward only presented        307

                                     values traceable to the question and context; they are not evidence that the associated           308

                                     column is the intended one. These low-bandwidth features keep adjudication inspectable            309

                                     but explain why large tie sets remain possible.                                                   310

                                           Abstention has two layers. Pre-ranking abstention occurs when no candidate passes           311

                                     safety and execution requirements or when required state coverage is incomplete. Post-            312

                                     ranking ambiguity is currently resolved by stable order rather than abstention because no         313

                                     pre-registered margin threshold exists in v3. The high tie rates observed later show that         314

                                     future work should freeze an ambiguity policy—for example, abstaining on unresolved top           315

                                     ties or requesting a user clarification—before examining new outcomes.                            316

                                           Robustness under tested conditions is represented by four observable mechanisms:            317

                                     database mutation denial, bounded execution, complete named-state eligibility, and de-            318

                                     terministic failure handling. Candidate-order stability is assessed separately in Results.        319

                                           The replay verifies SHA-256 for two 720-row prediction ledgers and the 25,920-row             374

                                     constructed-state ledger before reading them. For each question it pools the eight existing         375

                                     5. Discussion                                                                                           681


                                     5.1. Model-Dependent Use of Context and Values                                                          682


                                          The GridDB and BIRD studies show that additional structure is useful only when a                   683

                                     model can exploit it. Structural hints sharply changed projected-column adherence, yet                  684

                                     none of the nine primary GridDB execution effects survived Holm correction. Presented val-              685

                                     ues improved Qwen first-candidate matches on the paired component subset but produced                   686

                                     no change for Granite. BIRD produced another model-specific ordering: schema selection                  687

                                     was best for Qwen, whereas bounded repair had the highest Granite point estimate. These                 688

                                     patterns are consistent with schema-linking research in which context selection trades recall           689

                                     against distraction [16–18]. More context is not uniformly better. Its effect depends on the            690

                                     model snapshot, the serialization, and the type of evidence added.                                      691

                                          This dependence supports the separation of the Query Analyst and Schema Cartog-                    692

                                     rapher. Their records allow a later study to vary intent extraction, schema retrieval, or               693

                                     value presentation without changing the executor and adjudicator. It also cautions against              694

                                     treating projected-column conformity as a semantic endpoint. A query can adopt the                      695

                                     requested result shape while retaining the wrong table, predicate, value, or join.                      696



                                     5.2. Why Validation-Aware Selection Changed the Historical Pool                                         697


                                         Validation-aware ranking added 20 net reference matches relative to fixed order                     698

                                     through 22 rescues and 2 harms. The change is consistent with a simple mechanism:                       699

                                     execution failures, result-shape inconsistency, ordering mismatch, and weak value evidence              700




                                                                                                       https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 12, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                               21 of 25




                                     remove or demote candidates that a source-order rule would otherwise select. Because all            701

                                     selectors used the same precomputed ledger, the comparison isolates selection behavior              702

                                     within this pool rather than differences in candidate generation.                                   703

                                           The result is practically informative even though it is descriptive. It shows that            704

                                     retaining execution and trace evidence can change which existing candidate is chosen, and           705

                                     it identifies the questions on which the change helps or harms. A prospective comparison            706

                                     must regenerate candidates under equal call and token budgets, but the present ledger               707

                                     supplies a concrete hypothesis and a reproducible selector interface for that test.                 708



                                     5.3. Limited Increment from Constructed-State Evidence                                              709


                                          Complete witness evidence added only one match beyond validation-only ranking.                 710

                                     The three witnesses cover an irrelevant relation, storage/index changes, and nullable               711

                                     schema extension. These transformations detect structural or execution brittleness but              712

                                     contain little information about coded statuses, intended time boundaries, units, or business       713

                                     definitions. Their limited incremental effect is therefore compatible with their design.            714

                                          Q039 illustrates the distinction. The nullable-extension witness rejects a wildcard            715

                                     projection and preserves an explicit two-column projection, but neither candidate resolves          716

                                     whether “scheduled” denotes a status or a date and neither settles the meaning of “after            717

                                     June”. Constructed states can expose a specific implementation weakness; richer semantic            718

                                     evidence or user clarification is needed to resolve the underlying intent.                          719



                                     5.4. Ties as the Main Ranking Bottleneck                                                            720


                                          Both adjudicating selectors tied at the highest score on 130/180 questions, with about         721

                                     5.4 candidates in the average top-tie set. Reversing candidate order changed the complete-          722

                                     witness result from 101 to 117–118 matches. This sensitivity reveals a bottleneck that              723

                                     aggregate accuracy alone would hide: the evidence can eliminate some inferior candidates            724

                                     but often cannot rank the survivors.                                                                725

                                          The next design problem is therefore not simply to generate more candidates. It is to          726

                                     obtain reference-free semantic evidence that breaks ties consistently, or to abstain when such      727

                                     evidence is unavailable. Candidate-order rules, ambiguity margins, clarification requests,          728

                                     and risk–coverage targets should be fixed on development data before an untouched                   729

                                     evaluation set is opened. The shared blackboard trace provides the observations needed to           730

                                     study each alternative without changing the upstream generation records.                            731



                                     5.5. Engineering Significance                                                                       732


                                           MA-SQLGrid provides a traceable research prototype for natural-language access                733

                                     to structured grid data. Developers can locate a failure at the intent, schema, candidate,          734

                                     execution, state, or selection stage instead of receiving only a final SQL string. Database-        735

                                     enforced read-only execution, resource limits, and complete-evidence gates also reduce              736

                                     several implementation risks that lexical SQL filtering alone does not address.                     737

                                           These mechanisms are a foundation for safer data access, not an operational autho-            738

                                     rization layer. An executable query can still return the wrong assets, omit time bounds,            739

                                     or misinterpret codes. Consequential use requires authenticated users, least-privilege              740

                                     views, institutional access policy, and inspection by qualified personnel. The scientific           741

                                     value of the architecture lies in making those remaining assumptions and errors visible and         742

                                     reproducible.                                                                                       743

                                     6. Limitations and Future Work                                                                                     746


                                          Data and external validity. GridDB is a synthetic database with eight tables, 98 rows,                        747

                                     and 180 evaluation questions, and its evaluation partition was visible during development.                         748

                                     BIRD broadens database coverage but is not a grid benchmark. RTS-GMLC, SimBench,                                   749

                                     and NERC-derived question–SQL assets remain machine-adjudicated silver resources. The                              750

                                     next evaluation should use unseen grid schemas and queries independently reviewed by                               751

                                     power-system and database specialists for projections, units, time semantics, ordering, and                        752

                                     tie handling.                                                                                                      753

                                          Experimental design. The historical candidate pool combines two backbones and four                            754

                                     prompt cells whose outcomes had been examined during earlier development. It supports                              755

                                     a descriptive selector comparison, not an end-to-end five-role effect. A new study should                          756

                                     hold model snapshot, decoding, candidate count, physical calls, token budget, executor,                            757

                                     and evaluator constant across a direct single-candidate baseline, staged one-candidate                             758

                                     handoffs, validation-aware multi-candidate selection, and the same selection design with                           759

                                     registered state evidence.                                                                                         760

                                          Semantic validity. Read-only execution and constructed states test admissibility, ex-                         761

                                     ecutability, and selected invariances. They do not establish that a query reflects a grid                          762

                                     professional’s intended business meaning. Future work should combine expert review                                 763

                                     with reference-free evidence for units, status definitions, time windows, topology rela-                           764

                                     tions, and result granularity. Model- or author-assisted triage should remain distinct from                        765

                                     independent expert adjudication.                                                                                   766

                                          Ranking stability. The high tie rate and candidate-order sensitivity show that the                            767

                                     current evidence score is weak when several candidates execute successfully. Future                                768

                                     protocols should calibrate semantic ranking, abstention margins, and clarification policies                        769

                                     on development-only data and report risk–coverage on an untouched evaluation set. A                                770

                                     trained schema linker and low-confidence full-schema fallback can be introduced within                             771

                                     the same trace interface, with every change receiving a new protocol identity.                                     772



                                     7. Conclusions                                                                                                     773


                                          MA-SQLGrid reframes power-grid Text-to-SQL as a traceable multi-role decision                                 774

                                     process. Five specialized roles separate intent analysis, schema mapping, candidate normal-                        775

                                     ization, read-only execution validation, and constructed-state evidence. A deterministic                           776




                                                                                                            https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 12, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                                          23 of 25




                                     controller records their handoffs through a shared blackboard trace, selects only eligible                     777

                                     candidates, and can abstain when required evidence is incomplete. This architecture pro-                       778

                                     vides a reproducible interface for locating errors that would be hidden by a final-SQL-only                    779

                                     pipeline.                                                                                                      780

                                          The experiments show that context and value evidence depend on the model and                              781

                                     evaluation setting. The clearest selection result arises within the historical pool: fixed                     782

                                     order produced 80/180 reference matches, validation-aware ranking produced 100/180,                            783

                                     and complete witness evidence produced 101/180. The 20–21-match difference supports                            784

                                     validation-aware selection as a promising design direction. At the same time, top-score ties                   785

                                     on 130/180 questions and substantial candidate-order sensitivity expose an unresolved                          786

                                     semantic-ranking problem.                                                                                      787

                                          MA-SQLGrid therefore offers a practical foundation for auditable power-grid Text-                         788

                                     to-SQL research rather than a completed deployment system. The next step is a call- and                        789

                                     budget-matched evaluation on unseen, independently expert-reviewed grid queries, with                          790

                                     pre-registered semantic evidence, tie handling, abstention, and risk–coverage analysis.                        791
