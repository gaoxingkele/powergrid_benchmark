# Excluded prior results

Protocols v0.1 and v0.2 are retained as incident and corrective-history records. They are excluded from R2 primary and exploratory outcome reporting because:

1. candidates originated from an event-focused excerpt asset rather than complete report PDFs;
2. reference text remained in some candidate pools; and
3. the nominal node-deletion counterfactual signal was algebraically identical to weighted degree.

R1 numerical results may be cited only to explain why the benchmark and method were rebuilt. They may not be compared with v0.3 as if they were valid earlier performance estimates.
