# Cover Letter Draft — Applied Sciences

Dear Editors,

Please consider the manuscript “Causal and Counterfactual Graph-Enhanced
Extractive Summarization (C²GES) for Power Grid Maintenance Reports” for
publication in *Applied Sciences*.

We disclose an important title-to-evidence boundary at submission. “For Power
Grid Maintenance Reports” states the intended downstream application, whereas
the evaluated population is a selected public NERC reliability, disturbance,
event-analysis, recommendation, and assessment technical-report proxy corpus.
The manuscript does not claim validated effectiveness, safety, or operator
usefulness on utility maintenance work orders or inspection records.

The contribution is a deterministic, auditable extractive workflow with a
typed textual graph and node-deletion path diagnostic that is mathematically
distinct from weighted degree. The formal ablation is negative: Full C²GES is
below the strict no-counterfactual graph condition by about 0.0033 ROUGE-L at
both sentence budgets, with intervals crossing zero. A completed
post-unblinding development-only search selected zero counterfactual weight in
12/12 leave-one-report-out folds. We report both findings prominently and do
not claim that the counterfactual channel improves lexical overlap.

The corrective evidence package binds the immutable 210-row prediction ledger,
the original freeze and authorization, an exact post-run sign-flip sensitivity,
a rights-safe 40-report inventory, tests, independent audits, incident history,
and figure lineage. Source PDFs and full extracted datasets are not
redistributed. The prediction ledger contains verbatim derived text and may be
provided to editors or reviewers only after the authors confirm the applicable
third-party permissions.

The corresponding-author email, exact funder wording/role, final AI-use
provenance, author declarations, repository release receipt, and file-by-file
rights determination must be completed before this draft is used in the
submission portal.
