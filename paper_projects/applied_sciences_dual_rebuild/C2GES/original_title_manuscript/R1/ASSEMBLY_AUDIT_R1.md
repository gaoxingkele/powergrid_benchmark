## 3. Claim migration and removals

The following original-concept claims are not carried forward as implemented or validated contributions: GridMaint-CausalSum, a trained GNN, T5 generation, BERTSum comparisons, Graph F1, QA accuracy, expert-adjudicated causal labels, a structural causal model, a physical grid simulator, and a demonstrated counterfactual-component gain. Where GNN, T5, or GridMaint are mentioned, the manuscript says that the audited assets do **not** contain them.

The authoritative positive result is limited to the frozen system-level ROUGE-1 contrasts against Lead and TextRank at `K=5`. The strict counterfactual contrast is reported negatively: no supported benefit at `K=5`, and Full is worse than strict no-CF on the exploratory unadjusted ROUGE-1 interval at `K=10`. The manuscript does not infer broad superiority or component effectiveness.

Machine silver role evidence is identified as a development signal, not human/expert gold. Silver-role coverage is treated as internal diagnostic consistency rather than expert causal fidelity.
