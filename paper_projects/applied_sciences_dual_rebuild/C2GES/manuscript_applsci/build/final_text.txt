                                       Abstract                                                                                                        5


                                       Selecting exact supporting sentences is useful only when evaluation prevents document                           6

                                       and label leakage. We evaluate C2 GES, an additive reranker combining query relevance,                          7

                                       role compatibility, and local positional smoothing, on a document-grouped FEVER con-                            8

                                       version with 8000 training, 1500 development, and 1500 test instances. Across one pilot                         9

                                       seed and four prospectively specified continuation seeds, predicted-label F1 was 0.4920                        10

                                       at the primary evidence budget, compared with 0.4864 for BM25. Neither the predefined                          11

                                       role-effect criterion nor the criterion for superiority across budgets was met. Additional                     12

                                       comparisons included nine existing scoring modes, two retrained structural ablations, and                      13

                                       two frozen zero-shot cross-encoders. At label-blind K = 3, the MiniLM cross-encoder                            14

                                       difference was +0.0102, with Holm-adjusted p = 0.1877. In a separate three-comparison                          15

                                       family, BGE differed from full C2 GES by −0.0021 and no contrast survived Holm ad-                             16

                                       justment. A crossed 5 × 5 upstream–downstream seed analysis yielded mean F1 0.4906;                            17

                                       upstream marginal means varied less than downstream marginal means. The study con-                             18

                                       tributes a leakage-controlled evaluation design, a traceable evidence-selection protocol,                      19

                                       and a bounded resource analysis for future power-grid reliability-report review. Public                        20

                                       power-grid reports define only the application setting because their candidate annotations                     21

                                       have not been independently adjudicated by domain experts.                                                     22

                                     2. Related Work                                                                                        74


                                     2.1. Evidence Retrieval and Query-Focused Ranking                                                      75


                                          FEVER evaluates both claim verification and retrieval of supporting evidence [1].                 76

                                     Evidence retrieval and query-focused summarization share a ranking structure, although                 77

                                     the present study supplies the candidate document and does not evaluate open-corpus page               78

                                     retrieval. Matching-based extractive models, iterative ranking, and distance-augmented                 79




                                                                                                      https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 5, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                                3 of 24




                                     sentence graphs motivate transparent sentence scoring [4,9,10]. These studies also show            80

                                     why ranking quality should be reported at multiple output budgets.                                 81

                                           Multi-stage retrieval work emphasizes that evidence recovery can fail even when a            82

                                     downstream verifier is capable, because later reasoning cannot recover a sentence that was         83

                                     never selected [11,12]. The present task is deliberately narrower: the source document             84

                                     is supplied and the output is a set of sentence identifiers. This boundary allows close            85

                                     inspection of reranking behavior but excludes page retrieval, multi-document aggregation,          86

                                     claim-veracity accuracy, and answer generation. Accordingly, “end-to-end” in this arti-            87

                                     cle refers only to the predicted-role-plus-sentence-selection workflow within a supplied           88

                                     document.                                                                                          89

                                           Query-focused benchmarks show that the same document may require different                   90

                                     extracts under different information needs [2,3]. That observation motivates conditioning          91

                                     signals but does not determine how they should be encoded or whether a coarse binary               92

                                     label adds information beyond the query text. The comparison between predicted-label               93

                                     and label-blind protocols is therefore the appropriate empirical test of the role hypothesis,      94

                                     while the oracle is only a diagnostic of what could happen if veracity were known.                 95



                                     2.2. Semantic and Lexical Ranking                                                                  96


                                          BM25 remains a strong lexical baseline grounded in probabilistic relevance modeling           97

                                     [13]. Dense sentence embeddings provide a complementary semantic signal; Sentence-                 98

                                     BERT supports efficient cosine-based sentence comparison [14]. C2 GES combines semantic            99

                                     and lexical query relevance but keeps the role and local channels separately inspectable.          100

                                     This differs from claiming that each channel supplies a statistically supported gain.              101

                                          Graph-based extractive systems model sentence relations, topics, discourse, or mul-           102

                                     tiplex connections [15–17]. Those models motivate relational information but are not               103

                                     equivalent to the positional smoothing used here. The C2 GES local channel does not infer          104

                                     event direction, a physical propagation graph, or causal intervention. Calling it a local          105

                                     chain identifies a proximity prior over document order, and its output must be interpreted         106

                                     at that level.                                                                                     107

                                          Recent neural and language-model rerankers explore pairwise, setwise, and listwise            108

                                     candidate comparison [18–20]. They expand the potential accuracy–cost frontier, but often          109

                                     add model, endpoint, and prompt dependencies. Our focus is a lightweight decomposable              110

                                     score and an evaluation contract that can later host stronger rerankers without changing           111

                                     candidates, splits, metrics, or audit rules. A fair future comparison must record model            112

                                     snapshots and compute and must not silently replace the present document-conditioned               113

                                     task with open retrieval.                                                                          114

                                          The closest verified power-grid NLP precedent in this venue combines semantic-                115

                                     dependency and lexical embeddings with RoBERTa for supervised entity–relation extrac-              116

                                     tion on a purpose-built grid-field corpus [21]. Its output, supervision, and candidate scope       117

                                     differ from ours: it predicts entity relations in domain text, whereas C2 GES ranks supplied-      118

                                     document sentence identifiers and is evaluated quantitatively on human-annotated FEVER             119

                                     evidence. The demonstrated novelty here is therefore the leakage-controlled, hash-bound            120

                                     protocol and decomposable evidence ledger, not superior relation extraction, a supported           121

                                     role-conditioning gain, or validated power-grid performance.                                       122



                                     2.3. Technical-Report Application Boundary                                                         123


                                          Computational analysis of power-grid reports motivates tools that preserve traceable          124

                                     evidence rather than returning unsupported summaries [5,6,22]. The local NERC collection           125

                                     contains public reports and automatically constructed candidate annotations. Because               126

                                     those annotations have not undergone independent domain-expert adjudication, they are              127




                                                                                                  https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 5, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                                   4 of 24




                                     used only to define an application scenario and future annotation protocol; quantitative              128

                                     results in this article come exclusively from human-annotated FEVER evidence.                         129

                                           Power-grid digitalization has created broad opportunities for AI-assisted forecasting,          130

                                     anomaly detection, control, and decision support [5,23,24]. Evidence selection addresses              131

                                     a different layer: it organizes the documentary basis for an engineering review. A useful             132

                                     system should return stable sentence identifiers, preserve the originating report, expose             133

                                     ranking provenance, and avoid presenting model-produced causal labels as expert findings.             134

                                     These requirements favor transparent ledgers and conservative evidence boundaries.                    135

                                           Role information is attractive because trigger, impact, propagation, and mitigation             136

                                     questions can share vocabulary while seeking different passages. Work on causal language              137

                                     and explanation datasets likewise distinguishes causes, consequences, and rationales                  138

                                     [25,26]. Yet a role label can duplicate query cues or leak a target. The three-protocol design        139

                                     addresses this ambiguity directly. A nonzero learned weight is not proof of incremental               140

                                     utility; only the paired predicted-versus-blind contrast can support the deployed role claim.         141

                                     3.2. Role-Provenance Protocols                                                                         185


                                          The oracle-label protocol exposes FEVER veracity to the selector and is a conditional             186

                                     diagnostic, not an end-to-end system. Predicted-label uses an upstream claim-text classifier:          187

                                     training roles are out-of-fold predictions from document-grouped folds, whereas develop-               188

                                     ment and test roles are produced by a classifier fitted only on training data. Label-blind             189

                                     assigns the same unknown role to every item. Apart from role provenance, the proto-                    190

                                     cols share candidates, split, encoder, objective, metric implementation, seeds, and cutoffs            191

                                     (Figure 1).                                                                                            192

                                          The contract isolates two estimands. The system comparison asks how a complete                    193

                                     variant behaves relative to BM25 at a chosen reading budget. The role-effect comparison                194

                                     asks whether changing only role provenance changes ranking quality. Conflating them                    195

                                     would allow a positive system comparison to be attributed to a role head even when the                 196

                                     blind model behaves similarly. Paired protocol rows and shared candidate pools prevent                 197

                                     that attribution error.                                                                                198

                                          For predicted-label training, the upstream classifier never predicts an item from a               199

                                     model fitted on another claim from the same source document. It uses claim text only                   200

                                     and a feature union of a lowercased word TF–IDF channel (1–2 grams) and a char_wb                      201

                                     TF–IDF channel (3–5 grams); both use Unicode accent stripping, sublinear term frequency,               202

                                     min_df=2, and at most 30,000 features. A class-balanced, L2-penalized logistic regression              203

                                     uses liblinear, C = 1.0, an intercept, at most 1000 iterations, and otherwise scikit-learn             204

                                     defaults. Five-fold StratifiedGroupKFold uses document IDs as groups, shuffling with                   205

                                     seed 2026. Fold models use seeds 2026–2030 to create training OOF predictions; a seed-                 206

                                     2026 model fitted to all training rows predicts development and test and is serialized as              207

                                     full_train_model.joblib. OOF fold models were not serialized.                                          208




                                                                                                      https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 5, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                                                                                                                                        6 of 24

                                     The local channel Gij smooths query salience over sentence position. It is a positional                 229

                                     consistency prior, not an inferred causal graph.                                                        230

                                          All query-channel normalizations are computed within the candidate document.                       231

                                     Scores therefore represent relative salience for one claim and should not be compared as                232

                                     calibrated probabilities across documents. A constant channel maps to zero rather than                  233

                                     being divided by a zero range. Semantic and lexical terms are combined before the final                 234

                                     mixture, keeping query relevance as one identifiable source in the downstream trace.                    235

                                          The role head receives frozen sentence and claim representations plus a learned                    236

                                     embedding of the protocol-specific role. A shallow nonlinear transformation returns a                   237

                                     bounded candidate score. Freezing the encoder reduces compute and makes across-seed                     238

                                     changes easier to attribute to the head and mixture, but it also limits task adaptation. It is a        239

                                     reproducibility choice for this study, not a claim of optimality.                                       240

                                          The local channel averages nearby query salience with distance-decaying weights                    241

                                     and then normalizes within the document. It can promote a sentence near other relevant                  242

                                     sentences, reflecting the common writing pattern in which context and evidence occupy a                 243

                                     short neighborhood. It cannot establish event direction or physical propagation. Candidate              244

                                     traces therefore label the value as positional consistency rather than causal structure.                245

                                     3.4. Five-Seed Statistical Protocol                                                                               278


                                          The first seed (2026) was completed and inspected during pipeline development.                               279

                                     Before seeds 2027–2030 were run, the endpoint, protocols, evidence budgets, aggregation                           280

                                           Table 8 and Figure 6 directly test whether the role source changes evidence ranking.                     448

                                     At the primary budget, predicted-label minus label-blind is +0.0010. The seed-level interval                   449

                                     is [-0.0016, 0.0036] and the hierarchical interval is [-0.0012, 0.0031]; both include zero. Every              450

                                     predefined role contrast at the primary budget likewise crosses zero. Role conditioning is                     451

                                     therefore a tested mechanism without a supported primary effect in this experiment.                            452

                                           The conclusion is based on uncertainty as well as magnitude. The positive mean                           453

                                     for predicted minus blind is too small to distinguish from zero under either seed-level or                     454

                                     hierarchical inference. Oracle comparisons also fail the same primary gate. Because the                        455

                                     oracle has access to strictly more privileged information, its failure to separate reliably from               456

                                     blind strengthens the scoped interpretation that binary veracity is not a decisive ranking                     457

                                     input in this document-conditioned setup.                                                                      458

                                           Other cutoffs do not reverse the predefined conclusion. The direction changes for some                   459

                                     contrasts and the hierarchical intervals include zero. Highlighting the most favorable ob-                     460

                                     served cell would therefore be a post hoc selection over a family of correlated comparisons.                   461

                                     We retain the full contrast matrix and report every role criterion explicitly. The negative                    462

                                     finding also guards against reading the role mixture weight as proof: a constrained model                      463

                                     can allocate weight to a channel even when replacing its input with a constant yields no                       464

                                     stable loss in test F1.                                                                                        465

                                     4.6. Qualitative Power-Grid Workflow                                                                             513


                                           The NERC material is used to specify how the method would enter a reliability-report                       514

                                     workflow. A reviewer begins with a question scoped to a role such as initiating event, root                      515

                                     cause, propagation or response, impact, or mitigation. The document parser produces stable                       516

                                     sentence identifiers; the selector then returns a short packet with component scores and                         517

                                     role provenance. A reviewer can accept, reject, or supplement the packet while retaining a                       518

                                     link to the official source report.                                                                              519

                                           This workflow differs from autonomous causal diagnosis. C2 GES does not infer grid                         520

                                     topology, reconstruct a disturbance sequence from measurements, or establish that one                            521

                                     event caused another. It ranks textual candidates. A high local-chain score indicates                            522

                                     proximity to other relevant text, and a high role score indicates model compatibility with an                    523

                                     input label. Neither is a substitute for engineering judgment or corroborating operational                       524

                                     data.                                                                                                            525

                                           The current candidate questions and evidence identifiers are valuable for refining                         526

                                     instructions and interfaces, but they remain unadjudicated because they were generated                           527

                                     and checked automatically. They can reveal parsing problems, broken identifiers, or unclear                      528

                                     role definitions and can support illustrated traces. They cannot provide a defensible domain                     529

                                     F1 value or establish improvement over a baseline. For this reason the manuscript does not                       530

                                     include a NERC leaderboard, quantitative claim, or selected numerical case result.                               531




                                                                                                               https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 5, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                                20 of 24




                                          A future validation should separate annotation development from sealed testing.                532

                                     Domain experts should first refine role definitions and examples on development reports.            533

                                     Independent annotators should then select evidence without seeing model outputs, dis-               534

                                     agreements should be retained, and adjudication should follow a documented rule. Only               535

                                     after the manual and model are frozen should a sealed report subset be scored. This se-             536

                                     quence keeps model-assisted annotation from becoming circular evidence for the same                 537

                                     model.                                                                                              538



                                     5. Discussion                                                                                       539


                                          Document-grouped splitting, out-of-fold role construction, and explicit oracle labeling        540

                                     make the evaluation auditable. Under that protocol, predicted-label C2 GES shows a small            541

                                     positive comparison with BM25 at moderate evidence budgets, while BM25 remains better               542

                                     at the smallest budget. The result illustrates a budget-dependent benchmark trade-off               543

                                     rather than a general advantage for either method.                                                  544



                                     5.1. Supported Interpretation                                                                       545


                                           The strongest supported contribution is methodological transparency. The study                546

                                     exposes how documents are partitioned, how role labels are obtained, where gold data enter,         547

                                     how uncertainty respects source clustering, and how every table connects to immutable               548

                                     predictions. The resulting evidence does not support a role-driven gain; it supports an             549

                                     auditable reranker whose relative behavior depends on the output budget.                            550

                                           Evidence-selection systems are commonly evaluated under different output budgets              551

                                     because a single returned sentence and a short evidence packet impose different precision–          552

                                     recall trade-offs. In the present benchmark, BM25 is competitive at the smallest budget,            553

                                     whereas the mixed reranker is a candidate for target-domain validation when a larger                554

                                     packet is acceptable. The observed differences are modest and do not establish utility in           555

                                     technical-report review.                                                                            556

                                           Interpretability here has a precise, limited meaning: the final score is decomposable,        557

                                     candidate identifiers are retained, and role provenance is visible. It does not mean that           558

                                     individual components are causal explanations of model behavior, that the role head                 559

                                     corresponds to human reasoning, or that a selected sentence is factually correct outside the        560

                                     FEVER annotation. This distinction is especially important in reliability analysis, where           561

                                     explanatory language can be mistaken for physical causation.                                        562

                                           The role mechanism itself is not supported by the predefined primary contrast.                563

                                     Predicted-label and label-blind results are practically close, and their uncertainty intervals      564

                                     cross zero. This finding rules out statements that the role head is the source of the observed      565

                                     moderate-budget advantage. Plausible explanations include limited information in binary             566

                                     FEVER roles, upstream classification error, and mixture floors that constrain the label-blind       567

                                     and role-aware solutions similarly. These are hypotheses for new experiments, not post hoc          568

                                     conclusions.                                                                                        569

                                           The crossed seed matrix narrows one of these explanations. Across the five upstream           570

                                     fits tested, upstream marginal variation was smaller than downstream marginal variation             571

                                     and the nonnegative method-of-moments estimate for the upstream component was zero.                 572

                                     Interaction/residual variation remained, and the document-composition range was much                573

                                     wider than either seed-marginal range. Thus the result does not rescue the role hypothesis          574

                                     by attributing its failure to one fixed upstream ledger, although it remains conditional on         575

                                     this classifier, corpus, and finite seed set.                                                       576

                                           The oracle protocol provides a sensitivity analysis only. Its proximity to end-to-end         577

                                     protocols suggests that access to the gold veracity label does not create a large measured          578

                                     advantage here, but the result cannot be deployed because veracity is unavailable at                579




                                                                                                   https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 5, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                               21 of 24




                                     selection time. The study also does not establish open-corpus retrieval quality: the correct       580

                                     source document is supplied, and only sentences within it are ranked.                              581



                                     5.2. Implications for Model Development                                                            582


                                           The unsupported role effect indicates that simply increasing the seed count around           583

                                     the same binary role and constrained mixture would not address the underlying ambiguity.           584

                                     More informative tests include a query-only learned selector, a role-head removal that             585

                                     also removes its floor, a no-floor mixture, and role labels aligned with domain information        586

                                     needs rather than FEVER veracity. Each should preserve the document split and paired               587

                                     evaluation so that improvements cannot be attributed to easier candidates.                         588

                                           The two cross-encoder evaluations provide contemporary interaction-reranker ref-             589

                                     erences on identical sentence pools under frozen snapshots, but they do not establish an           590

                                     accuracy–cost frontier. Their runtime boundaries differ from learned-system training, and          591

                                     neither comparison family produced a Holm-adjusted finding. BGE was statistically unre-            592

                                     solved against full C2 GES and BM25 under the prespecified randomization criterion, while          593

                                     its negative point contrast against MiniLM also failed that criterion after adjustment. Addi-      594

                                     tional rerankers and neural listwise methods remain useful replication targets. Reporting          595

                                     only accuracy would be insufficient for a technical-report tool: throughput, peak memory,          596

                                     model size, offline encoding, and trace availability should accompany each comparison.             597

                                           The result also illustrates the value of negative evidence in applied machine learning.      598

                                     A failed component hypothesis prevents an organization from treating a semantically                599

                                     appealing feature as validated. Preserving the negative result, source ledger, and decision        600

                                     criterion makes subsequent changes comparable and reduces the temptation to rename a               601

                                     post hoc pattern as the original contribution.                                                     602



                                     5.3. Limitations and Planned Validation                                                            603


                                          The seed sample is small; all five seeds are reported, including the pilot run that           604

                                     preceded the four-seed prospective continuation. Hierarchical resampling captures seed             605

                                     and document variation but does not cover distribution shift to new corpora. Exact                 606

                                     document disjointness does not exclude redirect aliases or semantic near-duplicates. The           607

                                     local channel is positional smoothing rather than causal structure, and the resource monitor       608

                                     does not measure GPU memory or sub-sampling-interval peaks.                                        609

                                          The power-grid application remains qualitative. The local NERC candidates were                610

                                     generated and checked automatically and do not constitute an independently adjudicated             611

                                     human-gold benchmark. A quantitative domain study requires a frozen manual, multiple               612

                                     independent domain reviewers, recorded disagreements, adjudication, agreement statistics,          613

                                     and a sealed test subset. Until that evidence is available, no NERC performance value can          614

                                     be reported.                                                                                       615

                                          Additional limitations follow from the metric and corpus. FEVER may provide                   616

                                     incomplete evidence sets, and exact sentence-ID F1 does not grade semantic adequacy or             617

                                     the usefulness of a packet to an engineer. The binary SUPPORTS/REFUTES role is much                618

                                     coarser than the causal and operational categories implied by reliability-report review.           619

                                     The benchmark supplies a source document, so page retrieval errors, OCR quality, report            620

                                     segmentation, tables, figures, and multi-document evidence are outside scope.                      621

                                          External validity is also limited by genre. Wikipedia prose is comparatively regular          622

                                     and differs from incident narratives, recommendations, appendices, and tabular event               623

                                     chronologies. A model that is auditable on FEVER may still fail on long reports, scanned           624

                                     PDFs, or domain abbreviations. The title communicates the application goal, while the              625

                                     abstract and data section explicitly identify the quantitative corpus to prevent that goal         626

                                     from being mistaken for completed domain validation.                                               627




                                                                                                  https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 5, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                                        22 of 24




                                          Finally, reproducibility artifacts do not eliminate governance obligations. FEVER                      628

                                     licensing and attribution must accompany any redistributed conversion. Public availability                  629

                                     of a NERC PDF does not automatically authorize redistribution of extracted text, and                        630

                                     automatically constructed annotations must retain their machine provenance. The release                     631

                                     package must therefore separate redistributable code and derived statistics from source                     632

                                     text whose redistribution has not been confirmed.                                                           633



                                     6. Conclusions                                                                                              634


                                           This study evaluates C2 GES under a leakage-controlled three-protocol design. The                     635

                                     five-seed results, comprising one pilot and four prospectively specified continuation seeds,                636

                                     show budget-dependent behavior relative to BM25 but no reliable gain from predicted                         637

                                     role conditioning over the label-blind system. A finite exploratory analysis reports all                    638

                                     nine existing modes, while separately specified experiments retrain true no-floor and true                  639

                                     no-role variants and evaluate frozen MiniLM and BGE cross-encoders. Neither cross-                          640

                                     encoder comparison family produced a Holm-adjusted finding, and the floor-removal                           641

                                     variants do not support a positive claim. A crossed 25-pipeline sensitivity study found                     642

                                     less marginal variation across the five upstream fits than across the five downstream fits,                 643

                                     without changing the role-effect conclusion. The supported contribution is a traceable                      644

                                     evidence-selection method, an explicit role-provenance evaluation, and a bounded resource                   645

                                     analysis for future power-grid reliability-report review. Open-corpus retrieval, additional                 646

                                     modern rerankers, and independently adjudicated power-grid evidence remain prospective                      647

                                     work.                                                                                                       648
