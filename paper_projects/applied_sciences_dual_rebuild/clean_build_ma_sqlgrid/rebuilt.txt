                                       Abstract                                                                                                        6


                                       Natural-language access to power-grid databases requires traceable schema grounding,                            7

                                       database-enforced read-only execution, and explicit handling of missing evidence. We                            8

                                       present MA-SQLGrid, a five-role software architecture coordinated through an append-only                        9

                                       blackboard and deterministic adjudicator. Its implemented boundary combines SQLite                             10

                                       read-only mode, query_only, an authorizer, disabled extensions, resource limits, and                           11

                                       fail-closed named-state coverage. Earlier GridDB and BIRD experiments evaluate frozen                          12

                                       prompting workflows rather than autonomous multi-agent generation. A separate 700-                             13

                                       call prospective study tests presented-value and reference-free selection components. A                        14

                                       no-generation release-v3 re-execution applies three selectors to a shared 5760-attempt col-                    15

                                       lection for eight historical candidates on 180 GridDB questions. Because frozen pre-run                        16

                                       tests accessed same-item gold-derived outcomes, this release is descriptive. Fixed order,                      17

                                       validation-only ranking, and complete three-witness selection obtained 80/180, 100/180,                        18

                                       and 101/180. The two adjudicating selectors tied at the highest score on 130/180 ques-                         19

                                       tions; reversing candidate order produced 117–118/180. Their sole difference, Q039, is a                       20

                                       constructed and semantically ambiguous projection trace. The evidence supports a repro-                        21

                                       ducible architecture with tested read-only execution and bounded-evidence mechanisms,                          22

                                       but not five-role superiority, qualified power-grid semantic validity, deployment safety, or                   23

                                       universal robustness.                                                                                          24

                                           The original MA-SQLGrid concept addresses this problem through five specialized              45

                                     roles. A Query Analyst interprets the request, a Schema Cartographer maps it to the                46

                                     database, an SQL Synthesizer packages candidate queries, a Validation Engine checks safety         47

                                     separate deterministic adjudicator resolves the resulting evidence. This separation makes          49

                                     the trace inspectable: a reviewer can distinguish what the analyst inferred, which schema          50

                                     frozen prompting procedures rather than five interacting agents. These assets can motivate         57

                                     modules, supply baselines, and test replay interfaces, but they cannot be renamed as results       58

                                     of the new coordination core.                                                                      59

                                     transfer, and constructed-state agreement? Third, when generation is held fixed at eight           63

                                     already frozen candidates per question, how do fixed-order, validation-only, and complete          64

                                     metamorphic selectors behave under the same evidence-collection operations? The third              65

                                     question estimates historical-pool offline selection only; schema grounding is retained as         66

                                     trace evidence and no five-role end-to-end, new-generation, dialogue, or deployment effect         67

                                     is estimated.                                                                                      68

                                     2. Related Work                                                                                     89


                                     2.1. Text-to-SQL Evaluation and Schema Generalization                                               90


                                          Text-to-SQL systems map a natural-language question and a database schema to                   91

                                     a query. Spider established a cross-domain setting with previously unseen databases,                92

                                     drawing attention to table selection, joins, grouping, nesting, and ordering [1]. BIRD              93

                                     extends public evaluation toward larger and more content-rich databases [2]. A benchmark            94

                                     result depends on the exact split, evaluator, database engine, execution boundary, and              95

                                     model configuration. We therefore report only the completed BIRD Mini-Dev protocol and              96

                                     make no claim about public datasets that were discussed but not run.                                97

                                          Exact SQL match and execution evaluation answer different questions. String match              98

                                     can penalize equivalent SQL, whereas result equality can reward nonequivalent queries               99

                                     on an insufficiently discriminating database state. A robustness evaluation should retain           100

                                     predicted SQL, record every failure, and specify the state on which equality was observed.          101

                                     MA-SQLGrid uses strict result equality for inherited snapshot analyses and separately re-           102

                                     ports constructed-state agreement and bounded metamorphic invariance. Neither auxiliary             103

                                     endpoint is semantic certification.                                                                 104



                                     2.2. Schema Linking, Context Packages, and Output Contracts                                         105


                                          Schema linking connects question spans to tables, columns, values, and foreign-key             106

                                     relations [3,8,9]. Full-schema context preserves recall but can increase token cost and             107

                                     distraction. Compact context reduces the search space but risks dropping a required join            108

                                     key or predicate. Value examples can disambiguate coded attributes, while question-                 109

                                     derived instructions can identify aggregation, result shape, ordering, or a limit.                  110

                                          The inherited GridDB factorial study isolates two implemented prompt factors: a full-          111

                                     DDL/global-values package versus a compact/domain-grounded package, and absence                     112

                                     versus presence of a composite structural/SQL-operation hint. The compact package is not            113

                                     a pure schema-length manipulation because it also changes value presentation and adds               114

                                     corpus-tailored normalization. The hint is not merely formatting because it can include             115

                                     aggregation, grouping, projection, ordering, and limit guidance. We retain these precise            116

                                     labels instead of assigning broader causal interpretations.                                         117

                                          Constrained generation and execution guidance reduce invalid candidates. PICARD                118

                                     constrains decoding so inadmissible SQL continuations can be rejected during generation             119

                                     [4]. CHESS combines contextual grounding and SQL synthesis components [7]; other                    120

                                     prompt-based methods investigate decomposition and representation choices [5,6,10]. MA-             121

                                     SQLGrid differs at the coordination boundary: its new core accepts externally produced              122

                                     candidates, checks them with deterministic contracts, and records evidence before selection.        123

                                     It does not claim that the current lexical cartographer supersedes learned schema-linking           124

                                     systems.                                                                                            125



                                     2.3. Agentic Decomposition and Auditable Control                                                    126


                                          Agentic decomposition can make intermediate decisions explicit, but naming pipeline            127

                                     stages as agents does not establish a multi-agent benefit. Such evidence requires a com-            128

                                     parison that holds the model, data, decoding configuration, candidate count, and physical           129




                                                                                                   https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 8, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                                       4 of 20




                                     generation budget constant. Otherwise, a multi-candidate system can improve simply                        130

                                     because it receives more samples.                                                                         131

                                          MA-SQLGrid treats an agent as a role with a typed contract rather than assuming                      132

                                     that every role must be a separately hosted model. The Analyst and Cartographer can                       133

                                     be deterministic or model-assisted; the Synthesizer can receive candidates from a frozen                  134

                                     generator; the Validator uses a fixed read-only policy; the Critic consumes named-state                   135

                                     evidence; and the Adjudicator is deterministic. This design allows components to be                       136

                                     replaced without changing the blackboard schema and prevents gold-guided selection                        137

                                     from being hidden behind an opaque debate label.                                                          138



                                     2.4. Power-Grid Data and External Validity                                                                139


                                           Power-system datasets combine topology, equipment attributes, measurements, time                    140

                                     series, costs, and constraints. RTS-GMLC and SimBench provide reproducible engineering                    141

                                     resources that can be transformed into relational structures [11,12]. Their availability does             142

                                     not automatically create a text-to-SQL benchmark: intended projections, units, ordering,                  143

                                     ties, and reference SQL require semantic review. The current RTS-GMLC, SimBench, and                      144

                                     NERC-derived candidates are consequently machine-adjudicated silver resources, not                        145

                                     expert gold.                                                                                              146

                                           DKA-SQL is a direct power-grid text-to-SQL precedent [13]. Its published corpus,                    147

                                     method, models, and evaluation boundary differ from ours. Because no official imple-                      148

                                     mentation was used, the current BIRD protocol is neither a DKA-SQL reproduction nor a                     149

                                     head-to-head comparison.                                                                                  150

                                     3.1. Task and Evidence Boundary                                                                           152


                                         For question xi , read-only database Di , and introspected schema Si , a generator                    153

                                     produces one or more candidates                                                                           154

                                     Each candidate must satisfy a single-statement read-only contract. The accepted leading                   156

                                     form is SELECT or WITH; comments, multiple statements, write operations, schema changes,                  157

                                     attachments, and pragmas are rejected. A candidate is eligible for adjudication only when                 158

                                     it is safe and executable.                                                                                159

                                            The offline evaluator may later compare the selected query with a reference result.                160

                                     3.4. Deterministic Adjudication and Abstention                                                       243


                                          Eligibility always requires safety and successful execution. The default rule assigns           244

                                     40 validation points for safety, 40 for execution, 10 for question-derived aggregate-shape           245

                                     conformity, 5 for question-derived ordering conformity, and up to 5 for lexical value hits. In       246

                                     no-counterfactual mode, ties preserve original candidate order. In required-counterfactual           247

                                     mode, incomplete coverage fails closed before ranking and the registered pass threshold is           248

                                     enforced. If no candidate remains eligible, the controller abstains. These are deterministic         249

                                     recorded engineering rules, but v3 does not establish that their design preceded access to           250

                                     derived outcomes for the 180 items. Weight, pass-threshold, and reversed-order results are           251

                                     therefore descriptive sensitivity analyses.                                                          252

                                     5. Discussion                                                                                           470


                                     5.1. What the Evidence Supports                                                                         471


                                           The combined evidence supports four bounded findings. First, explicit structural                  472

                                     and SQL-operation instructions strongly alter projected-column adherence, but surface                   473

                                     conformity does not establish correct execution. Second, the audited prospective 700-                   474

                                     call component experiment finds that presented value evidence matters for one frozen                    475

                                     Qwen snapshot while showing no effect for Granite; this is component evidence, not a                    476

                                     five-role outcome. Third, candidate selection and prompting are backbone-dependent: the                 477

                                     component selector did not meet its registered efficacy rule, and the descriptively best                478

                                     BIRD method differed between Qwen and Granite. Fourth, on the historical eight-slot                     479

                                     pool, validation ranking changed the observed finite-set score relative to fixed order, but             480

                                     complete witness evidence changed only Q039, and top-score ties on 130/180 items made                   481

                                     results strongly dependent on arbitrary source order.                                                   482

                                           These findings justify separating intent, schema grounding, synthesis, validation, and            483

                                     state evidence. They do not establish that five roles are superior to one call or that newly            484

                                     generated multi-agent candidates would reproduce the offline gain. The architecture’s                   485

                                     demonstrated value is methodological and safety-bounded: typed contracts, database-                     486

                                     enforced eligibility, complete-evidence gates, deterministic selection, abstention, incident            487

                                     retention, and a sealed gold boundary make later comparisons reproducible and falsifiable.              488



                                     5.2. Interpreting the Retrospective Replay                                                              489


                                          The replay asks whether inherited ledgers contain enough distinct executable candi-                490

                                     dates to exercise the coordination interfaces. For 172 of 180 questions, they do. This reduces          491

                                     implementation risk and identifies eight fail-closed cases that require a registered fallback           492

                                     or a new matched generation policy.                                                                     493

                                          The replay cannot estimate a coordination effect. Its pool mixes two backbones                     494

                                     and four prompt packages, so generation condition and diversity are confounded. It                      495

                                     also lacks reference-free counterfactual equivalence evidence. Post hoc gold scoring of                 496

                                     selected outputs would remain exploratory and cannot substitute for a prospective matched               497

                                     comparison.                                                                                             498



                                     5.3. Interpreting the Descriptive Re-Execution                                                          499


                                           The release-v3 study hash-binds the selection view, rules, code, tests, and state inputs          500

                                     and verifies the software fact that each execution path directly loaded raw gold only after             501

                                     all blackboards were sealed. This does not establish outcome blindness: its frozen test suite           502

                                     had accessed gold-derived v2 evaluation rows for the same items before v3. The additive                 503

                                     supersession notice is the first authority for interpreting the immutable release package               504

                                     and invalidates its historical prospective, prespecified, and outcome-unseen labels. The                505




                                                                                                      https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 8, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                               17 of 20




                                     study is therefore a deterministic descriptive re-execution over a historical candidate pool.      506

                                     Equal candidate budget means that each selector received the same eight pre-existing slots;        507

                                     it does not mean equal historical model calls between source conditions, and it does not           508

                                     turn those candidates into contemporaneous agent outputs.                                          509

                                          The small contrast between validation-only and complete-witness selection is a mech-          510

                                     anism trace, not a gain estimate. V3 uses three different query-blind database hashes              511

                                     and exercises an irrelevant-relation witness, an index/storage witness, and a nullable             512

                                     schema-extension witness. These are constructed operator families rather than indepen-             513

                                     dent samples from a deployment population. The full gate changes only Q039 because                 514

                                     M3 exposes wildcard-projection arity while preserving explicit-column projections. The             515

                                     natural-language request itself admits unresolved status and date-boundary interpretations,        516

                                     so the frozen-reference match cannot be promoted to power-grid semantic correctness. An            517

                                     earlier v1 diagnostic used three same-hash insertion files and is retained as an excluded          518

                                     incident; v2 is retained as a provenance-incomplete predecessor. The supersession notice           519

                                     excludes their prospective interpretations and none of these predecessors supports the             520

                                     current scientific claim.                                                                          521

                                          The tie audit quantifies a second limitation: 130/180 questions have multiple highest-        522

                                     scoring candidates for each adjudicating selector, with approximately 5.4 candidates in the        523

                                     average top-tie set. The original-order rule consequently chooses source slots without se-         524

                                     mantic evidence and selects Qwen-origin candidates on 178/180 items. Reporting only the            525

                                     0.5556 default or choosing the 0.6556 reverse-order sensitivity after outcome access would         526

                                     be misleading. A later rule-development study requires development-only calibration,               527

                                     richer reference-free evidence, and a genuinely untouched evaluation resource.                     528



                                     5.4. Power-Grid Use and Reproducibility                                                            529


                                           Database-enforced read-only execution, resource limits, syntactic validity, and result-      530

                                     shape conformity are necessary safeguards, not operational certification. A safely executed        531

                                     query can still return the wrong assets, omit time bounds, misinterpret codes, or mishandle        532

                                     ties. Table/column/function allowlists constrain SQL operations inside this local evaluator;       533

                                     user authorization requires a separately authenticated identity, institutional access policy,      534

                                     and data-owner decision. The additive FINAL raw-cell-byte, budgeted-result-byte, and               535

                                     output-width controls were not used in release v3 and cannot be invoked to reinterpret its         536

                                     results. They bound returned values within one Python/SQLite process, not SQLite’s entire          537

                                     temporary-memory or process-memory footprint. Multi-state witnesses reduce accidental              538

                                     snapshot equality but cannot cover all distributions or schema changes. Generated SQL              539

                                     and returned source rows require authorized human inspection before any maintenance,               540

                                     protection, dispatch, or asset-management decision.                                                541

                                           Negative results are part of the contribution. Zero of nine primary factorial execution      542

                                     tests survives correction. The deterministic component selector meets its efficacy rule for        543

                                     neither snapshot. No registered multi-state effect survives correction. Granite and Qwen do        544

                                     not share the same best BIRD method. Immutable ledgers, direct re-execution, registered            545

                                     test families, portable manifests, and explicit incidents preserve these outcomes rather than      546

                                     collapsing them into a success narrative.                                                          547



                                     6. Limitations and Future Work                                                                     548


                                          The primary domain corpus is one synthetic database with eight tables, 98 rows,               549

                                     and 180 evaluation questions. Its evaluation partition was visible during development.             550

                                     The 70 structural groups are dependence proxies, and 58 are singletons. Results cannot             551

                                     be generalized to production schemas, permissions, missing-data patterns, units, access-           552

                                     control policies, or workloads.                                                                    553




                                                                                                  https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 8, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                                18 of 20




                                           Only two quantized local snapshots were evaluated. Model family, size, quantization,          554

                                     serving software, and serialization may interact. The compact package bundles schema                555

                                     reduction, values, normalization, and serialization; the hint bundles projection, aggregation,      556

                                     grouping, ordering, and limit rules. The inherited factorial experiment identifies package          557

                                     effects rather than contributions of individual internal rules.                                     558

                                           The five-role core and frozen release-v3 executor pass 30 unit/adversarial/regression         559

                                     tests; the separate FINAL executor passes 14 additive tests, including four new BLOB-               560

                                     accounting and trace/boundary methods. The 21-entry freeze manifest is not a test count,            561

                                     and the invalid transient 33/33 run is excluded. The system has not completed a new-                562

                                     generation experiment on an evaluation resource whose outcomes were previously inac-                563

                                     cessible to the study process. Its Analyst and Cartographer are deterministic skeletons,            564

                                     its Synthesizer packages external candidates, and its adjudication weights have not been            565

                                     independently optimized. Release v3 descriptively re-executes selection over historical             566

                                     candidates and previously evaluated items; it does not estimate generation, communica-              567

                                     tion, latency, token, or autonomous-agent effects. Existing formal-v5 agreement labels are          568

                                     gold-relative and intentionally excluded from selection.                                            569

                                           RTS-GMLC, SimBench, and NERC question–SQL assets remain machine-adjudicated                   570

                                     silver data. Qualified review is required for intended projections, units, ordering, tie            571

                                     handling, and result granularity before external accuracy is reported.                              572

                                           Future work should execute a new hash-locked, call-matched comparison of four con-            573

                                     temporaneous generation conditions: a single direct candidate; staged question/schema               574

                                     handoffs with one candidate; a fixed multi-candidate budget with validation and adjudi-             575

                                     cation but no counterfactual evidence; and the same candidate pool with a pre-registered            576

                                     multi-family reference-free state suite. Model snapshot, question order, database state,            577

                                     decoding, candidate count, physical calls, and evaluator must be held constant. The pro-            578

                                     tocol must pre-register execution accuracy, valid SQL, abstention, tokens, latency, failure         579

                                     taxonomy, clustered inference, multiplicity, and incident retention. The candidate-order            580

                                     rule must be chosen on development-only evidence before the untouched evaluation set is             581

                                     opened.                                                                                             582

                                           Further work can replace lexical grounding with a trained schema linker, add a                583

                                     low-confidence full-schema fallback, compare deterministic adjudication with budget-                584

                                     matched voting, and evaluate risk–coverage calibration. Qualified power-system/database             585

                                     reviewers must independently assess domain semantics; LLM or author-assisted triage                 586

                                     cannot be relabeled as independent expert gold. Every change should receive a new                   587

                                     protocol identity rather than modifying a completed run.                                            588



                                     7. Conclusions                                                                                      589


                                           MA-SQLGrid reframes power-grid text-to-SQL as an auditable coordination problem.              590

                                     Five typed software roles separate question analysis, schema mapping, candidate packag-             591

                                     ing, safety and execution validation, and named-state criticism. A deterministic controller         592

                                     records their handoffs, selects only eligible candidates, and abstains when required evi-           593

                                     dence is incomplete. Here, multi-agent denotes this inspectable role decomposition, not             594

                                     demonstrated benefit from five autonomous language-model agents. The frozen executor                595

                                     enforces read-only and resource limits at the SQLite boundary rather than relying on lexical        596

                                     filtering alone; the separate FINAL variant adds tested raw-cell-byte, budgeted-result-             597

                                     byte, width, and function controls that are not retroactive to v3 and are not whole-process         598

                                     memory isolation.                                                                                   599

                                           The inherited experiments provide a substantial but bounded evidence base. The                600

                                     1440-prediction GridDB study shows strong projected-column instruction uptake but                   601

                                     no primary execution effect surviving Holm correction. The 700-call component study                 602




                                                                                                   https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 8, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                                           19 of 20




                                     supports presented values for one Qwen snapshot, not Granite, and does not validate its                        603

                                     selector under the registered rule. The 25,920-row state study finds no corrected factorial                    604

                                     agreement effect. The 5000-call BIRD protocol supplies a non-grid public comparison                            605

                                     whose best method depends on the backbone and whose methods use unequal call counts.                           606

                                     The retrospective replay confirms candidate coverage for 172 of 180 questions without                          607

                                     accuracy claims.                                                                                               608

                                          The descriptive offline re-execution records 80/180 for fixed order, 100/180 for                          609

                                     validation-only, and 101/180 for complete witnesses. Both adjudicating selectors have                          610

                                     highest-score ties on 130/180 questions, average tie multiplicity is approximately 5.4, and                    611

                                     reverse order changes the complete-witness sensitivity result to 117–118/180. The sole                         612

                                     Q039 difference is a constructed, outcome-exposed, semantically ambiguous projection                           613

                                     trace. These facts, the historical mixed-source pool, development-visible GridDB, and                          614

                                     absent qualified semantic review prevent a general multi-agent superiority or power-grid                       615

                                     deployment claim. The original title is retained as the framework identity; robust refers                      616

                                     only to explicitly tested mutation-denial, bounded-execution, complete-evidence, and                           617

                                     three-witness mechanisms, not universal or operational robustness.                                             618
