# MDPI / Applied Sciences Requirements Snapshot

Checked: 2026-08-08 (Asia/Shanghai)

Authoritative sources:

- MDPI Layout Style Guide: <https://www.mdpi.com/authors/layout>
- Applied Sciences journal page and Instructions link:
  <https://www.mdpi.com/journal/applsci>
- MDPI research and publication ethics: <https://www.mdpi.com/ethics>

Round-1 implementation requirements:

1. Front matter contains title, full author list, affiliations, abstract, and
   keywords.  Yang Yong is designated corresponding author; the email remains a
   manual submission field until the authors provide it.
2. Research-article body follows Introduction, Materials and Methods, Results,
   Discussion, and optional Conclusions.  Related Work and data/task subsections
   may be nested where they improve the computational paper's logic.
3. Figures and tables are cited in text, carry captions, and have source
   lineage.  Supplementary objects use the `S` prefix.
4. Back matter includes Supplementary Materials, Author Contributions, Funding,
   Institutional Review Board Statement, Informed Consent Statement, Data
   Availability Statement, Acknowledgments, Conflicts of Interest, and
   References.  Not-applicable ethics statements remain explicit.
5. Author Contributions use CRediT-compatible roles and include “All authors
   have read and agreed to the published version of the manuscript.”
6. Funding records grant number `521300250006`; the funding-agency name must not
   be invented and remains subject to author confirmation.
7. Data/code restrictions are stated even when material cannot be distributed.
   For the current projects: third-party-restricted material is available from
   the corresponding author on reasonable request, subject to permission and
   applicable license, and a local editor/reviewer verification packet is
   prepared.
8. Generative-AI use in text, data, graphics, study design, analysis, or
   interpretation is disclosed with tool/purpose and author responsibility.
9. References are numbered in order of appearance and every factual literature
   claim is supported by a verified source; no bibliography-only orphan entries
   or invented DOI metadata are permitted.
