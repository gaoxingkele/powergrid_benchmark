                                       Abstract                                                                                                        6


                                       The title states an aspirational application to power-grid maintenance reports; the evaluated                   7

                                       population is a selected public NERC technical-report proxy corpus, so maintenance trans-                       8

                                       fer remains untested. We present C2 GES, a deterministic extractive framework combining                         9

                                       centroid relevance, lexical role evidence, a typed textual proxy graph, and node-deletion                      10

                                       path utility; the graph does not identify physical causal effects. Conservative gates retained                 11

                                       27 of 40 complete PDFs: 12 development and 15 test reports, yielding 12,924 non-summary                        12

                                       candidates. In one authorized corrective run, Full C2 GES achieved Executive-Summary                           13

                                       ROUGE-L F1 of 0.1060 at five sentences and 0.1276 at ten. It exceeded Semantic-MMR and                         14

                                       TextRank on this split, but under equal sentence counts Full averaged 103.0/214.5 and                          15

                                       110.7/199.9 more words, respectively; these are not length-controlled advantages. Full was                     16

                                       below the strict no-counterfactual graph ablation by about 0.0033 at both budgets, with                        17

                                       intervals crossing zero. A post-unblinding development-only search of 147 configurations                       18

                                       selected zero counterfactual weight in all 12 leave-one-report-out folds. The path mecha-                      19

                                       nism is therefore an auditable structural diagnostic, not a demonstrated ROUGE-improving                       20

                                       component. ROUGE measures lexical overlap, not quality, safety, or utility.                                    21

                                     2. Related Work                                                                                        60


                                     2.1. Extractive Technical-Document Summarization                                                       61


                                          Extractive methods retain source text and are therefore naturally auditable. Lead,                62

                                     centroid, and graph-ranking methods remain useful comparators because they reveal                      63

                                     whether complex selectors add value over position or centrality [10–12]. TextRank is a                 64

                                     primary graph-ranking reference [13]. Long reports also require diversity control; relevance-          65

                                     only selection can repeat a local event while omitting consequences or mitigation. The                 66

                                     present task is global report summarization: each method receives identical candidates                 67

                                     from one report and returns five or ten sentences in source order.                                     68



                                     2.2. Graphs, Semantic Ranking, and Causal Language                                                     69


                                          Graph summarizers model relations among sentences, entities, topics, or discourse                 70

                                     units [4,14,15]. C2 GES instead uses an interpretable typed sentence graph, trading learned            71

                                     capacity for deterministic construction and auditability. Semantic-MMR supplies a stronger             72

                                     non-graph baseline by combining dense centroid relevance with pairwise diversity.                      73

                                          Causal language requires an explicit boundary. A text-derived directed edge can reflect           74

                                     rhetorical order, lexical overlap, or a heuristic role transition. It is not a validated structural    75

                                     equation or a physical power-system mechanism [6]. We therefore use “causal” for the                   76

                                     intended role vocabulary and “counterfactual” for node-deletion structural sensitivity,                77

                                     while repeatedly identifying both as proxies.                                                          78

                                     2.3. Power-Grid Text Analytics                                                                     79


                                           Language technologies have been considered for grid models, operational records,             80

                                     and infrastructure analysis [16–19]. Supervised entity–relation extraction from a grid-field       81

                                     corpus is related but does not solve report-level summarization [20]. Our study evaluates          82

                                     authentic public technical reports but does not claim validated maintenance-workflow               83

                                     utility.                                                                                           84



                                     3. Materials and Methods                                                                           85


                                     3.1. Task, Scope, and Evidence Class                                                               86


                                          Let report D = {s1 , . . . , sn } contain non-summary candidate sentences. For budget         87

                                     K ∈ {5, 10}, method m returns Ŷm,K ⊆ D, with |Ŷm,K | = min(K, n). The selected sentences          88

                                     are restored to source order. The official Executive Summary is the reference only and is          89

                                     never candidate input.                                                                             90

                                          ROUGE-1, ROUGE-2, and ROUGE-L F1 quantify lexical overlap between the extract                 91

                                     and that Executive Summary [21]. We use ROUGE-L as the registered primary endpoint.                92

                                     In this design, “better ROUGE” means greater Executive-Summary lexical overlap; it is              93

                                     not synonymous with accuracy, factual sufficiency, causal-chain correctness, maintenance           94

                                     quality, safety, or operator usefulness.                                                           95

                                          This is a post-audit corrective evaluation. Earlier versions of the test population were      96

                                     inspected while diagnosing R1, so the v0.3.1 freeze cannot recover outcome-unseen confir-          97

                                     matory status. The formal output is therefore descriptive evidence for a fixed corrective          98

                                     benchmark. A new sealed holdout would be required for confirmation.                                99

                                     3.6. Known Linguistic Failure Modes                                                                  167


                                          No qualified experts supplied role or relation gold labels. Accordingly, Table 1 is             168

                                     a summary of executable behavior rather than validated semantic annotation; the exact                169

                                     lexicon, normalization, transitions, and tests are preserved in the packaged code snapshot,          170

                                     while the public repository remains unsynchronized. Ambiguous lexical ties abstain                   171

                                     mechanically, but an unambiguous wrong cue can still assign a role. Negated statements (for          172

                                     example, a sentence saying that an outage did not occur) can activate an event cue because           173

                                     negation scope is not modeled. Hypothetical, conditional, quoted, or historical events               174

                                     can likewise receive factual-looking roles. A transition may connect lexically compatible            175

                                     sentences within 12 positions even when discourse context does not support the relation.             176

                                     These cases define an error taxonomy for future expert evaluation; they are not counts of            177

                                     verified errors in the present corpus.                                                               178

                                     5. Discussion                                                                                                                                         301


                                     5.1. Supported Interpretation                                                                                                                         302


                                          The rebuild establishes a cleaner experimental object than R1: complete-PDF candi-                                                               303

                                     dates, multi-level summary-leakage checks, a recorded development search, a path-based                                                                304

                                     score distinct from degree, a Semantic-MMR baseline, and report-level paired estimates.                                                               305

                                     Full C2 GES had higher mean Executive-Summary ROUGE-L than Semantic-MMR and                                                                           306

                                     TextRank on this retained split, but used markedly more words at both equal-sentence                                                                  307

                                     budgets. The unregistered exact sign-flip sensitivity retained the ROUGE directions after                                                             308

                                     six-value Holm adjustment under a stated sign-exchangeability assumption; it does not                                                                 309

                                     control output length or turn the corrective split into confirmatory evidence.                                                                        310




                                                                                                                                             https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 5, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                                 12 of 15




                                           System-level baseline differences should not be attributed to the named counterfactual         311

                                     mechanism. Strict no-CF produced the highest observed ROUGE-1, ROUGE-2, and ROUGE-                   312

                                     L at both budgets, while the Full-minus-no-CF intervals crossed zero. The proper conclusion          313

                                     is that path deletion is an identifiable structural feature whose incremental lexical-overlap        314

                                     value was not demonstrated.                                                                          315



                                     5.2. Causal and Counterfactual Boundaries                                                            316


                                          The graph organizes text into role-conditioned paths. Direction and path score are              317

                                     reproducible hypotheses, not validated relations in grid physics. Node deletion measures             318

                                     the dependence of graph utility on one sentence; it is not a potential-outcome estimand, a           319

                                     do-intervention on grid state, or evidence that an event would change if a sentence were             320

                                     absent. The title must therefore be read together with these boundaries.                             321



                                     5.3. Maintenance-Workflow Transfer                                                                   322


                                          NERC reliability and disturbance reports can inform maintenance-oriented lessons-               323

                                     learned review, but work orders and inspection records differ in vocabulary, length, asset           324

                                     granularity, narrative structure, and reference-summary availability. Current evidence               325

                                     measures Executive-Summary lexical overlap on a selected NERC technical-report proxy                 326

                                     corpus and motivates, but does not validate, a maintenance-review use case. It does not              327

                                     establish effectiveness for operational maintenance reports.                                         328



                                     5.4. Limitations                                                                                     329


                                           The test set contains only 15 selected public reports from one organization and de-            330

                                     rives from a population inspected during corrective reconstruction. Official Executive               331

                                     Summaries are long references, and ROUGE/redundancy do not measure factual suffi-                    332

                                     ciency, engineering usefulness, unsafe omission, or causal-chain correctness. Equal sentence         333

                                     counts yielded unequal word budgets, and fused table, heading, footnote, or line-break               334

                                     blocks occurred among extraction units; therefore current baseline advantages are not                335

                                     length controlled. Semantic-MMR used a fixed coefficient of 0.5 whereas C2 GES received a            336

                                     144-configuration development search, so comparator tuning budgets were asymmetric.                  337

                                     Role and edge construction lack blinded qualified-expert validation, and the ambigu-                 338

                                     ity/negation/hypothetical taxonomy has no expert-gold error counts. Third-party rights               339

                                     restrict redistribution of PDFs and verbatim extracted text. The public repository has not           340

                                     yet been asserted to match the exact final-candidate package.                                        341

                                           Post hoc tuning after seeing these results cannot rehabilitate the current test as confir-     342

                                     matory. The completed 147-configuration development-only calibration selected zero CF                343

                                     in all 12 folds and did not support its best nonzero setting. It did not access test input or        344

                                     formal output files, does not replace v0.3.1, and must not be evaluated on the revealed 15           345

                                     reports. Any redesigned integration requires a new, genuinely unseen holdout.                        346



                                     5.5. Future Validation                                                                               347


                                          Future work should freeze a license-cleared corpus of work orders or maintenance                348

                                     narratives at report or site level and create an untouched external holdout. Quali-                  349

                                     fied power-grid personnel should independently score source faithfulness, coverage of                350

                                     cause/event/impact/mitigation, engineering usefulness, and unsafe omissions; disagree-               351

                                     ment and adjudication should be retained, and inter-rater agreement reported. LLM                    352

                                     judgments may support annotation workflow experiments but must not be labeled expert                 353

                                     validation.                                                                                          354

                                     6. Conclusions                                                                                               355


                                           C2 GES is rebuilt as a deterministic, auditable extractive method evaluated on full                    356

                                     NERC technical reports. The exact title names an aspirational maintenance application,                       357

                                     not a validated population. The typed path-deletion score is mathematically distinct                         358

                                     from weighted degree, the benchmark has explicit leakage and extraction audits, and the                      359

                                     comparison includes Semantic-MMR and a strict single-channel ablation. Full achieved                         360

                                     Executive-Summary ROUGE-L F1 of 0.1060 and 0.1276 at five and ten sentences and had                          361

                                     higher values than Semantic-MMR and TextRank on this retained split; however, it averaged                    362

                                     103.0/214.5 and 110.7/199.9 more words, respectively, so no length-controlled superiority                    363

                                     is claimed. Full was lower than strict no-CF by approximately 0.0033 at both budgets, with                   364

                                     intervals crossing zero, and post-unblinding development calibration selected zero CF in                     365

                                     12/12 folds. The study therefore supports an auditable structural diagnostic and bounded                     366

                                     lexical-overlap comparisons, but not a counterfactual-component gain, real-world causal                      367

                                     identification, summary safety, or maintenance-work-order effectiveness. The extracts                        368

                                     are for source navigation, not operational decisions. Confirmation requires a new sealed,                    369

                                     title-concordant holdout and qualified human validation.                                                     370
