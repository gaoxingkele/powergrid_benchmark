## Cross-cutting correction: reviewer-only finding promoted into the canonical v2 analysis

The old `shape_ok` target was condition-dependent. The v2 endpoint is now **successful SQLite execution with the projected-column count equal to the same frozen, project-authored target for each question in every condition**. It does not measure row granularity, ordering, or semantic correctness. Execution outcomes are unchanged for all 1440 rows.

Consequently, the structural-hint main effect changes from **+0.4944 to +0.4083** for Qwen (new 95% template-cluster bootstrap CI **[+0.2115,+0.6122]**) and from **+0.4528 to +0.3556** for Granite (new CI **[+0.1352,+0.5793]**). The common-target context effects are +0.0306 and +0.0444, and interactions are -0.0722 and 0.0000; their intervals cross zero. The common-target Granite-minus-Qwen hint modifier is -0.0528, CI [-0.1707,+0.0784], so the old shape-magnitude/backbone interpretation is withdrawn. Under the corrected inferential framework, McNemar is descriptive only; the registered 70-cluster randomization values are raw **0.020690** and Holm **0.144829**.
