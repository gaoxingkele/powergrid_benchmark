                                       Abstract                                                                                                        6


                                       Natural-language access to power-grid databases requires traceable schema grounding,                            7

                                       database-enforced read-only execution, and explicit handling of missing evidence. This                          8

                                       paper presents MA-SQLGrid, a five-role framework coordinated through an append-only                             9

                                       blackboard and deterministic adjudicator. Its revised executor uses SQLite read-only URI                       10

                                       mode, query_only, a mutation-denying authorizer, disabled extensions, and time, opcode,                        11

                                       row, table, and column limits. Counterfactual evidence is fail-closed: when named states                       12

                                       are required, incomplete coverage makes a candidate ineligible. Earlier GridDB and BIRD                        13

                                       experiments evaluate frozen prompting workflows, not autonomous multi-agent genera-                            14

                                       tion. A new no-generation, prospective-from-freeze offline study applies three selectors to                    15

                                       the same shared precomputed evidence collection for eight historical candidates on each                        16

                                       of 180 GridDB questions. The selection view was built from gold-free frozen prompts; all                       17

                                       boards were sealed before post-selection gold evaluation; 5760 candidate–state attempts,                       18

                                       including 332 failures, were retained; and two reproductions were identical. Fixed-order                       19

                                       accuracy was 80/180 (0.4444). Validation-only ranking achieved 100/180 (0.5556), whereas                       20

                                       complete reference-free metamorphic selection achieved 101/180 (0.5611). The selectors                         21

                                       differed on only Q039: the M3 nullable-schema-extension witness rejected a wildcard pro-                       22

                                       jection and retained an explicit-column projection. Thus the one-item increment is a narrow                    23

                                       projection-stability case, not a general counterfactual or multi-agent gain. Both selectors                    24

                                       covered 180/180 and harmed two first-candidate successes. Reversed tie order yielded                           25

                                       117–118/180 in sensitivity analysis, demonstrating substantial rule dependence. Inherited                      26

                                       BIRD Mini-Dev results remain a non-grid portability comparison with unequal method                             27

                                       call counts. The evidence supports a reproducible safety and coordination architecture                         28

                                     1. Introduction                                                                                    34


                                           Power-grid organizations maintain relational data about assets, locations, topology,         35

                                     sensor readings, work orders, technicians, and maintenance activity. SQL provides precise          36

                                     access to these records, but it requires knowledge of table names, join keys, identifiers,         37

                                     units, aggregation conventions, and local coding rules. Text-to-SQL systems aim to reduce          38

                                     this access barrier by translating a natural-language request into an executable query. In an      39

                                     engineering workflow, however, a fluent query is not sufficient evidence of correctness. A         40

                                     query can execute while selecting the wrong status field, omitting a required filter, using        41

                                     an unintended join path, or returning the expected number of columns with the wrong                42

                                     content.                                                                                           43

                                           Cross-database benchmarks such as Spider and BIRD have made schema generaliza-               44

                                     tion and realistic database content central evaluation concerns [1,2]. Schema-aware models,        45

                                     constrained decoding, context selection, decomposition, and execution guidance address             46

                                     different failure surfaces [3–7]. These methods also expose a design problem: generation           47

                                     steps are frequently bundled together. A system may change schema serialization, value             48

                                     presentation, domain normalization, structural hints, candidate count, and repair policy           49

                                     simultaneously. An observed score difference then cannot be assigned cleanly to schema             50

                                     linking, reasoning, or validation.                                                                 51

                                           The original MA-SQLGrid concept addresses this problem through five specialized              52

                                     roles. A Query Analyst interprets the request, a Schema Cartographer maps it to the                53

                                     database, an SQL Synthesizer packages candidate queries, a Validation Engine checks safety         54

                                     and execution, and a Counterfactual Critic records evidence from named database states. A          55

                                     separate deterministic adjudicator resolves the resulting evidence. This separation makes          56

                                     the trace inspectable: a reviewer can distinguish what the analyst inferred, which schema          57

                                     elements were retained, which candidates were proposed, which candidates executed, and             58

                                     why a final candidate was selected or rejected.                                                    59

                                           The architecture must nevertheless be separated from the evidence used to evaluate           60

                                     it. The inherited GridDB factorial study was not a multi-agent execution. It used one              61

                                     model generation per question and prompt cell, followed by deterministic parsing, read-            62

                                     only validation, execution, and offline scoring. The BIRD study likewise compared four             63

                                     frozen prompting procedures rather than five interacting agents. These assets can motivate         64

                                     modules, supply baselines, and test replay interfaces, but they cannot be renamed as results       65

                                     of the new coordination core.                                                                      66

                                           This distinction leads to three questions. First, can role-specific handoffs be imple-       67

                                     mented with a database-enforced, gold-isolated, fail-closed boundary? Second, what do the          68

                                     completed experiments establish about context packages, presented values, public-database          69

                                     transfer, and constructed-state agreement? Third, when generation is held fixed at eight           70

                                     already frozen candidates per question, how do fixed-order, validation-only, and complete          71

                                     metamorphic selectors behave under the same evidence-collection operations? The third              72

                                     question estimates historical-pool offline selection only; schema grounding is retained as         73

                                     trace evidence and no five-role end-to-end, new-generation, dialogue, or deployment effect         74

                                     is estimated.                                                                                      75

                                           The paper makes four contributions. First, it defines a testable five-role coordination      76

                                     core, a database-enforced SQLite executor, and a deterministic adjudicator whose required          77

                                     counterfactual coverage fails closed. Second, it conservatively integrates a 1440-prediction       78

                                     GridDB factorial experiment, a 700-call component study, a 25,920-row multi-state ledger,          79

                                     and a 5000-call BIRD comparison while retaining their original identities and evidence             80

                                     classes. Third, it reports a hash-frozen offline selection study over the existing eight-slot      81

                                     pool using three query-blind metamorphic witnesses with distinct database hashes; all three        82

                                     methods choose only after the same shared 32-attempt evidence collection per question is           83




                                                                                                  https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 5, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                                 3 of 18




                                     complete. Fourth, it reports negative and sensitivity evidence: the full condition changes          84

                                     one Q039 wildcard-to-explicit-column decision relative to validation-only, while tie order          85

                                     materially changes the result.                                                                      86

                                         No Spider or WikiSQL experiment is reported, no unsupported large power-grid                    87

                                     question–SQL corpus is asserted, and the new offline result is not relabeled as autonomous          88

                                     multi-agent generation. Qualified domain review and a matched new-generation experi-                89

                                     ment remain future gates.                                                                           90



                                     2. Related Work                                                                                     91


                                     2.1. Text-to-SQL Evaluation and Schema Generalization                                               92


                                          Text-to-SQL systems map a natural-language question and a database schema to                   93

                                     a query. Spider established a cross-domain setting with previously unseen databases,                94

                                     drawing attention to table selection, joins, grouping, nesting, and ordering [1]. BIRD              95

                                     extends public evaluation toward larger and more content-rich databases [2]. A benchmark            96

                                     result depends on the exact split, evaluator, database engine, execution boundary, and              97

                                     model configuration. We therefore report only the completed BIRD Mini-Dev protocol and              98

                                     make no claim about public datasets that were discussed but not run.                                99

                                          Exact SQL match and execution evaluation answer different questions. String match              100

                                     can penalize equivalent SQL, whereas result equality can reward nonequivalent queries               101

                                     on an insufficiently discriminating database state. A robustness evaluation should retain           102

                                     predicted SQL, record every failure, and specify the state on which equality was observed.          103

                                     MA-SQLGrid uses strict result equality for inherited snapshot analyses and separately re-           104

                                     ports constructed-state agreement and bounded metamorphic invariance. Neither auxiliary             105

                                     endpoint is semantic certification.                                                                 106



                                     2.2. Schema Linking, Context Packages, and Output Contracts                                         107


                                          Schema linking connects question spans to tables, columns, values, and foreign-key             108

                                     relations [3,8,9]. Full-schema context preserves recall but can increase token cost and             109

                                     distraction. Compact context reduces the search space but risks dropping a required join            110

                                     key or predicate. Value examples can disambiguate coded attributes, while question-                 111

                                     derived instructions can identify aggregation, result shape, ordering, or a limit.                  112

                                          The inherited GridDB factorial study isolates two implemented prompt factors: a full-          113

                                     DDL/global-values package versus a compact/domain-grounded package, and absence                     114

                                     versus presence of a composite structural/SQL-operation hint. The compact package is not            115

                                     a pure schema-length manipulation because it also changes value presentation and adds               116

                                     corpus-tailored normalization. The hint is not merely formatting because it can include             117

                                     aggregation, grouping, projection, ordering, and limit guidance. We retain these precise            118

                                     labels instead of assigning broader causal interpretations.                                         119

                                          Constrained generation and execution guidance reduce invalid candidates. PICARD                120

                                     constrains decoding so inadmissible SQL continuations can be rejected during generation             121

                                     [4]. CHESS combines contextual grounding and SQL synthesis components [7]; other                    122

                                     prompt-based methods investigate decomposition and representation choices [5,6,10]. MA-             123

                                     SQLGrid differs at the coordination boundary: its new core accepts externally produced              124

                                     candidates, checks them with deterministic contracts, and records evidence before selection.        125

                                     It does not claim that the current lexical cartographer supersedes learned schema-linking           126

                                     systems.                                                                                            127



                                     2.3. Agentic Decomposition and Auditable Control                                                    128


                                          Agentic decomposition can make intermediate decisions explicit, but naming pipeline            129

                                     stages as agents does not establish a multi-agent benefit. Such evidence requires a com-            130




                                                                                                   https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 5, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                                       4 of 18




                                     parison that holds the model, data, decoding configuration, candidate count, and physical                 131

                                     generation budget constant. Otherwise, a multi-candidate system can improve simply                        132

                                     because it receives more samples.                                                                         133

                                          MA-SQLGrid treats an agent as a role with a typed contract rather than assuming                      134

                                     that every role must be a separately hosted model. The Analyst and Cartographer can                       135

                                     be deterministic or model-assisted; the Synthesizer can receive candidates from a frozen                  136

                                     generator; the Validator uses a fixed read-only policy; the Critic consumes named-state                   137

                                     evidence; and the Adjudicator is deterministic. This design allows components to be                       138

                                     replaced without changing the blackboard schema and prevents gold-guided selection                        139

                                     from being hidden behind an opaque debate label.                                                          140



                                     2.4. Power-Grid Data and External Validity                                                                141


                                           Power-system datasets combine topology, equipment attributes, measurements, time                    142

                                     series, costs, and constraints. RTS-GMLC and SimBench provide reproducible engineering                    143

                                     resources that can be transformed into relational structures [11,12]. Their availability does             144

                                     not automatically create a text-to-SQL benchmark: intended projections, units, ordering,                  145

                                     ties, and reference SQL require semantic review. The current RTS-GMLC, SimBench, and                      146

                                     NERC-derived candidates are consequently machine-adjudicated silver resources, not                        147

                                     expert gold.                                                                                              148

                                           DKA-SQL is a direct power-grid text-to-SQL precedent [13]. Its published corpus,                    149

                                     method, models, and evaluation boundary differ from ours. Because no official imple-                      150

                                     mentation was used, the current BIRD protocol is neither a DKA-SQL reproduction nor a                     151

                                     head-to-head comparison.                                                                                  152

                                     3.1. Task and Evidence Boundary                                                                           154


                                         For question xi , read-only database Di , and introspected schema Si , a generator                    155

                                     produces one or more candidates                                                                           156

                                     Each candidate must satisfy a single-statement read-only contract. The accepted leading                   158

                                     form is SELECT or WITH; comments, multiple statements, write operations, schema changes,                  159

                                     attachments, and pragmas are rejected. A candidate is eligible for adjudication only when                 160

                                     it is safe and executable.                                                                                161

                                            The offline evaluator may later compare the selected query with a reference result.                162

                                     and any derivative correctness field are forbidden from all five agents and the Adjudicator.              165

                                           The Query Analyst produces a structured question-only intent record containing                204

                                     lexical tokens, detected aggregations, ordering requirements, and an explicit limit when            205

                                     present. It does not select SQL or see correctness. The Schema Cartographer maps lexical            206

                                     intent to tables, columns, and foreign-key edges. The current skeleton uses deterministic           207

                                     overlap and records unmatched tokens; a richer GridDB adapter adds disclosed domain                 208

                                     rules and graph expansion.                                                                          209

                                           The SQL Synthesizer packages externally produced SQL strings. It contains no model            210

                                     client, performs no hidden API call, canonicalizes whitespace and semicolons, removes               211

                                     exact duplicates without changing first occurrence, and assigns stable identifiers. The             212

                                     Validation Engine separates lexical admissibility from the executor trust boundary. The             213

                                     R2 executor opens a frozen database through a URI with mode=ro&immutable=1, enables                 214

                                     SQLite query_only, disables loadable extensions, and installs an authorizer that admits             215

                                     only selection, reads, approved functions, and recursive-query opcodes. Writes, DDL,                216

                                     transactions, attachments, pragmas, metadata enumeration, dangerous file/extension                  217

                                     functions, and unauthorized table or column reads are denied. A progress handler enforces           218

                                     registered time and opcode limits, and result fetching enforces a row cap. Every attempt            219

                                     records database and SQL hashes, authorization or resource failure, elapsed time, row               220

                                     count, and result hash; no automatic retry removes a failure. These controls establish              221

                                     database-enforced read-only and bounded execution for the tested SQLite boundary. They              222

                                     do not establish user entitlement, data minimization, process isolation, or operational             223

                                     authorization.                                                                                      224

                                           The Counterfactual Critic consumes named state results, rejects duplicate state iden-         225

                                     tifiers, and records evaluated, passed, failed, and missing states. When counterfactual             226

                                     evidence is optional, missing evidence remains diagnostic and does not break prior no-              227

                                     counterfactual behavior. When the caller requires counterfactual evidence, coverage must            228

                                     equal the complete registered state set and the pass threshold must be met; otherwise the           229

                                     candidate is ineligible. Thus an incomplete 1/1 record cannot outrank a complete 10/11              230

                                     record. Existing formal-v5 labels compare predictions with gold and remain forbidden                231

                                     during selection.                                                                                   232



                                     3.4. Deterministic Adjudication and Abstention                                                      233


                                           Eligibility always requires safety and successful execution. The default rule assigns         234

                                     40 validation points for safety, 40 for execution, 10 for question-derived aggregate-shape          235

                                     conformity, 5 for question-derived ordering conformity, and up to 5 for lexical value hits. In      236

                                     no-counterfactual mode, ties preserve original candidate order. In required-counterfactual          237

                                     mode, incomplete coverage fails closed before ranking and the registered pass threshold is          238

                                     enforced. If no candidate remains eligible, the controller abstains. The weights and tie order      239

                                     are fixed engineering rules rather than values optimized on the 180 outcomes; the offline           240

                                     study therefore reports prespecified weight, pass-threshold, and reversed-order sensitivity.        241

                                           For this paper, robustness is a vector rather than a universal label (Table 3). Only          242

                                     tested components are reported as evidence.                                                         243

                                     3.6. Component, Multi-State, and Public Protocols                                                    260


                                           The separately frozen component study contains 700 scored calls. E1 compares can-              261

                                     didate generation with and without presented value evidence. E2 requests up to three                 262

                                     candidates and compares a deterministic reference-answer-independent selector with the               263

                                     first candidate. Selection is sealed before gold loading. Rescue, harm, and oracle-at-three          264

                                     are descriptive; oracle-at-three is a gold-only upper-bound diagnostic.                              265

                                           The v5 reliability protocol reproduced all 1440 T0 labels and then executed every              266

                                     prediction over 18 states. The primary 66-question analysis uses a logical-AND endpoint              267

                                     5. Discussion                                                                                                      415


                                     5.1. What the Evidence Supports                                                                                    416


                                          The combined evidence supports four bounded findings. First, explicit structural                              417

                                     and SQL-operation instructions strongly alter projected-column adherence, but surface                              418

                                     conformity does not establish correct execution. Second, presented value evidence can                              419

                                     matter for one frozen model snapshot while showing no effect for another. Third, candidate                         420

                                     selection and prompting are backbone-dependent: the component selector did not meet its                            421

                                     registered efficacy rule, and the descriptively best BIRD method differed between Qwen                             422

                                     and Granite. Fourth, on the historical eight-slot pool, validation ranking improved the                            423

                                     observed finite-set score relative to fixed order, but complete metamorphic evidence added                         424

                                     only 1/180 and tie-order sensitivity was large.                                                                    425




                                                                                                                https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 5, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                                15 of 18




                                          These findings justify separating intent, schema grounding, synthesis, validation, and         426

                                     state evidence. They do not establish that five roles are superior to one call or that newly        427

                                     generated multi-agent candidates would reproduce the offline gain. The architecture’s               428

                                     demonstrated value is methodological and safety-bounded: typed contracts, database-                 429

                                     enforced eligibility, complete-evidence gates, deterministic selection, abstention, incident        430

                                     retention, and a sealed gold boundary make later comparisons reproducible and falsifiable.          431



                                     5.2. Interpreting the Retrospective Replay                                                          432


                                          The replay asks whether inherited ledgers contain enough distinct executable candi-            433

                                     dates to exercise the coordination interfaces. For 172 of 180 questions, they do. This reduces      434

                                     implementation risk and identifies eight fail-closed cases that require a registered fallback       435

                                     or a new matched generation policy.                                                                 436

                                          The replay cannot estimate a coordination effect. Its pool mixes two backbones                 437

                                     and four prompt packages, so generation condition and diversity are confounded. It                  438

                                     also lacks reference-free counterfactual equivalence evidence. Post hoc gold scoring of             439

                                     selected outputs would remain exploratory and cannot substitute for a prospective matched           440

                                     comparison.                                                                                         441



                                     5.3. Interpreting the New Offline Study                                                             442


                                          Unlike the retrospective coverage replay, the R2 study froze the selection view, rules,        443

                                     code, and state inputs before executing the selectors and withheld gold until all blackboards       444

                                     were sealed. It therefore supports a prospective-from-freeze statement about deterministic          445

                                     selection over a historical candidate pool. Equal candidate budget means that each selector         446

                                     received the same eight pre-existing slots; it does not mean equal historical model calls be-       447

                                     tween those source conditions, and it does not turn those candidates into contemporaneous           448

                                     agent outputs.                                                                                      449

                                          The small contrast between validation-only and complete metamorphic selection is               450

                                     scientifically important. V3 uses three different query-blind database hashes and exercises         451

                                     an irrelevant-relation witness, an index/storage witness, and a nullable schema-extension           452

                                     witness. These are three operator families rather than independent samples from a de-               453

                                     ployment population. The full gate changes only Q039, specifically because M3 exposes               454

                                     wildcard-projection arity while preserving explicit-column projections. An earlier v1 diag-         455

                                     nostic used three same-hash insertion files and is retained as an excluded incident; v2 is          456

                                     retained as a provenance-incomplete diagnostic predecessor; none of those predecessor               457

                                     outcomes enter the v3 release claim.                                                                458

                                          The reversed-order sensitivity exposes a second limitation: many candidates share              459

                                     the same coarse safety and intent scores. The deterministic tie rule consequently selects a         460

                                     backbone/prompt slot without semantic evidence. Reporting only the 0.5556 default or                461

                                     choosing the 0.6556 sensitivity after outcome access would be misleading. A later rule-             462

                                     development study requires development-only calibration, richer reference-free evidence,            463

                                     and a genuinely untouched evaluation resource.                                                      464



                                     5.4. Power-Grid Use and Reproducibility                                                             465


                                           Database-enforced read-only execution, resource limits, syntactic validity, and result-       466

                                     shape conformity are necessary safeguards, not operational certification. A safely executed         467

                                     query can still return the wrong assets, omit time bounds, misinterpret codes, or mishandle         468

                                     ties. The executor’s optional table/column policy is a testable authorization hook, not             469

                                     evidence that a utility user’s permissions were configured. Multi-state witnesses reduce            470

                                     accidental snapshot equality but cannot cover all distributions or schema changes. Gen-             471

                                     erated SQL and returned source rows require authorized human inspection before any                  472

                                     maintenance, protection, dispatch, or asset-management decision.                                    473




                                                                                                   https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 5, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                                16 of 18




                                           Negative results are part of the contribution. Zero of nine primary factorial execution       474

                                     tests survives correction. The deterministic component selector meets its efficacy rule for         475

                                     neither snapshot. No registered multi-state effect survives correction. Granite and Qwen do         476

                                     not share the same best BIRD method. Immutable ledgers, direct re-execution, registered             477

                                     test families, portable manifests, and explicit incidents preserve these outcomes rather than       478

                                     collapsing them into a success narrative.                                                           479



                                     6. Limitations and Future Work                                                                      480


                                           The primary domain corpus is one synthetic database with eight tables, 98 rows,               481

                                     and 180 evaluation questions. Its evaluation partition was visible during development.              482

                                     The 70 structural groups are dependence proxies, and 58 are singletons. Results cannot              483

                                     be generalized to production schemas, permissions, missing-data patterns, units, access-            484

                                     control policies, or workloads.                                                                     485

                                           Only two quantized local snapshots were evaluated. Model family, size, quantization,          486

                                     serving software, and serialization may interact. The compact package bundles schema                487

                                     reduction, values, normalization, and serialization; the hint bundles projection, aggregation,      488

                                     grouping, ordering, and limit rules. The inherited factorial experiment identifies package          489

                                     effects rather than contributions of individual internal rules.                                     490

                                           The five-role core and database executor are implemented and pass 20 unit/adversarial         491

                                     tests, but the system has not completed a prospectively frozen new-generation experiment.           492

                                     Its Analyst and Cartographer are deterministic skeletons, its Synthesizer packages external         493

                                     candidates, and its adjudication weights have not been independently optimized. The R2              494

                                     offline study evaluates selection over historical candidates; it does not estimate generation,      495

                                     communication, latency, token, or autonomous-agent effects. Existing formal-v5 agreement            496

                                     labels are gold-relative and intentionally excluded from selection.                                 497

                                           RTS-GMLC, SimBench, and NERC question–SQL assets remain machine-adjudicated                   498

                                     silver data. Qualified review is required for intended projections, units, ordering, tie            499

                                     handling, and result granularity before external accuracy is reported.                              500

                                           Future work should execute a new hash-locked, call-matched comparison of four con-            501

                                     temporaneous generation conditions: a single direct candidate; staged question/schema               502

                                     handoffs with one candidate; a fixed multi-candidate budget with validation and adjudi-             503

                                     cation but no counterfactual evidence; and the same candidate pool with a pre-registered            504

                                     multi-family reference-free state suite. Model snapshot, question order, database state,            505

                                     decoding, candidate count, physical calls, and evaluator must be held constant. The pro-            506

                                     tocol must pre-register execution accuracy, valid SQL, abstention, tokens, latency, failure         507

                                     taxonomy, clustered inference, multiplicity, and incident retention. The candidate-order            508

                                     rule must be chosen on development-only evidence before the untouched evaluation set is             509

                                     opened.                                                                                             510

                                           Further work can replace lexical grounding with a trained schema linker, add a                511

                                     low-confidence full-schema fallback, compare deterministic adjudication with budget-                512

                                     matched voting, and evaluate risk–coverage calibration. Qualified power-system/database             513

                                     reviewers must independently assess domain semantics; LLM or author-assisted triage                 514

                                     cannot be relabeled as independent expert gold. Every change should receive a new                   515

                                     protocol identity rather than modifying a completed run.                                            516



                                     7. Conclusions                                                                                      517


                                          MA-SQLGrid reframes power-grid text-to-SQL as an auditable coordination problem.               518

                                     Five specialist roles separate question analysis, schema mapping, candidate packaging,              519

                                     safety and execution validation, and counterfactual criticism. A deterministic controller           520

                                     records their handoffs, selects only eligible candidates, and abstains when required evidence       521




                                                                                                   https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 5, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                                           17 of 18




                                     is incomplete. The R2 executor enforces read-only and resource limits at the SQLite                            522

                                     boundary rather than relying on lexical filtering alone.                                                       523

                                          The inherited experiments provide a substantial but bounded evidence base. The                            524

                                     1440-prediction GridDB study shows strong projected-column instruction uptake but                              525

                                     no primary execution effect surviving Holm correction. The 700-call component study                            526

                                     supports presented values for one Qwen snapshot, not Granite, and does not validate its                        527

                                     selector under the registered rule. The 25,920-row state study finds no corrected factorial                    528

                                     agreement effect. The 5000-call BIRD protocol supplies a non-grid public comparison                            529

                                     whose best method depends on the backbone and whose methods use unequal call counts.                           530

                                     The retrospective replay confirms candidate coverage for 172 of 180 questions without                          531

                                     accuracy claims.                                                                                               532

                                          The new offline study adds one bounded result: validation-only reached 100/180 and                        533
