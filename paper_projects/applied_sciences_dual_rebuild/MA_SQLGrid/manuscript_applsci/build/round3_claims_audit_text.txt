                                     2. Related Work                                                                                    98


                                     2.1. Text-to-SQL and Cross-Database Evaluation                                                     99


                                           Text-to-SQL research has progressed from template and grammar systems to neu-                100

                                     ral parsers and instruction-tuned language models. Spider established a cross-domain,              101

                                     cross-database setting in which train and test databases differ, forcing models to interpret       102

                                     unfamiliar schemas rather than memorize query templates [1]. Its difficulty also highlighted       103

                                     the need to evaluate structural decisions such as joins, nesting, aggregation, grouping, and       104

                                     ordering. Later benchmarks and surveys catalogued continuing challenges in schema                  105

                                     linking, compositional generalization, ambiguous questions, and realistic database content         106

                                     [3,6,7].                                                                                           107

                                           Execution-based evaluation answers a question that string match cannot: whether              108

                                     the predicted query produces the intended result on a particular database state. Exact             109

                                     SQL match can penalize semantically equivalent queries, whereas execution equality can             110

                                     fail to distinguish nonequivalent queries on an insufficiently discriminating database.            111

                                     Applied work should therefore freeze the execution contract, retain the predicted SQL,             112

                                     and state the database boundary. In this study, strict result equality is primary; projected-      113

                                     column conformity and error categories are separate diagnostics. We do not claim semantic          114

                                     equivalence beyond the frozen database instances.                                                  115

                                           Constrained decoding and execution guidance reduce invalid outputs. PICARD incre-            116

                                     mentally rejects tokens that cannot form admissible SQL [2]. Execution-guided approaches           117

                                     test candidate queries against a database and use failures or results during selection. MA-        118

                                     SQLGrid uses a lighter boundary: the generator is prompted once under a frozen condition,          119

                                     then a deterministic parser and read-only validator classify and execute the output. No            120

                                     gold SQL or gold result is available during generation or candidate selection.                     121

                                           Large language models make prompt context a central design variable. Schema seri-            122

                                     alization, examples, column descriptions, value samples, and explicit output instructions          123

                                     compete for a finite context budget. Empirical studies show that model performance                 124

                                     can depend strongly on representation and demonstration choices [4,5,8]. Yet combined              125

                                     prompt revisions do not reveal causal contribution. Our factorial design estimates two             126

                                     interventions separately and shows that a prompt feature can improve response conformity           127

                                     more reliably than semantic execution.                                                             128

                                     2.2. Schema Linking, Context Selection, and Response Contracts                                         129


                                           Schema linking connects question spans to tables, columns, values, and relationships             130

                                     [9–11]. A full schema preserves all available elements but may introduce distractors, long-            131

                                     range dependencies, and token cost. Compact context aims to retain question-relevant                   132

                                     elements while removing low-relevance structure. This creates a recall–precision trade-off:            133

                                     excluding a needed join key is catastrophic, while retaining unrelated tables may confuse a            134

                                     generator. Deterministic introspection and selection make this trade-off auditable.                    135

                                           Output contracts describe what a response should contain. In text-to-SQL, such                   136

                                     guidance can specify projected fields, row granularity, ordering, aggregation, group-                  137

                                     ing/HAVING, or LIMIT behavior. The intervention tested here is consequently broader                    138

                                     than an answer-format hint and is explicitly corpus-tailored. We record only a common-                 139

                                     target projected-column indicator independently of execution equality. The two endpoints               140

                                     must not be merged: high column-count conformity shows reception of one structural                     141

                                     instruction, not correctness of the returned database content.                                         142

                                           Recent domain-oriented systems combine schema retrieval, value grounding, decom-                 143

                                     position, and repair, including CHESS and the Applied Sciences DKA-SQL study [12,13].                  144

                                     DKA-SQL is a direct power-grid text-to-SQL precedent, so our novelty is not being the                  145

                                     first grid text-to-SQL framework. Its published evidence, workflow, models, and corpora                146

                                     differ materially from ours, and no official implementation or immutable prompt/code                   147

                                     release was located in the Round-1 audit; we therefore make no numerical superiority or                148

                                     reproduction claim. A 2 × 2 design estimates the bundled context-package effect, composite-            149

                                     hint effect, and difference-in-differences interaction. Repeating the same design for two              150

                                     backbones is a sensitivity analysis, not a general model-family study.                                 151



                                     2.3. Power-Grid Databases and AI-Assisted Access                                                       152


                                           Power-system datasets mix network topology, asset attributes, time series, costs, con-           153

                                     straints, maintenance records, and event narratives. RTS-GMLC provides a reproducible                  154

                                     test-system resource with buses, branches, generators, loads, renewable profiles, reserves,            155

                                     and dispatch-related data [14]. SimBench supplies benchmark networks for multiple volt-                156

                                     age levels and scenarios [15]. Both resources can be transformed into relational databases             157

                                     that exercise selections, joins, aggregation, ranking, and topology-oriented questions.                158

                                           These public engineering resources are attractive for external validation, but database          159

                                     availability does not create a text-to-SQL benchmark automatically. Natural-language                   160

                                     questions and reference SQL must be semantically reviewed, units and tie handling must                 161

                                     be specified, and ambiguous projections must be resolved. Automatically generated pairs                162

                                     are useful for building and testing the pipeline, yet their agreement with a model output is           163

                                     not human-gold accuracy. The present paper treats the external resources accordingly.                  164

                                           Recent work applies language models to grid-domain information extraction, includ-               165

                                     ing a semantic-dependency and lexical-embedding RoBERTa design in this journal [16].                   166

                                     Relational-provenance research in the same venue further motivates preserving query and                167

                                     source lineage [17]. The central risk is that fluent output can be mistaken for validated              168

                                     engineering reasoning. Text-to-SQL offers a comparatively inspectable interface because                169

                                     the generated query and returned rows can be reviewed. That advantage depends on a                     170

                                     read-only execution policy, stable database provenance, and conservative claims about                  171

                                     what the execution demonstrates.                                                                       172

                                     template. The two executed packages are                                                                            180



                                                 Ci,0,h = Pfull (Si , V ) ⊕ hH ( xi ),       Ci,1,h = Pcompact ( xi , Si , V ) ⊕ hH ( xi ),      (2)    181

                                     isolated schema-length intervention. H is a GridDB-tailored composite structural/SQL-                              185

                                                                                 ybi,c,h = R(yei,c,h , Di ).                                     (3)    188

                                     opened read-only. It never sees yi⋆ or its result while selecting a candidate.                                     191

                                          The experiment estimates prompt intervention effects, not a sealed generalization score.                      192

                                     3.3. Prompt Packages and Factor Audit                                                               207


                                            The full package is constant across questions apart from the question text: all eight-       208

                                     table DDL and a sorted global dictionary drawn from permitted non-ID categorical, date,             209

                                     and text columns. The compact package first matches permitted values, applies frozen                210

                                     corpus-tailored phrase and normalization rules, selects seed tables/columns, expands                211

                                     foreign-key connections to at most six tables, and serializes joins, matched values, and            212

                                     predicate hints. If selection omits a needed element, the model cannot recover it from gold         213

                                     metadata.                                                                                           214

                                            The composite hint can specify the expected projected-column count, row granularity,         215

                                     ordering need, COUNT/AVG/SUM/MIN/MAX, GROUP BY/HAVING, exact projection fields, de-                 216

                                     scending order, or LIMIT. Its serialization also differs between full and compact packages.         217

                                     The estimand is therefore the effect of this frozen composite package as actually delivered;        218

                                     it is not the effect of a generic answer-format sentence.                                           219

                                            For the representative question “How many alarmed readings does each asset                   220

                                     have?”, F00 contains complete DDL and the global dictionary; F01 adds a two-column,                 221

                                     multi-row, ordered COUNT(*) hint. F10 contains six graph-expanded tables, the                       222

                                     sensor_readings.asset_id = assets.asset_id join and the normalization alarm_flag                    223

                                     = 1; F11 adds the corresponding two-column, multi-row, ordering and count instructions.             224

                                     Across all 180 questions, question ID, database, common safety instruction, model settings,         225

                                     one-call policy, parser, validator, and offline evaluator are invariant. Prompt-field auditing      226

                                           After the factorial analysis and review exposed the bundled nature of the prompt              385

                                     execution, and presented-value coverage; it seals its choice before gold SQL or results             396

                                     selection-change, and oracle-at-three counts are descriptive, and oracle-at-three is never a        398

                                     5. Discussion                                                                                                                                                                     560


                                     5.1. What the Evidence Supports                                                                                                                                                   561


                                          The clearest factorial result is the composite hint on the common-target projected-                                                                                          562

                                     column manipulation check. Both backbones show increases larger than 0.35 and survive                                                                                             563

                                     the separate secondary family. Explicit structural and SQL-operation instructions therefore                                                                                       564

                                     change projected-column adherence in this bounded experiment. Because the hint also                                                                                               565

                                     contains aggregation, grouping, projection, ordering, and limit rules, this result cannot be                                                                                      566

                                     reduced to generic formatting advice or promoted as independent semantic validation.                                                                                              567




                                                                                                                                                                      https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 5, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                               20 of 24




                                           Factorial execution is more conditional. All nine primary tests fail Holm correction,        568

                                     even where composition-sensitivity intervals exclude zero. This divergence is not contradic-       569

                                     tory: randomization tests implement the predeclared finite-set promotion rule, whereas the         570

                                     intervals show how estimates change when the empirical group composition is reweighted.            571

                                     Neither is evidence from a random sample of databases or questions.                                572

                                           The multi-state stress test strengthens the negative conclusion. Requiring one archived      573

                                     SQL program to agree with the gold query across 15 constructed states reduces every                574

                                     cell’s success count relative to or equal to its T0 count, and no factorial suite effect is        575

                                     promoted. This demonstrates that a single frozen snapshot can overstate reliability for            576

                                     some predictions. It does not prove full semantic equivalence: the states are deterministic        577

                                     constructions from one small database, the automatic subset covers only 12 normalized-SQL          578

                                     clusters, and all order-sensitive questions remain outside the primary suite.                      579

                                           The compact/domain-grounded package does not show a uniformly positive indepen-              580

                                     dent effect. For Qwen its execution main effect is negative with an upper interval endpoint        581

                                     at zero; for Granite it is small and uncertain. The package is substantially shorter, but          582

                                     it simultaneously changes schema coverage, value presentation, join serialization, and             583

                                     normalization. It is therefore neither a pure schema-selection ablation nor evidence that          584

                                     shorter prompts improve accuracy.                                                                  585

                                           The prospective component results sharpen but do not universalize the architecture.          586

                                     Presented value evidence passes the efficacy rule for Qwen only; its zero Granite estimate         587

                                     and non-promoted modifier preclude a replicated or universal value-grounding claim. The            588

                                     deterministic selector yields rescues for both backbones but does not pass either adjusted         589

                                     test. Deployment teams should therefore validate the complete model–prompt–database                590

                                     combination under execution rather than assume that a stage label guarantees benefit.              591



                                     5.2. Projected Columns versus Semantic Correctness                                                 592


                                           The gap between the common-target diagnostic and execution equality is a warning             593

                                     against surface-only evaluation. In Qwen F01, projected-column conformity is 0.9667 while          594

                                     execution equality is 0.7167. In Granite F01 the corresponding values are 0.8778 and 0.5556.       595

                                     A model can return the requested number of columns yet select the wrong table, field, filter,      596

                                     join, or aggregation.                                                                              597

                                           The projected-column diagnostic remains useful because it localizes one interface            598

                                     failure and shows that an intervention is received. It should be reported next to, not             599

                                     substituted for, execution. Row granularity and ordering were present in prompts but are           600

                                     not validated by this endpoint. Where a database state is insufficiently discriminating,           601

                                     manual semantic review or test-database augmentation is also needed.                               602



                                     5.3. Implications for Power-Grid Data Access                                                       603


                                          An operational interface should expose the generated SQL, database snapshot, schema           604

                                     context, and execution status. Read-only enforcement prevents accidental mutation but              605

                                     does not prevent a semantically wrong query from returning plausible rows. High-                   606

                                     consequence decisions therefore require review of both the SQL and the source data.                607

                                          The external diagnostic is useful precisely because it fails visibly. Missing-column          608

                                     errors indicate that the model inferred familiar field names rather than grounding to the          609

                                     actual schema. Prompt repair should target these mismatches, then be evaluated on new or           610

                                     sealed items rather than repeatedly optimizing against the same 91 visible candidates.             611

                                          RTS-GMLC and SimBench broaden the engineering structures represented in the                   612

                                     project: topology, generation, constraints, time series, and network elements. Their inclu-        613

                                     sion does not by itself validate maintenance-database generalization. A credible external          614




                                                                                                  https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 5, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                                21 of 24




                                     benchmark needs domain experts to verify terminology, units, expected projections, accept-          615

                                     able equivalences, and tie rules.                                                                   616



                                     5.4. Reproducibility and Negative Results                                                           617


                                           The quarantined Qwen incident demonstrates why result directories need immutable              618

                                     identity and process accounting. A directory containing more generations than the regis-            619

                                     tered Cartesian design is not repaired by truncating it. The clean rerun provides a separate        620

                                     evidence chain, and the audit confirms that the contaminated output contributed no canon-           621

                                     ical row.                                                                                           622

                                           Hash-bound artifacts reduce transcription and provenance errors. They do not make             623

                                     an exposed test partition sealed, resolve a missing data license, or turn automatic candidates      624

                                     into human labels. Reproducibility and evidential validity are related but distinct.                625

                                           The compact/domain-grounded package NO–GO is a useful result. The stage remains               626

                                     available for future selectors, but the paper does not assign it an independent benefit that        627

                                     the current data do not support. The prospective E2 selector also remains a NO–GO for               628

                                     promoted efficacy despite descriptive rescues. E1 supports presented value evidence for             629

                                     one snapshot only. BIRD model baselines remain unexecuted, and the E4 latency records               630

                                     remain diagnostic rather than comparative-efficiency evidence.                                      631



                                     5.5. Limitations                                                                                    632


                                           The primary corpus is one synthetic 8-table, 98-row maintenance-oriented database             633

                                     and only 180 evaluation questions. Questions map to 70 normalized-SQL dependence-                   634

                                     proxy groups, 58 of them singletons; grouped procedures respect this mapping, but it                635

                                     is not an authoring-template identifier and the corpus cannot represent the diversity of            636

                                     production schemas, permissions, missing values, units, or data quality.                            637

                                           The evaluation partition was visible in earlier development. Gold fields are excluded         638

                                     from prompts and the formal runs are frozen, but prior analyst exposure prevents a sealed-          639

                                     test claim. The two model snapshots use Q4_K_M quantization and one llama.cpp revision.             640

                                     Results may differ for full-precision weights, alternative quantization, other runtimes,            641

                                     stochastic decoding, or hosted models.                                                              642

                                           Only one generation is made per factorial prompt and one formal response per com-             643

                                     ponent condition. Temperature zero and fixed runtime settings improve repeatability, but            644

                                     they do not quantify decoding variance or server nondeterminism. The second backbone                645

                                     is sensitivity analysis, not replication, and no cross-backbone component effect satisfies          646

                                     a replication rule. Multi-run inference would require a prospectively frozen hierarchical           647

                                     analysis and materially greater compute.                                                            648

                                           Execution equality is database-state dependent. Different SQL statements may agree            649

                                     accidentally on the current data, and minor projection differences may be judged wrong              650

                                     under the strict contract. The contract is intentionally fixed, yet no single automatic metric      651

                                     captures every notion of semantic equivalence.                                                      652

                                           The 15-state suite addresses accidental equality only within its constructed witnesses.       653

                                     It was designed retrospectively, does not constitute an independent human semantic                  654

                                     audit, and cannot certify behavior on unconstructed values or schemas. Two blinded                  655

                                     technical reviewers conservatively held all 114 order-sensitive questions; qualified domain         656

                                     reviewers must still resolve intended ordering, ties, units, and result granularity before          657

                                     those questions support semantic claims.                                                            658

                                           E4 records paired telemetry under a frozen order, but the required efficiency attestation     659

                                     is missing. Token deltas remain valid descriptive outputs; latency and throughput are               660

                                     diagnostic, and no energy or memory measurement supports deployment-efficiency claims.              661




                                                                                                   https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 5, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                              22 of 24




                                     A confirmatory efficiency study requires independently verified process isolation, thermal        662

                                     state, common hardware, and separate generation/database timing.                                  663

                                          External candidates have not completed human review. Their automatic references              664

                                     and the Qwen diagnostic are development evidence only. No claim of RTS-GMLC or                    665

                                     SimBench accuracy, cross-database generalization, or operational readiness follows from           666

                                     them.                                                                                             667

                                          GridDB redistribution permission is unresolved, and derived RTS-GMLC and Sim-                668

                                     Bench artifacts have source-specific notice and database-license obligations. A public            669

                                     artifact release must undergo human license review and receive a permanent repository             670

                                     DOI. Author identities, contributions, funding, conflicts, acknowledgments, and AI-use            671

                                     disclosure also require author confirmation.                                                      672



                                     5.6. Future Work                                                                                  673


                                           The immediate external study should complete two independent expert reviews of              674

                                     all candidates, retain disagreements, and adjudicate them under the frozen manual. A              675

                                     subsequent confirmatory set should be newly authored or deeply rewritten after the prompt         676

                                     and schema-repair work is frozen. This preserves a genuinely unexposed test.                      677

                                           The compact/domain-grounded builder should be redesigned around reference-free              678

                                     coverage safeguards. Candidate columns and relationships could be accompanied by                  679

                                     explicit connectivity checks and a fallback to the full package when join paths appear            680

                                     incomplete. The observed offline table/column recall cannot be used online because it             681

                                     depends on gold-required fields. Any replacement safeguard constitutes a new intervention         682

                                     and must be evaluated prospectively.                                                              683

                                           The completed E2 replay should be followed only by a newly frozen selector revision;        684

                                     tuning against its visible rescues would make the present set development data. Efficiency        685

                                     confirmation requires controlled warm and cold runs, common hardware, token accounting,           686

                                     resident and accelerator memory, energy where possible, and separate model-generation             687

                                     and SQL-execution latency.                                                                        688

                                           After explicit human launch authorization, the technically frozen BIRD Mini-Dev pro-        689

                                     tocol can add transparent direct, decomposition, schema-selection, and bounded execution-         690

                                     feedback baselines on the same two snapshots. Its 500/500 SQLite 3.40.1 gold-query                691

                                     preflight is a mechanical gate, not model evidence. It must not be described as DKASQL            692

                                     reproduction, because no official implementation was found. Additional backbones, sizes,          693

                                     quantizations, decoding settings, and repeated runs would test whether the finite-set             694

                                     execution contrasts persist.                                                                      695



                                     6. Conclusions                                                                                    696


                                          MA-SQLGrid provides an auditable case study of context grounding for power-grid              697

                                     text-to-SQL. Across two independently audited local backbones, a GridDB-tailored com-             698

                                     posite structural/SQL-operation hint improves a common-target projected-column ma-                699

                                     nipulation check, but none of nine primary factorial execution tests survives family-wise         700

                                     correction. The compact/domain-grounded package does not show an independent posi-                701

                                     tive execution effect. In the separate 700-call component study, presented value evidence         702

                                     improves Qwen execution under the frozen rule but not Granite execution; deterministic            703

                                     candidate selection is not promoted for either snapshot. A retrospective 15-state logical-        704

                                     AND stress test likewise promotes none of nine factorial reliability effects, while exposing      705

                                     lower agreement than the original snapshot in every cell.                                         706

                                          The practical conclusion is not that one prompt recipe or component universally wins.        707

                                     Model, schema/value context, handcrafted normalization, operation guidance, and can-              708

                                     didate selection form a coupled system that must be evaluated with database execution.            709




                                                                                                 https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 5, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                                    23 of 24




                                     Hash-bound ledgers, explicit finite-set families, multi-state witnesses, composition sen-               710

                                     sitivity, sealed candidate selection, and negative results make this bounded conclusion                 711

                                     reproducible. External RTS-GMLC and SimBench work remains a mechanically validated                      712

                                     pilot until independent experts certify the question–SQL pairs and a sealed follow-up set               713

                                     is executed; BIRD remains an unexecuted model protocol despite its completed technical                  714
