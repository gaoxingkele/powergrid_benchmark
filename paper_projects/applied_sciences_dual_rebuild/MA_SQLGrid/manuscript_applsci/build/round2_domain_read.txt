                                       Abstract                                                                                          6


                                       Natural-language access to power-grid maintenance data requires generated SQL to be               7

                                       grounded in the supplied database context, remain read-only, and return the intended              8

                                       result. We present MA-SQLGrid as a controlled study of two frozen, GridDB-tailored                9

                                       prompt interventions: a context-package factor (complete DDL and a global value inventory        10

                                       versus question-conditioned schema, joins, matched values, and normalization rules) and a        11

                                       composite structural/SQL-operation hint. Qwen2.5-Coder-7B Q4_K_M and Granite-3.3-8B              12

                                       Q4_K_M each produced one prediction for every cell of a paired 2 × 2 design over 180             13

                                       questions (1440 predictions total). All execution verdicts were independently re-executed.       14

                                       The structural hint increased a common-target projected-column diagnostic by 0.4083 (95%         15

                                       template-cluster bootstrap interval [0.2115, 0.6122]) for Qwen and 0.3556 ([0.1352, 0.5793])     16

                                       for Granite. Its execution-equality effects were less stable: 0.2306 ([0.0573, 0.4343]) and      17

                                       0.1583 ([-0.0207, 0.3689]), respectively. The compact/domain-grounded package had no             18

                                       independently supported positive execution effect, while an execution three-way modifier         19

                                       of 0.1889 ([0.0088, 0.4286]) showed that the context–hint interaction differed between the       20

                                       two snapshots. The compact package reduced mean Qwen input tokens from 2442–2490 to              21

                                       457–503, but this observational prompt-length contrast is not a controlled latency result.       22

                                       GridDB is synthetic, development-visible, and small; RTS-GMLC and SimBench candidates            23

                                     2. Related Work                                                                                    99


                                     2.1. Text-to-SQL and Cross-Database Evaluation                                                     100


                                           Text-to-SQL research has progressed from template and grammar systems to neu-                101

                                     ral parsers and instruction-tuned language models. Spider established a cross-domain,              102

                                     cross-database setting in which train and test databases differ, forcing models to interpret       103

                                     unfamiliar schemas rather than memorize query templates [1]. Its difficulty also highlighted       104

                                     the need to evaluate structural decisions such as joins, nesting, aggregation, grouping, and       105

                                     ordering. Later benchmarks and surveys catalogued continuing challenges in schema                  106

                                     linking, compositional generalization, ambiguous questions, and realistic database content         107

                                     [3,6,7].                                                                                           108

                                           Execution-based evaluation answers a question that string match cannot: whether              109

                                     the predicted query produces the intended result on a particular database state. Exact             110

                                     SQL match can penalize semantically equivalent queries, whereas execution equality can             111

                                     fail to distinguish nonequivalent queries on an insufficiently discriminating database.            112

                                     Applied work should therefore freeze the execution contract, retain the predicted SQL,             113

                                     and state the database boundary. In this study, strict result equality is primary; projected-      114

                                     column conformity and error categories are separate diagnostics. We do not claim semantic          115

                                     equivalence beyond the frozen database instances.                                                  116

                                           Constrained decoding and execution guidance reduce invalid outputs. PICARD incre-            117

                                     mentally rejects tokens that cannot form admissible SQL [2]. Execution-guided approaches           118

                                     test candidate queries against a database and use failures or results during selection. MA-        119

                                     SQLGrid uses a lighter boundary: the generator is prompted once under a frozen condition,          120

                                     then a deterministic parser and read-only validator classify and execute the output. No            121

                                     gold SQL or gold result is available during generation or candidate selection.                     122

                                           Large language models make prompt context a central design variable. Schema seri-            123

                                     alization, examples, column descriptions, value samples, and explicit output instructions          124

                                     compete for a finite context budget. Empirical studies show that model performance                 125

                                     can depend strongly on representation and demonstration choices [4,5,8]. Yet combined              126

                                     prompt revisions do not reveal causal contribution. Our factorial design estimates two             127

                                     interventions separately and shows that a prompt feature can improve response conformity           128

                                     more reliably than semantic execution.                                                             129

                                     2.2. Schema Linking, Context Selection, and Response Contracts                                         130


                                           Schema linking connects question spans to tables, columns, values, and relationships             131

                                     [9–11]. A full schema preserves all available elements but may introduce distractors, long-            132

                                     range dependencies, and token cost. Compact context aims to retain question-relevant                   133

                                     elements while removing low-relevance structure. This creates a recall–precision trade-off:            134

                                     excluding a needed join key is catastrophic, while retaining unrelated tables may confuse a            135

                                     generator. Deterministic introspection and selection make this trade-off auditable.                    136

                                           Output contracts describe what a response should contain. In text-to-SQL, such                   137

                                     guidance can specify projected fields, row granularity, ordering, aggregation, group-                  138

                                     ing/HAVING, or LIMIT behavior. The intervention tested here is consequently broader                    139

                                     than an answer-format hint and is explicitly corpus-tailored. We record only a common-                 140

                                     target projected-column indicator independently of execution equality. The two endpoints               141

                                     must not be merged: high column-count conformity shows reception of one structural                     142

                                     instruction, not correctness of the returned database content.                                         143

                                           Recent domain-oriented systems combine schema retrieval, value grounding, decom-                 144

                                     position, and repair, including CHESS and the Applied Sciences DKA-SQL study [12,13].                  145

                                     DKA-SQL is a direct power-grid text-to-SQL precedent, so our novelty is not being the                  146

                                     first grid text-to-SQL framework. Its published evidence, workflow, models, and corpora                147

                                     differ materially from ours, and no official implementation or immutable prompt/code                   148

                                     release was located in the Round-1 audit; we therefore make no numerical superiority or                149

                                     reproduction claim. A 2 × 2 design estimates the bundled context-package effect, composite-            150

                                     hint effect, and difference-in-differences interaction. Repeating the same design for two              151

                                     backbones is a sensitivity analysis, not a general model-family study.                                 152



                                     2.3. Power-Grid Databases and AI-Assisted Access                                                       153


                                           Power-system datasets mix network topology, asset attributes, time series, costs, con-           154

                                     straints, maintenance records, and event narratives. RTS-GMLC provides a reproducible                  155

                                     test-system resource with buses, branches, generators, loads, renewable profiles, reserves,            156

                                     and dispatch-related data [14]. SimBench supplies benchmark networks for multiple volt-                157

                                     age levels and scenarios [15]. Both resources can be transformed into relational databases             158

                                     that exercise selections, joins, aggregation, ranking, and topology-oriented questions.                159

                                           These public engineering resources are attractive for external validation, but database          160

                                     availability does not create a text-to-SQL benchmark automatically. Natural-language                   161

                                     questions and reference SQL must be semantically reviewed, units and tie handling must                 162

                                     be specified, and ambiguous projections must be resolved. Automatically generated pairs                163

                                     are useful for building and testing the pipeline, yet their agreement with a model output is           164

                                     not human-gold accuracy. The present paper treats the external resources accordingly.                  165

                                           Recent work applies language models to grid-domain information extraction, includ-               166

                                     ing a semantic-dependency and lexical-embedding RoBERTa design in this journal [16].                   167

                                     Relational-provenance research in the same venue further motivates preserving query and                168

                                     source lineage [17]. The central risk is that fluent output can be mistaken for validated              169

                                     engineering reasoning. Text-to-SQL offers a comparatively inspectable interface because                170

                                     the generated query and returned rows can be reviewed. That advantage depends on a                     171

                                     read-only execution policy, stable database provenance, and conservative claims about                  172

                                     what the execution demonstrates.                                                                       173

                                     template. The two executed packages are                                                                            181

                                     3.3. Prompt Packages and Factor Audit                                                               208


                                            The full package is constant across questions apart from the question text: all eight-       209

                                     table DDL and a sorted global dictionary drawn from permitted non-ID categorical, date,             210

                                     and text columns. The compact package first matches permitted values, applies frozen                211

                                     corpus-tailored phrase and normalization rules, selects seed tables/columns, expands                212

                                     foreign-key connections to at most six tables, and serializes joins, matched values, and            213

                                     predicate hints. If selection omits a needed element, the model cannot recover it from gold         214

                                     metadata.                                                                                           215

                                     ordering need, COUNT/AVG/SUM/MIN/MAX, GROUP BY/HAVING, exact projection fields, de-                 217

                                     scending order, or LIMIT. Its serialization also differs between full and compact packages.         218

                                     The estimand is therefore the effect of this frozen composite package as actually delivered;        219

                                     it is not the effect of a generic answer-format sentence.                                           220

                                     have?”, F00 contains complete DDL and the global dictionary; F01 adds a two-column,                 222

                                     multi-row, ordered COUNT(*) hint. F10 contains six graph-expanded tables, the                       223

                                     sensor_readings.asset_id = assets.asset_id join and the normalization alarm_flag                    224

                                     = 1; F11 adds the corresponding two-column, multi-row, ordering and count instructions.             225

                                     5. Discussion                                                                                                                                                  448


                                     5.1. What the Evidence Supports                                                                                                                                449


                                          The clearest supported effect is the composite hint on the common-target projected-                                                                       450

                                     column diagnostic. Both backbones show increases larger than 0.35, and both template-                                                                          451

                                     cluster intervals exclude zero. Explicit structural and SQL-operation instructions therefore                                                                   452

                                     change model output structure reliably in this bounded experiment. Because the hint also                                                                       453

                                     contains aggregation, grouping, projection, ordering, and limit rules, this result cannot be                                                                   454

                                     reduced to generic formatting advice.                                                                                                                          455

                                          Execution is more conditional. Qwen shows a positive average hint effect with an                                                                          456

                                     interval above zero; Granite shows a positive point estimate but an interval that crosses                                                                      457

                                     zero. F01 favors Qwen at the point and interval levels, but not after cluster-randomization                                                                    458

                                     family correction, while F11 is tied. The intervention cannot be evaluated apart from the                                                                      459

                                     context package and backbone with which it is paired.                                                                                                          460




                                                                                                                                                https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 5, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                                 15 of 19




                                          The compact/domain-grounded package does not show a uniformly positive indepen-                 461

                                     dent effect. For Qwen its execution main effect is negative with an upper interval endpoint          462

                                     at zero; for Granite it is small and uncertain. The package is substantially shorter, but            463

                                     it simultaneously changes schema coverage, value presentation, join serialization, and               464

                                     normalization. It is therefore neither a pure schema-selection ablation nor evidence that            465

                                     shorter prompts improve accuracy.                                                                    466

                                          The nonzero three-way modifier is operationally important. It does not prove that one           467

                                     backbone is generally better. Instead, it shows that the same prompt intervention can inter-         468

                                     act differently with two instruction-tuned, quantized models. Deployment teams should                469

                                     therefore validate the complete model–prompt–database combination under execution,                   470

                                     rather than transfer an ablation result from one backbone.                                           471



                                     5.2. Projected Columns versus Semantic Correctness                                                   472


                                           The gap between the common-target diagnostic and execution equality is a warning               473

                                     against surface-only evaluation. In Qwen F01, projected-column conformity is 0.9667 while            474

                                     execution equality is 0.7167. In Granite F01 the corresponding values are 0.8778 and 0.5556.         475

                                     A model can return the requested number of columns yet select the wrong table, field, filter,        476

                                     join, or aggregation.                                                                                477

                                           The projected-column diagnostic remains useful because it localizes one interface              478

                                     failure and shows that an intervention is received. It should be reported next to, not               479

                                     substituted for, execution. Row granularity and ordering were present in prompts but are             480

                                     not validated by this endpoint. Where a database state is insufficiently discriminating,             481

                                     manual semantic review or test-database augmentation is also needed.                                 482



                                     5.3. Implications for Power-Grid Data Access                                                         483


                                          An operational interface should expose the generated SQL, database snapshot, schema             484

                                     context, and execution status. Read-only enforcement prevents accidental mutation but                485

                                     does not prevent a semantically wrong query from returning plausible rows. High-                     486

                                     consequence decisions therefore require review of both the SQL and the source data.                  487

                                          The external diagnostic is useful precisely because it fails visibly. Missing-column            488

                                     errors indicate that the model inferred familiar field names rather than grounding to the            489

                                     actual schema. Prompt repair should target these mismatches, then be evaluated on new or             490

                                     sealed items rather than repeatedly optimizing against the same 91 visible candidates.               491

                                          RTS-GMLC and SimBench broaden the engineering structures represented in the                     492

                                     project: topology, generation, constraints, time series, and network elements. Their inclu-          493

                                     sion does not by itself validate maintenance-database generalization. A credible external            494

                                     benchmark needs domain experts to verify terminology, units, expected projections, accept-           495

                                     able equivalences, and tie rules.                                                                    496



                                     5.4. Reproducibility and Negative Results                                                            497


                                           The quarantined Qwen incident demonstrates why result directories need immutable               498

                                     identity and process accounting. A directory containing more generations than the regis-             499

                                     tered Cartesian design is not repaired by truncating it. The clean rerun provides a separate         500

                                     evidence chain, and the audit confirms that the contaminated output contributed no canon-            501

                                     ical row.                                                                                            502

                                           Hash-bound artifacts reduce transcription and provenance errors. They do not make              503

                                     an exposed test partition sealed, resolve a missing data license, or turn automatic candidates       504

                                     into human labels. Reproducibility and evidential validity are related but distinct.                 505

                                           The compact/domain-grounded package NO–GO is a useful result. The stage remains                506

                                     available for future selectors, but the paper does not assign it an independent benefit that the     507

                                     current data do not support. Similarly, validator repair, BIRD baselines, and comparative            508




                                                                                                    https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 5, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                                16 of 19




                                     efficiency were planned but lack completed prospective execution and common accounting.             509

                                     They remain future experiments rather than empty cells to fill with convenient numbers.             510



                                     5.5. Limitations                                                                                    511


                                           The primary corpus is one synthetic 8-table, 98-row maintenance-oriented database             512

                                     and only 180 evaluation questions. Questions share 70 normalized SQL-template clusters;             513

                                     bootstrap and sign-flip procedures respect this grouping, but the corpus cannot represent           514

                                     the diversity of production schemas, permissions, missing values, units, or data quality.           515

                                           The evaluation partition was visible in earlier development. Gold fields are excluded         516

                                     from prompts and the formal runs are frozen, but prior analyst exposure prevents a sealed-          517

                                     test claim. The two model snapshots use Q4_K_M quantization and one llama.cpp revision.             518

                                     Results may differ for full-precision weights, alternative quantization, other runtimes,            519

                                     stochastic decoding, or hosted models.                                                              520

                                           Only one generation is made per prompt. Temperature zero and fixed runtime settings           521

                                     improve repeatability, but they do not quantify decoding variance or server nondetermin-            522

                                     ism. Multi-run inference would require a prospectively frozen hierarchical analysis and             523

                                     materially greater compute.                                                                         524

                                           Execution equality is database-state dependent. Different SQL statements may agree            525

                                     accidentally on the current data, and minor projection differences may be judged wrong              526

                                     under the strict contract. The contract is intentionally fixed, yet no single automatic metric      527

                                     captures every notion of semantic equivalence.                                                      528

                                           No common efficiency experiment is reported. Run timestamps differ in server port             529

                                     and operating circumstances and are not comparable latency measures. Token counts,                  530

                                     warm-up, memory, energy, and database execution time require a dedicated protocol on                531

                                     identical hardware.                                                                                 532

                                           External candidates have not completed human review. Their automatic references               533

                                     and the Qwen diagnostic are development evidence only. No claim of RTS-GMLC or                      534

                                     SimBench accuracy, cross-database generalization, or operational readiness follows from             535

                                     them.                                                                                               536

                                           GridDB redistribution permission is unresolved, and derived RTS-GMLC and Sim-                 537

                                     Bench artifacts have source-specific notice and database-license obligations. A public              538

                                     artifact release must undergo human license review and receive a permanent repository               539

                                     DOI. Author identities, contributions, funding, conflicts, acknowledgments, and AI-use              540

                                     disclosure also require author confirmation.                                                        541



                                     5.6. Future Work                                                                                    542


                                           The immediate external study should complete two independent expert reviews of                543

                                     all candidates, retain disagreements, and adjudicate them under the frozen manual. A                544

                                     subsequent confirmatory set should be newly authored or deeply rewritten after the prompt           545

                                     and schema-repair work is frozen. This preserves a genuinely unexposed test.                        546

                                           The compact/domain-grounded builder should be redesigned around reference-free                547

                                     coverage safeguards. Candidate columns and relationships could be accompanied by                    548

                                     explicit connectivity checks and a fallback to the full package when join paths appear              549

                                     6. Conclusions                                                                                          563


                                           MA-SQLGrid provides an auditable factorial case study of context grounding for                    564

                                     power-grid text-to-SQL. Across two independently audited local backbones, a GridDB-                     565

                                     tailored composite structural/SQL-operation hint consistently improves a common-target                  566

                                     projected-column diagnostic. Its execution effect is positive in direction for both snapshots           567

                                     but statistically clearer for Qwen, and the package–hint execution interaction differs be-              568

                                     tween them. The compact/domain-grounded package does not show an independent                            569

                                     positive execution effect.                                                                              570

                                     system that must be evaluated with database execution. The ledgers, hashes, corrected end-              573
