                                       Natural-language access to power-grid maintenance data requires generated SQL to be                 7

                                       grounded in the supplied database context, remain read-only, and return the intended result.        8

                                       We present MA-SQLGrid as a controlled study of two frozen, GridDB-tailored prompt                   9

                                       interventions: a context-package factor and a composite structural/SQL-operation hint.             10

                                       Qwen2.5-Coder-7B Q4_K_M and Granite-3.3-8B Q4_K_M each produced one prediction for                 11

                                       every cell of a paired 2-by-2 design over 180 questions (1440 predictions total). After Holm       12

                                       adjustment of a nine-test execution family, no factorial effect or cross-backbone modifier was     13

                                       promoted; a separate secondary manipulation-check family showed increased projected-               14

                                       column adherence under the hint. A prospectively frozen 700-call component study found             15

                                       that presented value evidence improved Qwen execution by 0.1059 (95% composition-                  16

                                       sensitivity interval [0.0282, 0.2013], adjusted p = 0.0310) but not Granite execution, while       17

                                       deterministic candidate selection was not promoted. We additionally re-executed every              18

                                       archived prediction on 15 deterministic semantic stress states and three insertion-order           19

                                       diagnostics. Two blinded technical reviewers held all 114 order-sensitive questions, leaving       20

                                       66 questions for an automated 15-state logical-AND endpoint. Suite rates ranged from               21

                                       0.6212 to 0.8182 across cells, and none of nine suite effects survived Holm correction. This       22

                                       retrospective stress test reduces single-snapshot ambiguity but is not a human semantic            23

                                       audit. GridDB is a synthetic, development-visible 8-table/98-row database; no multi-run            24

                                     2. Related Work                                                                                    100


                                     2.1. Text-to-SQL and Cross-Database Evaluation                                                     101


                                           Text-to-SQL research has progressed from template and grammar systems to neu-                102

                                     ral parsers and instruction-tuned language models. Spider established a cross-domain,              103

                                     cross-database setting in which train and test databases differ, forcing models to interpret       104

                                     unfamiliar schemas rather than memorize query templates [1]. Its difficulty also highlighted       105

                                     the need to evaluate structural decisions such as joins, nesting, aggregation, grouping, and       106

                                     ordering. Later benchmarks and surveys catalogued continuing challenges in schema                  107

                                     linking, compositional generalization, ambiguous questions, and realistic database content         108

                                     [3,6,7].                                                                                           109

                                           Execution-based evaluation answers a question that string match cannot: whether              110

                                     the predicted query produces the intended result on a particular database state. Exact             111

                                     SQL match can penalize semantically equivalent queries, whereas execution equality can             112

                                     fail to distinguish nonequivalent queries on an insufficiently discriminating database.            113

                                     Applied work should therefore freeze the execution contract, retain the predicted SQL,             114

                                     and state the database boundary. In this study, strict result equality is primary; projected-      115

                                     column conformity and error categories are separate diagnostics. We do not claim semantic          116

                                     equivalence beyond the frozen database instances.                                                  117

                                           Constrained decoding and execution guidance reduce invalid outputs. PICARD incre-            118

                                     mentally rejects tokens that cannot form admissible SQL [2]. Execution-guided approaches           119

                                     test candidate queries against a database and use failures or results during selection. MA-        120

                                     SQLGrid uses a lighter boundary: the generator is prompted once under a frozen condition,          121

                                     then a deterministic parser and read-only validator classify and execute the output. No            122

                                     gold SQL or gold result is available during generation or candidate selection.                     123

                                           Large language models make prompt context a central design variable. Schema seri-            124

                                     alization, examples, column descriptions, value samples, and explicit output instructions          125

                                     compete for a finite context budget. Empirical studies show that model performance                 126

                                     can depend strongly on representation and demonstration choices [4,5,8]. Yet combined              127

                                     prompt revisions do not reveal causal contribution. Our factorial design estimates two             128

                                     interventions separately and shows that a prompt feature can improve response conformity           129

                                     more reliably than semantic execution.                                                             130

                                     2.2. Schema Linking, Context Selection, and Response Contracts                                         131


                                           Schema linking connects question spans to tables, columns, values, and relationships             132

                                     [9–11]. A full schema preserves all available elements but may introduce distractors, long-            133

                                     range dependencies, and token cost. Compact context aims to retain question-relevant                   134

                                     elements while removing low-relevance structure. This creates a recall–precision trade-off:            135

                                     excluding a needed join key is catastrophic, while retaining unrelated tables may confuse a            136

                                     generator. Deterministic introspection and selection make this trade-off auditable.                    137

                                           Output contracts describe what a response should contain. In text-to-SQL, such                   138

                                     guidance can specify projected fields, row granularity, ordering, aggregation, group-                  139

                                     ing/HAVING, or LIMIT behavior. The intervention tested here is consequently broader                    140

                                     than an answer-format hint and is explicitly corpus-tailored. We record only a common-                 141

                                     target projected-column indicator independently of execution equality. The two endpoints               142

                                     must not be merged: high column-count conformity shows reception of one structural                     143

                                     instruction, not correctness of the returned database content.                                         144

                                           Recent domain-oriented systems combine schema retrieval, value grounding, decom-                 145

                                     position, and repair, including CHESS and the Applied Sciences DKA-SQL study [12,13].                  146

                                     DKA-SQL is a direct power-grid text-to-SQL precedent, so our novelty is not being the                  147

                                     first grid text-to-SQL framework. Its published evidence, workflow, models, and corpora                148

                                     differ materially from ours, and no official implementation or immutable prompt/code                   149

                                     release was located in the Round-1 audit; we therefore make no numerical superiority or                150

                                     reproduction claim. A 2 × 2 design estimates the bundled context-package effect, composite-            151

                                     hint effect, and difference-in-differences interaction. Repeating the same design for two              152

                                     backbones is a sensitivity analysis, not a general model-family study.                                 153



                                     2.3. Power-Grid Databases and AI-Assisted Access                                                       154


                                           Power-system datasets mix network topology, asset attributes, time series, costs, con-           155

                                     straints, maintenance records, and event narratives. RTS-GMLC provides a reproducible                  156

                                     test-system resource with buses, branches, generators, loads, renewable profiles, reserves,            157

                                     and dispatch-related data [14]. SimBench supplies benchmark networks for multiple volt-                158

                                     age levels and scenarios [15]. Both resources can be transformed into relational databases             159

                                     that exercise selections, joins, aggregation, ranking, and topology-oriented questions.                160

                                           These public engineering resources are attractive for external validation, but database          161

                                     availability does not create a text-to-SQL benchmark automatically. Natural-language                   162

                                     questions and reference SQL must be semantically reviewed, units and tie handling must                 163

                                     be specified, and ambiguous projections must be resolved. Automatically generated pairs                164

                                     are useful for building and testing the pipeline, yet their agreement with a model output is           165

                                     not human-gold accuracy. The present paper treats the external resources accordingly.                  166

                                           Recent work applies language models to grid-domain information extraction, includ-               167

                                     ing a semantic-dependency and lexical-embedding RoBERTa design in this journal [16].                   168

                                     Relational-provenance research in the same venue further motivates preserving query and                169

                                     source lineage [17]. The central risk is that fluent output can be mistaken for validated              170

                                     engineering reasoning. Text-to-SQL offers a comparatively inspectable interface because                171

                                     the generated query and returned rows can be reviewed. That advantage depends on a                     172

                                     read-only execution policy, stable database provenance, and conservative claims about                  173

                                     what the execution demonstrates.                                                                       174

                                     3.3. Prompt Packages and Factor Audit                                                               209


                                            The full package is constant across questions apart from the question text: all eight-       210

                                     table DDL and a sorted global dictionary drawn from permitted non-ID categorical, date,             211

                                     and text columns. The compact package first matches permitted values, applies frozen                212

                                     corpus-tailored phrase and normalization rules, selects seed tables/columns, expands                213

                                     foreign-key connections to at most six tables, and serializes joins, matched values, and            214

                                     predicate hints. If selection omits a needed element, the model cannot recover it from gold         215

                                     metadata.                                                                                           216

                                            The composite hint can specify the expected projected-column count, row granularity,         217

                                     ordering need, COUNT/AVG/SUM/MIN/MAX, GROUP BY/HAVING, exact projection fields, de-                 218

                                     scending order, or LIMIT. Its serialization also differs between full and compact packages.         219

                                     The estimand is therefore the effect of this frozen composite package as actually delivered;        220

                                     it is not the effect of a generic answer-format sentence.                                           221

                                     have?”, F00 contains complete DDL and the global dictionary; F01 adds a two-column,                 223

                                     multi-row, ordered COUNT(*) hint. F10 contains six graph-expanded tables, the                       224

                                     sensor_readings.asset_id = assets.asset_id join and the normalization alarm_flag                    225

                                     = 1; F11 adds the corresponding two-column, multi-row, ordering and count instructions.             226

                                     5. Discussion                                                                                                                                                                     562


                                     5.1. What the Evidence Supports                                                                                                                                                   563


                                          The clearest factorial result is the composite hint on the common-target projected-                                                                                          564

                                     column manipulation check. Both backbones show increases larger than 0.35 and survive                                                                                             565

                                     the separate secondary family. Explicit structural and SQL-operation instructions therefore                                                                                       566

                                     change projected-column adherence in this bounded experiment. Because the hint also                                                                                               567

                                     contains aggregation, grouping, projection, ordering, and limit rules, this result cannot be                                                                                      568

                                     reduced to generic formatting advice or promoted as independent semantic validation.                                                                                              569




                                                                                                                                                                      https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 5, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                               20 of 24




                                           Factorial execution is more conditional. All nine primary tests fail Holm correction,        570

                                     even where composition-sensitivity intervals exclude zero. This divergence is not contradic-       571

                                     tory: randomization tests implement the predeclared finite-set promotion rule, whereas the         572

                                     intervals show how estimates change when the empirical group composition is reweighted.            573

                                     Neither is evidence from a random sample of databases or questions.                                574

                                           The multi-state stress test strengthens the negative conclusion. Requiring one archived      575

                                     SQL program to agree with the gold query across 15 constructed states reduces every                576

                                     cell’s success count relative to or equal to its T0 count, and no factorial suite effect is        577

                                     promoted. This demonstrates that a single frozen snapshot can overstate reliability for            578

                                     some predictions. It does not prove full semantic equivalence: the states are deterministic        579

                                     constructions from one small database, the automatic subset covers only 12 normalized-SQL          580

                                     clusters, and all order-sensitive questions remain outside the primary suite.                      581

                                           The compact/domain-grounded package does not show a uniformly positive indepen-              582

                                     dent effect. For Qwen its execution main effect is negative with an upper interval endpoint        583

                                     at zero; for Granite it is small and uncertain. The package is substantially shorter, but          584

                                     it simultaneously changes schema coverage, value presentation, join serialization, and             585

                                     normalization. It is therefore neither a pure schema-selection ablation nor evidence that          586

                                     shorter prompts improve accuracy.                                                                  587

                                           The prospective component results sharpen but do not universalize the architecture.          588

                                     Presented value evidence passes the efficacy rule for Qwen only; its zero Granite estimate         589

                                     and non-promoted modifier preclude a replicated or universal value-grounding claim. The            590

                                     deterministic selector yields rescues for both backbones but does not pass either adjusted         591

                                     test. Deployment teams should therefore validate the complete model–prompt–database                592

                                     combination under execution rather than assume that a stage label guarantees benefit.              593



                                     5.2. Projected Columns versus Semantic Correctness                                                 594


                                           The gap between the common-target diagnostic and execution equality is a warning             595

                                     against surface-only evaluation. In Qwen F01, projected-column conformity is 0.9667 while          596

                                     execution equality is 0.7167. In Granite F01 the corresponding values are 0.8778 and 0.5556.       597

                                     A model can return the requested number of columns yet select the wrong table, field, filter,      598

                                     join, or aggregation.                                                                              599

                                           The projected-column diagnostic remains useful because it localizes one interface            600

                                     failure and shows that an intervention is received. It should be reported next to, not             601

                                     substituted for, execution. Row granularity and ordering were present in prompts but are           602

                                     not validated by this endpoint. Where a database state is insufficiently discriminating,           603

                                     manual semantic review or test-database augmentation is also needed.                               604



                                     5.3. Implications for Power-Grid Data Access                                                       605


                                          An operational interface should expose the generated SQL, database snapshot, schema           606

                                     context, and execution status. Read-only enforcement prevents accidental mutation but              607

                                     does not prevent a semantically wrong query from returning plausible rows. High-                   608

                                     consequence decisions therefore require review of both the SQL and the source data.                609

                                          The external diagnostic is useful precisely because it fails visibly. Missing-column          610

                                     errors indicate that the model inferred familiar field names rather than grounding to the          611

                                     actual schema. Prompt repair should target these mismatches, then be evaluated on new or           612

                                     sealed items rather than repeatedly optimizing against the same 91 visible candidates.             613

                                          RTS-GMLC and SimBench broaden the engineering structures represented in the                   614

                                     project: topology, generation, constraints, time series, and network elements. Their inclu-        615

                                     sion does not by itself validate maintenance-database generalization. A credible external          616




                                                                                                  https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 5, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                                21 of 24




                                     benchmark needs domain experts to verify terminology, units, expected projections, accept-          617

                                     able equivalences, and tie rules.                                                                   618



                                     5.4. Reproducibility and Negative Results                                                           619


                                           The quarantined Qwen incident demonstrates why result directories need immutable              620

                                     identity and process accounting. A directory containing more generations than the regis-            621

                                     tered Cartesian design is not repaired by truncating it. The clean rerun provides a separate        622

                                     evidence chain, and the audit confirms that the contaminated output contributed no canon-           623

                                     ical row.                                                                                           624

                                           Hash-bound artifacts reduce transcription and provenance errors. They do not make             625

                                     an exposed test partition sealed, resolve a missing data license, or turn automatic candidates      626

                                     into human labels. Reproducibility and evidential validity are related but distinct.                627

                                           The compact/domain-grounded package NO–GO is a useful result. The stage remains               628

                                     available for future selectors, but the paper does not assign it an independent benefit that        629

                                     the current data do not support. The prospective E2 selector also remains a NO–GO for               630

                                     promoted efficacy despite descriptive rescues. E1 supports presented value evidence for             631

                                     one snapshot only. BIRD model baselines remain unexecuted, and the E4 latency records               632

                                     remain diagnostic rather than comparative-efficiency evidence.                                      633



                                     5.5. Limitations                                                                                    634


                                           The primary corpus is one synthetic 8-table, 98-row maintenance-oriented database             635

                                     and only 180 evaluation questions. Questions map to 70 normalized-SQL dependence-                   636

                                     proxy groups, 58 of them singletons; grouped procedures respect this mapping, but it                637

                                     is not an authoring-template identifier and the corpus cannot represent the diversity of            638

                                     production schemas, permissions, missing values, units, or data quality.                            639

                                           The evaluation partition was visible in earlier development. Gold fields are excluded         640

                                     from prompts and the formal runs are frozen, but prior analyst exposure prevents a sealed-          641

                                     test claim. The two model snapshots use Q4_K_M quantization and one llama.cpp revision.             642

                                     Results may differ for full-precision weights, alternative quantization, other runtimes,            643

                                     stochastic decoding, or hosted models.                                                              644

                                           Only one generation is made per factorial prompt and one formal response per com-             645

                                     ponent condition. Temperature zero and fixed runtime settings improve repeatability, but            646

                                     they do not quantify decoding variance or server nondeterminism. The second backbone                647

                                     is sensitivity analysis, not replication, and no cross-backbone component effect satisfies          648

                                     a replication rule. Multi-run inference would require a prospectively frozen hierarchical           649

                                     analysis and materially greater compute.                                                            650

                                           Execution equality is database-state dependent. Different SQL statements may agree            651

                                     accidentally on the current data, and minor projection differences may be judged wrong              652

                                     under the strict contract. The contract is intentionally fixed, yet no single automatic metric      653

                                     captures every notion of semantic equivalence.                                                      654

                                           The 15-state suite addresses accidental equality only within its constructed witnesses.       655

                                     It was designed retrospectively, does not constitute an independent human semantic                  656

                                     audit, and cannot certify behavior on unconstructed values or schemas. Two blinded                  657

                                     technical reviewers conservatively held all 114 order-sensitive questions; qualified domain         658

                                     reviewers must still resolve intended ordering, ties, units, and result granularity before          659

                                     those questions support semantic claims.                                                            660

                                           E4 records paired telemetry under a frozen order, but the required efficiency attestation     661

                                     is missing. Token deltas remain valid descriptive outputs; latency and throughput are               662

                                     diagnostic, and no energy or memory measurement supports deployment-efficiency claims.              663




                                                                                                   https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 5, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                              22 of 24




                                     A confirmatory efficiency study requires independently verified process isolation, thermal        664

                                     state, common hardware, and separate generation/database timing.                                  665

                                          External candidates have not completed human review. Their automatic references              666

                                     and the Qwen diagnostic are development evidence only. No claim of RTS-GMLC or                    667

                                     SimBench accuracy, cross-database generalization, or operational readiness follows from           668

                                     them.                                                                                             669

                                          GridDB redistribution permission is unresolved, and derived RTS-GMLC and Sim-                670

                                     Bench artifacts have source-specific notice and database-license obligations. A public            671

                                     artifact release must undergo human license review and receive a permanent repository             672

                                     DOI. Author identities, contributions, funding, conflicts, acknowledgments, and AI-use            673

                                     disclosure also require author confirmation.                                                      674



                                     5.6. Future Work                                                                                  675


                                           The immediate external study should complete two independent expert reviews of              676

                                     all candidates, retain disagreements, and adjudicate them under the frozen manual. A              677

                                     subsequent confirmatory set should be newly authored or deeply rewritten after the prompt         678

                                     and schema-repair work is frozen. This preserves a genuinely unexposed test.                      679

                                           The compact/domain-grounded builder should be redesigned around reference-free              680

                                     coverage safeguards. Candidate columns and relationships could be accompanied by                  681

                                     explicit connectivity checks and a fallback to the full package when join paths appear            682

                                     incomplete. The observed offline table/column recall cannot be used online because it             683

                                     depends on gold-required fields. Any replacement safeguard constitutes a new intervention         684

                                     and must be evaluated prospectively.                                                              685

                                           The completed E2 replay should be followed only by a newly frozen selector revision;        686

                                     tuning against its visible rescues would make the present set development data. Efficiency        687

                                     confirmation requires controlled warm and cold runs, common hardware, token accounting,           688

                                     resident and accelerator memory, energy where possible, and separate model-generation             689

                                     and SQL-execution latency.                                                                        690

                                           After explicit human launch authorization, the technically frozen BIRD Mini-Dev pro-        691

                                     tocol can add transparent direct, decomposition, schema-selection, and bounded execution-         692

                                     feedback baselines on the same two snapshots. Its 500/500 SQLite 3.40.1 gold-query                693

                                     preflight is a mechanical gate, not model evidence. It must not be described as DKASQL            694

                                     reproduction, because no official implementation was found. Additional backbones, sizes,          695

                                     quantizations, decoding settings, and repeated runs would test whether the finite-set             696

                                     execution contrasts persist.                                                                      697



                                     6. Conclusions                                                                                    698


                                          MA-SQLGrid provides an auditable case study of context grounding for power-grid              699

                                     text-to-SQL. Across two independently audited local backbones, a GridDB-tailored com-             700

                                     posite structural/SQL-operation hint improves a common-target projected-column ma-                701

                                     nipulation check, but none of nine primary factorial execution tests survives family-wise         702

                                     correction. The compact/domain-grounded package does not show an independent posi-                703

                                     tive execution effect. In the separate 700-call component study, presented value evidence         704

                                     improves Qwen execution under the frozen rule but not Granite execution; deterministic            705

                                     candidate selection is not promoted for either snapshot. A retrospective 15-state logical-        706

                                     AND stress test likewise promotes none of nine factorial reliability effects, while exposing      707

                                     lower agreement than the original snapshot in every cell.                                         708

                                          The practical conclusion is not that one prompt recipe or component universally wins.        709

                                     Model, schema/value context, handcrafted normalization, operation guidance, and can-              710

                                     didate selection form a coupled system that must be evaluated with database execution.            711




                                                                                                 https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 5, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                                    23 of 24




                                     Hash-bound ledgers, explicit finite-set families, multi-state witnesses, composition sen-               712

                                     sitivity, sealed candidate selection, and negative results make this bounded conclusion                 713

                                     reproducible. External RTS-GMLC and SimBench work remains a mechanically validated                      714

                                     pilot until independent experts certify the question–SQL pairs and a sealed follow-up set               715

                                     is executed; BIRD remains an unexecuted model protocol despite its completed technical                  716

                                     freeze and gold-query preflight.                                                                        717
