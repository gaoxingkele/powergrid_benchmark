## c2ges_algorithm_framework_vector

Overall C²GES framework. Complete reports pass candidate and leakage gates before conservative lexical role assignment. A typed textual proxy graph supplies graph salience and qualified-path deletion loss, which join centroid relevance, role evidence, and position prior in the frozen five-channel score. Greedy selection penalizes lexical redundancy and returns source-linked sentences. The registered No-CF condition sets only the absolute C coefficient to zero without renormalization.
