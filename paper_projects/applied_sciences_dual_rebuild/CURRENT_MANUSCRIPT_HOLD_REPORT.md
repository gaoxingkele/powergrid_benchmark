## MA-SQLGrid hold reasons

The current draft is useful as a prose and reference baseline, but its abstract
and results are dominated by the repeatedly inspected 180-question synthetic
GridDB split. The new paper must distinguish:

- legacy GridDB reproduction/diagnostics;
- the registered 2 x 2 context-scope by answer-shape design;
- RTS-GMLC and SimBench development evidence;
- any later independently reviewed and sealed external test evidence.

No old strict-execution gain may be generalized to cross-database performance.
Projection-tolerant reversal, validator harm, model/provider identity, cost, and
failure rates must remain visible.

## C2GES hold reasons

The current abstract reports a 4000/800/800 conversion and a significant learned
role gain. Those statements are ineligible because the earlier corpus contains
cross-split Wikipedia-document leakage and exposes oracle labels without a clean
end-to-end distinction.

The accepted W3 pilot instead uses 8000/1500/1500 document-disjoint instances.
At seed 2026, oracle-label, predicted-label, and label-blind protocols are
statistically indistinguishable across K, while BM25 is significantly stronger
at K=1. Therefore the current title may be retained only provisionally. If the
five-seed result confirms a null role effect, the smallest scientifically honest
adjustment is to retain `C2GES` and the evidence-selection/application wording
while removing or subordinating `Causal-Role-Aware` as the main claimed advance.

## Release conditions

The hold can be lifted only when:

1. canonical predictions and registered statistics are frozen;
2. every abstract/result number maps to a canonical artifact and row key;
3. claims are downgraded when confidence intervals or robustness results require
   it;
4. figures and tables are generated from canonical data rather than copied from
   legacy drafts;
5. declarations, data availability, license limitations, and AI-use disclosure
   are complete;
6. the rebuilt source compiles without errors, undefined references, or missing
   assets and passes all three review/revision rounds.
