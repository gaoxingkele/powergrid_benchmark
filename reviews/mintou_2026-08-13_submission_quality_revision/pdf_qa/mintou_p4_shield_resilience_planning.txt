                                          Distribution network planning under load, distributed-energy-resource, and outage uncer-            2

                                          tainty requires portfolios that remain useful as binding stresses change. SHIELD-MOEA ex-           3

                                          tends non-dominated sorting with selective worst-scenario screening and disjoint scenario           4

                                          draws for search and final evaluation. On a reproducible SimBench-derived benchmark                 5

                                          with 72 candidate actions, five objectives, and eight uncertainty experiments, the method           6

                                          32 comparisons with four stochastic baselines are Holm-significant wins; sixteen gaps to            9

                                          the search ranges (+6.04%). Feasibility repair carries the main measured gain. Screening           12

                                         This paper proposes SHIELD-MOEA, a scenario-selective extension of non-dominated                42

                                    sorting for distribution resilience planning. The algorithm retains a standard multi-objective       43

                                    backbone–crowding-based selection, hybrid GA/DE variation, and greedy feasibility repair–            44

                                    and places its contribution at the optimizer–uncertainty interface. Every few generations, it        45

                                    identifies the K scenarios on which the current population performs worst and uses them              46

                                    for search-phase fitness. The final front is evaluated on a complete scenario set generated          47

                                    with disjoint random seeds, preventing direct reuse of search realizations in the reported           48

                                    score. We report mean and sampled worst-envelope hypervolume and supplement the                      49

                                    proxy evaluation with pandapower AC power flow.                                                      50

                                         Real resilience-investment records are utility-internal, so evaluation uses a repro-            51

                                    ducible SimBench-derived benchmark [7]. Statistics from the 18 highest-stress subnets                52

                                    generate 72 candidate actions. Eight experiments vary load, DER, and outage uncertainty,             53

                                    including an unseen-stress setting whose evaluation scenarios are harsher than the search            54

                                    scenarios. Candidate generation is shared with CARS-MODE (Section 2.4), as declared in               55

                                         The contributions of this paper are:                                                            57


                                    1.    A disjoint-evaluation protocol for scenario-aware evolutionary planning. SHIELD-               58

                                          MOEA scores final fronts on scenario draws not used during search, preventing direct           59

                                          reuse of the realizations that guided optimization. An unseen-range experiment                 60

                                          provides an additional test of search-range specialization (Sections 4.3 and 4.6).             61

                                    2.    A selective worst-K screening mechanism with quantified evaluation economy. At                 62

                                          the default configuration, screening reduces recorded search-phase plan–scenario               63

                                          evaluations by 65% (51,200 to 17,920). No front-quality difference is detected against         64

                                          no screening, and periodic re-screening remains unresolved against a fixed generation-         65

                                          1 subset; screening is therefore an exposure-and-economy mechanism rather than a               66

                                          demonstrated quality mechanism (Section 6.3).                                                  67

                                    3.    A statistically grounded framework result with component attribution. Over eight               68

                                          experiments and 30 seeds per stochastic method, SHIELD-MOEA reaches pooled                     69

                                          mean hypervolume 0.2740, +5.09% over matched-repair NSGA-II and +5.56% over                    70

                                          plain NSGA-II, with 32/32 Holm-significant stochastic-baseline wins. Repair is the             71

                                          dominant resolved component (-8.10% when removed), whereas hybrid variation,                   72

                                          dynamic re-screening, and the explicit resilience objective remain unresolved against          73

                                          their direct controls (Sections 6.1 and 6.3).                                                  74

                                    0.2. Related Work                                                                                    94


                                        SHIELD-MOEA draws on three bodies of literature that have so far evolved largely                 95

                                    in parallel: resilience-oriented distribution planning under N-k and extreme-weather                 96

                                    uncertainty, scenario generation and reduction for stochastic power system optimization,             97

                                    and hybrid multi-objective evolutionary algorithms with robustness-aware objectives.                 98

                                    Below we discuss each thread and identify the gap that motivates this paper.                         99



                                    0.2.1. Resilience-Oriented Distribution Planning and Hardening Decisions                             100


                                          Panteli and Mancarella [8] distinguish high-impact, low-probability events from or-            101

                                    dinary reliability and hardening from operational adaptation. Bhusal et al. [9] document             102

                                    the rapid growth of planning-stage resilience research. The dominant paradigm is robust              103

                                    optimization over an N − k or weather-driven uncertainty set. Yuan et al. [3] formulate              104

                                    a two-stage robust problem with adversarial component outages. Ma et al. [5] extend                  105

                                    this logic to joint hardening and distributed-generation placement with wind-dependent               106

                                    fragility, while Lin and Bie [6] add reconfiguration and DG islanding. Panteli et al. [10]           107

                                    connect weather intensity to component failure through fragility curves.                             108

                                          Practice-oriented studies consider resilient storage siting [11], zone-wise planning           109

                                    under decision-dependent uncertainty [12], and genetic-algorithm-driven feeder hardening             110

                                    [13]. Related work addresses resilient renewable microgrids [14] and islanding with fault            111

                                    recovery [15]. A parallel strand grounds planning claims in full AC physics. Mahmoudi                112

                                    and Alizadeh quantify errors caused by linearized expansion-planning models [16], while              113

                                    Trivic and Savic combine NSGA-II storage planning with power-flow analysis [17]. This                114

                                    evidence motivates the AC validation layer in Section 6.5.                                           115

                                          A consistent pattern emerges across these studies. The trilevel robust models protect          116

                                    against a single worst case and collapse the objective space to one scalar, so the planner           117

                                    never sees the cost-resilience trade surface. The metaheuristic studies expose multiple              118

                                    objectives, but they evaluate candidate plans on a scenario set fixed before the search starts,      119

                                    and the same scenarios serve both to steer the search and to grade its output. None of them          120

                                    guards against a plan that has quietly overfitted the scenario sample it was optimized on.           121



                                    0.2.2. Scenario Generation, Reduction, and Screening in Stochastic Power System                      122

                                    Optimization                                                                                         123


                                          The second thread represents uncertainty through scenarios. Classical reduction se-            124

                                    lects a subset while minimizing probability distance; reference approaches include the               125

                                    Fortet–Mourier formulation [18] and forward/backward reduction [19]. Power-system                    126

                                    applications adopted these methods early [20], and importance sampling made stochastic               127

                                    unit commitment tractable [21]. Generative models later reproduced spatiotemporal re-                128

                                    newable behavior without parametric assumptions [22]. Applications include stochastic                129

                                    microgrid dispatch [23], distribution reconfiguration [24], and joint siting and sizing [25].        130

                                          Recent work moves scenario machinery inside optimization. Li et al. learn an ambigu-           131

                                    ity set for distributionally robust DG planning [26], while Zhou et al. use extreme-event            132




                                                                                                    https://doi.org/10.3390/en1010000
Version August 13, 2026 submitted to Energies                                                                                4 of 24



                                    scenarios in integrated energy planning [27]. Decision-dependent uncertainty [28] is the           133

                                    closest mathematical-programming analogue to a scenario set that changes with candidate            134

                                    decisions. Wu et al. preserve extreme wind, PV, and load combinations during generation            135

                                    [29], paralleling the worst-K focus used here.                                                     136

                                          Most reductions are executed once, judged by distributional fidelity, and reused for         137

                                    every evaluation. The scenarios guiding search are therefore also used to grade the result.        138

                                    This distinction may be less important in a single monolithic solve. In population search,         139

                                    however, thousands of plans are compared while the binding scenarios can change with               140

                                    the population. SHIELD-MOEA separates search guidance from final scoring and tests                 141

                                    whether updating the guidance subset adds value.                                                   142



                                    0.2.3. Hybrid Multi-Objective Evolutionary Algorithms and Robustness-Aware Objectives              143


                                         The third thread concerns the optimizer. NSGA-II [30] and MOEA/D [31] remain                  144

                                    common frameworks, with hypervolume [32] measuring front quality. Differential evo-                145

                                    lution [33] supplies mutation behavior complementary to GA crossover, and Das and                  146

                                    Suganthan [34] review blended DE strategies. Jin and Branke [35] survey optimization               147

                                    under uncertain fitness, while Deb and Gupta [36] treat robustness as an objective over            148

                                    perturbation neighborhoods.                                                                        149

                                         Recent work evaluates candidate solutions across fixed scenario sets [37] or combines         150

                                    decision preferences with variable-level uncertainty [38]. In both cases, the uncertainty set      151

                                    remains fixed during the run. SHIELD-MOEA tests periodic scenario-subset updates and,              152

                                    through a fixed worst-K control, whether those updates matter.                                     153

                                         Recent power-planning studies hybridize evolutionary variation. Examples include              154

                                    an improved NSGA-II for storage-and-EV co-optimization [39], a three-stage TER-NSGA-II             155

                                    for resilient backbone-grid planning [40], and an enhanced beluga whale optimizer for              156

                                    storage planning [41]. Under uncertainty, such methods commonly average fitness over               157

                                    a fixed scenario sample or solve a robust reformulation before evolutionary search. The            158

                                    former incurs repeated evaluation cost, whereas the latter leaves the scenario set outside         159

                                    the evolving search state.                                                                         160

                                         SHIELD-MOEA instead treats the evaluated scenario subset as a search-time quantity.           161

                                    The subset is periodically updated toward scenarios that are difficult for the current popu-       162

                                    lation. Evaluation on a disjoint scoring set then reports both mean and sampled worst-case         163

                                    hypervolume. The targeted controls in Section 6.3 test whether periodic updating adds              164

                                    value beyond a fixed generation-1 worst-K set.                                                     165



                                    0.2.4. Relation to CARS-MODE and Gap Statement                                                     166


                                          CARS-MODE [42] also studies evolutionary distribution planning on public benchmark- 167
                                    derived portfolios. It adapts DE control parameters and repairs budget violations in an eco- 168
                                    nomic DER-and-storage setting. SHIELD-MOEA instead studies the optimizer–uncertainty 169
                                    interface. Its NSGA-II kernel combines GA/DE variation and repair, while screening sup- 170
                                    plies search scenarios that remain disjoint from the final scoring draws. Load, DER, and 171
                                    outage perturbations define the resilience setting. Only candidate generation is shared; the 172
                                    methods and claims are independent.                                                           173

                                          The gap lies at the interface of search and uncertainty. Resilience studies often grade 174
                                    a robust solution or Pareto front on the same scenarios that guide optimization. Offline 175
                                    reduction is blind to the evolving population, while hybrid MOEAs usually adapt operators 176
                                    rather than exposure. SHIELD-MOEA combines worst-scenario screening, disjoint-draw 177
                                    final scoring, and sampled worst-case hypervolume reporting. The fixed worst-K control 178
                                    determines whether the adaptive update, rather than screening alone, is necessary.            179

                                    0.3.3. Scenario Model and the Eight Experiments                                                            212


                                         Each experiment fixes ranges for the three scenario coordinates and draws |S| = 16                    213

                                    scenarios uniformly with a fixed seed. Two independent scenario sets exist per experiment:                 214

                                    a search set (seed A) that methods may use during optimization, and an evaluation set (seed                215

                                    B, disjoint) on which every reported metric is computed. Table 1 lists the eight experiments.              216

                                         The last experiment is the strictest test of the disjoint-evaluation design: its evaluation           217

                                    scenarios are drawn from ranges that never intersect the search ranges, so any method that                 218

                                    has specialized to its training scenarios is penalized at scoring time.                                    219

                                    0.4. SHIELD-MOEA                                                                                           227


                                          Figure 1 closes the method’s scenario-search loop. Each population induces a worst-K                 228

                                    screened scenario subset, the hybrid variation operators generate candidate plans, budget                  229

                                    repair restores feasibility, and robust environmental selection returns survivors. Unseen-                 230

                                    stress and AC evaluations branch from the final archive and remain outside the optimization                231

                                    loop.                                                                                                      232

                                          SHIELD-MOEA exposes screening, repair, outage information, and the resilience                        233

                                    objective as individual switches in the main ablation study. Additional matched controls                   234

                                    compare hybrid variation with GA-only and DE-only search and periodic re-screening                         235

                                    with a fixed generation-1 worst-K subset. This separation permits direct attribution for                   236

                                    the main switches and targeted tests of the two multi-option mechanisms. The method                        237

                                    is a resilience-oriented extension of NSGA-II [30]: it retains constraint-dominated non-                   238

                                    dominated sorting and crowding-distance selection, and adds hybrid variation, scenario                     239

                                    screening, and feasibility repair.                                                                         240

                                    0.4.1. Non-Dominated Sorting Kernel (NSGA-II Framework)                                                         267


                                         The kernel maintains a population of 40 binary plans over 40 generations. Environ-                         268

                                    mental selection is constraint-dominated non-dominated sorting (feasible plans dominate                         269

                                    infeasible ones; infeasible plans compare by budget violation) with crowding-distance                           270

                                    truncation, following the standard NSGA-II procedure [30]. Initial plans are sparse random                      271

                                    selections (3-18% density) repaired to the budget. Rationale: a standard, well-understood                       272

                                    kernel ensures that whatever advantage appears is attributable to the scenario interface,                       273

                                    not to bespoke selection machinery.                                                                             274



                                    0.4.2. Hybrid GA/DE Variation                                                                                   275


                                         Each generation produces 40 offspring. Half use uniform crossover followed by bit-flip                     276

                                    mutation at rate 1/n. The other half use binary rand/1 differential evolution in relaxed                        277

                                    space, followed by stochastic bit sampling with probabilities 0.9 and 0.08 for high and low                     278

                                    activation. GA recombination preserves co-selected action blocks, while DE introduces                           279

                                    direction-informed variation. Section 6.3 tests whether this hybrid improves on DE-only                         280

                                    and GA-only controls.                                                                                           281



                                    0.4.3. Worst-K Scenario Screening (The Adaptive Interface)                                                      282


                                         At generation 1 and every Ts = 5 generations thereafter, the algorithm evaluates the                       283

                                    current population on each of 16 search scenarios, ranks scenarios by the population’s                          284

                                    mean normalized scalarized objective (worst first), and retains the top K = 4. Until the                        285

                                    next screening event, parent–offspring selection uses only the active set. Let Np = 40,                         286

                                    Nu = 2Np = 80, G = 40, |S| = 16, and Nr = 8 screening rounds (generations 1, 6, . . . ,                         287

                                    36). The unscreened code path records Cfull = GNu |S| = 51,200 plan–scenario evaluations.                       288

                                    The screened path records the disjoint terms Cactive = GNu K = 12,800 for union selection                       289

                                    and Crank = Nr Np |S| = 5,120 for ranking the current population, totaling 17,920. Thus                         290

                                    screening reduces this implementation counter by 65%; wall-clock savings are evaluated                          291

                                    separately.                                                                                                     292



                                    0.4.4. Feasibility Repair                                                                                       293


                                          Every initial and offspring plan exceeding the budget is repaired greedily: the selected                  294

                                    action with the worst aggregate benefit-to-cost ratio is dropped repeatedly until the plan                      295

                                    is affordable. Rationale: under a hard budget, penalty-carrying infeasible plans waste                          296

                                    population slots and blur dominance comparisons; repair keeps the entire population in                          297

                                    the fundable region. The ablation study shows this operator carries the largest share of the                    298

                                    method’s advantage (Section 6.3).                                                                               299

                                    0.4.5. Resilience Objective and Outage Awareness in Search                                            300


                                         The survivability objective -S(x,s) is part of the search-phase objective vector by default;     301

                                    the corresponding ablation hides this column from selection while leaving the final five-             302

                                    objective evaluation untouched. A second, sharper ablation zeroes the outage severity                 303

                                    sigma during search only, making the search outage-blind while the evaluation still stresses          304

                                    the plans. These two switches let us ask separately whether seeing the survivability trade-           305

                                    off and experiencing outage stress during search matter – and the answers turn out to differ          306

                                    (Sections 6.3 and 6.5).                                                                               307



                                    0.4.6. Direct-Reuse-Leakage-Controlled Evaluation and Worst-Envelope Rationale                        308


                                          No method – proposed, baseline, or ablation – is ever scored on scenarios it searched on.       309

                                    The evaluation scenario set is drawn with a disjoint random seed (and, in the unseen-stress           310

                                    experiment, from disjoint ranges); hypervolume is computed on the feasible non-dominated              311

                                    front of each method’s final population against this set alone. Screening therefore influences        312

                                    which plans are found, but has no channel into how they are graded.                                   313

                                          We report two hypervolume variants. The standard mean hypervolume averages                      314

                                    each objective over the 16 evaluation scenarios, then computes the dominated volume.                  315

                                    The worst-case hypervolume takes the per-objective worst value over the 16 evaluation                 316

                                    scenarios before computing the dominated volume. This is a deliberate design choice, not a            317

                                    standard metric: in a resilience context, a plan whose mean performance is high but whose             318

                                    tail scenario is poor (e.g., a plan that collapses under the single worst stress combination)         319

                                    is an unacceptable plan. The worst-case readout is, however, more conservative and its                320

                                    bounds depend on the scenario sample; we present it as a complementary diagnostic to the              321

                                    mean metric, not as a replacement. The two metrics are computed under separate fixed                  322

                                    normalization bounds (Section 5.3) and are comparable within their respective columns                 323

                                    only.                                                                                                 324

                                    0.7. Discussion                                                                                                     567


                                          What the evidence supports, component by component. The framework-level re-                                   568

                                    sult is a 5.56% pooled margin over plain NSGA-II, 32/32 significant stochastic-baseline                             569

                                    comparisons, and positive margins in the sampled worst-case and unseen-range diagnos-                               570

                                    tics. Repair contributes most. Screening reduces recorded calls by 65%, while no quality                            571

                                    difference is detected against no screening; periodic re-screening does not beat a fixed                            572

                                    generation-1 worst-K set. Hybrid GA/DE does not beat DE-only. The AC composition                                    573

                                    check shows a lower feasibility rate when outage exposure is removed (0.574 versus 0.685),                          574

                                    but this fixed-mapping difference is descriptive rather than a seeded causal estimate. The                          575

                                    method should therefore be read as an evaluated pipeline with repair and direct-reuse                               576

                                    leakage control, not as evidence that every named operator is necessary.                                            577

                                          Scenario selection as a design axis. Screening reduces objective calls, and no quality                        578

                                    difference is detected against no screening; equivalence is not established. Dynamic updates                        579

                                    also do not outperform a fixed generation-1 worst-K set. The supported design principle is                          580

                                    selective search evaluation with a disjoint scoring set, not periodic adaptation itself. Using                      581

                                    one scenario sample for both search decisions and final grading would leave the reported                            582

                                    score vulnerable to scenario overfitting.                                                                           583

                                          Positioning against CARS-MODE. The companion study [42] shares only the                                       584

                                    candidate-derivation pipeline; it self-adapts operator parameters for an economically                               585




                                                                                                                  https://doi.org/10.3390/en1010000
Version August 13, 2026 submitted to Energies                                                                                20 of 24



                                    framed DER-integration problem, while this paper holds operators fixed and makes sce-               586

                                    nario exposure adaptive for a resilience-framed problem. Neither method contains the                587

                                    other’s mechanism.                                                                                  588

                                         Practitioner reading. The full pipeline produces 4.8–7.0% higher mean hypervolume              589

                                    than matched NSGA-II across the tested regimes. Ablations associate most of this difference         590

                                    with repair rather than screening or hybrid variation. The selected compromise plans have           591

                                    AC-feasible rates within the range of the strongest alternatives and avoid the loading              592

                                    pattern observed for cost-first selection. Removing outage exposure leaves proxy quality            593

                                    nearly unchanged but reduces AC feasibility in the displayed cases, supporting its inclusion        594

                                    when resilience is the planning objective.                                                          595

                                    0.8. Limitations                                                                                    597


                                          We state the boundaries of this study explicitly.                                             598


                                    1.    Recovery and restoration are not modeled. The scenario coordinate sigma scales a              599

                                          survivability proxy, and the planning stage does not model post-event restoration,            600

                                          network reconfiguration, or intentional islanding. The N-1 contingency appears only           601

                                          in the AC validation layer. An element-level N-k sampling model with fragility-curve-         602

                                          driven failures and post-event restoration optimization – in the tradition of [10,11] –       603

                                          would deepen the resilience claim and simultaneously decorrelate survivability from           604

                                          reliability (Section 6.3).                                                                    605

                                    2.    Costs are synthetic. Candidate costs are analytic functions of subnet statistics in an        606

                                          uncalibrated cost unit; no engineering-economic conclusion (payback, budget sizing)           607

                                          should be drawn before calibration against published utility investment data.                 608

                                    3.    Single planning benchmark family. All eight optimization experiments instantiate              609

                                          one 72-candidate pool from one SimBench-derived extraction. CIGRE MV and IEEE                 610

                                          33-bus extend only the AC validation layer; they do not provide independently                 611

                                          optimized candidate pools. The component attribution therefore remains specific to            612

                                          the SimBench-derived planning problem.                                                        613

                                    4.    The AC layer validates plan compositions, not nodal designs. Compositions are                 614

                                          mapped to six networks by fixed placement rules. A nodal siting/sizing formulation            615

                                          on each feeder would provide a stronger transfer test and may change the low IEEE             616

                                          33-bus feasible rates.                                                                        617

                                    5.    Statistical caveats are part of the record. The advantage over NSGA-II loses signifi-         618

                                          cance at population size 60 in the sensitivity sweep (p = 0.104), the scenario-screening      619

                                          ablation remains unresolved in all eight experiments, and the NoResilienceObj ab-             620

                                          lation nominally exceeds the full method; none of these facts is smoothed over in             621

                                          Sections 6.3 and 6.4.                                                                         622

                                    6.    Proxy objectives. The planning-stage objectives are engineering proxies; all electrical       623

                                          statements rest on the pandapower validation layer, and claims outside its coverage           624

                                          (six networks, six scenarios, composition granularity) are not made.                          625

                                    7.    The worst-case hypervolume is sample-dependent. As a single-sample worst-case                 626

                                          readout over 16 scenarios, it does not bound the true extremal performance of any             627

                                          plan; it is a diagnostic complement to the mean metric, not a certification of robustness.    628

                                    8.    Hybrid and dynamic-screening necessity are not established. The dedicated controls            629

                                          now exist, but they return null results: hybrid GA/DE is statistically inseparable from       630

                                          DE-only, and periodic re-screening is inseparable from a fixed generation-1 worst-            631

                                          K set in all eight experiments. These mechanisms remain motivated defaults, not               632

                                          demonstrated sources of the headline gain.                                                    633

                                    0.9. Conclusions                                                                                  635


                                          This paper asked how an evolutionary resilience-planning method can use selective           636

                                    scenario exposure without grading final fronts on the same realizations that guided search.       637

                                    SHIELD-MOEA combines worst-K screening with disjoint search and evaluation draws                  638

                                    and reports sampled worst-envelope hypervolume alongside the mean. The experiments                639

                                    support this separation as a reproducible evaluation design; periodic updating itself does        640

                                    not improve front quality relative to a fixed worst-K control.                                    641

                                          The full method has pooled mean hypervolume 5.09% above matched-repair NSGA-                642

                                    II and 5.56% above plain NSGA-II over eight experiments and 30 seeds per stochastic               643

                                    method. It wins all 32 Holm-corrected comparisons with stochastic baselines; sixteen              644

                                    additional deterministic-rule gaps are descriptive and favor SHIELD-MOEA. The method              645

                                    retains a 6.04% mean margin on unseen stress and a 5.36% margin on sampled worst-case             646

                                    hypervolume. Across four SimBench networks, CIGRE MV, and IEEE 33-bus, its mapped                 647

                                    compositions improve aggregate AC feasibility over no planning (0.685 versus 0.389) but           648

                                    trail matched-repair NSGA-II (0.694).                                                             649

                                          Component attribution is narrower. Repair contributes most. Screening reduces               650

                                    recorded objective calls; no mean-hypervolume difference is detected, and equivalence             651

                                    is not established. Dynamic re-screening does not beat fixed worst-K; hybrid variation            652

                                    does not beat DE-only; and the resilience objective is a decision-support feature rather than     653

                                    a performance feature. Under the fixed composition mapping, removing outage-aware                 654

                                    search reduces six-network AC feasibility from 0.685 to 0.574 and raises mean maximum             655

                                    loading from 52.7% to 67.0%.                                                                      656

                                          Post-event recovery, element-level N − k modeling, monetary calibration, a second in-       657

                                    dependently optimized benchmark system, and nodal AC differentiation remain necessary             658

                                    extensions. Within the tested setting, the main contribution is a scenario-selective planning     659

                                    workflow with disjoint final evaluation, a resolved repair effect, and explicit evidence on       660

                                    where screening and hybridization do not add quality.                                             661
