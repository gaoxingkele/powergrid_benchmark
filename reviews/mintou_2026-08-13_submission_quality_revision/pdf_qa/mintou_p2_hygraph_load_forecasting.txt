                                        Abstract                                                                                                        1


                                        Cross-correlated regional load series motivate graph and attention mechanisms, but single-                      2

                                        run accuracy tables rarely isolate whether cross-series aggregation itself contributes. CSA-                    3

                                        LoadNet combines a shared temporal encoder, cross-series attention, and a compact predic-                       4

                                        tion head for day-ahead multi-region load forecasting. Ten seeded runs under a leakage-free                     5

                                        temporal protocol show that, on the six-country Open Power System Data benchmark, the                           6

                                        full model improves 24-hour mean absolute percentage error over a multilayer perceptron                         7

                                        (0.0323 versus 0.0337; Holm-adjusted p = 0.0085) and over the no-aggregation ablation                           8

                                        (0.0323 versus 0.0346; p = 0.0011). No difference among Poincaré, Euclidean, and equal-                         9

                                        weight parameterizations is resolved at the available precision. We additionally rebuild                       10

                                        Ausgrid as an exact 12-leaf, four-region, one-root hierarchy and evaluate independent,                         11

                                        bottom-up, top-down, and OLS reconciliation with ten seeds for every method. DLinear                           12

                                        with bottom-up reconciliation is best (hierarchy-weighted sMAPE 0.2802); CSA-LoadNet                           13

                                        with OLS reaches 0.2895, while both coherent methods have zero structural violation. The                       14

                                        results support a narrow day-ahead aggregation claim on OPSD, distinguish accuracy from                        15

                                        coherence, and reject general architectural superiority.                                                       16

                                        0.1. Introduction                                                                                              20


                                             Day-ahead load forecasts inform unit commitment, cross-border exchange scheduling,                        21

                                        reserve dimensioning, and distribution-level flexibility procurement. In multi-region                          22

                                        forecasting, such as countries in an interconnection or profile classes in a distribution                      23

                                        network, the individual series share weather regimes, calendar structure, and economic                         24

                                        rhythms. This raises a practical question: does cross-series information improve on forecasts                  25

                                        constructed independently for each series?                                                                     26

                                          These findings leave a component-level question unresolved. Cross-series models               36

                                     are often evaluated as complete architectures, making it difficult to determine whether            37

                                     aggregation itself helps, whether a particular weighting geometry matters, and whether             38

                                     any gain persists across horizons and aggregation structures.                                      39

                                          CSA-LoadNet is a compact neural forecaster in which a shared encoder processes each           40

                                     region’s load history and cross-series attention aggregates the other encodings. Attention         41

                                     weights can use Poincaré distance with fixed or learned curvature, Euclidean distance, or          42

                                     equal averaging. Each mechanism is an independent switch.                                          43

                                          The primary component comparison contains the full model and five single-switch               44

                                     ablations. A targeted ten-seed comparison with MLP is conducted on OPSD and SimBench               45

                                     after a preliminary three-seed architecture screen; the screen is reported separately and          46

                                     is not treated as equal-strength confirmatory evidence. Ausgrid solar-home data provide            47

                                     an exact hierarchical test against ten models under common reconciliation. Pairwise                48

                                     comparisons use two-sided Mann–Whitney U tests with Holm correction within each                    49

                                     dataset–horizon block and its primary metric.                                                      50

                                          The paper makes three contributions:                                                          51


                                     1.    A component-identifiable cross-series forecaster. CSA-LoadNet separates the shared           52

                                           temporal encoder, cross-series aggregation, distance weighting, and curvature choice         53

                                           into independently testable switches. This design distinguishes the value of pooling         54

                                           other series from the value of a particular weighting geometry.                              55

                                     2.    A multi-setting evaluation of aggregation, horizon, and hierarchy. Strict temporal           56

                                           splits are used on OPSD and SimBench at 1 h and 24 h horizons. An exact 12-leaf,             57

                                           four-region, one-root Ausgrid hierarchy then compares independent forecasts with             58

                                           bottom-up, top-down, and OLS reconciliation under a common summing structure.                59

                                     3.    An empirical map of where aggregation helps. On OPSD at 24 h, CSA-LoadNet                    60

                                           improves over the targeted MLP comparator and the no-aggregation ablation. The               61

                                           tested weight forms remain unresolved, and the method does not lead at OPSD 1 h or           62

                                           on the exact Ausgrid hierarchy. The result identifies a day-ahead aggregation effect         63

                                           while separating forecast accuracy from structural coherence.                                64

                                     0.2. Related Work                                                                                  71


                                          Three threads frame this study: the deep-architecture lineage of short-term load              72

                                     forecasting, cross-series and graph-structured forecasting, and rigorous evaluations that          73

                                     compare complex forecasters with simple baselines. An extended review with full citation           74

                                     context accompanies the released evidence package.                                                 75



                                     0.2.1. Deep Architectures for Short-Term and Day-Ahead Load Forecasting                            76


                                          Recurrent networks were among the first deep models to displace classical load                77

                                     forecasters. Building on the LSTM cell [9], Kong et al. demonstrate short-term forecasting         78

                                     at the residential scale [10]. DeepAR extends shared training to large panels of related           79

                                     series [11], an idea reflected in CSA-LoadNet’s shared encoder. Temporal convolutional             80




                                                                                           https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics1010000
Version August 13, 2026 submitted to Electronics                                                                                  3 of 19



                                     networks offer a lower-cost alternative [12], while the Temporal Fusion Transformer [13]               81

                                     and Informer [14] extend attention-based forecasting.                                                  82

                                          Applied load forecasting increasingly uses hybrid models, including LSTM–CNN                      83

                                     fusion [15], BiLSTM–Transformer combinations [16], decomposition pipelines [17], and                   84

                                     LSTM–XGBoost ensembles [18]. Evidence for the added component, however, is commonly                    85

                                     limited to one accuracy table. Multi-seed distributions and component-level tests remain               86

                                     uncommon.                                                                                              87



                                     0.2.2. Cross-Series and Graph-Structured Load Forecasting                                              88


                                           A second thread exploits correlation among regional or nodal series. LSTNet models               89

                                     dependence through joint convolutional–recurrent channels [19]. Graph formulations                     90

                                     emerged in traffic forecasting [20], and MTGNN learns adjacency end to end [21]. Power-                91

                                     load examples include LoadSeer [4], GCN–BiLSTM–Adaboost [1], spatio-temporal graph                     92

                                     transformers [2], and GCN–Transformer integration [3]. A parallel literature treats multiple           93

                                     series as a hierarchy requiring reconciliation [22,23], with Ausgrid as a common testbed               94

                                     [24].                                                                                                  95

                                           These graph-based studies propose increasingly elaborate weighting mechanisms.                   96

                                     Their comparisons rarely test the selected weighting against equal averaging under an                  97

                                     identical seeded protocol. Consequently, the contribution of aggregation can be conflated              98

                                     with that of its parameterization.                                                                     99



                                     0.2.3. Simple Baselines and Honest Evaluation                                                          100


                                           The third thread concerns evaluation discipline. Sound out-of-sample practice was                101

                                     codified early [25], and temporal leakage from naive cross-validation is well documented               102

                                     [26]. Common metrics also have known failure modes; MAPE becomes unstable near zero                    103

                                     loads [27], directly motivating the SimBench and Ausgrid choices. Diebold–Mariano testing              104

                                     [28] made statistical comparison a standard forecasting question. The M competitions [6,7],            105

                                     DLinear [5], and later best-practice guides [8] further emphasize strong simple baselines.             106

                                           This evaluation literature mainly operates at whole-model granularity. The present               107

                                     study extends it to individual forecaster components, using multiplicity-corrected tests to            108

                                     determine which component claims survive. Section 6.3 applies that logic to the aggregation            109

                                     weight forms.                                                                                          110

                                     0.3. Datasets, Tasks, and Split Protocol                                                               112

                                     0.3.1. Datasets and Forecast Tasks                                                                     113


                                          We evaluate on three public datasets chosen to span three distinct multi-series                   114

                                     regimes: country-level national loads, distribution-system load profiles, and a cus-                   115

                                     tomer/region/system hierarchy (Table 1).                                                               116

                                          Each horizon-h task predicts each series’ load h hours beyond the forecast origin from            117

                                     the preceding 168 h of that pool’s history; sliding the origin hourly across the test segment          118

                                     produces the full evaluation trajectory. The 24 h task is the day-ahead task around which              119

                                     this paper’s claims are organized.                                                                     120

                                     0.4.1. Shared Temporal Encoder                                                                                      184


                                          For each series i in the pool, the past 168 hours of per-series z-normalized load                              185

                                     are passed through a shared multilayer perceptron 168 → 96 → 48, yielding a temporal                                186

                                     encoding hi ∈ R48 . Sharing one encoder across all series follows the cross-learning principle                      187

                                     established by panel forecasters such as DeepAR [11]: every series’ history contributes                             188

                                     gradient signal to the same temporal features, which matters when pools are small (6–17                             189

                                     series here).                                                                                                       190

                                     0.6.3. Aggregation Helps, but the Tested Weight Forms Do Not Separate                                   307


                                          Cross-series aggregation and aggregation-weight form produce different empirical                   308

                                     conclusions. The no-aggregation comparison separates on OPSD at 24 h, whereas the                       309

                                     ten-seed comparisons among Poincaré, Euclidean, equal-weight, and curvature variants do                 310

                                     not resolve a difference:                                                                               311


                                     •     Poincaré vs. Euclidean distance weights: not separable in any of the five settings                312

                                           (Holm p = 1 in all five; largest observed mean gap below 0.5% relative).                          313

                                     •     Poincaré vs. equal-weight averaging: not separable in any setting (Holm p = 1 in all              314

                                           five). Learned attention coefficients, whatever their geometry, do not demonstrably               315

                                           beat uniform pooling of the neighbor encodings at these pool sizes.                               316

                                     •     Learnable vs. fixed curvature: not separable in any setting (Holm p = 1 in all five). The         317

                                           adaptive-curvature parameter, the most distinctive ingredient of the original design,             318

                                           has no measurable effect.                                                                         319

                                     0.7. Discussion                                                                                           394

                                     0.7.1. Aggregation, Weighting, and Hierarchical Coherence                                                 395


                                           The ten-seed component comparison supports cross-series aggregation over the no-                    396

                                     aggregation control on OPSD at 24 h. The targeted MLP comparison separates in the same                    397

                                     setting. By contrast, the analysis does not distinguish Poincaré, Euclidean, equal-weight, or             398

                                     curvature variants, so the observed benefit concerns aggregation rather than the geometry                 399

                                     used to weight neighbors. The preliminary three-seed architecture screen explains the                     400

                                     choice of MLP for confirmation but does not carry the same evidential weight.                             401

                                           The exact Ausgrid experiment answers a different question. It constructs all four                   402

                                     regional aggregates and the root from the same 12 leaves and applies common reconciliation                403

                                     violations, yet DLinear remains more accurate than CSA-LoadNet. Coherence is therefore                   405

                                     a constraint that can be enforced after forecasting; it is not evidence that the underlying              406

                                     forecaster is accurate.                                                                                  407

                                          Taken together, the experiments locate rather than generalize the benefit. Pool context             408

                                     is useful in the OPSD day-ahead setting, whereas additional weighting complexity is                      409

                                     unsupported at the evaluated pool sizes. Multiple temporal origins and independently                     410

                                     repeated weather years would test whether this pattern persists beyond the selected splits.              411



                                     0.7.2. Why Aggregation Helps at 24 h and Not at 1 h                                                      412


                                        The horizon asymmetry in Figure 3 admits a simple reading. At one-hour lead, each                     413

                                    country’s own most recent observations carry nearly all recoverable information, and any                  414

                                    capacity spent on pool context competes with capacity spent on the target’s own dynamics                  415

                                    — hence a plain MLP on the target window wins outright. At day-ahead lead, the target’s                   416

                                    terminal observations are 24 h stale; what generalizes across that gap is shared structure                417




                                                                                                 https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics1010000
Version August 13, 2026 submitted to Electronics                                                                               15 of 19



                                    — synchronized daily and weekly shapes, common weather-driven regimes — which is                      418

                                    exactly what a pooled context vector over five neighboring national loads can supply.                 419

                                    The TemporalOnly column supports this reading quantitatively: its deficit against the full            420

                                    model is decisive at OPSD 24 h (p = 0.0011), directionally present but non-significant at the         421

                                    1 h settings, and absent on Ausgrid where the pool is uninformative. We offer this as an              422

                                    interpretation consistent with the evidence, not as a tested mechanism.                               423



                                     0.7.3. Implications for the Cross-Series Forecasting Literature                                      424


                                          Our results neither reject cross-series modeling nor support additional weighting               425

                                     complexity. In the OPSD multi-region day-ahead setting, aggregation improves MAPE by                 426

                                     6.5% and survives both the prespecified test and paired sensitivity analysis. By contrast, no        427

                                     difference among the tested weight forms is resolved in any setting; equivalence within a            428

                                     prespecified tolerance was not tested. This contrast shows why mechanism claims require              429

                                     repeated component-level evaluation. In this study, the OPSD and SimBench decision sets              430

                                     were feasible on CPU, while the larger exact-hierarchy rerun used a single RTX 3090.                 431

                                     0.8. Limitations                                                                                     433


                                     1.    Claims are confined to day-ahead, pool-level multi-region forecasting. The sin-                434

                                           gle significance-backed superiority result is OPSD 24 h (vs. MLP and vs. the no-               435

                                           aggregation ablation). We make no claim at the 1 h horizon — on OPSD 1 h the                   436

                                           method significantly loses to the MLP (Holm p = 0.0348) — and no claim on SimBench,            437

                                           where both horizons are inseparable from the MLP (24 h: p = 0.084 with the MLP                 438

                                           mean ahead; 1 h: p = 1 with means nearly identical).                                           439

                                     2.    The method does not transfer to the exact customer/region/system hierarchy as im-              440

                                           plemented. Under the common OLS reconciliation, CSA-LoadNet significantly loses                441

                                           to DLinear (Holm p < 0.001), while its contrasts against MLP, TCN, and PatchTST-               442

                                           lite remain unresolved under the primary family. Bottom-up, top-down, and OLS                  443

                                           reconciliation are included and the coherent variants satisfy the accounting identi-           444

                                           ties exactly, but reconciliation does not make the proposed forecaster more accurate           445

                                           than the strongest linear baseline. MinT with covariance shrinkage and end-to-end              446

                                           coherence-constrained training remain untested.                                                447

                                     3.    The weight-form inseparability is a bounded negative. It holds for pools of 6–17               448

                                           series on these three public benchmarks under our training budget; larger pools,               449

                                           sparser correlation structure, or genuinely tree-structured populations could sepa-            450

                                           rate the weight parameterizations. Our finding licenses skepticism, not a universal            451

                                           impossibility statement.                                                                       452

                                     4.    Bounded training budgets. All models, baselines included, use compact configura-               453

                                           tions with bounded epochs and strided training samples. OPSD and SimBench were                 454

                                           trained on CPU and the exact Ausgrid experiment on one RTX 3090. Budgets are                   455

                                           matched within each comparison block, but absolute errors and even rankings could              456

                                           change under architecture-specific large-scale tuning.                                         457

                                     5.    One fixed hierarchy and one temporal split. The corrected Ausgrid study removes                458

                                           the leaf/aggregate inconsistency and uses ten seeds for every model, but it still              459

                                           tests one deterministic 12-leaf grouping and one chronological split. Alternative              460

                                           geographic groupings, rolling origins, and larger customer populations could yield             461

                                           different reconciliation rankings.                                                             462

                                     6.    No exogenous weather inputs. All models forecast from load history and calendar                463

                                           information alone; weather-aware extensions remain untested on real data in this               464

                                           study, and no claim about them is made.                                                        465




                                                                                             https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics1010000
Version August 13, 2026 submitted to Electronics                                                                               16 of 19



                                     7.    Weather-year sensitivity is untested. The OPSD conclusions rest on a single chrono-            466

                                           logical split of the 2015–2018 period, whose test segment (October 2017 through                467

                                           December 2018, Table 1) spans essentially one weather-year. Because all inputs are             468

                                           load history and calendar features, an unusually mild or extreme weather-year could            469

                                           shift both absolute errors and the size of the cross-series aggregation benefit — syn-         470

                                           chronized weather regimes are precisely the shared structure we conjecture the pooled          471

                                           context exploits (Section 7.2). The findings have not been replicated across alternative       472

                                           multi-year split positions, and we make no claim of weather-year robustness.                   473

                                     0.9. Conclusions                                                                                     475


                                          This study determines which components of a cross-series attention forecaster earn              476

                                     empirical support. On the public six-country OPSD benchmark, CSA-LoadNet significantly               477

                                     outperforms the ten-seed MLP comparator at 24 h (mean MAPE 0.0323 versus 0.0337; Holm                478

                                     p = 0.0085). It also outperforms its no-aggregation ablation (Holm p = 0.0011) over ten              479

                                     seeded runs under a leakage-free temporal protocol. The remaining four compact neural                480

                                     families were screened with three seeds and do not carry the same confirmatory weight.               481

                                     Cross-series aggregation is therefore supported only for the stated day-ahead multi-region           482

                                     setting.                                                                                             483

                                          The form of the aggregation weights is not supported as a differentiator. No difference         484

                                     among hyperbolic, Euclidean, equal-weight, and fixed-curvature variants is resolved in the           485

                                     tested non-hierarchical settings; equivalence was not tested. Outside the OPSD day-ahead             486

                                     setting, the method loses at 1 h on OPSD, does not separate from MLP on SimBench, and                487

                                     loses to DLinear on the exact Ausgrid hierarchy. Bottom-up and OLS reconciliation make               488

                                     forecasts coherent but do not change that ranking. The resulting contribution is a setting-          489

                                     specific aggregation effect, a clear separation between accuracy and coherence, and an               490

                                     empirical limit on additional weighting complexity. The public datasets, full configurations,        491

                                     per-seed results, and hierarchy construction are released to support reproduction and                492

                                     extension.                                                                                           493
