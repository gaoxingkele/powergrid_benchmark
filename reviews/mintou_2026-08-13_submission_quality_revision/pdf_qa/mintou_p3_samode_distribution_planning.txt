                                          Abstract                                                                                                       1


                                          Distribution utilities allocate limited budgets among feeder reinforcement, distributed-                       2

                                          energy-resource installations, storage, and automation upgrades. CARS-MODE is a binary                         3

                                          multi-objective differential evolution algorithm combining self-adaptive control parameters,                   4

                                          a success-driven two-strategy mutation pool, constraint-aware budget repair, and crowding-                     5

                                          based diversity preservation. On a reproducible SimBench-derived benchmark with 18                             6

                                          subnetworks, 72 candidate actions, five objectives, and seven scenarios, CARS-MODE                             7

                                          attains pooled mean hypervolume 0.04218, 6.22% above NSGA-II with matched budget                               8

                                          repair. After removing deterministic-output pseudo-replication, it has a higher mean in all                    9

                                          56 stochastic-baseline comparisons and 55 remain significant after within-scenario Holm                       10

                                          correction; seven Weighted Sum point comparisons are reported descriptively. Direct                           11

                                          DE controls are also separated: CARS-MODE exceeds GDE3 and NSDE by 8.57% and                                  12

                                          8.52%, respectively, with significant wins in all 14 scenario-level comparisons. A combined                   13

                                          fixed-parameter, single-strategy control is nominally 0.60% higher and remains unresolved                     14

                                          in all seven scenarios, so the proxy evidence does not attribute the gain to the parameter-                   15

                                          and-strategy adaptation bundle. A composition-level alternating-current check on four                         16

                                          SimBench medium-voltage networks shows that proxy ranking is not electrical ranking:                          17

                                          CARS-MODE reaches a feasible rate of 0.611, compared with 0.500 without planning and                          18

                                          0.667 for NSGA-II. The study therefore supports the constrained multi-objective framework                     19

                                          against the tested controls while treating adaptation and physical transfer as bounded                        20

                                          findings.                                                                                                     21

                                          Evolutionary multi-objective algorithms are the workhorse of this literature, but two         33

                                    practical frictions persist. The first is algorithmic: differential evolution (DE), one of the      34

                                    strongest continuous optimizers, is sensitive to its control parameters and mutation strategy       35

                                    [1], and the planning problem is binary, budget-constrained, and rugged. A single fixed DE          36

                                    configuration can therefore stall. Self-adaptive DE variants (jDE [2], SaDE [3]) addressed          37

                                    this limitation on continuous benchmarks two decades ago, whereas many energy-planning              38

                                    studies still use either fixed-parameter DE or an off-the-shelf NSGA-II. The second friction        39

                                    is evidential: studies based on private networks or unchecked objective proxies are difficult       40

                                    to reproduce and easy to over-interpret. Comparative optimizer research emphasizes                  41

                                    consistent evaluation protocols [4]. In this study, repeated-run statistical comparisons are        42

                                    therefore paired with an explicit power-flow check of the surrogate planning objectives.            43

                                          CARS-MODE is a binary multi-objective DE with four switchable mechanisms. It                  44

                                    self-adapts each individual’s scale factor F and crossover rate CR in the jDE manner. A             45

                                    success-driven pool selects between rand/1 and best/1 mutation, deterministic repair                46

                                    removes low-benefit actions until the budget is met, and crowding truncation preserves              47

                                    front diversity.                                                                                    48

                                          The evidence uses public data and reproducible implementations. SimBench deter-               49

                                    ministically supplies the benchmark [5], while nine baselines and four ablations use real           50

                                    algorithm implementations, including pymoo references where available [6]. Standard hy-             51

                                    pervolume is computed under fixed method-independent normalization. Every stochastic                52

                                    method uses 30 seeded runs and Holm-corrected Mann–Whitney tests; the deterministic                 53

                                    Weighted Sum rule is compared descriptively.                                                        54

                                          The study’s defining design decision is a two-level evaluation. The planning objectives       55

                                    are engineering indices computed from SimBench subnet statistics — fast enough to support           56

                                    a rigorous statistical protocol, but proxies nonetheless. We therefore add a second level: the      57

                                    compromise plan composition of every method is mapped onto four real SimBench MV                    58

                                    networks and checked with pandapower AC load flow [7] across six stress scenarios (peak             59

                                    load, load growth, extreme growth, high DER infeed, and an N-1 contingency). The two                60

                                    levels disagree in an instructive way, and we report the disagreement as a finding rather           61

                                    than hiding it.                                                                                     62

                                          The contributions of this paper are:                                                          63


                                    1.    A constraint-aware multi-objective DE framework with testable mechanism groups.               64

                                          CARS-MODE integrates jDE parameter control, a success-driven two-strategy pool,               65

                                          deterministic budget repair, and crowding diversity. Repair and diversity are removed         66

                                          individually; a combined control replaces adaptive parameters and the strategy                67

                                          pool with fixed F/CR and rand/1. The design therefore separates the framework-                68

                                          scenario (Sections 2 and 5).                                                                  75

                                    0.2. Related Work                                                                                    94


                                        Three threads frame this work: distribution network expansion planning with DER                  95

                                    and storage, evolutionary and swarm metaheuristics in power system planning, and self-               96

                                    adaptive differential evolution with constraint handling.                                            97



                                    0.2.1. Distribution Network Expansion Planning with DER and Storage                                  98


                                          Active distribution network planning has moved from single-objective conductor                 99

                                    sizing to portfolio decisions that co-optimize reinforcement, DER siting, storage, and               100

                                    flexibility. Recent surveys map this shift: Prenc [8] catalogues the optimization principles         101

                                    applied in planning and operation of active distribution networks, and Saldaña-González et           102

                                    al. [9] organize the elements of modern planning models, including the entry of generative-          103

                                    AI scenario tools. On the modeling side, robustness to uncertainty dominates: Liu et al. [10]        104

                                    plan expansions under distributional uncertainty via Wasserstein-distance ambiguity sets             105

                                    and dual relaxation, and Wang et al. [11] coordinate source–network–storage expansion                106

                                    against WGAN-GP-generated scenarios. Reliability-driven formulations add restoration                 107

                                    and islanding to reinforcement choices [12], while Ferreira et al. [13] extend the planning          108

                                    boundary upward to the transmission–distribution interface, and Chen et al. [14] specialize          109

                                    it to hybrid AC/DC campus networks for data centers. Closest to our task, He et al. [15]             110

                                    co-optimize distribution networks with storage and electric vehicles using an improved               111

                                    NSGA-II, and Alrashidi et al. [16] integrate DG, capacitor banks, and EV charging stations           112

                                    in radial feeders through a classification-based global optimization scheme.                         113

                                          Across this thread the algorithmic engine is almost always a Pareto-based genetic              114

                                    algorithm or a mathematical-programming reformulation; DE appears rarely, and when                   115

                                    the engine is improved, the improvement is seldom isolated by ablation. Equally relevant             116

                                    to us is what these papers evaluate on: private or single test systems are the norm, and             117

                                    the mapping from optimization objective to AC feasibility is usually asserted rather than            118

                                    measured. Our study is designed to make this mapping explicit and measurable.                        119



                                    0.2.2. Evolutionary and Swarm Metaheuristics in Power System Planning                                120


                                         The broader planning literature in energy venues provides many metaheuristic vari-              121

                                    ants. Qi et al. [17] apply enhanced beluga whale optimization to vulnerability-driven                122

                                    storage planning; Demirbas et al. [18] develop an enhanced coati algorithm for static and            123

                                    dynamic transmission expansion; and Cadena-Albuja et al. [19] compare DE with other                  124

                                    optimizers for energy-limited economic dispatch. Two recurring limitations motivate our              125

                                    protocol. First, hybrid or “improved” algorithms are not always accompanied by mecha-                126

                                    nism controls, making the source of added value difficult to identify. We therefore isolate          127

                                    repair and diversity and test the parameter-and-strategy controller against a combined               128




                                                                                                    https://doi.org/10.3390/en1010000
Version August 13, 2026 submitted to Energies                                                                                       4 of 22



                                    fixed control. Second, independent runs, rank-based tests, and standard indicators such as                129

                                    hypervolume [20] are applied unevenly in some application studies, and benchmarking                       130

                                    audits document the associated reproducibility risks [4,21,22]. We use a standard indicator,              131

                                    30 seeded runs, and Holm-corrected non-parametric tests so that single-digit percentage                   132

                                    differences can be distinguished from seed variation.                                                     133



                                    0.2.3. Self-Adaptive Differential Evolution and Constraint Handling                                       134


                                          DE [1] owes much of its practical success to parameter adaptation and constraint                    135

                                    handling. jDE [2] encodes F and CR in each individual and resamples them with a small                     136

                                    probability per generation. SaDE [3] additionally learns mutation-strategy probabilities                  137

                                    from recent successes; Das and Suganthan [23] review this lineage. Success-history adap-                  138

                                    tation (SHADE) [24] and composite strategy–parameter schemes [25] extend the same                         139

                                    principle, while Ahmad et al. [26] survey recent developments. Multi-objective DE vari-                   140

                                    ants place these mechanisms under Pareto selection, commonly using non-dominated                          141

                                    sorting and crowding [27] or decomposition [28]. Constraint-handling alternatives include                 142

                                    penalties, feasibility-first domination, and repair [29,30]. Repair is especially attractive for          143

                                    knapsack-like budgets because it returns evaluations to the fundable region.                              144

                                          The reviewed studies provide limited evidence on combining jDE/SaDE-style adapta-                   145

                                    tion and greedy budget repair inside a binary multi-objective DE for distribution-planning                146

                                    portfolios and then inspecting the resulting plans with AC power flow. Our controls isolate               147

                                    repair and diversity and compare the full controller with a fixed-parameter, single-strategy              148

                                    alternative. Because the latter changes two adaptive subcomponents together, it tests their               149

                                    joint contribution rather than identifying either one separately. The AC check is important               150

                                    because adaptation is commonly justified by indicator gains alone; here the joint proxy-                  151

                                    level contrast is unresolved, while the selected compromise plans show a favorable but                    152

                                    exploratory electrical pattern (Section 6.3).                                                             153



                                    0.2.4. Gap Statement                                                                                      154


                                         In summary, distribution-planning studies supply the task context but do not consis-                 155

                                    tently isolate algorithmic components or validate proxy objectives electrically. Metaheuristic            156

                                    studies supply algorithmic variants, while the DE literature supplies adaptation mech-                    157

                                    anisms, but their combination in budget-constrained binary planning with power-flow                       158

                                    validation remains limited. Table 1 compares these features in representative recent works.               159

                                    This paper studies that intersection through a reproducible self-adaptive multi-objective                 160

                                    DE, component-level ablations, repeated statistical tests, and a separate AC inspection that              161

                                    explicitly reports disagreement with the proxy ranking.                                                   162

                                    0.4.1. jDE Self-Adaptive Control Parameters                                                                        248


                                         Each individual carries its own ( Fi , CRi ), initialized at (0.5, 0.9). In every generation,                 249

                                    an individual redraws Fi ∼ U (0.1, 0.9) and CRi ∼ U (0, 1) with probability τ = 0.1 [2].                           250

                                    Per-individual parameter carrying allows settings associated with surviving trials to persist,                     251

                                    while stochastic resampling continues to explore the ranges. The sensitivity study in Section                      252

                                    6.4 sweeps τ and finds a flat response.                                                                            253



                                    0.4.2. Success-Driven Two-Strategy Pool                                                                            254


                                         Mutation chooses between DE/rand/1 (a uniformly drawn base vector) and DE/best/1                              255

                                    (a base vector drawn from the current feasible-first non-dominated front), with probabilities                      256

                                    proportional to recent success masses [3]. A trial is successful if it constraint-dominates its                    257

                                    parent through lower violation or, at equal violation, Pareto improvement. Success masses                          258

                                    decay by 0.95 per generation and have a floor of 0.2, preventing either strategy from being                        259

                                    excluded. The pool combines exploratory rand/1 with the more intensive best/1 and lets                             260

                                    observed successes determine their sampling probabilities.                                                         261



                                    0.4.3. Constraint-Aware Budget Repair                                                                              262


                                         Every decoded plan that exceeds the budget is repaired deterministically. The selected                        263

                                    action with the lowest aggregate benefit-to-cost ratio is removed repeatedly until the plan is                     264

                                    affordable. This avoids spending evaluations and selection slots on penalty-carrying plans                         265

                                    and concentrates search near the budget boundary. The 0.82x scenario directly stresses this                        266

                                    mechanism.                                                                                                         267



                                    0.4.4. Crowding-Based Diversity Preservation                                                                       268


                                         Environmental selection is elitist (µ + λ): parents and trials are pooled, sorted by                          269

                                    constraint-domination fronts, and truncated to the population size with crowding distance                          270

                                    [27] breaking ties in the last admitted front. The corresponding ablation replaces crowding                        271

                                    with a random tie-break. Motivation: with only 40 individuals covering a five-objective                            272

                                    front, losing spread control collapses the front to a few clusters — the ablation quantifies                       273

                                    exactly how much (-33.56%, Section 6.2).                                                                           274

                                    0.5.1. Methods Compared                                                                                  289


                                          Table 4 lists the fourteen methods: CARS-MODE, nine baselines, and four ablations.                 290

                                    NSGA-II, NSGA-II with matched repair, MOEA/D, GDE3, and NSDE are pymoo imple-                            291

                                    mentations [6] on the identical constrained problem; the two direct DE controls operate                  292

                                    in [0, 1] and use the same fixed 0.5 binary decoder. GA, binary PSO [31], and standard DE                293

                                    algorithms on the same method-independent objective definition.                                          296

                                          Ablation-NoDER is a problem-variant probe rather than an operator switch: excluded                 297

                                    candidates receive prohibitive cost in the search problem only, while evaluation stays in                298

                                    the full objective space, so its hypervolumes remain comparable. We flag this asymmetry                  299

                                    wherever it matters (Section 6.2).                                                                       300



                                    0.5.2. Protocol                                                                                          301


                                          Each stochastic method runs 30 independent seeds per experiment. Weighted Sum                      302

                                    is deterministic: the uniform 2940-row implementation archive retains its repeated invo-                 303

                                    cations, but its effective inferential sample size is one per experiment. Thus, seed-level               304

                                    tests cover twelve stochastic opponents; the seven Weighted Sum gaps are descriptive. All                305

                                    population-based methods share population 40 and 40 generations. Stochastic seeds are                    306

                                    derived by hashing the (paper, experiment, method) triple with the run index, so method                  307

                                    cells do not share a random stream.                                                                      308



                                    0.5.3. Evaluation Metric and Statistics                                                                  309


                                        The primary metric is the standard hypervolume of the feasible non-dominated front,                  310

                                    computed with pymoo’s exact indicator. Objectives use fixed per-experiment normalization                 311

                                    bounds obtained from a seeded reference sample: the empty plan, all 72 single-action plans,              312




                                                                                                 https://doi.org/10.3390/en1010000
Version August 13, 2026 submitted to Energies                                                                                         10 of 22



                                    and 2048 random feasible plans, followed by a 5% margin. The reference point is 1.1 in                       313

                                    every normalized dimension. The metric contains no method-aware term, and an empty                           314

                                    feasible front scores zero. We compare CARS-MODE with each stochastic opponent using                         315

                                    two-sided Mann–Whitney U tests per experiment (n = 30 per group) and apply Holm                              316

                                    correction over the twelve eligible opponents within each experiment [32] at α = 0.05.                       317

                                    Rank-biserial correlation and 5000-resample bootstrap intervals quantify effect size and                     318

                                    mean-difference uncertainty. Weighted Sum is reported as an n = 1 point comparison                           319

                                    0.8. Limitations                                                                                         525


                                          We state the boundaries of this study explicitly.                                                  526


                                    1.    Composition-level, not nodal, planning. The optimizer selects subnet-level actions,                527

                                          and the AC validation maps plan compositions onto concrete networks with fixed                     528

                                          rules that choose buses by stress heuristics. Node-level siting and sizing — where                 529

                                          method differentiation at the electrical layer would have to be demonstrated — is                  530

                                          not performed, and the AC stage’s per-method sample (72 binary cases from one                      531

                                          compromise plan per experiment) supports qualitative patterns, not significance tests.             532

                                          Consequently, the high-DER scenario’s No-Plan advantage is a mapping-rule artifact                 533

                                          (Section 6.3) rather than a genuine electrical finding.                                            534

                                    2.    Proxy objectives. The five planning objectives are analytic indices of SimBench subnet             535

                                          statistics, not power-flow quantities; Section 6.3 measures, rather than assumes, their            536

                                          relation to AC feasibility, and finds it imperfect. Claims about “planning quality” in             537

                                          this paper mean proxy hypervolume unless explicitly stated otherwise.                              538

                                    3.    Costs are not monetarily calibrated. Candidate costs are synthetic cost units derived              539

                                          from network statistics. No engineering-economic conclusion (payback, deferral                     540

                                          value) can be drawn before calibration against published utility investment figures.               541




                                                                                                        https://doi.org/10.3390/en1010000
Version August 13, 2026 submitted to Energies                                                                                19 of 22



                                    4.    Single benchmark family. All seven scenarios derive from one SimBench network                 542

                                          family. Standard distribution test systems (IEEE 33/69-bus) with nodal candidates             543

                                          are the natural second family and remain future work.                                         544

                                    5.    Deterministic planning stage. The planning-stage evaluation uses a single nominal             545

                                          operating point per scenario; stochastic load/DER/outage variation enters only at             546

                                          the AC validation stage. A scenario-stochastic planning stage would strengthen the            547

                                          robustness reading.                                                                           548

                                    6.    Baseline configuration caveats. MOEA/D’s failure here is a failure of the specific            549

                                          penalty-based pymoo configuration on this problem; a tuned decomposition method               550

                                          might be competitive, and the comparison should not be quoted against MOEA/D                  551

                                          generally.                                                                                    552

                                    7.    The direct DE comparison is still bounded. GDE3 and NSDE now provide Pareto-                  553

                                          based multi-objective DE controls under the same population and generation budget,            554

                                          but JADE-, SHADE-, and L-SHADE-derived multi-objective implementations remain                 555

                                          absent. The evidence supports CARS-MODE against the nine implemented baselines,               556

                                          not the entire adaptive-DE family.                                                            557

                                    0.9. Conclusions                                                                                    559


                                          This study evaluates a constraint-aware, strategy-pool multi-objective DE for distribution-560
                                    planning portfolios. On the SimBench-derived benchmark, CARS-MODE exceeds NSGA-II 561
                                    with matched repair by 6.22% pooled hypervolume across seven scenarios and 30 seeds 562
                                    per stochastic method. It has a higher mean in all 56 stochastic-baseline comparisons; 55 563
                                    are Holm-significant. Seven additional deterministic Weighted Sum comparisons are de- 564
                                    scriptive and favor CARS-MODE. Direct comparisons with GDE3 and NSDE yield margins 565
                                    of 8.57% and 8.52%, with significant wins in all fourteen scenario-level tests.                  566

                                          The component and electrical results impose a narrower interpretation. Budget repair 567
                                    and crowding diversity carry the proxy gain, decreasing hypervolume by 6.84% and 33.56% 568
                                    when removed. The combined parameter-and-strategy adaptation bundle is unresolved: its 569
                                    fixed-parameter, single-strategy control is 0.60% higher in pooled mean. In the descriptive 570
                                    pandapower composition check, the corresponding seed-0 plan maps to 0.569 overall 571
                                    AC feasibility versus 0.611 for the full method; this observation does not identify either 572
                                    adaptive subcomponent as the cause. The proxy winner is itself mid-pack electrically.            573

                                          CARS-MODE is therefore supported as a consistently higher-HV proxy optimizer 574
                                    across the seven tested scenarios. Its parameter-and-strategy controller is an implemented 575
                                    but empirically unresolved bundle, whereas repair and diversity carry the measured proxy 576
                                    gains. Separate parameter-only and strategy-only controls remain necessary for finer 577
                                    attribution. The disagreement between a 6.22% matched-baseline proxy advantage and the 578
                                    mid-pack AC rank is the central validity finding: proxy fronts should generate candidates 579
                                    for electrical checking rather than substitute for it. Nodal siting, multi-seed AC mapping, 580
                                    monetary calibration, a second benchmark family, and further multi-objective DE controls 581
                                    remain necessary extensions.                                                                     582
