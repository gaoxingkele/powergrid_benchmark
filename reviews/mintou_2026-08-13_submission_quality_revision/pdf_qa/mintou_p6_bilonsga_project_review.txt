BiLo-NSGA: Project-Level Local Search for Budget-Constrained
Power-Grid Portfolio Optimization
Yubin Lin, Jingbo Zhang, Xiaoyu Huang, Dishan Yang, Jiyu Li


                                       Economic and Technological Research Institute of State Grid Fujian Electric Power Co., Ltd., Fuzhou 350000,

                                       Abstract                                                                                                       1


                                       Utility review boards must select grid investment portfolios under hard annual budgets, yet                    2

                                       independent project scores ignore portfolio interactions and generic evolutionary operators                    3

                                       do not express changes in review terms. BiLo-NSGA embeds forward insertion, atomic                             4

                                       delete–insert substitution, dependency-aware bonuses, and feasibility recovery within                          5

                                       non-dominated sorting while recording accepted moves. On a reproducible benchmark                              6

                                       with 120 candidates, eight scenarios, and 30 seeds per stochastic method, BiLo-NSGA                            7

                                       attains pooled mean hypervolume 0.17190, 1.12% above NSGA-II. Across five stochastic                           8

                                       baselines, it has 37 positive mean differences in 40 comparisons, 36 Holm-significant wins,                    9

                                       and no significant loss; 16 gaps against two deterministic scoring rules are descriptive and                  10

                                       favor BiLo-NSGA. A 1600-neighbor Pareto local-search control is significantly lower in                        11

                                       all eight scenarios. Component evidence is asymmetric: removing forward insertion is                          12

                                       harmful in three scenarios under the primary family, although the ablation has the higher                     13

                                       pooled mean, whereas atomic substitution and standalone deletion are unresolved. Public                       14

                                       reliability and MTEP16 backtests provide descriptive external consistency. The evidence                       15

                                       supports project-level local search with scenario-dependent forward-side effects; atomic                      16

                                       substitution contributes reviewable replacement semantics rather than a demonstrated                          17

                                       accuracy gain.                                                                                                18



                                       Keywords: power grid investment planning; project portfolio selection; budget constraint;                     19

                                       multi-objective evolutionary optimization; NSGA-II; local search; decision traceability                       20

                                          BiLo-NSGA retains a standard NSGA-II kernel and changes the variation stage through           37

                                     0.2. Related Work                                                                                  87


                                          Our work sits at the intersection of three research threads: budget-constrained com-          88

                                     binatorial optimization and project portfolio selection, investment planning and project           89

                                     review practice in power grids, and local-search-enhanced multi-objective evolutionary             90

                                     algorithms (MOEAs). We review each thread in turn and then position BiLo-NSGA against              91

                                     them and against its companion method TRACE-MOEA.                                                  92



                                     0.2.1. Budget-Constrained Combinatorial Optimization and Project Portfolio Selection               93


                                          Selecting items under a budget is the multi-objective knapsack setting used by Zitzler        94

                                     and Thiele [1] to compare Pareto-based schemes. Problem-aware search is important in this          95

                                     landscape. Jaszkiewicz [2] shows that local search over scalarizing functions can improve          96

                                     on purely recombinative MOEAs, a result reviewed by Lust and Teghem [3] and later                  97

                                     knapsack taxonomies [4]. Many-objective knapsack problems further challenge standard               98

                                     MOEAs [5].                                                                                         99

                                          Portfolio studies respond with tailored search. Doerner et al. [6] apply Pareto ant           100

                                     colony optimization, while Carazo et al. [7] model dependencies, schedules, and resource           101

                                     limits with explicit feasibility handling. Energy applications include coupled risk–benefit        102

                                     portfolios [8] and hybrid exact–heuristic investment planning [9]. These methods usually           103

                                     enforce budgets after variation through penalties or repair. They do not define the neigh-         104

                                     borhood itself as insertion under slack, deletion under violation, and substitution near           105

                                     budget parity.                                                                                     106



                                     0.2.2. Power Grid Investment Planning and Project Review Decision Methods                          107


                                          Grid investment research divides between expansion optimization and multi-criteria            108

                                     review. Optimization studies coordinate transmission and storage [10], value reliability           109

                                     through interruption cost [11], assess grid-side storage returns [12], and balance battery         110

                                     investment against operating benefit [13]. These formulations usually size a specific asset        111

                                     class from a small set of alternatives rather than select from more than one hundred               112

                                     heterogeneous projects.                                                                            113

                                          Review practice commonly uses AHP [14] and TOPSIS [15]. Recent extensions add                 114

                                     objective weighting and explainability [16], while site-selection reviews show growing             115

                                     use of multi-objective optimization [17]. MCDM is traceable but scores projects largely            116

                                     independently, so it misses budget crowding-out and dependencies. Simulation-based                 117

                                     assessments of substations and commissioning delays [18] add network context but do                118

                                     not solve the portfolio search. The open gap is therefore a portfolio-aware method with a          119

                                     reviewable decision path [19].                                                                     120



                                     0.2.3. Local-Search-Enhanced Multi-Objective Evolutionary Algorithms                               121


                                           Hybridizing evolutionary search with local refinement is a long-standing multi-              122

                                     objective strategy. Ishibuchi and Murata [20] apply weighted-sum local moves after genetic         123

                                     variation, while Knowles and Corne [21] show that a (1 + 1) local search with a Pareto             124

                                     archive can approximate nondominated fronts competitively. NSGA-II [22], MOEA/D                    125

                                     [23], and NSGA-III [24] rely primarily on selection and recombination; Neri and Cotta [25]         126

                                     survey the many memetic extensions built around them.                                              127

                                           Power-system studies apply improved NSGA-II variants to distribution-network co-             128

                                     optimization [26], offshore-wind storage configuration [27], and resilient backbone-grid           129




                                                                                                  https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 13, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                                                     4 of 24



                                     planning [28]. Enhanced swarm methods address storage planning [29] and transmission                                     130

                                     expansion [30]. These methods modify initialization, operators, or stages, but their local                               131

                                     moves, when present, remain numeric perturbations inherited from continuous bench-                                       132

                                     marks.                                                                                                                   133

                                          Portfolio-scheduling studies also combine NSGA-II with Pareto local search [31]. More                               134

                                     recent methods tailor local refinement to cardinality and pre-assignment constraints [32].                               135

                                     However, these hybrids neither express moves as adding, removing, or substituting a                                      136

                                     reviewable project nor retain the accepted move sequence. BiLo-NSGA treats that domain-                                  137

                                     level trajectory as a traceable output.                                                                                  138



                                     0.2.4. Relation to TRACE-MOEA and Gap Statement                                                                          139


                                          TRACE-MOEA [33] addresses power-grid project review through preference-adaptive                                     140

                                     ranking, review-rule repair, and a trace archive. Its contribution acts at selection and                                 141

                                     ranking. BiLo-NSGA instead changes variation through forward insertion, atomic substi-                                   142

                                     tution, dependency-aware bonuses, and feasibility recovery. The two studies share the                                    143

                                     versioned candidate generator and the public NERC and MTEP16 source corpora. Their                                       144

                                     problem objectives, algorithm implementations, scenario definitions, run archives, selected                              145

                                     portfolios, and reported comparisons are paper-specific.                                                                 146

                                          The resulting gap is specific. Existing knapsack MOEAs generally handle budgets                                     147

                                     through penalties or post-variation repair. Grid MCDM is traceable but portfolio-blind,                                  148

                                     and memetic MOEAs express refinement numerically rather than as reviewable project                                       149

                                     decisions. BiLo-NSGA combines non-dominated sorting with forward insertion, atomic                                       150

                                     replacement, feasibility recovery, and a per-move decision trace. The claim concerns this                                151

                                     intersection rather than universal superiority over memetic optimization.                                                152

                                     0.3.2. Benchmark Construction from Public Data                                                                      175


                                          Because real utility investment-review records are institution-internal and unavailable                        176

                                     for publication, we derive a candidate pool deterministically from three public sources                             177

                                     (Table 1). The full derivation code is released; every rule below is inspectable.                                   178

                                          RTS-GMLC zone-level aggregates of load, branch ratings, permanent-outage rates,                                179

                                     generator capacity, and renewable share generate three candidate archetypes per zone.                               180

                                     Analytic functions map these aggregates to costs and benefits; for example, reinforcement                           181

                                     benefit scales with outage pressure, while renewable-support benefit scales with the gap                            182

                                     between load and installed renewable capacity. From SimBench, the sixteen subnets                                   183

                                     with the highest combined load and line-length stress each yield feeder-reinforcement,                              184

                                     storage-flexibility, and protection-automation candidates. Their attributes depend on                               185

                                     subnet load, line count, and the distributed-energy-resource gap. NERC report metadata                              186

                                     then adjusts attributes at the project-kind level. Candidates in the same zone or feeder                            187

                                     share a dependency group, producing 120 candidates in six kinds with a block structure.                             188

                                          We emphasize what this construction is and is not. It is a reproducible, public, engineering-                  189

                                     plausible proxy for the review problem – every attribute traces to public grid statistics or                        190

                                     public reliability-report metadata through published rules. It is not a set of expert-labeled                       191

                                     review outcomes, and its cost coefficients are in synthetic cost units, not calibrated currency                     192

                                     (Section 8).                                                                                                        193

                                     0.4. BiLo-NSGA                                                                                            209


                                           Figure 1 places two project-vocabulary moves between global offspring generation                    210

                                     and dependency-aware feasibility recovery. Forward insertion spends useful slack. Atomic                  211

                                     substitution removes one weak selection and evaluates one affordable replacement before                   212

                                     either change can be committed. The candidates then compete under the same constrained                    213

                                     non-dominated sorting rule as the baseline kernel. Forward insertion produces the only                    214

                                     significant local-operator gains; substitution supplies review semantics without a demon-                 215

                                     strated hypervolume gain.                                                                                 216

                                           BiLo-NSGA is a memetic multi-objective algorithm: a standard non-dominated sorting                  217

                                     kernel provides global search and Pareto pressure, and project-vocabulary local moves                     218

                                     provide budget-aware intensification and recorded decision events. Each component below                   219

                                     is individually switchable, enabling the one-switch and legacy-rule comparisons in Section                220

                                     0.4.1. Non-Dominated Sorting Kernel                                                                                       247


                                          The kernel is a binary NSGA-II with uniform crossover and bit-flip mutation at rate                                  248

                                     1/n. Constraint dominance ranks feasible solutions above infeasible ones and compares                                     249

                                     infeasible solutions by violation. Crowding distance truncates the population to 40 over                                  250

                                     40 generations. Keeping this standard kernel isolates the proposed contribution in the                                    251

                                     variation stage.                                                                                                          252



                                     0.4.2. Forward Insertion                                                                                                  253


                                          After variation, half of the offspring undergo a forward pass of depth at most eight.                                254

                                     While slack remains, the affordable unselected project with the highest equal-weight benefit-                             255

                                     to-cost ratio is tentatively inserted. The insertion is retained only if it improves normalized                           256

                                     scalarized fitness, defined as the sum of normalized objectives plus a violation penalty. The                             257

                                     pass stops at the first rejection. This move converts budget slack left by recombination into                             258

                                     a candidate improvement.                                                                                                  259

                                     0.4.3. Atomic Backward–Forward Substitution                                                           260


                                          The substitution pass has depth at most four. It removes the selected project with               261

                                     the lowest benefit-to-cost ratio only provisionally, uses the released budget to choose the           262

                                     highest-scoring affordable replacement, and evaluates the resulting portfolio once. Accep-            263

                                     tance commits both edits; rejection restores both. The pass stops at the first rejection and          264

                                     never proposes from a portfolio with fewer than two retained projects. This construction              265

                                     makes replacement reviewable as one decision rather than inferring it from unrelated insert           266

                                     and delete events. Section 6.3 shows that the semantic improvement does not yield a                   267

                                     significant hypervolume change.                                                                       268



                                     0.4.4. Dependency-Aware Move Bonus                                                                    269


                                          When ranking insertion candidates, the benefit-to-cost score is multiplied by 1.06               270

                                     if another member of the candidate’s dependency group is already selected. The bonus                  271

                                     represents the possibility that related investments, such as reinforcement and automation             272

                                     on the same feeder, form a coherent program. Section 6.3 treats the dependency-density                273

                                     control as a direct test of this design choice.                                                       274



                                     0.4.5. Feasibility Recovery                                                                           275


                                           Every initial or offspring portfolio that violates the budget is repaired deterministically.    276

                                     The selected project with the lowest benefit-to-cost ratio is removed until the portfolio             277

                                     is affordable. Because each step removes one selected project and the empty portfolio is              278

                                     feasible, repair terminates after at most ∥ x ∥0 drops. Each restoration step is recorded in the      279

                                     decision trace.                                                                                       280



                                     0.4.6. Per-Move Decision Trace                                                                        281


                                           Every accepted move–forward_insert, backward_substitute, and repair_drop–is                     282

                                     logged with its generation and project identifier(s). A substitution record stores both the           283

                                     removed and inserted project. The trail is a pure output: no trace statistic enters any               284

                                     objective, constraint, or selection decision. BiLo-NSGA logs on average 3668 moves per                285

                                     run, and 99.6% of projects appearing in the final non-dominated front are covered by at               286

                                     least one logged move (Table 4). These values measure archive production, not explanation             287

                                     quality.                                                                                              288

                                     0.6.4. External Consistency: NERC Rule-Based Backtest                                                             426


                                          Hypervolume measures optimization quality on the benchmark objectives; it cannot                             427

                                     show whether selected portfolios point at real risk. We therefore backtest selections against                     428

                                     cached NERC reliability reports using a rule designed to reduce construct circularity. Each                       429

                                     candidate receives a NERC-topic kind weight multiplied by a stress percentile computed                            430

                                     from raw RTS/SimBench attributes before the NERC-based adjustment of Section 3.2.                                 431

                                     The priority-capture ratio measures oversampling of high-priority candidates relative to                          432

                                     a uniform draw, with 1.0 denoting parity. Kendall’s τ measures rank alignment between                             433

                                     selection frequency and priority. Figure 6 shows both review scenarios.                                           434

                                          BiLo-NSGA oversamples documented-risk candidates, with capture ratios of 1.617                               435

                                     in budget-constrained selection and 1.301 in reliability-prioritized review. These values                         436

                                     are above NSGA-II in the first setting (1.575) and below it in the second (1.403). Kendall                        437

                                     τ values of 0.109 and 0.073 are not significant. AHP-TOPSIS has the highest nominal                               438

                                     alignment because its reliability-type attributes overlap the rule. The backtest therefore                        439

                                     indicates descriptive external consistency only, not method superiority or ground-truth                           440

                                     validity. The stress-percentile component is independently derived, but kind-level weights                        441

                                     retain construct overlap with pool generation.                                                                    442

                                     0.7. Discussion                                                                                         549


                                          Why the resolved operator effects are forward-side. Under deterministic repair,                    550

                                     populations remain near the budget boundary. Forward insertion can exploit residual slack               551

                                     directly. Atomic substitution must find a removal, an affordable replacement, and a joint               552

                                     scalar improvement in one step, while repair has already removed many weak projects.                    553

                                     This makes substitution opportunities rarer, but the explanation remains an inference from              554

                                     the observed event and ablation patterns rather than a proved mechanism.                                555

                                          The operator effects depend on the scenario. The no-forward ablation has a higher                  556

                                     pooled mean, yet the full method wins significantly in three scenarios; no corresponding                557

                                     significant gain exists for atomic substitution. The full substitution rule is retained for             558

                                     reviewable replacement semantics. A look-ahead or dominance-based acceptance rule is                 559

                                     the appropriate redesign target.                                                                     560

                                          What the external-consistency checks add. The NERC rule check (Section 6.4) shows               561

                                     concentration on documented risk patterns but retains construct overlap with pool gen-               562

                                     eration. The MTEP16 backtest (Section 6.5) uses independent historical outcomes and                  563

                                     2016-vintage features, but its project-level diagnostics ignore portfolio dependence and face        564

                                     a severe outcome base rate. Together, they test whether optimized portfolios immediately             565

                                     contradict two public-record views; they do not replace expert-labeled feasibility outcomes          566

                                     or calibrated costs.                                                                                 567

                                          Positioning against TRACE-MOEA. The companion study [33] uses a preference-                     568

                                     adaptive ranking layer, repair, and a trace archive over the same public candidate pipeline.         569

                                     BiLo-NSGA instead studies project-vocabulary variation under a hard budget. Its central              570

                                     questions concern forward insertion, atomic substitution, and the 6.35% cost of budget-              571

                                     blind search. The methods share candidate generation but not their core operators, scenario          572

                                     design, or analyses.                                                                                 573

                                          Practical implications. The method’s NSGA-II margin ranges from -0.64% at 0.75x                 574

                                     to +3.30% at 1.20x, with a 2.74x runtime factor; all evolutionary runs remain sub-second.            575

                                     A forward-only implementation is defensible where the review process does not require                576

                                     atomic replacement records. Pareto Local Search is a useful direct comparator but is                 577

                                     substantially weaker and slower under the matched neighbor budget.                                   578

                                          The recorded move history covers 99.6% of projects in the returned fronts with at least         579

                                     one operation, but explanation sufficiency has not been validated with human evaluators. If          580

                                     single-criterion regulatory alignment is dominant, AHP-TOPSIS remains competitive. The               581

                                     MTEP16 backtest supplies only weak-form evidence that BiLo-NSGA selections correlate                 582

                                     with real outcomes.                                                                                  583

                                           of utility planning engineers to independently rank a stratified subset of the 120             588

                                           candidates, and report the rank correlation between expert priorities and the bench-           589

                                           mark’s derived attributes; this converts the proxy claim into a measurable calibration         590

                                           statement.                                                                                     591

                                     0.8. Limitations                                                                                    614


                                           We state the boundaries of this study explicitly.                                             615


                                     1.    Proxy review task without expert labels. The 120 candidates and their attributes are          616

                                           derived from public grid statistics and reliability-report metadata through published         617

                                           rules; no expert-labeled review outcomes exist in the benchmark. All claims are               618

                                           therefore claims about algorithmic performance on a reproducible public proxy, not about      619

                                           validated real-world review quality. Expert-labeled subsets (with rank-correlation            620

                                           analysis against the proxy) are the direct remedy.                                            621

                                     2.    Costs are not monetarily calibrated. Candidate costs are synthetic cost units gen-            622

                                           erated from network aggregates. Calibrating coefficients against published utility            623

                                           investment figures is required before any engineering-economic claim.                         624

                                     3.    External checks do not reach the expert-label rung. The NERC rule backtest retains            625

                                           kind-level construct overlap with pool generation. MTEP16 outcome snapshots                   626

                                           were not used in fitting, but decision-time appendix status is prognostic and the             627

                                           project-level tests do not preserve portfolio dependence or a multiplicity family. The        628

                                           approved-plan pool also has an approximately 98% strict-label build rate and almost           629

                                           no renewable projects. These checks are descriptive consistency evidence.                     630

                                     4.    Single benchmark family. All eight scenarios instantiate one 120-candidate pool from          631

                                           one RTS-GMLC + SimBench + NERC derivation. A second pool from independent                     632

                                           public systems would test whether the forward/substitution asymmetry and the                  633

                                           budget-margin pattern generalize.                                                             634

                                     5.    No load-flow verification. Portfolio feasibility is budgetary, not electrical; a              635

                                           pandapower-based load-flow check of selected portfolios is a natural robustness               636

                                           addition.                                                                                     637

                                     6.    The direct local-search comparison is still bounded. Pareto Local Search adds a               638

                                           matched-budget add/delete/swap neighborhood baseline, and BiLo-NSGA signif-                   639




                                                                                                   https://doi.org/10.3390/app1010000
Version August 13, 2026 submitted to Appl. Sci.                                                                                21 of 24



                                           icantly exceeds it in all eight scenarios. However, this is one implementation and             640

                                           does not cover the full family of memetic, large-neighborhood, or exact knapsack               641

                                           algorithms. No universal local-search superiority claim is made.                               642

                                     0.9. Conclusions                                                                                     644


                                           BiLo-NSGA defines local-search moves in project-review terms: insertion under slack,           645

                                     atomic delete–insert substitution, dependency-aware bonuses, feasibility recovery, and               646

                                     per-move logging. On the 120-candidate public proxy benchmark, it improves pooled                    647

                                     hypervolume over NSGA-II by 1.12%. Across five stochastic baselines, it records 36 signif-           648

                                     icant wins among 40 comparisons and no significant loss; 16 deterministic-rule gaps are              649

                                     descriptive and favor BiLo-NSGA. Pareto Local Search is significantly lower in all eight             650

                                     scenarios. The NSGA-II margin ranges from -1.31% to +3.30% across settings.                          651

                                           The component result is asymmetric. Under the primary within-scenario family,                  652

                                     removing forward insertion is significantly harmful in three scenarios but raises the pooled         653

                                     mean. Removing atomic substitution raises the pooled mean by 0.61%, and the contrasts                654

                                     involving atomic substitution and legacy deletion remain unresolved under the declared               655

                                     comparison family. Forward insertion therefore produces the only resolved local-operator             656

                                     gains. Atomic substitution remains useful as a single recorded replacement operation, but            657

                                     its acceptance rule requires redesign before it can be presented as a performance mechanism;         658

                                     move-history statistics never enter optimization ranking.                                            659

                                           The NERC and MTEP16 backtests provide descriptive external consistency. MTEP16                 660

                                     broad capture is 1.071 and the raw point-biserial association is 0.088; strict-label ordering is     661

                                     unresolved. The study consequently supports BiLo-NSGA as a project-level local-search                662

                                     framework on the public proxy benchmark, with forward-only search as the lean configu-               663

                                     ration when atomic replacement records are unnecessary. Expert labels, calibrated costs, a           664

                                     second benchmark family, broader local-search comparisons, and load-flow verification                665

                                     remain necessary to evaluate practical review effectiveness.                                         666
