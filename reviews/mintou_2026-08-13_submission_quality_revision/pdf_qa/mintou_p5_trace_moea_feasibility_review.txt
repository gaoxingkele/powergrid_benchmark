                                          Abstract                                                                                                       1


                                          Utilities reviewing large grid-project queues need portfolio-aware optimization together                       2

                                          with a record of the interventions that shape each recommendation. TRACE-MOEA com-                             3

                                          bines preference-guided ranking, deterministic budget repair, and a quarantined decision                       4

                                          trace that records repair drops and preference-elite injections without entering the evalu-                    5

                                          ation metric. On a reproducible five-objective benchmark of 120 candidate projects, the                        6

                                          method reaches pooled mean hypervolume 0.17425 across seven review scenarios and 30                            7

                                          seeds per stochastic method, 0.89% above NSGA-II (0.17270). Among 28 comparisons with                          8

                                          four stochastic baselines, TRACE-MOEA has 27 positive mean differences and 24 Holm-                            9

                                          significant wins, with no significant loss; 21 gaps against three deterministic ranking rules                 10

                                          are descriptive and all favor TRACE-MOEA. A separate 270-run budget scan yields higher                        11

                                          hypervolume than R-NSGA-II and NSGA-II at all three tested budgets. Component attribu-                        12

                                          tion is narrower: removing preference adaptation changes pooled hypervolume by 0.17%,                         13

                                          and the direct contrast is unresolved after cross-scenario correction. The decision trace                     14

                                          covers 98.6% of projects in returned fronts, but its effect on human review remains untested.                 15

                                          Public reliability and project-outcome checks provide descriptive external consistency                        16

                                          rather than validated review performance.                                                                     17

                                          evolutionary algorithm; preference adaptation; decision traceability; hypervolume; external                   19

                                          validity backtest                                                                                             20

                                          TRACE-MOEA is designed to produce both portfolios and review evidence. A                        38

                                    preference-adaptive layer maintains objective-weight vectors and injects their best-response          39

                                    portfolios as elites. Every five generations, the vectors are perturbed and retained according        40

                                    to the dispersion of their induced responses. Deterministic repair removes low-benefit                41

                                    selections until the budget is met. A quarantined archive timestamps repair drops and                 42

                                    preference-elite injections; no trace statistic enters an objective, constraint, or selection         43

                                    decision.                                                                                             44

                                          Because genuine utility review records with feasibility labels are confidential, evalua-        45

                                    tion is conducted on a reproducible proxy benchmark. The pool of 120 projects is generated            46

                                    by published deterministic rules from RTS-GMLC zones, SimBench networks, and meta-                    47

                                    data from 40 cached public NERC reliability reports. Seven scenarios cover portfolio                  48

                                    optimization, distribution-only review, reliability and renewable preferences, budget tight-          49

                                    ening, preference-aware support, and traceability. A five-objective formulation encodes               50

                                    the investment-effectiveness lens. A two-rung external-consistency ladder then checks                 51

                                    the selected portfolios against a NERC-derived rule and against historical MISO MTEP16                52

                                    project outcomes.                                                                                     53

                                          The paper makes three contributions:                                                            54


                                    1.    An inspectable portfolio-search architecture. TRACE-MOEA combines a constrained                 55

                                          non-dominated sorting kernel with preference-vector updates and deterministic bud-              56

                                          get repair. A quarantined output channel records preference-elite injections and repair         57

                                          drops without feeding trace variables back into the objectives, constraints, or selection       58

                                          rule.                                                                                           59

                                    2.    A reproducible evaluation for large grid-project queues. The five-objective bench-              60

                                          mark contains 120 candidates in six archetypes derived from RTS-GMLC, SimBench,                 61

                                          and public NERC metadata. Seven review scenarios, 30 seeded runs, general-purpose               62

                                          and preference-based optimizers, component controls, and a matched three-budget                 63

                                          scan evaluate front quality, preference distance, computation, and trace production.            64

                                    3.    A separation of optimization quality, component effects, and external consistency.              65

                                          The complete framework improves pooled proxy hypervolume over NSGA-II and the                   66

                                          implemented R-NSGA-II control, whereas the preference-layer removal is unresolved.              67

                                    0.2. Related Work                                                                                    82


                                         Three research threads intersect in this work: investment portfolio decision methods            83

                                    for power grids, preference-guided evolutionary multi-objective optimization, and the                84

                                    traceability of algorithmic decisions.                                                               85



                                    0.2.1. Grid Investment Decisions: Optimization and Review Scoring                                    86


                                          Transmission expansion planning treats the network topology itself as the decision             87

                                    variable and has been surveyed comprehensively from both the modeling [1] and the                    88

                                    practice side [2]; stochastic engineering-economic formulations bring market and regulatory          89

                                    uncertainty into the objective [10]. The present work addresses a different sub-problem:             90

                                    selecting from a pre-specified list of candidate projects under a hard budget, which is the          91

                                    institutional form of most utility investment cycles.                                                92

                                          For this sub-problem, the energy-domain literature relies heavily on MCDA. The                 93

                                    surveys of Wang et al. [3] and Kumar et al. [4] document the use of AHP and TOPSIS across            94

                                    energy investment appraisal tasks, and this tradition remains active: recent studies combine         95

                                    fuzzy-AHP with distribution-transformer maintenance priorities [5] and explainable multi-            96

                                    criteria scoring with strategic energy-investment ranking [33]. Portfolio-explicit alternatives      97

                                    exist in operational research. Robust Portfolio Modeling (RPM) computes non-dominated                98

                                    project portfolios under incomplete preference information [6] and has been applied to               99

                                    infrastructure maintenance programming [7].                                                          100

                                          Recent power-system planning studies have strengthened the application layer                   101

                                    through coordinated transmission-storage planning [29], interruption-cost estimation for             102

                                    reliability investment [30], grid-side storage investment-return analysis [31], and hybrid           103

                                    exact-heuristic treatment of non-analytical investment costs [32]. These studies provide in-         104

                                    creasingly detailed engineering or economic validation, but they optimize system expansion           105

                                    or asset configuration rather than a reviewable selection process for a large, pre-specified         106

                                    project queue. Hybrid grid-investment models also couple risk and benefit dimensions                 107

                                    using MCDA fronts [11]. The remaining gap is therefore not portfolio optimization alone. It          108

                                    is the combination of portfolio-level search over more than one hundred candidates, online           109

                                    preference adaptation, active budget repair, and an exported decision record that can be             110

                                    inspected independently of the optimization metric.                                                  111



                                    0.2.2. Preference-Guided Evolutionary Multi-Objective Optimization                                   112


                                         Dominance-based MOEAs [8] and decomposition-based ones [9,12] treat all trade-off               113

                                    directions as equally interesting, which motivated two decades of research on preference             114

                                    injection into evolutionary multi-objective optimization. Representative approaches include          115

                                    reference-point guidance [13], achievement-scalarizing function methods [14], preference-            116

                                    modified dominance relations [15], and comprehensive taxonomies of a priori, interactive,            117

                                    and a posteriori preference articulation [16,17].                                                    118

                                         Two developments in this thread are particularly relevant to the present design. RVEA           119

                                    demonstrated that reference vectors can be adapted during the run to the geometry of the             120

                                    objective space [18], establishing run-time preference adaptation as a legitimate mechanism–         121

                                    though on continuous benchmarks with no constraint repair and no decision recording.                 122

                                    The second is the field’s empirical evaluation of preference guidance. Li et al. [19] showed         123

                                    that measuring preference-based EMO fairly is itself a hard problem, and a subsequent                124

                                    holistic study found that reference-point guidance can degrade search performance on                 125

                                    some problem classes [20]. The present results extend that caution to a constrained binary           126

                                    portfolio problem: the preference-adaptive layer contributes only weakly on top of a                 127

                                    well-repaired constrained kernel. What no existing work in this thread provides is the               128

                                    specific combination required here: adaptive preference structure on a budget-repaired               129




                                                                                                    https://doi.org/10.3390/en1010000
Version August 13, 2026 submitted to Energies                                                                                          4 of 23



                                    combinatorial problem, with the preference decisions retained for downstream review                          130

                                    rather than discarded after optimization.                                                                    131



                                    0.2.3. Traceability and Explainability of Algorithmic Decisions                                              132


                                         The explainable AI (XAI) literature has matured a vocabulary of transparency and                        133

                                    post-hoc explanation [21] and an account of what human decision-makers accept as ex-                         134

                                    planations [22], but the object of study is typically the learned predictor rather than the                  135

                                    optimization pipeline. The energy-domain XAI survey of Machlev et al. [23] finds ap-                         136

                                    plications concentrated in forecasting and security assessment, not in optimization. For                     137

                                    metaheuristics specifically, explainability has been identified as an open research direction                138

                                    rather than an available toolbox [24].                                                                       139

                                         Provenance research supplies taxonomies of why, how, and where records are gen-                         140

                                    erated [25], while the W3C PROV recommendation standardizes derivation records [26].                         141

                                    The algorithmic-accountability literature further distinguishes process artifacts generated                  142

                                    during computation from post-hoc reconstructions [27]. TRACE-MOEA applies these                              143

                                    ideas inside an MOEA: the decision trace records search interventions as they occur and is                   144

                                    quarantined from the evaluation objective.                                                                   145



                                    0.2.4. Differentiation from the Companion Study                                                              146


                                          BiLo-NSGA [28] uses the same public candidate generator but a different mechanism.                     147

                                    It modifies variation through insertion, substitution, and dependency-aware local moves                      148

                                    under a hard budget. TRACE-MOEA acts at selection and recording through preference                           149

                                    elitism, repair, and a decision trace. It also adds a compliance-and-evidence objective                      150

                                    and uses review-preference scenarios. Both papers apply independently executed NERC                          151

                                    and MTEP16 checks to paper-specific selected portfolios. They share source corpora and                       152

                                    public backtest records, but not method operators, optimization runs, selected portfolios,                   153

                                    or inferential claims.                                                                                       154

                                          The study targets the intersection of four requirements: search over more than 100                     155

                                    grid candidates, adaptive preference coverage, explicit budget repair, and a decision trace                  156

                                    quarantined from evaluation. It also tests whether selected portfolios align with public                     157

                                    project outcomes rather than only with the benchmark objectives. Its empirical scope is                      158

                                    the public proxy and the two external-consistency checks; expert validation of real utility                  159

                                    review remains future work.                                                                                  160

                                    0.3.2. Candidate Pool from Public Data                                                                      193


                                         Real review dossiers are not published, so the pool is generated by deterministic,                     194

                                    released rules from three public sources. Table 1 summarizes the derivation; the full source                195

                                    profile is available at evidence/source/real_project_review_source_profile.csv.                             196

                                         RTS-GMLC zone aggregates (load, branch ratings and outage rates, generator ca-                         197

                                    pacities and renewable shares) produce three archetypes per zone — transmission rein-                       198

                                    forcement, reliability automation, and renewable support — with costs and benefits as                       199

                                    analytic functions of the aggregates. The sixteen highest-stress SimBench subnets each                      200

                                    yield a feeder-reinforcement, a storage-flexibility, and a protection-automation candidate                  201

                                    driven by subnet load, line statistics, and the distributed-resource gap. The metadata of                   202

                                    40 cached public NERC reliability documents (28 event reports) then adjusts attributes at                   203

                                    the archetype level: event-report counts raise reliability values uniformly, inverter-based-                204

                                    resource and storage mentions raise the renewable attribute for the two renewable-facing                    205

                                    archetypes, and document counts feed the evidence score. The result is 120 candidates in                    206

                                    six archetypes with zone- and feeder-level dependency-group structure.                                      207

                                         The epistemic status of this pool must be stated explicitly: it is an engineering-plausible,           208

                                    fully reproducible proxy whose every attribute traces to public data through published                      209

                                    rules. It contains no expert-labeled review outcomes, and its cost coefficients are synthetic               210

                                    units calibrated to grid-case branch and generation statistics rather than to actual utility                211

                                    expenditure records (see Section 8).                                                                        212

                                    0.4. TRACE-MOEA                                                                                                225


                                         Figure 1 separates optimization state from trace state. Preference-guided responses                       226

                                    and deterministic budget repair operate around a constrained NSGA-II kernel, while their                       227

                                    review-specific events are copied through one-way links to an append-only decision trace.                      228

                                    The trace has no return edge: no event count, coverage statistic, or event attribute enters an                 229

                                    objective, constraint, fitness, or selection rule.                                                             230

                                         The algorithm augments a constrained non-dominated sorting kernel with three                              231

                                    switchable components: the preference-adaptive ranking layer, the budget repair operator,                      232

                                    and the decision trace. Each design choice is exercised by a dedicated ablation in Section                     233

                                    0.4.1. Constrained Non-Dominated Sorting Kernel                                                      257


                                          Portfolios are binary vectors evolved by uniform crossover (crossover probability              258

                                    0.9) and bit-flip mutation at rate 1/n, with constraint-dominated non-dominated sorting              259

                                    (feasible solutions dominate infeasible ones; infeasible solutions are ordered by the mag-           260

                                    nitude of budget violation) and crowding-distance truncation. The population size is 40              261

                                    and the generation limit is 40. Initialization uses a low-density strategy (3–15% selection          262

                                    probability per candidate) so that early portfolios begin near the fundable region. The              263

                                    kernel is intentionally unremarkable: the paper’s claims rest on the three review-specific           264

                                    components described below, not on exotic selection machinery.                                       265



                                    0.4.2. Preference-Adaptive Ranking Layer                                                             266


                                         The layer maintains K = 8 objective-weight vectors w_1, . . . , w_K on the five-                267

                                    dimensional simplex. One vector is initialized from the scenario’s stakeholder weighting             268

                                    (Table 2); the remaining seven are sampled from a symmetric Dirichlet(1) distribution. The           269

                                    layer acts twice per generation cycle:                                                               270


                                    •     Preference elitism. For each weight vector w_k, the union of parents and offspring is          271

                                          scored by the weighted sum of min-max-normalized objectives plus a violation penalty           272

                                          for infeasible portfolios. The best-response portfolio (the one with lowest scalarized         273

                                          score) is injected into the next population if it was dropped during non-dominated             274

                                          sorting and crowding-distance truncation. Each injection is logged as an archive event         275

                                          carrying the weight-vector index and the portfolio’s project identifiers.                      276

                                    •     Adaptive re-selection. Every five generations, the current vector set is perturbed             277

                                          with independent Gaussian noise (sigma = 0.1, followed by renormalization to the               278

                                          simplex). The best responses of all K original and K perturbed vectors are computed            279

                                          on the current generation’s combined parent-offspring pool, and the K vectors whose            280

                                          responses are maximally spread in the normalized objective space (determined by                281

                                          greedy max-min dispersion selection) survive into the next five-generation window.             282


                                          The layer is designed for review settings in which a decision maker cannot specify exact       283

                                    reference points in advance, an assumption common in preference-guided EMO (Section                  284

                                    2.2). It identifies weighting directions that yield distinct feasible portfolios and preserves       285

                                    representatives from those directions. Whether this mechanism improves hypervolume is                286

                                    evaluated separately; Section 6.2 shows that its isolated pooled effect is small.                    287



                                    0.4.3. Budget Repair                                                                                 288


                                          Any initial or offspring portfolio that exceeds the budget is repaired by iteratively          289

                                    dropping the selected project with the lowest equal-weight benefit-to-cost ratio (summed             290

                                    reliability, renewable, compliance, and evidence benefits divided by cost) until the portfolio       291

                                    becomes affordable. Each drop event is recorded in the archive with the generation number            292

                                    and the project identifier. The repair operator is deterministic: given the same portfolio, the      293

                                    drop sequence is identical across seeds.                                                             294

                                          The purpose of explicit repair is to keep the population inside the fundable region            295

                                    where the review question is actually posed, rather than leaving constraint pressure to              296

                                    selection alone. The ablation Ablation-NoFeasibilityRepair disables this operator and                297

                                    relies entirely on constraint-dominated sorting for budget feasibility.                              298



                                    0.4.4. Decision Trace Archive                                                                        299


                                        The archive is an append-only log of two event types: repair_drop (from Section 4.3)             300

                                    and preference_elite (from Section 4.2). Each event carries the generation number, the               301

Version August 13, 2026 submitted to Energies                                                                                 9 of 23



                                    affected project identifiers, and for preference-elite events the responsible weight-vector         302

                                    index. Three properties define the archive’s role:                                                  303


                                    •     Completeness for the mechanisms that alter portfolios outside standard variation.             304

                                          Every repair drop and every preference injection is recorded. Standard crossover              305

                                          and mutation operations are not logged because they are the domain-general search             306

                                          operators; the archive captures the review-specific interventions.                            307

                                    •     Quarantine. No archive statistic — not count, not coverage, not entropy — appears             308

                                          in any objective function, constraint inequality, or selection criterion. The archive is      309

                                          purely descriptive, reported separately in Section 6.3.                                       310

                                    •     Descriptive reporting. Across all 210 proposed-method runs (7 scenarios x 30 seeds),          311

                                          the archive averages 1126 events per run, and 98.6% of the projects present in the            312

                                          final feasible non-dominated front appear in at least one recorded event (evidence:           313

                                          real_project_review_leaderboard.csv, row 2, trace columns). This means that for               314

                                          nearly any project that a reviewer sees recommended, the archive can answer when              315

                                          it was protected by which preference direction and which competing projects were              316

                                          dropped during budget repair.                                                                 317

                                    0.7. Discussion                                                                                         559


                                          Preference guidance and the constrained kernel. The three significant comparisons                 560

                                    with NSGA-II occur in preference-emphasized scenarios (Table 5), but the direct NoPrefer-               561

                                    enceRanking ablation is unresolved after correction across its seven scenarios. Preference-             562

                                    injected portfolios appear to overlap those already preserved by crowding, which may                    563

                                    explain the small marginal effect of the adaptive layer. The traceability scenario also favors          564

                                    the risk-blind variant within its scenario family, but the contrast falls just outside the second       565

                                    cross-scenario correction. These patterns motivate targeted tests of preference-vector count            566

                                    and update cadence rather than attribution to a single component.                                       567

                                          Public-record consistency. AHP-TOPSIS is favored on the NERC view partly because                  568

                                    its reliability attributes overlap the priority rule. The MTEP view measures capture within             569

                                    an already-approved plan and is constrained by its high build rate. The two diagnostics                 570

                                    show that optimized portfolios retain some alignment with public records, but they do not               571

                                    establish review accuracy.                                                                              572

                                          Relation to the companion study. BiLo-NSGA [28] asks how variation-stage, project-                573

                                    vocabulary local moves behave under hard budgets. TRACE-MOEA studies selection-stage                    574

                                    preference adaptation and a quarantined decision trace. The problem formulations differ in              575

                                    dimensionality (five objectives versus four), operators, scenarios, and optimization records.           576

                                    Both papers use separately executed NERC and MTEP16 consistency checks on their own                     577

                                    selected portfolios. Sharing the candidate generator and public source records makes data               578

                                    derivation comparable; it does not make the algorithmic evidence interchangeable.                       579

                                          Use in a planning workflow. Against NSGA-II, the pooled hypervolume gain is below                 580

                                    1%. TRACE-MOEA additionally supplies per-project intervention records unavailable from                  581

                                    the baselines in Table 3. These records allow an analyst to inspect which projects were                 582

                                    inserted through preference protection or removed during budget repair. Whether that                    583

                                    inspection improves review time, consistency, or contestability requires human evaluation.              584

                                          The direct R-NSGA-II comparison changes what can be claimed about preference                      585

                                    guidance. TRACE-MOEA performs better on this benchmark in both front coverage and                       586

                                    mean preference distance, including across the three frozen budgets, but the result is not              587

                                    evidence that adaptive weights are universally better than reference points. R-NSGA-II                  588

                                    uses one declared point and one matched configuration; different points, normalization,                 589




                                                                                                       https://doi.org/10.3390/en1010000
Version August 13, 2026 submitted to Energies                                                                                19 of 23



                                    or niching parameters could change its behavior. The defensible advance is that a direct            590

                                    preference-family control now exists and the proposed method survives it under the                  591

                                    disclosed setup.                                                                                    592

                                          Against AHP-TOPSIS, the portfolio-level hypervolume gain is 29.4%, and both back-             593

                                    tests show that the optimized portfolios do not lose all alignment with documented risk.            594

                                    This comparison remains a proxy-objective result, not proof of superior utility decisions.          595

                                    Mean runtime is 0.113 s per run, so seed ensembles and budget what-if analyses are inex-            596

                                    0.8. Limitations                                                                                    600


                                          Seven limitations constrain the scope of the claims in this paper.                            601


                                    1.    Proxy task without expert ground truth. All main-benchmark claims address algo-               602

                                          rithmic performance on a reproducible public proxy whose objectives are engineering           603

                                          proxies. No expert-labeled review outcomes exist. An annotated subset with inter-             604

                                          rater agreement and rank correlation against the proxy objectives remains undone.             605

                                          The NERC and MTEP checks assess descriptive alignment but do not validate review              606

                                          correctness.                                                                                  607

                                    2.    External consistency is assessed descriptively only. Neither rung establishes exter-          608

                                          nal validity because portfolio-preserving confirmatory inference and expert ground            609

                                          truth are absent. AHP-TOPSIS and Weighted Sum also attain the highest capture on              610

                                          individual views. The MTEP check operates within an already-approved plan, with               611

                                          an approximately 98% strict-label build rate and only 19 explicit negatives.                  612

                                    3.    Uncalibrated economics. Candidate costs are synthetic units derived from network              613

                                          aggregates (branch ratings, generator capacities, line lengths); benefits are engineering     614

                                          proxies derived from the same aggregates. No engineering-economic conclusion —                615

                                          in currencies, rates of return, tariffs, or cost-effectiveness thresholds — can be drawn      616

                                          from this paper, and none is offered.                                                         617

                                    4.    Traceability is measured, not validated. The decision archive’s coverage (98.6%)              618

                                          shows that the record is nearly complete, and its quarantine prevents trace statistics        619

                                          from entering objectives, selection, or the reported metric. Whether the archive actu-        620

                                          ally improves board decisions, review speed, stakeholder contestability, or regulatory        621

                                          compliance requires a human-subjects study that has not been performed.                       622

                                    5.    Single benchmark family with no electrical verification. All seven scenarios in-              623

                                          stantiate one 120-candidate pool derived from one combination of public sources.              624

                                          Portfolio feasibility is budgetary (the repair operator enforces the cost constraint) but     625

                                          not electrical: no candidate portfolio has been checked for AC power-flow feasibility         626

                                          using the cached SimBench or RTS-GMLC network models. A second, independently                 627

                                          constructed pool and an OPF-based feasibility check of recommended portfolios are             628

                                          natural extensions.                                                                           629

                                    6.    Preference-layer hyperparameters remain fixed. The adaptive layer was tested with             630

                                          K = 8 weight vectors and one update cadence (every five generations). The three-level         631

                                          budget scan tests constraint tightness and includes a direct preference-based control,        632

                                          but it does not vary K, update cadence, objective weights, or pool size. The design has       633

                                          been evaluated at the tested budgets; the direct optimization benefit of the preference       634

                                          layer remains unresolved.                                                                     635

                                    7.    One direct preference-based comparator is not a family survey. R-NSGA-II closes               636

                                          the absence of a reference-point control and is evaluated under the same population,          637

                                          generations, constraints, and three budget levels. However, the study does not                638




                                                                                                    https://doi.org/10.3390/en1010000
Version August 13, 2026 submitted to Energies                                                                               20 of 23



                                           include RVEA, NSGA-III with preference articulation, or interactive decision-maker          639

                                           feedback. The result supports TRACE-MOEA against the implemented R-NSGA-II                  640

                                           configuration, not superiority over the broader preference-based EMO family.                641

                                    0.9. Conclusions                                                                                   643


                                          TRACE-MOEA combines a preference-adaptive weight-vector population, determin-                644

                                    istic budget repair, and a quarantined decision trace archive. The archive records review-         645

                                    specific interventions without entering the evaluation, while repair maintains budget              646

                                    feasibility throughout search.                                                                     647

                                          On the five-objective, 120-project proxy benchmark, TRACE-MOEA attains pooled                648

                                    mean hypervolume 0.17425, 0.89% above NSGA-II (0.17270). Across four stochastic base-              649

                                    lines and seven scenarios, it has 27 positive mean differences in 28 comparisons, 24 Holm-         650

                                    significant wins, and no significant loss. Twenty-one deterministic-rule gaps are descriptive      651

                                    and all favor TRACE-MOEA. In the separate 270-run scan, TRACE-MOEA significantly                   652

                                    exceeds R-NSGA-II and NSGA-II at all three budgets while retaining the lowest descrip-             653

                                    tive preference distance. The archive covers 98.6% of recommended projects and remains             654

                                    quarantined from the metric.                                                                       655

                                          The NERC consistency backtest yields priority-capture ratios of 1.34–1.55. The MISO          656

                                    MTEP16 backtest yields broad capture of 1.070–1.079 and a raw point-biserial correlation           657

                                    up to 0.169. Because the public-record tests do not preserve portfolio dependence or               658

                                    a comparison family, these values support descriptive external consistency rather than             659

                                    confirmatory above-chance alignment.                                                               660

                                          The preference-adaptive layer changes pooled hypervolume by 0.17%, but its di-               661

                                    rect effect is unresolved after cross-scenario correction. Its observable function is to add       662

                                    preference-labeled interventions to the decision trace. The schedule-risk contrast is likewise     663

                                    unresolved after the second correction, and the external checks remain descriptive. The            664

                                    method is therefore supported as a traceable portfolio-optimization framework on the pub-          665

                                    lic proxy; expert-labeled utility data and human evaluation of the trace remain necessary          666

                                    before drawing conclusions about review effectiveness.                                             667
