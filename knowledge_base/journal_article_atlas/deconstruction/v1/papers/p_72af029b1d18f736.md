## 框架与Symmetry主题

p1 explicitly equates active power symmetry with supply-demand balance. Eq5 command balance is substantive conservation constraint, but no group action/invariance/equivariance proof or dedicated symmetry ablation. Do not classify as mathematically symmetry-preserving neural architecture.

输入为频差、积分、总功率；五类专家生成样本，与在线经历混合训练，策略发总功率命令并经分配器约束。缺样本比例、网络规模、种子、训练步、权重、离策略校正和系统参数，不能复现。
